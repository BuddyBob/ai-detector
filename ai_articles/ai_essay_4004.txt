higher education experts have debated the potential benefits and drawbacks of extending school days for decades. adding one and a half hours to the school day could provide students with more time to complete courses and allow teachers to cover more material. on the other hand, students may become overwhelmed with increased workload and lengthened days could increase stress levels. a prominent argument for adding one and a half hours to the school day is that it could provide students with more diverse learning opportunities. this could include additional courses, clubs, arts education, and tutoring. students may also benefit from having more time to build relationships with peers in a supervised environment. on the other hand, the impact of adding one and a half hours to the school day could be varied and unpredictable. teachers may become overburdened with increased class sizes or with the need to plan additional courses. students may also struggle to keep up with more demanding academic requirements, resulting in a decline in concentration and motivation. ultimately, before extending the school day, it is important to consider the impact that it will have on students and teachers. careful research and planning should be conducted to ensure that any potential benefits outweigh the drawbacks.
