becoming a surgeon requires a great deal of dedication and hard work. after completing undergraduate schooling, individuals must go through four years of medical school to earn a doctor of medicine m.d. or doctor of osteopathic medicine d.o.. after completing medical school, individuals must complete, on average, another five to seven years of residency training in a hospital, depending on the specialty, in order to receive a license. once a license has been obtained, individuals can specialize in various areas such as cardiac surgery, neurosurgery, orthopedic surgery, general surgery, and plastic surgery. committing a large amount of time to the job is essential for those wishing to pursue a career in surgery.
