i generally agree that work today is more stressful than in the past. there are many reasons for this, but one of the main reasons is that work has become more complex. in the past, most people worked in one centralized location, and tasks were largely standardized. nowadays, work is often scattered around various locations and tasks can be very different from one person to the next. this can make it difficult to get a sense of balance and feel like youre actually enjoying your work. another reason work is more stressful today is that we are now constantly multitasking. back in the day, most people only had to worry about one task at a time. today, however, we are constantly switching between different tasks and often have to keep up with multiple deadlines. this can be very difficult and lead to feelings of stress and anxiety. finally, there is the issue of burnout. todays workers are often pushed to their limits and are required to put in long hours without any downtime. this can lead to feelings of exhaustion and frustration, which in turn can lead to stress and anxiety.
