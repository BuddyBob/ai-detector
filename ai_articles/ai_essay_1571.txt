dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the united states should change from the current electoral college system to instead elect the president by popular vote. while the electoral college process was designed with the aim of giving smaller states more influence, recent presidential elections have shown that it can undermine the principle of one person, one vote. the electoral college system discourages voter turnout as it means that many votes essentially do not count towards the national outcome. for example, presidential candidates often focus their resources and time only in swing states that could vote either way, while largely ignoring states that are considered safe wins for one party. this makes the campaign centred around a handful of swing states rather than a nationwide discussion of issues. it also means that many americans feel their votes do not truly matter, as the winner is determined statebystate rather than nationally. adopting a popular vote system could increase participation as every valid vote would directly impact the national outcome. some argue the electoral college protects the interests of small states. however, in recent elections we have seen candidates can lose the national popular vote but still win in the electoral college. this undermines the principle of majority rule. a popular vote would make every citizens vote equal regardless of what state they live in. while the interests of smaller states would still be represented through their congressional elections, the president should be elected based on a purely national majority. a popular vote could also help address concerns about faithless electors who vote against the candidate who won their state. there have been increasing instances of this in recent elections. going to a popular vote removes electors from the process entirely so the outcome would simply and directly reflect thewill of the voters nationwide. in summary, the united states should transition to a popular vote system for electing the president to strengthen our democratic process. every vote would directly impact the national outcome regardless of location. this best upholds the standards of an equal, democratic election. therefore, i urge you to support a transition to electing the president based on a national popular vote total. thank you for your consideration.
