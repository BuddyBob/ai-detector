as technology continues to evolve, the world has become increasingly reliant on artificial intelligence and machine learning. despite their advantages, the increasing use of ai raises a number of ethical concerns about how it should be used. this essay will explore the moral implications of using artificial intelligence ai in our everyday lives from both utilitarian and deontological perspectives. from a utilitarian perspective, ai offers tremendous potential benefits for society at large as its use can increase efficiency and reduce costs by automating mundane tasks that would otherwise require human labor. for example, selfdriving cars are already being tested around the world with great success they promise to not only reduce traffic accidents but also free up millions of hours spent driving every year while reducing fuel consumption and emissions. by freeing people up from tedious manual labour, ai could also provide more leisure time for all members of society which could lead to increased creativity or productivity depending on how it is used. on the other hand, some argue that ai could be misused to create an exploitative system wherein machines take over jobs traditionally performed by humans leading to higher unemployment rates. furthermore, this could lead to a widening gap between those who have easy access and those who dont in terms of job opportunities which exacerbates economic inequality globally thus creating social unrest amongst those most affected by this new trend. additionally theres always a risk that algorithms programmed into these machines may contain underlying bias such as racism or sexism due either through intentional programming or unconscious biases held within development teams which can further exacerbate existing inequalities in society if left unchecked or unmonitored effectively opening up avenues for corruption . . from a deontological standpoint meanwhile, questions arise concerning whether robotsmachines are capable of possessing morality like humans do when making decisions based upon ethical principles? it is argued that treating any form of machine intelligence with respect requires us firstly understanding what goal we want them serve  are we looking towards machines taking on roles typically undertaken by human beings
