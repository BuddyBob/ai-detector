the facial action coding system is a new technology that can detect human emotions. its like a computer program that can read your face and know how youre feeling. some people think its a good idea to use this technology in classrooms to see how students are feeling. but, i dont think its a good idea. first of all, its an invasion of privacy. students should have the right to privacy in the classroom. if a computer is watching their every move and reading their emotions, its like big brother is always watching. its creepy and it makes me feel uncomfortable just thinking about it. secondly, this technology cant always be accurate. sometimes people have poker faces or they might be feeling something thats hard to read. the computer might think someone is feeling happy when theyre actually feeling sad. its not always going to be right. thirdly, this technology could be used to label students. if a student is always showing anger or frustration, the computer might think theyre a problem student. but, maybe theyre just having a bad day or theyre struggling with something personal. its not fair to judge students based on their emotions. lastly, this technology takes away from the human connection. teachers should be able to read their students emotions and connect with them on a personal level. if a computer is doing it for them, it takes away from that connection. its like a robot is teaching the class instead of a person. in conclusion, using the facial action coding system in classrooms is not a good idea. its an invasion of privacy, its not always accurate, it could label students, and it takes away from the human connection. students should have the right to privacy and teachers should be able to connect with them on a personal level.
