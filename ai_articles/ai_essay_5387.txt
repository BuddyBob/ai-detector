nowadays, electronic dictionaries edictionaries have been increasingly popular among students. however, teachers think that the overuse of edictionaries might have more disadvantages than advantages for english learning. i partially agree with this point of view. on the one hand, edictionaries are very helpful tools for looking up words quickly and easily. they provide definitions, synonyms, and examples of how the word is used. this can be very helpful for students who are trying to improve their vocabulary. on the other hand, edictionaries can also be a crutch for students. if students rely too much on edictionaries, they may not develop their spelling ability as well. additionally, edictionaries can be distracting and can take away from the immersive experience of learning a language. in conclusion, i think that edictionaries can be helpful tools, but students should not rely on them too much. they should also try to develop their spelling ability and experience the immersive nature of learning a language.
