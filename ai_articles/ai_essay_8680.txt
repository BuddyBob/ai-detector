the importance of mental health in the workplace cannot be overstated. with an increasingly competitive job market and a tendency toward longer work hours, it is becoming more important for employers to take steps towards ensuring that their employees are mentally healthy and engaged at all times. mental health has a direct correlation to onthejob performance research has shown that when individuals feel supported by their employer, they are able to function better both cognitively and emotionally which leads to increased productivity and morale in the workplace. on the other hand, when employees experience high levels of stress or anxiety due to work demands, it can lead not only to poor job performance but also physical ailments such as headaches or digestive issues. furthermore, if left unchecked these conditions can have long term repercussions such as burnout or depression which can drastically reduce employee engagement and satisfaction with their role. fortunately there are steps employers can take in order to create a safe environment for their staff while addressing any mental health concerns before they become too severe. creating clear pathways of communication between managers and staff so employees feel comfortable speaking up about any issues they may have offering workshops on emotional intelligence increasing access to healthcare providers who specialize in mental health services providing flexible working schedules where possible  this allows employees plenty of time for relaxation activities such as exercise or socializing outside of work hours offering unpaid leave days so that workers may rest if necessary without fear of being penalized financially. . caring for ones workforce should always be high on an organizations list of priorities because without investment into mental wellbeing there will inevitably be consequences in regards to team motivation, efficiency, effectivenessand ultimately profitability! creating policies designed around personal wellness helps demonstrate an organisations dedication towards looking after its people  leading not only happier lives but potentially increasing overall business success too!
