one advantage of studentdesigned projects for summer assignments is that students can use their creativity to develop an individualized project which will likely be more engaging. this allows them to explore their interests while receiving academic credit. additionally, studentdesigned projects allow students to take ownership of their learning, which can be empowering and give an increase in their selfconfidence. on the other hand, teacherdesigned projects can provide structure and focus and help students stay motivated. furthermore, the teacher is looking out for the class as a whole in deciding the focus of the project, thus potentially providing a better framework for success. however, with teacherdesigned projects, individuality may be sacrificed, as the projects are often assigned to a group of students as one whole.
