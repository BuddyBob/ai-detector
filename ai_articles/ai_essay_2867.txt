the potential consequences of disobeying curfew for teenagers can be severe depending on the city or state where the curfew is being enforced. if the curfew is established by law, then it is punishable by law. depending on the severity of the offense, legal penalties can range from fines to jail time. for those who live in a city or state that does not have a statutory curfew, breaking curfew can still result in consequences from the parents such as restricted access to cars and phones, fewer privileges, or grounding. as an alternative to legal penalties, parents can choose to impose other forms of discipline such as discussion, restriction of privileges, or other punishments. ultimately, it is important that parents and teens understand the consequences of breaking curfew and take the necessary steps to ensure it is followed.
