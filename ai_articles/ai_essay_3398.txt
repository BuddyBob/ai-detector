first impressions are an important part of forming relationships, but they can also be difficult to change. on the one hand, first impressions are impossible to change because they are formed in mere moments and can be hard to undo. once someone has formed a first impression of a person, it can last an entire lifetime. for example, if someone makes a bad impression at first glance, this can be difficult to turn around and it could leave a lasting, negative impact on their reputation. on the other hand, first impressions can be changed. it takes a lot of time and effort to right the wrongs of first impressions, but it is possible. for instance, if someone makes a bad first impression, they can take steps to demonstrate they are a kind, thoughtful, and hardworking person. through meaningful and deep conversations, along with good actions, an individual can slowly start to change someones perception of them and form a more positive first impression.
