when it comes to setting curfews for teenagers, it is a difficult and oftentimes divisive decision between parents, children and society. on the one hand, setting a pm weekday and midnight curfew for teenagers instills a sense of responsibility and safety. it sends the message that young people should be careful about the choices they make and that staying out too late can put them at risk. furthermore, it can provide structure and ensure that teens are completing their educational and extracurricular activities, and getting enough sleep to remain healthy and alert for the next day. on the other hand, setting such a restriction on teenagers could appear to be an attempt to prevent them from exploring the world and engaging in ageappropriate activities  such as group events, movies, or other extracurricular activities  and instead keeps them at home in a supervised environment. moreover, it could teach young people that their parents dont fully trust them to make the right decisions and that they should not be expected to be in control of their own lives. overall, whether or not to set a pm weekday and midnight curfew for teenagers is an issue that must be weighed carefully. it is important to consider both sides of the argument and to choose the best course of action that works for each individual family.
