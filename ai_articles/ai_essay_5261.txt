i disagree that work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. in fact, i believe that we have more leisure time and fewer stressful work than before. first of all, we have more leisure time nowadays. this is because technological advancements have allowed us to work more efficiently and get more done in less time. this means that we have more free time to do things we enjoy. for example, with the invention of the computer, data entry and calculation can be done much faster than before, leaving us with more leisure time. furthermore, automation has allowed us to automate mundane and repetitive tasks, meaning that we can spend less time on them and more time on leisure activities. second, the nature of work has changed. with the onset of the digital age, a lot of work can be done remotely. this has allowed us to work from the comfort of our homes, which reduces stress levels and provides more leisure time. additionally, many jobs now have flexible working hours, which further reduces stress and allows us to manage our time more effectively. furthermore, employers now prioritize a good worklife balance, which means that employees are not expected to work long hours and are encouraged to take time off when needed. finally, the concept of leisure has also changed. we now have access to a wide range of activities that are designed to provide us with relaxation and entertainment. this has allowed us to find activities that we can enjoy without feeling stressed. for example, we can take up yoga classes or listen to calming music to help us relax. in conclusion, i do not believe that work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. in fact, advances in technology have allowed us to work more efficiently, have more free time and access a wider range of leisure options.
