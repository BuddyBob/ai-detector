the world has become increasingly interconnected in recent years, with the rise of global networks in various fields, including economics, communication, and transportation. as a result, it is undeniable that every aspect of society, from education to politics, and the sciences, will benefit from international influences. in this essay, i will argue that the growth of global networks will bring about positive changes in various aspects of society. one area that will benefit from international influences is education. today, students can learn about different cultures and perspectives from anywhere in the world through online courses and educational resources. moreover, students can take advantage of study abroad programs to immerse themselves in other cultures and gain invaluable experience. for example, a student in the united states can take an online course from a professor in south africa or study abroad in japan and learn about the local culture and language. by exposing students to diverse perspectives and experiences, the growth of global networks enables them to become more wellrounded and informed individuals. another area that will benefit from international influences is politics. the rise of global networks has made it easier for people to connect and collaborate across borders to address common challenges such as climate change, terrorism, and inequality. for example, the paris agreement on climate change, signed by  countries, is a result of international collaboration and cooperation. the agreement demonstrates that countries can work together to address global problems and create a more sustainable future. additionally, the growth of global networks has made it easier for people to engage in political activism and advocate for their rights. social media platforms such as twitter and facebook have played a vital role in organizing protests and movements worldwide, such as the arab spring and the black lives matter movement. the arts will also benefit from international influences. as artists from different countries and cultures collaborate and share their work, they create new and innovative forms of art that draw on diverse traditions and influences. for example, the fusion of traditional african rhythms with modern electronic music has given rise to the genre of afrobeat, which has become increasingly popular worldwide. similarly, the fusion of western classical music with indian classical music has given rise to a new form of music known as indowestern fusion. finally, the sciences will benefit from international influences. the growth of global networks has made it easier for researchers to collaborate and share their findings across borders. scientists can now access vast amounts of data and collaborate with colleagues from around the world to solve complex problems. for example, the development of vaccines for covid is a result of international collaboration and cooperation among scientists and researchers from different countries. in conclusion, i strongly agree that every aspect of society, including education, politics, the arts, and the sciences, will benefit greatly from international influences. the growth of global networks has made it easier for people to connect, collaborate, and share ideas across borders, resulting in new and innovative forms of knowledge and creativity. as we continue to embrace the benefits of international collaboration and cooperation, we can create a more interconnected and inclusive world.
