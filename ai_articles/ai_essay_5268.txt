in todays fastpaced world, work has become an integral part of our lives. it is often said that work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. in my opinion, i agree with this statement, and in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my answer. firstly, the modern workplace is more competitive than ever before. with the rise of globalization, companies are now competing on a global scale, and this has led to an increase in work pressure. employees are expected to work longer hours, meet tight deadlines, and constantly improve their skills to stay ahead of the competition. this has resulted in a more stressful work environment, where employees are constantly under pressure to perform. secondly, technology has made work more demanding. with the advent of smartphones, laptops, and other digital devices, employees are now expected to be available . this means that they are constantly checking their emails, responding to messages, and working on projects even when they are not in the office. this has led to a blurring of the line between work and leisure time, making it difficult for employees to switch off and relax. finally, the nature of work has changed. in the past, many jobs were manual or involved physical labor, which allowed workers to take breaks and rest their bodies. however, with the rise of the service sector, many jobs now involve sitting at a desk for long periods, which can lead to health problems such as back pain, eye strain, and obesity. this has made work less leisurely and more stressful, as employees are now required to sit for long hours without breaks. in conclusion, work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. the modern workplace is more competitive, technology has made work more demanding, and the nature of work has changed. to address this issue, employers need to create a more balanced work environment that allows employees to take breaks, switch off from work, and maintain a healthy worklife balance.
