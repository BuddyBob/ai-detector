when it comes to learning english, many people believe that both reading and speaking are equally important skills. the reason for this is that both skills help learners to understand the english language and to communicate with others. reading is important because it allows learners to understand the english language on a deeper level. by reading, learners can learn about the grammar and vocabulary of the english language. this knowledge can help learners to improve their english reading skills. speaking is also important because it allows learners to practice their english skills. speaking allows learners to practice their pronunciation and fluency. this practice can help learners to improve their speaking skills. overall, both reading and speaking are important skills for chinese learners of english. both skills help learners to improve their english skills.
