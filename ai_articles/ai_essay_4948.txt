there are two schools of thought when it comes to the media and how it covers famous people. some people think that the famous people are given an unfair shake and that they should be given more privacy. other people believe that this is the price of their fame. i agree with the latter opinion. famous people are in the public eye by choice. they have chosen to put themselves out there and be in the spotlight. with that choice comes the understanding that they are going to be subject to more scrutiny than the average person. the media is going to be interested in their every move and they are going to have to get used to having their lives dissected in the public eye. this is not to say that the media is always fair in its coverage of famous people. there are times when the media can be unfair and intrusive. however, this is the price of fame. famous people have to accept that they are going to be under a microscope and that they are not going to be able to have the same level of privacy as everyone else. in conclusion, i believe that the price of fame is that famous people have to give up some privacy. they have chosen to be in the public eye and they should accept the consequences that come with that choice.
