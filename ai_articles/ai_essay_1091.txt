cell phones are a big part of our lives and its hard to imagine a day without them. but, should we be allowed to use them while driving? in my opinion, the answer is no. firstly, using a cell phone while driving is a major distraction. it takes your eyes off the road and your hands off the wheel. this increases the chances of accidents and its not worth the risk. according to statistics, texting and driving is more dangerous than drunk driving. thats crazy! just think about how many lives could be saved if people didnt use their phones while driving. secondly, its not just texting thats the problem. even talking on the phone is a distraction. you have to concentrate on the conversation and not on the road. and what about when youre navigating? you have to look at the screen and not at the road. its just not safe. thirdly, there are already laws in place to prevent people from using their phones while driving. in most states, its illegal to text and drive. but, people still do it. so, why not make it illegal to use your phone in any capacity while driving? its better to be safe than sorry. lastly, there are alternatives. if you need to make a call or text someone, pull over to a safe location and do it then. its not that hard. or, use a handsfree device. there are lots of options available. in conclusion, using a cell phone while driving is a dangerous and unnecessary risk. its a distraction, its illegal in most states, and there are alternatives. so, drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle. its time to put safety first and make our roads safer for everyone.
