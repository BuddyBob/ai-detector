there is no one answer to this question, as the skill most important for chinese learners of english will vary depending on the individual. however, i believe that speaking is more important than reading, for several reasons. first, speaking is the most natural way to learn a language. when we speak, we use all of our senses to learn the language  we hear the words, see the facial expressions and body language of the speaker, and feel the tone and emotions in their voice. this makes it much easier to remember the words and phrases we hear, and to use them in conversation. second, speaking is a great way to improve your fluency. if you only read english, you may be able to understand the words, but you will not be able to use them in conversation. speaking helps you to use the language in a natural way, and to develop your fluency. finally, speaking is a great way to improve your pronunciation. when you hear the words spoken aloud, you can better understand the pronunciation and how to say them correctly. this is important, especially if you want to be able to understand native english speakers. overall, i believe that speaking is the most important skill for chinese learners of english. it is the most natural way to learn the language, it helps you to improve your fluency and pronunciation, and it is a great way to practice your conversation skills.
