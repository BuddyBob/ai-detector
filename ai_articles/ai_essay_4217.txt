afterschool programs have proven to be a powerful way to help younger students succeed in school. in such programs, older students help younger students with schoolrelated tasks such as homework, projects, and study preparation. the benefits of these programs can be seen in improved understanding of tasks, enhanced time management, and improved social skills. several educational facilities have already established such afterschool programs, which often involve a large number of students. in addition to helping to complete traditional school tasks, these programs present students with the opportunity to engage in a variety of educational and recreational activities. based on the data collected from existing programs, it is proposed that a more comprehensive, organized afterschool program should be implemented. this program would provide students with the necessary support and resources to succeed academically. it could also offer students the chance to be mentored by more experienced peers, allowing them to gain realworld knowledge while having fun at the same time.
