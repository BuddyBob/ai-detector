summer break is a muchanticipated respite from the stresses of school. in recent years, summer breaks have been trending shorter, leaving students with less time to relax or take advantage of travel opportunities. for many international students, summer breaks have become especially short. with limited time off from school, they may not have the opportunity to visit family or explore their own cultures. this can have a major impact on student satisfaction and preparedness for the school year. despite potential challenges, a shorter summer break can still provide some benefits. it gives students the time to learn about different cultures and gain knowledge from historical sites, as well as hone their skills in areas such as language and cultural fluency. however, the negative effect of a shorter summer break cannot be ignored. students may not have enough time to travel or visit family, resulting in more stress and limited opportunities to form meaningful connections. ultimately, it is essential to investigate the opportunities and drawbacks of shorter summer breaks in order to explore the best ways of preparing students for the school year. ultimately, a balance needs to be struck in order to ensure both academic and personal success.
