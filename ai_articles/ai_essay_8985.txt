sportsmanship is an important concept that is often overlooked. it involves not only the action of playing sports, but also the respect for ones opponents, teammates, and even referees. sportsmanship can manifest itself both on and off the field in many different ways. on the field it involves playing fairly within the rules of any given sport as well as displaying good behavior such as cheering after a great play by either team or shaking hands with opponents after a game. off the field it can be showing proper etiquette when interacting with others at sporting events or supporting your team without being overly aggressive towards other teams fans. by demonstrating good sportsmanship athletes help create an environment of mutual respect between fans, players and coaches alike which enhances everyones experience with competitive sports. in order to foster an atmosphere of fair play in youth athletics parents should lead by example and demonstrate strong levels of sportsmanship while attending their childrens games or practices. they should refrain from criticizing officials calls from sidelines instead they should focus on motivating their child to do her best regardless of whether she makes mistakes or not during a game or practice session. parents are also encouraged to use postgame discussions between themselves and their child to teach lessons about practicing resilience rather than pointing out plays where errors were made this approach helps instill values such as resilience that will remain beneficial far beyond just onfield success in youth athletic endeavors. . parents may choose to further emphasize good sportsmanship among young athletes by becoming involved in league organizations like little league baseball or pop warner football these
