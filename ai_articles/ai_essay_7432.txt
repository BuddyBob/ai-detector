if you want to become a doctor, you need to conform to the medical system and its rules. by conforming to these systems, individuals can build successful careers that are recognized by society. team members need to conform to the groups norms and expectations. for example, if a team is working on a project, everyone needs to contribute their unique skills and talents while also conforming to the groups overall vision and goals. by doing so, the team can achieve success that is recognized by the organization. thirdly, conformity helps individuals navigate social hierarchies and power structures. in many organizations, there are strict hierarchies and power structures that individuals need to navigate in order to succeed. by conforming to these structures, individuals can gain access to resources, opportunities, and mentorship that can help them advance their careers. for example, if an individual wants to be promoted to a higher position in their organization, they need to conform to the expectations of their superiors and demonstrate their ability to lead and manage others. finally, conformity can help individuals build strong networks and relationships that can lead to economic success. in order to build strong networks, individuals need to conform to the norms and expectations of the communities they wish to join. for example, if an individual wants to build a network of investors, they need to conform to the expectations of the investment community and demonstrate their ability to generate returns on investments. by doing so, they can build strong relationships that can lead to economic success. in conclusion, while individuality is important, conformity is necessary for personal economic success. conformity helps individuals fit into society and succeed in their chosen careers, succeed in group settings, navigate social hierarchies and power structures, and build strong networks and relationships. while it may feel counterintuitive to
