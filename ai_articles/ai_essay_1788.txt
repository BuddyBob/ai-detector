while some believe the face on mars is evidence of ancient aliens, the facts tell a different story. as a scientist at nasa who has studied this landmark extensively, i can explain that the face is simply a natural landform formed through normal geological processes over millions of years. many initial observations of the face seemed to suggest artificial, or designed, elements. it appeared symmetrical from above with obvious eye and mouthlike features. however, upon closer examination, what appeared symmetrical is actually quite asymmetrical. the eyes and mouth are of uneven sizes and shape. natural formations often take on pareidolic, or facelike, appearances without being intentionally designed. scientists have also taken core samples from the area to analyze the geological layers and composition. the results show the face is comprised of the same scree slopes and bedrock common across the cydonia region of mars. infrared images further prove the material making up the face is identical to the surrounding terrain. if it were artificially constructed, one would expect to see different materials used. erosion over eons best explains the formation of what can initially appear to be a designed shape. wind has sculpted away at the landscape, hollowing out sheltered areas below steep slopes to form overhangs that project faces into space photos. changing temperatures and seasons exacerbate this natural weathering process. given enough time, even random patterns in rock can take on misleading iconic forms through erosion alone. with evidence the face on mars consists of ordinary geological strata and demonstrates patterns of natural weathering common across the planet, the most logical conclusion is that it too was formed through normal geological activity rather than alien intervention. while some might prefer more exciting explanations, as scientists we must follow the facts, and in this case they point to a natural rather than an artificial origin for this martian landmark.
