there is no one definitive answer to this question. some people believe that the best way to travel is independently, while others believe that traveling with a group led by a tour guide is the best way to go. there are pros and cons to both options, and the best way to travel ultimately depends on the individual travelers preferences and needs. some people prefer to travel independently so that they can go at their own pace and make their own itinerary. this allows them to explore a destination in depth and to see things that they may not have otherwise seen if they were traveling with a group. additionally, independent travelers often have more flexibility in terms of their schedule and can change their plans on a moments notice if they hear about something interesting that they want to check out. on the other hand, traveling with a group led by a tour guide has many advantages. for one, group travel is often more affordable than traveling independently. additionally, group travel can be a great way to meet new people and make friends. tour guides are also experts on the destinations that they visit and can provide valuable insights and information that independent travelers would not be able to get on their own. ultimately, the best way to travel is the way that best suits the individual traveler. some people prefer to travel independently, while others prefer the convenience and structure of group travel. the important thing is to consider your own needs and preferences and to choose the option that will make your travel experience the best it can be.
