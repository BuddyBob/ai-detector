wont limiting car usage make it harder for us to get around? well, not necessarily. by investing in good public transportation systems, like buses and trains, we can still get where we need to go without relying on cars. and lets not forget about the benefits of carpooling and ridesharing  not only do they reduce the number of cars on the road, but they can also save us money on gas and reduce traffic congestion. finally, limiting car usage can actually help our communities become more connected and livable. when we rely on cars too much, we tend to spend more time isolated in our vehicles and less time interacting with our neighbors and community members. by walking or biking, we can see and engage with our surroundings, which can help build a stronger sense of community. so, what can we do to limit car usage?
