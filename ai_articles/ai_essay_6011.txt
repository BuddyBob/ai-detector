there are a few reasons why travel as a group is the best way to travel. first, it allows for a more personalized experience. tour guides can take care of all the details so that everyone feels like a part of the group. second, traveling as a group can save you money. instead of paying for individual tickets, you can save by purchasing travel insurance and planning your trip together. finally, it allows for a more communal experience. instead of spending individual time with friends or family, you can spend the trip together and get to know each other better.
