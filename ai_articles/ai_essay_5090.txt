the skill of english that is more important for chinese learners is speaking. there are many reasons why speaking is more important than reading. first, speaking is the most basic skill that students need in order to communicate in english. second, reading can be done in any language, but speaking is limited to the language that is being spoken. third, reading is passive, while speaking is active. finally, reading requires time, while speaking can be done immediately. all of these reasons make speaking more important than reading for chinese learners. however, there are also some benefits to reading. first, reading can help students learn new vocabulary and grammar. second, reading can build a strong foundation for speaking. finally, reading can be fun.
