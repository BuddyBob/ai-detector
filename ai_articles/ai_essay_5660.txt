there are pros and cons to both sending paper and electronic cards for holiday greetings. on the one hand, electronic cards are cheaper, faster, and easier to send on the other hand, paper cards are more personal and tactile. i think that, overall, electronic cards are the better option. they are cheaper because you dont have to pay for postage, and they are faster because you dont have to wait for the card to be delivered in the mail. they are also easier to send, because all you have to do is input the recipients email address and hit send. however, electronic cards do not have the same personal touch as paper cards. you cant feel the texture of the paper, you cant smell the card, and you cant flip through the pages to see all the different pictures. paper cards are also more customizable, because you can choose the design, the font, and the text. in my opinion, the pros of electronic cards outweigh the cons. they are cheaper, faster, and easier to send, and they still convey your holiday wishes.
