it is true that young people today do not devote as much of their time to helping their communities as they used to. however, this is not necessarily true of all young people and there are still ways that young people today can contribute to their communities. one reason young people may not be contributing to their communities as much as they used to is because of the increased demands of their lives. today, young people often have to balance school or work, extracurricular activities, and social obligations all at the same time. this leaves little time for volunteering and community service. additionally, young people today face an increasingly competitive job market and often have to work longer and harder to obtain the same opportunities as their predecessors. this can leave them feeling drained and with little enthusiasm for volunteering. however, even with these demands, young people today are still able to contribute to their communities in meaningful ways. for example, they can participate in virtual volunteering, which allows them to participate in activities such as writing articles, designing websites, and creating social media campaigns from the comfort of their own homes. additionally, young people can volunteer their time in more traditional ways, such as participating in cleanup days in their local parks or helping out in local shelters. in addition to volunteering, young people can also contribute to their communities by participating in advocacy efforts. for example, they can join local organizations that are working to bring about social and political change in their communities. they can also use their voices to support causes that are important to them by participating in marches, writing letters or articles, or speaking out on social media. finally, young people can contribute to their communities by simply being engaged and informed citizens. they can attend local government meetings, participate in voter registration drives, and stay up to date on local news and issues. in conclusion, it is true that young people today do not dedicate as much of their time to helping their communities as their predecessors did. however, there are still ways that young people can contribute, such as through virtual volunteering, traditional volunteering, advocacy efforts, and civic engagement. by doing so, young people can ensure that their communities are better places to live and work.
