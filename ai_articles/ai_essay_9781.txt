going on a hike is not only an enjoyable activity, but it can also be a great way to learn about the environment. whether you are just starting to explore hiking or have already been out on more than one trail, there are many ways that taking part in this form of recreation can benefit your life. for starters, hiking is physically demanding in a good way. it forces you to use your body and muscles differently than when sitting at a desk all day or lounging around the house. the varied terrain found while exploring trails helps build strength and endurance while providing plenty of fresh air and sunshine. hiking is perfect for those looking to get into better shape but do not enjoy going to the gym or find running too boring even short hikes give good physical results over time!. another major advantage of hitting the trails is that it gives us an opportunity to connect with nature in its purest form  without technology getting in the way. taking some time away from our screens allows us completely immerse ourselves within different habitats and appreciate their beauty firsthand rather than just observing them through pictures and videos online. even if you dont leave with any particular knowledge gained from hiking, chances are you will feel much more relaxed afterwards due to being surrounded by calming natural scenes during your journey outdoors!. going on hikes also provides us with new perspectives regarding environmental issues such as climate change, conservation efforts, deforestation etc., which we may not necessarily think about otherwise when stuck inside four walls all day long. through careful observation along trails we begin seeing whats actually happening right before our eyes  how landscapes are changing due to human influence or lack thereof, how animals interact with each other depending upon their surroundings etc. this awareness can help inform decisions later down the road when voting for politicians dedicated towards protecting public
