there are pros and cons to both paper and electronic cards, but in general, i believe that electronic cards are better. one pro to paper cards is that they are more personal. you can handwrite a message on the card, and this creates a more intimate connection with the recipient. electronic cards, on the other hand, are sent through email and lack the personal touch of a paper card. another pro to paper cards is that they are more tactile. they feel nice to hold and can be fun to open. electronic cards, on the other hand, are often just text on a screen and lack the sensory experience of a paper card. one con to paper cards is that they can be more expensive. you have to purchase the card, the envelope, and the postage, and this can add up. electronic cards, on the other hand, are often free and can be sent to a large number of people at once. another con to paper cards is that they can be more timeconsuming. you have to find a card, write a message, put it in an envelope, and mail it. electronic cards, on the other hand, can be sent with just a few clicks. overall, i believe that electronic cards are better than paper cards. they are cheaper, faster, and more personal.
