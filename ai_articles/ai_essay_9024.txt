the musical stylings of the s new wave genre revolutionized popular music in ways that are still felt today. emerging from the postpunk and protopunk scenes, this genre combined elements of punk rock with modern synthpop, creating a truly unique sound that is immediately recognizable even decades later. the influence of s new wave can be seen in many aspects of current music culture, such as fashion and style. the era saw some iconic bands come to fruition, including duran duran and the human league they created hits like rio and dont you want me. their songs featured a mix of synthesizers and electric guitars which produced a sound unprecedented at the time. additionally, their colorful outfits were often inspired by art movements like new romanticisma clear indication that artists were no longer afraid to express themselves through fashion choices. at its core, s new wave was all about being experimental with sounds and styles while also embracing technology to create something entirely different than what had been done before it. this daring innovation resulted in an incredibly memorable era for pop culture as a whole  one marked by powerful female voices e.g., cyndi laupers girls just wanna have fun vibrant fashion trends neon, big hair scrunchie, shoulder pads, cosmic imagery e.g., the band devos energy dome hats. even now, some elements remain relevant for example neon has made a recent comeback on runways around the world as well as among streetwear brands. overall, it is clear that s new wave not only left an indelible mark on society but continues to shape our present day music scenefrom songwriting influences to visual aesthetics  proving itself to be more than just another passing trend .
