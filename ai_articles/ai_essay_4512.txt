the primary objective of university education is a matter of much debate. some believe it is primarily a tool to prepare students for employment, while others think itserves other functions as well. overview of a particular subject. by taking classes in a wide range of topics, students can gain a greater understanding of the different aspects of their chosen field. this can help to give them a invaluable skill for any future employee, as the ability to think critically can help them to make better decisions and solve complex problems. third, university education can also help to develop a students social and communication skills. through interacting with other students, lecturers, and potential employers, students are able to practice their communication skills. in addition, they can gain valuable insights into the world of work, which can give them an edge in the job market. finally, university education can provide students with the opportunity to develop a broad range of skills
