the question of whether artists or critics provide society with something of lasting value is one that has been debated for centuries. some argue that critics are vital to the artistic process, providing feedback and evaluation that helps artists improve their work. others believe that it is the artists themselves who create something truly timeless and valuable. in my opinion, it is the artist who ultimately gives society something of lasting value, and i will explain my reasons in this essay. first and foremost, it is the artist who creates something out of nothing, bringing their unique perspective and creativity to the world. whether it is a painting, a novel, a piece of music, or a film, it is the artists imagination and skill that brings it into existence. without the artist, there would be no art to critique. critics, on the other hand, are simply reacting to what the artist has already created. while criticism can be helpful and informative, it is ultimately the artist who is responsible for creating something that resonates with people and stands the test of time. furthermore, art is a deeply personal and subjective experience, and what one person finds valuable or meaningful may not be the same for another. critics often have their own biases and preferences, and their evaluations may not reflect the true value of a work of art. for example, the impressionist painters were often criticized and dismissed by art critics of their time, but today their works are considered some of the most valuable and influential in the history of art. this demonstrates that the true value of a work of art may not be immediately recognized or appreciated by critics, but it can still have a lasting impact on society. additionally, the value of art is not just in its aesthetic qualities, but also in the ideas and emotions it expresses. art has the power to move people, to challenge their beliefs, and to inspire them to think and feel in new ways. it can capture the essence of a particular time and place, or offer a timeless message that resonates across generations. this is something that cannot be measured or evaluated by critics alone, but is instead a result of the artists vision and intention. to illustrate this point, consider the works of shakespeare. while his plays were certainly appreciated in his time, it is only through the passage of centuries that his genius has truly been recognized. his plays offer insights into the human condition that are still relevant today, and continue to be performed and studied around the world. it is the power and depth of shakespeares writing that has given it lasting value, not the opinions of critics who may have dismissed or praised it in his time. in conclusion, while critics certainly have a role to play in the artistic process, it is ultimately the artist who gives society something of lasting value. through their creativity, imagination, and vision, artists bring new ideas and experiences into the world that can inspire, challenge, and move people in profound ways. the value of art cannot be measured by critics alone, as it is a deeply personal and subjective experience that speaks to the human soul. it is the artist who creates something truly timeless and valuable, and it is our duty as a society to recognize and appreciate their contributions.
