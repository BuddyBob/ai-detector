it is often said that pragmatism is the key to success in any field of life whether it is academic, social, business, or political. practicality is seen as the cornerstone of successful individuals and entities. however, i disagree with this statement on the grounds that idealism and pragmatism are not mutually exclusive. in this essay, i will explore the strengths and weaknesses of both idealism and pragmatism. firstly, let us examine the advantages of pragmatism. pragmatism is the practice of dealing with problems in a sensibly and realistically way that is based on practical rather than theoretical considerations. pragmatism focuses on what works and what can be done, rather than what should be done. this approach is beneficial in many ways. for instance, it is less timeconsuming than idealistic approaches, it is more flexible, and it allows for quick adaptation to changing circumstances. in business, for example, a pragmatic approach might mean focusing on shortterm goals and objectives, while also keeping an eye on the longterm vision. in the current fastpaced world, where trends and technologies change rapidly, businesses need to be agile and adaptable to succeed. a pragmatic approach will help businesses to adapt quickly to new trends and technologies, while also keeping their eye on their longterm goals. however, idealism also has its strengths. idealism is the practice of pursuing ideas and moral principles rather than practicality. idealism focuses on what should be done rather than what can be done. idealism is beneficial because it allows for innovation, creativity, and the pursuit of higher ideals. idealism is often what leads to groundbreaking scientific discoveries, artistic masterpieces, or political revolutions. in science, for example, idealism has led to many significant discoveries that have changed the world. scientists such as albert einstein, isaac newton, and galileo galilei were idealists who pursued their ideas even when they seemed impossible or impractical. their idealism led to discoveries that changed the world and advanced human knowledge. in conclusion, while pragmatism has its advantages, idealism is also necessary for success in any field of life. it is important to strike a balance between the two approaches. pragmatism is necessary for achieving shortterm goals and adapting to changing circumstances, while idealism is necessary for pursuing higher ideals and pushing the boundaries of what is possible. therefore, we should not dismiss idealism in the pursuit of pragmatism. instead, we should seek to integrate the two approaches in order to achieve the best possible outcomes.
