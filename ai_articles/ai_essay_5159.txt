it is indisputable that education is expensive. however, the consequences of a failure to educate can be even more expensive, both for the individual and for society as a whole. consider the individual. a failure to educate can mean a lifetime of poverty and unemployment. it can also mean a lifetime of living in a society where one is unable to take full advantage of the opportunities that are available. consider also the consequences for society as a whole. a failure to educate can mean a workforce that is not able to meet the needs of businesses and a population that is not able to participate in the democratic process. it can also mean a population that is not able to take full advantage of the opportunities that are available in a globalized world. in conclusion, it is clear that education is expensive. however, the consequences of a failure to educate can be even more expensive. this is why it is so important to ensure that all members of society have access to education.
