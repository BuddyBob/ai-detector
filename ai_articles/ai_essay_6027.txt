some people believe that it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than it is for them to learn facts. i believe that understanding ideas and concepts is more important for students than learning facts. for example, lets say you are studying for a test. you know the material well, but you want to learn more about the topic. you could read articles, watch videos, or take notes. but if you only want to know the information, you will not be able to pass the test. on the other hand, if you understand the concepts behind the material, you will be able to understand the material better and pass the test. for example, if you know the difference between an algebraic equation and a geometric equation, you will be able to understand the material better and pass the test. so, understanding ideas and concepts is more important for students than learning facts.
