online classes and homeschooling offer several benefits to students struggling in school, those considering dropping out, and those eager to finish classes quickly. for students struggling in school, they can provide an alternate way to learn in a safe and comfortable environment with tailored, oneonone instruction. for students who are considering dropping out, online classes or homeschooling can provide an alternate way to complete their course requirements and receive a diploma without having to face the traditional school setting. for students who would like to finish classes quickly, online classes or homeschooling can provide them with the flexibility to move at their own pace and speed up their education. through these methods of education, all sorts of students can find success and motivation in their studies.
