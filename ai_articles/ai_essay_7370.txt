in todays world, luxuries and conveniences are a large part of everyday life and many people view them as harmless. however, it can be argued that these luxuries and conveniences, while seemingly harmless, can prevent people from developing into truly strong and independent individuals. on the surface, luxuries and conveniences can appear to make life easier and more enjoyable. for instance, modern technology, such as computers and smartphones, can provide access to a wealth of knowledge and entertainment, while also streamlining mundane tasks. similarly, modern conveniences, such as washing machines, dishwashers, and microwaves, can make everyday tasks much simpler and quicker. all of this can make life more comfortable and enjoyable but it can also lead to unhealthy dependence and potential feelings of entitlement. in the past, people had to rely on their own physical strength and skills to do things such as chop wood, build furniture, and hunt for food. as a result, people developed strength and resilience over time, as well as a sense of independence and selfreliance. in contrast, todays luxuries and conveniences can lead to a lack of engagement with the physical world, leaving people dependent upon technology and outside sources for even the most basic of tasks. this can result in a lack of physical and mental strength, and limit a persons ability to take care of themselves and survive in an independent manner. in addition, many luxuries and conveniences can lead to feelings of entitlement and a distorted sense of reality. for example, people may become used to instant gratification and become less willing to work hard for something. this can lead to a lack of motivation and ambition, as well as a false sense of superiority, where people start to expect the world to revolve around them. all of these things can prevent people from developing into strong and independent individuals. to conclude, luxuries and conveniences, while seemingly harmless, can have an adverse effect on a persons development. they can lead to physical and mental weakness, dependence, and feelings of entitlement, which can all inhibit a persons ability to develop into a truly strong and independent individual. therefore, it is important to be mindful of how luxuries and conveniences can affect our lives, and strive to strike a balance between comfort and selfreliance.
