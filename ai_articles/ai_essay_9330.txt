rowing is one of the oldest competitive sports in the world and has developed into a highly technical yet enjoyable activity. its an excellent fullbody workout that can be enjoyed on both still water, such as calm lakes or reservoirs, and moving water like rivers or oceans. despite its long history, rowing remains one of the most popular recreational activities today due to its physical benefits and unique sense of adventure. the sport begins with choosing a boat suited to your skill level whether its an oarboat scull for independent rowers, a pair of oars controlled by two people or even four person boats for larger groups. rowers then learn how to properly maneuver their boat using technique learned from experienced coaches  including proper footwork when shifting body weight for balance  as well as learning how to use muscles efficiently while maintaining good posture throughout each stroke cycle. in addition to providing great physical conditioning through aerobic exercise and strength training, rowing also encourages strong mental discipline among participants who must work together as a team in order to achieve success in competition. with practice comes an increased understanding of individual strengths that allows teams to build upon them in order create fast times across different distances. . on top of these physical and mental benefits, spending time outdoors on the water provides rewards that cannot be found anywhere else  calming waters with beautiful scenery often make up part of any given route making for peaceful breaks between strokes which are accompanied by birdsong echoing off lake shores or distant waves crashing on beaches depending on where you choose to go rowing!. whether done recreationally amongst friends or competitively amongst peers at regattas around the country  rowing offers something special unmatched by other activities a chance at being truly present out amidst nature whilst taking advantage of all that this timeless sport has offer!
