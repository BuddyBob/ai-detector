common for them to skimp on the amount of sleep they are getting. from tests and homework to extracurricular activities and social obligations, modern teens have their hands full with responsibilities, making it difficult for them to get a good nights rest. the consequences of not getting enough shut eye can be severe when it comes to teenagers. without adequate rest, concentration levels and alertness tend to plummet while exhaustion increases over time. as a result students may suffer from poor academic performance due diminished ability to focus or retain information. furthermore lack of energy can lead teens further away from physical activity which is necessary for staying fit and healthy leading in turn to higher risk for obesity and other health issues such as diabetes or heart disease later down the line. additionally research suggests that chronic sleep loss could even lead teens towards addictive behaviors like smoking or drinking alcohol as an attempt at self medication against fatigue induced stress. unfortunately many parents who see their children struggling may try pushing harder rather than finding solutions that would help relieve burden from schoolrelated tasks so kids can get some much needed rest . on top of this , media overload has become a major factor here with constant access by phone , tablet or computer devices , impacting
