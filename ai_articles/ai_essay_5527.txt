there is no doubt that our society has become increasingly wasteful in recent years. we are producing more and more plastic bags and other forms of rubbish that are difficult to dispose of properly. this is having a negative impact on our environment, as the rubbish accumulates and causes pollution and other problems. there are several reasons why this is happening. firstly, many people now see disposable items such as plastic bags as convenient, and are not willing to go to the extra effort of carrying a reusable bag. secondly, companies are producing more and more disposable items, often using nonbiodegradable materials, in an effort to make more money. and finally, many people do not realise the damage that their waste is doing to the environment, and do not take the necessary steps to dispose of it properly. all of these factors are contributing to the development of a throwaway society. we need to become more aware of the problems that this is causing, and take steps to reduce our wastefulness. we can do this by using reusable bags and other items, and by disposing of rubbish properly. if we all make a concerted effort to do this, we can make a real difference to the health of our environment.
