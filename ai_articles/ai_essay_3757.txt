having multiple opinions can be beneficial when making a choice. hearing different perspectives can make it easier to consider all of the pros and cons. listening to others can provide insight into both why a choice should be made and why it should be avoided. it can also prompt creative solutions for the issue at hand. additionally, advice from close friends or family can be a huge help in making decisions. they are likely to be more informed about the situation and can provide a unique perspective. for these reasons, getting input from multiple sources can be a great way to make a better decision.
