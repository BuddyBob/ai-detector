many people consider the importance of a balanced diet to be essential for overall health and wellbeing. in todays hectic world it can often be difficult to ensure that we get everything our body needs from the foods we eat, but there are several key steps which should be taken in order to make sure our nutritional needs are met. one of the most important elements of a balanced diet is eating plenty of fresh fruit and vegetables every day. not only do they provide us with important vitamins and minerals, they also contain powerful antioxidants which can help protect us against disease. additionally, as many fruits and vegetables have high water content, they contribute significantly towards helping us stay hydrated throughout the day without consuming unhealthy beverages such as sugary soft drinks or energy drinks. eating at least five servings per day is recommended for adults by nutritionists around the world. another way to maintain a healthy balance in our diets is by limiting processed foods wherever possible. these items tend to be very high in sugar, salt, saturated fats or transfats  all things that should generally avoided if we want optimum physical health over time due to their potentially dangerous side effects on cholesterol levels, blood pressure etc. furthermore, these types of foodstuffs often lack any real nutritional value so its best practice for them not comprise too large a portion of ones daily caloric intake if youre looking for more than just an immediate feeling satisfaction!. finally when considering how much carbohydrates versus proteins versus fats you should consume each day its worth bearing in mind your own lifestyle choices athletes may need more carbs while those trying to lose weight may require fewer calories overall
