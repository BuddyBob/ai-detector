there is no question that celebrities are often treated unfairly by the media. the press is constantly intruding on their privacy, and they are often the target of vicious rumors and gossip. however, i believe that this is the price of their fame. celebrities are public figures, and as such, they have a responsibility to the public. they cannot expect to have the same level of privacy as ordinary citizens. in addition, they are often paid very handsomely for their work, and so they should not complain too much about the intrusion of the press. there are, of course, some exceptions. for example, it is not fair to stalk celebrities or to invade their homes. however, in general, i believe that celebrities should accept the fact that they are public figures and that they will be treated accordingly by the press.
