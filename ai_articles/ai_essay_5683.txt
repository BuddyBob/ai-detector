in todays world, environmental concerns have become a priority for many people. one of the ways to reduce paper waste is by using electronic cards instead of paper cards for holiday greetings. while some people believe that electronic cards do not have the same charm as paper cards, i disagree. in my opinion, electronic cards have their own unique flavor and offer several advantages over paper cards. firstly, electronic cards are more convenient than paper cards. with electronic cards, you can send your greetings to multiple people at once, without having to worry about postage or delivery times. this is especially useful for people who have friends and family living in different parts of the world. additionally, electronic cards can be customized with personal messages, photos, and videos, making them more personal and meaningful than paper cards. secondly, electronic cards are more environmentally friendly
