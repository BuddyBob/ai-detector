on issues in ways that no speaker or reformer ever could. one reason why scandals can be useful is that they often bring attention to important problems that might have otherwise gone unnoticed. for future. in conclusion, while scandals can be seen as negative occurrences that should be avoided at all costs, i believe that they can be useful in the sense that they have the power to focus our attention on important problems, lead to important reforms and changes, and serve as cautionary tales. while scandals are never pleasant or desirable, we should not automatically dismiss them as useless or harmful. instead, we should recognize the potential benefits that they can bring, and use them as opportunities to learn, grow, and improve our society.
