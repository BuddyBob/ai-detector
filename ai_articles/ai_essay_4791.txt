there are two schools of thought when it comes to teaching children how to interact with others  teaching them to compete or to cooperate. those who believe in teaching children to compete think that it will help them in life, as they will need to be competitive in order to be successful. those who believe in teaching children to cooperate think that it will help them get along better with others, which is an important life skill. i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. one reason for this is that it is important for children to get along with others. if they can learn to cooperate at a young age, they will be more likely to have successful relationships with others as they grow older. another reason is that cooperation is a key ingredient for teamwork. if children can learn to work together, they will be able to accomplish tasks that they could not do on their own. finally, cooperation is important for problem solving. if children can learn to cooperate and come up with solutions to problems, they will be better prepared to handle difficult situations as they grow older. there are many benefits to teaching children to cooperate. i believe that it is the most important life skill that they can learn.
