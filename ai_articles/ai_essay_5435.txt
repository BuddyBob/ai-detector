nowadays, more and more students use electronic dictionaries. in fact, the number of students using them has been increasing steadily over the past few years. the main reason for this is the fact that they are convenient and easy to use. they can be used in the classroom, at home, and even at work. however, there are some disadvantages of using electronic dictionary. first of all, it is not possible to check the accuracy of the information provided by the computer. therefore, if you are not sure about the spelling of a word, you will not be able to correct it. another disadvantage is that it can take a long time to find the correct information. this is especially true when you have a lot of information to look through. it is also possible that you may miss some important information because you do not have the time or the patience to go through it all. finally, some students find it difficult to keep up with all the new words that are being added to their dictionary at the same time. if you find yourself in this situation, then it might be a good idea for you to consider using a paper dictionary instead of an electronic one.
