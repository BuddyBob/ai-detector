graduating high school early is an increasingly common option for students with an advanced academic track record. early grads are faced with a number of benefits and drawbacks that are unique to their situation. for example, early grads often get more homework and less downtime than their traditional fouryear graduation counterparts. they must also balance increased coursework with prestigious higher education opportunities as well as other life commitments. graduating early also comes with its consequences. stress, depression, and a sense of disconnection from friends and family are some of the most frequently cited drawbacks of graduating early. furthermore, those who graduate early may have to move away from home when they head off to college. ultimately, the pros and cons of graduating early should be evaluated and analyzed on a casebycase basis. to make the right decision, its essential to consider the students individual academic and career goals. some students may find graduating early advantageous, but others may realize that sticking with a traditional fouryear high school experience is in their best interest.
