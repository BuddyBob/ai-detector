connection  customers can request rides through apps with just a few clicks, often within minutes of making their order. the same technology also allows users to track the progress of their journey in offer fixed rates based on distance travelled which allow riders to calculate fares upfront without having to worry about being overcharged by drivers during peak times or when traveling longer
