our knowledge has always been a fundamental part of who we are. as technology advances so too does our capacity for learning, with every generation making leaps that allow us to explore further than before. in this essay we will discuss the evolution of technology in education and its effect on teaching methods throughout history. in ancient times it was mainly oral tradition which carried much of what was known from one generation to another  stories told around campfires passed down cultural values, laws and religious beliefs as well as giving people access to their own historical events and accounts. the advent of writing meant that ideas could be documented reliable enough for future generations but still posed challenges when it came to sharing information across large distances
