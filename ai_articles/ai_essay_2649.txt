online school offers several advantages when compared to traditional, inperson schooling. for example, when attending online classes, students dont have to worry about their clothing and appearance. this means they can work in the comfort of their own home, dressed however theyd like. along the same lines, students are not subject to the social pressure of their peers when attending online courses. on the other hand, while online schooling can be more convenient, the workload stress can be detrimental to student success. with traditional school, there is an instructor present to keep students on task, providing an overview of class work expectations. with online schooling, this structure is not always present, meaning it can be difficult for students to stay focused when they dont have the same level of guidance. finally, one of the key drawbacks to online school is the lack of extracurricular activities. while traditional school allows for students to participate in events such as plays and team sports, these activities are often more sparsely available in the online realm. this is of course due to the virtual nature of the school setting. without peers and tangible events to participate in, students run the risk of feeling a lack of community and companionship.
