everyone has experienced firsthand the importance of participating in extracurricular activities in school. from personal experience, i know that engaging in one of these activities can have a huge impact on your life. being part of a team or organization helped me make friends and build bonds. it gave me an opportunity to meet people who had similar interests and passions. on top of that, i found myself becoming more productive and staying organized with the commitment of the responsibilities it brought. it was by being part of these activities that i discovered new passions and hobbies. without taking part in these afterschool activities, i would have never been able to find my own callings. extracurricular activities are essential in selfdiscovery. it gives one a chance to develop skills and selfconfidence. it helps us appreciate the importance of teamwork and encourages us to have a sense of belonging. the opportunities for growth are endless. therefore, it is important for everyone to participate in at least one extracurricular activity. doing this could change ones life in a positive way and could ultimately open the door for opportunities of a lifetime.
