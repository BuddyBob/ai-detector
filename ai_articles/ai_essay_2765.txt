mandatory community service may be an effective tool in helping students to develop empathy and civic engagement. through this, students will become familiar with the rewards of helping underserved populations, understanding the struggles of those less fortunate than themselves. not only would this help students become more engaged citizens, but it would also create an avenue of connection between students of different backgrounds. schools would benefit from having students with a more developed sense of responsibility and compassion, as well as increased camaraderie between students. the end result is young people with the ability to be more civically engaged in their future life and work, as well as more connected within a diverse community.
