the university education is not just for preparing students for employment. there are many functions that the university education can serve. one function of the university education is to provide students with the opportunity to explore different fields of study. this allows students to gain a better understanding of the world around them and to develop skills that they may not have had the opportunity to learn in other settings. another function of the university education is to provide students with the opportunity to develop relationships with other students. these relationships can be beneficial in the future. for example, if a student is looking for a job, he or she may be able to find employment through connections made during the course of the university education. the university education also provides students with the opportunity to learn new skills. for example, students may learn how to write a research paper or how to speak in front of a group. these skills can be valuable in the future. finally, the university education can provide students with the opportunity to learn about different cultures. this is especially important for students who are planning to work in a foreign country. by learning about the culture of the country they are planning to work in, students are less likely to have cultural clashes when they arrive on the job.
