i disagree with the proposed policy change that students must have at least a b average in order to participate in sports or other activities. although academic excellence should be encouraged, this policy could have a negative effect on the school community, as it would unfairly restrict students from participating in activities that foster team building and social engagement. the policy could also negatively impact students with lower academic achievements or those who struggle with learning disabilities, who may find it too difficult to meet the required grade average. instead of a strict b average requirement, i believe schools should focus on helping their students excel in academics while emphasizing the importance of extracurricular activities, which can often help students emotionally and socially.
