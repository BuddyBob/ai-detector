to say that children should learn to cooperate would be to say that they should be taught to put the needs of others before their own. this may be a difficult concept for some children to grasp, but ultimately it is the most beneficial way to learn and grow. one example of why it is important for children to learn to cooperate is in the sport of gymnastics. gymnasts must be able to cooperate in order to perform their routines. they must be able to balance on one foot while holding onto the other, and they must be able to do all of this while performing a flip. if each gymnast were to focus on their own performance and not on how to help the rest of the team, the routine would not be as successful. another reason why it is important for children to learn to cooperate is in the classroom. teachers may ask students to work in groups to complete a project or to participate in a game. if all of the students are trying to do their own thing, the task may be difficult or impossible to complete. by cooperating, the students are able to work together and achieve their goals. finally, learning to cooperate is important for the development of all aspects of a childs life. by working together, children are able to build relationships and develop skills they may need in the future. this includes skills such as teamwork, problem solving, and communication.
