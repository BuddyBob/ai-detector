for centuries. it is a unique form of visual expression involving intricate folding and shaping of paper into objects such as animals, flowers, or abstract figures. in its simplest form, origami only requires two tools  paper and fingers  yet in skilled hands these materials can create amazing works of art that astound even nonartists with their complexity and elegance. the origins of origami are shrouded in mystery however many historians believe it originated from china sometime around  ad before spreading to japan around  ad where it has since flourished over the centuries. during this time period, different cultures created variations on traditional designs which resulted in a wide range of styles being developed across asia. originally used as part of religious ceremonies or special events such as weddings or births, origami eventually evolved into a popular recreational activity for children who delighted at turning flat squares into various shapes like birds or boats with just a few simple folds. today there are numerous types and styles of origami ranging from simple beginner projects to complex advanced models that require dozens sometimes hundreds of steps to complete properly. while beginners may be intimidated at first by all the nooks and crannies involved in making these complex designs each piece is actually composed from basic geometric shapes including squares, triangles, polygons etc., so mastering even difficult models becomes much simpler when broken down into those components parts. with practice anyone can learn how to make beautiful pieces using this timeless craft!. origami continues to evolve today inspired by modern trends featuring contemporary themes like pop culture characters combined with classic techniques refined over
