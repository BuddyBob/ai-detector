when it comes to the topic of personal health, few topics are as important as understanding and managing your stress. stress can be incredibly damaging for both physical and mental wellbeing, and so having strategies in place to limit its effect on our lives is essential. the importance of managing our stress levels has become even more significant over the last year due to the covid pandemic. this sudden change in lifestyle has seen people from all walks of life facing new challenges that are difficult to manage without feeling overwhelmed by emotions such as anger, fear, anxiety or sadness. it is understandable that when faced with so much uncertainty many find themselves unable to cope with increasing levels of stress. thankfully there are now a number of ways we can manage our own mental health. one step could be taking time out each day for activities such as meditation or yoga which help us learn how to relax and focus on ourselves instead of worrying about external factors outside our control. another way could be talking through any anxieties you might have with friends or family  something that may seem uncomfortable at first but will ultimately allow you process your feelings in a safe environment free from judgement. practicing selfcare is also essential whether its making sure you take regular breaks throughout the day away from screens or setting aside some time for yourself once a week where you focus solely on doing something enjoyable like reading a book or going for a walk  these small steps can make an enormous difference over time!. ultimately no matter what challenges we face good selfcare routines should always include strategies for reducing stress levels wherever possible  because only then can we ensure both emotional stability and long term physical wellbeing remains intact!
