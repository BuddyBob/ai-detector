drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while driving in todays world, cell phones have become remarkably integrated into our daily lives. however, using a cell phone while driving can be extremely dangerous and should be avoided. there are several reasons why drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity when operating a vehicle. first and foremost, cell phone use significantly increases accident risks. numerous scientific studies have found that drivers who use cell phones are much more likely to get into crashes serious enough to injure themselves or others. this is because cell phones demand attention that should be focused on the road instead. even handsfree usage impairs concentration and awareness of surroundings. secondly, cell phone conversations and other distractions greatly reduce reaction time needed in emergency road situations. a drivers hands should remain on the wheel and their eyes on the road ahead to anticipate and respond to potential hazards like suddenly stopped traffic. texting or talking ties up both mental and physical faculties better dedicated to safe vehicle operation. reaction times are noticeably slower when the mind is split between phone and road. in addition, cell phone involvements undermine traffic laws. drivers on cell phones are prone to speeding, ignoring signssignals, drifting between lanes, tailgating and other violations arising from lack of attentiveness. not only does this endanger themselves, but puts everyone sharing the road at risk of harm from reckless behavior. allowing phones permissions counteracts the cooperative regulations in place to structure orderly and safe transportation. in summary, cell phone technology should not come at the cost of human lives lost to preventable accidents. individual convenience is outweighed by collective safety on public roads. stricter laws banning all cell phone handling behind the wheel aim to deter deadly distractions and curb the significant risks of multitasking phones with multiton vehicles. for the protection of all citizens on the road, drivers full focus must remain with traffic, not telecommunications, while in control of motion machinery.
