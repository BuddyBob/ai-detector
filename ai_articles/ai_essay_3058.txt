talking to multiple people about a difficult situation is often the best course of action. when you speak to just one person, there is a limit to how much they can help you since they only know what you tell them. additionally, having a conversation with more than one person can provide you with a diverse range of perspectives on the situation. through hearing different viewpoints, you may gain insight that can help you more effectively address the issue. speaking to multiple people also allows you to create a support system that you may not get from talking to a single person. each person can offer a unique style of comfort or advice based on their personal experiences that can be beneficial to your situation. this can help bring to light possible solutions which you may have never considered on your own, as well as reinforce the idea that you are not alone in tackling the issue. overall, it is better to talk to multiple people when dealing with a difficult situation. doing so can provide you with a range of perspectives, create a support system, and give you more insight to the problem. ultimately, this can help ease the burdens that come with a difficult situation while allowing you to better address it.
