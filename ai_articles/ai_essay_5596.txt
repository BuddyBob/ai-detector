it is true that in many institutions, ones promotion is largely decided by whether one has obtained a graduate degree or not. however, i disagree that if this tendency goes to the extreme, young people may be misled. first of all, a degree certificate can reflect not only ones academic achievements but also ones potential. for example, when a student is applying for a job, the employer will not only look at the students academic performance but also his or her ability to work as a team, communication skills, and other soft skills. thus, a degree certificate can be used as a way to measure ones potential. secondly, a degree certificate can be used to open up more opportunities. for example, if one has obtained a graduate degree, he or she may be able to apply for more competitive jobs, which would offer better salary and better career prospects. in addition, having a degree certificate can also be seen as a sign of commitment and dedication, which can be a great asset for any organization. finally, a degree certificate can also be used as a way to measure ones knowledge and skills. for example, if one has obtained a degree in a certain field, it can be seen as a sign that he or she has mastered the necessary knowledge and skills for that particular field. this can be very beneficial for employers, as they can be assured that the candidate is qualified for the job. to conclude, i believe that a degree certificate can be a great way to measure ones academic achievements, potential, and knowledge and skills. thus, i disagree that if this tendency goes to the extreme, young people may be misled.
