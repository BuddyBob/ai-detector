i agree that for any work of art to have merit, it must be understandable to most people. without the audiences ability to comprehend the work, it cant truly be appreciated. in order for an artwork to be understood, it must have a clarity to it that is not too abstract or difficult to comprehend. a great example of a work of art that is understandable to most people is the novel to kill a mockingbird by harper lee. this novel is beloved by many people for its themes of justice, discrimination, and morality, and its characters are vibrant and easily comprehended. despite the fact that the novel takes place in the s, many of the themes and issues that it explores still resonate with readers today. this is because the novel is written in a way that is accessible to many people regardless of their age or background. as a result, it is able to move and inspire its audience in a way that is often not possible with more difficult works of art. the same can be said for a work of art such as a song. a great example of a song that is understandable to most people is imagine by john lennon. this song is beloved by many around the world because of its powerful antiwar message. the lyrics are clear and direct, and the message is easily understood by those who listen to it. it is able to communicate its message in a universal way that is difficult to do with more abstract works of art. in contrast, there are works of art that are difficult to understand. an example of such a work is the film the tree of life by terrence malick. while the film is visually stunning, it is quite abstract in its narrative and symbolism. as a result, many people find it difficult to comprehend, and it does not have the same impact on its audience as a more straightforward work of art like to kill a mockingbird. in conclusion, i believe that for any work of art to have merit, it must be understandable to most people. without the audiences ability to comprehend the work, it cant truly be appreciated. works of art such as to kill a mockingbird and imagine are great examples of how art can have a powerful impact on its audience when it is easily understood.
