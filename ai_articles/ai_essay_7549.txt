the rise of technology has brought unprecedented changes to the world, transforming the way people live, work and learn. one of the most significant impacts of technology has been on the travel industry, making it easier than ever before for people to explore different parts of the world. although television and worldwide computer connections have enabled humans to become familiar with places they have never visited, tourism will never become obsolete. in this essay, i will explain why tourism is still relevant, despite technologys influence on travel. firstly, television and computer connections can only provide a limited experience of a place. while they can give people an idea of what a place looks like, they cannot provide the full sensory experience that visiting a place can offer. for example, a tv show or youtube video may show the beaches of hawaii, but it cannot capture the smell of the ocean, the feeling of the sand between your toes, or the sound of the waves crashing against the shore. furthermore, tv shows and online content are often edited or curated to show only the most appealing aspects of a destination, and may not represent reality accurately. therefore, people who rely solely on these sources to form an opinion about a place may be disappointed when they actually visit. secondly, tourism provides economic benefits to local communities that technology cannot replicate. when tourists visit a place, they often stay in hotels, eat at restaurants, and buy souvenirs, creating jobs and boosting local economies. according to the world tourism organization unwto, the global tourism industry generated approximately  of the worlds total gdp in , and created millions of jobs worldwide. in contrast, watching tv shows or online content about a place does not provide any economic benefit to the local community. thirdly, tourism can have significant cultural and educational benefits. when people travel, they are exposed to new ways of life, different cultures, and diverse perspectives. this can broaden their understanding of the world, and promote tolerance and empathy towards people from different backgrounds. moreover, experiencing a place firsthand can be a powerful educational tool, as it enables people to see and learn about things that cannot be conveyed through a screen. for example, visiting museums and historical sites can help people gain a deeper appreciation of the worlds cultural heritage, and understand how events in the past have shaped the present. in conclusion, while technology has made it easier for people to become familiar with places they have never visited, tourism will never become obsolete. travel provides sensory experiences that cannot be replicated through a screen, and supports local economies. moreover, tourism promotes cultural exchange and education, enriching peoples lives in ways that technology cannot. therefore, while technology will undoubtedly continue to shape the way people travel, it will never replace the value of actually visiting a place.
