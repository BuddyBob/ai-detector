there is no denying that electronic cards are more environmentally friendly than paper cards. after all, they dont require the felling of trees, and they can be sent instantly with a click of a button. however, some people still prefer paper cards for holiday greetings, myself included. there are a few reasons for this. first, paper cards are more personal. when you receive a paper card in the mail, it feels like a more thoughtful gesture than an email. the sender has taken the time to pick out a card, write a message, and put it in the mail. its a physical reminder that you are cared for and thought of. second, paper cards can be saved and cherished. they can be put up on the fridge or in a scrapbook as a reminder of the holidays. electronic cards, on the other hand, are easily deleted and forgotten. finally, paper cards support local businesses. many small businesses, such as card shops and stationery stores, rely on holiday card sales to stay afloat. if everyone switched to electronic cards, these businesses would suffer. in the end, its up to the individual to decide whether paper or electronic cards are best for holiday greetings. personally, i will continue to use paper cards because i feel they are more personal and meaningful.
