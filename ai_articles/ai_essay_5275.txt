work has undergone significant changes in the past few decades, and it has become a topic of debate whether it is more stressful and less leisurely than it was in the past. in my opinion, i agree with the statement, and in this essay, i will explain why i believe so. firstly, the world has become increasingly competitive, and as a result, work has become more challenging and demanding. with the advent of globalization, companies face stiff competition from all over the world. this has led to companies trying to increase productivity and profits, which has resulted in longer work hours and increased work pressure. employees are expected to work long hours, meet strict deadlines, manage multiple tasks, and perform at their best, which can be very stressful. secondly, technology has changed the way we work. while technology has made many things easier, it has also added to work stress. employees are expected to be available around the clock, respond to emails and messages promptly, and be in constant communication with colleagues and clients. this has made it difficult for employees to switch off from work, leading to a blurring of work and personal life. lastly, there has been a shift in work culture. in the past, there was a clear distinction between work and leisure. employees had set work hours and were encouraged to take breaks and vacations. however, in todays work culture, employees are expected to be available at all times, and taking time off is often frowned upon. this has led to increased work stress and burnout. in conclusion, work has become more stressful and less leisurely than it was in the past. factors such as increased competition, changes in technology, and a shift in work culture have contributed to this. it is essential for employers to recognize the impact of these changes on their employees wellbeing and take steps to mitigate the stress and promote a healthy worklife balance.
