through this research project, we aim to investigate whether educational activities can be enjoyable. to answer our primary question, we will need to examine what types of educational activities people find enjoyable, and how individual preferences affect their enjoyment. we must also consider the benefits of engaging in educational activities that are enjoyable versus those that are not. finally, we must assess whether the benefits of enjoyable educational activities are greater than those of their nonenjoyable counterparts. by answering these questions, we will determine if educational activities can, indeed, be enjoyable.
