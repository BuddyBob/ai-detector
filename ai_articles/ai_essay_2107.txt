throughout history, many successful artists have gained perspective and wisdom from the criticism they have received. notable figures such as vincent van gogh and m.c. escher learnt from constructive criticism on their works despite not seeing the success they eventually did until after their deaths. in the educational field, criticism is just as important as praise when helping students reach their potential. receiving criticism is an essential part of building resilience and establishing a growth mindset. in learning, students should be encouraged to challenge their understanding of topics and not be afraid of making mistakes. you cannot learn without taking the risk of making mistakes and potentially receiving criticism, but this should not be seen as a negative thing. by offering students constructive criticism, teachers can create an environment where it is encouraged to ask questions and take risks in learning. students should also understand that criticism can be more than just the negative aspect that it is often seen as. it can also be an invaluable source of feedback that can expose them to new ideas and concepts. analyzing criticism can help students to learn and to understand concepts from a different perspective. with this understanding, students can acquire the skills of resilience and problemsolving. overall, the role of criticism as a tool of learning is essential, and its importance should be emphasized in the educational field. while positive reinforcement plays an important role, constructive criticism helps bring a complete educational picture, and in some cases, foster resilience and problemsolving skills in students.
