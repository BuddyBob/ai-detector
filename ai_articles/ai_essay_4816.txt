some people think that children should learn to compete, but others think that children should be taught to cooperate. i agree that children should be taught to cooperate. here are three reasons why. first, when children learn to cooperate, they learn to work together toward a common goal. this is important because it teaches children how to compromise and how to resolve conflicts. it also teaches children that they can achieve more when they work together than when they work alone. second, when children learn to cooperate, they learn to respect each others differences. this is important because it helps children to understand that everyone is unique and that we all have something to offer. it also helps children to be more tolerant of others and to be more accepting of differences. third, when children learn to cooperate, they learn to communicate with each other. this is important because it helps children to understand each others needs and feelings. it also helps children to develop strong relationships with each other. in conclusion, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. cooperation is an important skill that children need in order to be successful in life.
