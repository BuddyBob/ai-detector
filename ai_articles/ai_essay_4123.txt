extended school hours would not be in the best interest of students. forcing additional hours of attendance can lead to students not paying attention in class, feeling resentful towards teachers, and can even cause an uprising among students. this extended time can really be put to better use by making these extra hours to be an optional afterschool activity. this can give students the opportunity to learn more about life and the school that they are attending. learning outside of the classroom can also be more engaging for the students, since the topics that they are already studying can be explored more indepth.
