dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the electoral college system for electing the president of the united states should be replaced with a popular vote. while the electoral college was designed to ensure that smaller states still have a voice in presidential elections, evidence suggests it no longer serves this purpose and instead undermines the principle of one person, one vote. supporters of the electoral college argue that it encourages candidates to build broader coalitions and avoid focusing only on large states. however, in recent elections we have seen candidates concentrate their efforts and resources only in socalled swing states that could vote either way. this means that voters in the vast majority of states, whether reliably democratic or republican, are effectively discounted. a candidate could even lose the nationwide popular vote but still win the electoral college, as happened in  and . this undermines faith in our democratic system. it is also argued that without the electoral college, presidential candidates might ignore the interests of small states. however, campaign visits and attention are still strategically focused only on a handful of swing states under the current system. moreover, a popular vote would not change the fact that candidates must still appeal to a wide range of viewpoints. swing voters across all states, not just in top prize states, would still determine the outcome. some worry a popular vote could lead some states being taken for granted by candidates since their votes are assured. but large population centers already decide the outcome in their states. a national popular vote would value each individual voter equally rather than some votes counting more than others based simply on their state of residence. this aligns better with the principle of one person, one vote that is so fundamental to our democratic system. in conclusion, while the electoral college may have served other purposes in the past, evidence shows it no longer achieves its intended aims and instead undermines equal representation. i therefore believe it is time to replace this system with a popular vote that values all citizens votes equally. this would strengthen faith in our elections and help restore confidence that presidential candidates truly represent the will of the nationwide majority. for these reasons, i encourage you to support changing to a popular vote system.
