children should be taught to cooperate because it is a life skill that will help them in their personal relationships and in the workforce. in personal relationships, cooperation is key. if two people are constantly competing with each other, they will eventually drive each other crazy. cooperation allows people to work together to achieve a common goal. in the workforce, cooperation is also important. if everyone in a company is constantly competing with each other, the company will not be successful. cooperation allows people to work together to achieve a common goal. some people might argue that competition is a necessary life skill. however, i believe that cooperation is a more important life skill. cooperation allows people to get along with others, while competition can cause animosity and rivalry.
