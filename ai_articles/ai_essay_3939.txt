failure is an essential part of the journey to success. initially, failure can be demoralizing, but it is also a chance to learn and grow. taking calculated risks, trying new endeavors, and stepping out of ones comfort zone all lead to possible failure  and, ultimately, our biggest successes. one example of success through failure is thomas edisons invention of the electric light bulb. after numerous failed attempts, edison was undeterred and finally achieved success when he figured out a way to improve the carbonized filament. in addition to his newfound success, edison also gained wisdom and experience from his failures, allowing him to continually innovate and improve on his inventions. another example of failure leading to success is the story of disneys  movie snow white and the seven dwarfs. even though the movies plot was revolutionary for its time, disney encountered delays, budget cuts, and even rumors that the movie wouldnt be a hit. despite the setbacks which could have deterred many filmmakers disney embraced the challenge and the movie eventually became a huge success, garnering disney its first oscar. the success stories of edison and disney demonstrate how failure can actually fuel the motivation to keep trying. when faced with difficulty, disappointment, and delay, success is only possible if we can rise up and embrace failure as an opportunity to learn and strive for an even better outcome. we must accept failure as part of the process and use it to our advantage. failure is not an endpoint, but a bridge to success.
