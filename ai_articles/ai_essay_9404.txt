responsibilities among team members as well as predetermined deadlines on deliverables. without this type of guidance, organizations may find themselves spinning their wheels without making much progress towards the ultimate goal. creating and implementing a strategic business plan allows companies to analyze how efficiently tasks are being completed while also providing ways for them increase efficiency where needed and boost overall performance levels throughout the entire operation. in addition, creating and executing a successful strategy helps improve customer satisfaction by
