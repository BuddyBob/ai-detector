in todays technologically advanced world, it is rare to see an individual without a cell phone. it has become a crucial part of our daily routine, aiding us in communicating with friends and family, navigating through new areas, and even completing workrelated tasks. while the use of cell phones has increased drastically, the debate regarding the use of cell phones while operating a vehicle has also intensified. some people believe that drivers should be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle, while others think differently. firstly, those in favor of using cell phones while driving argue that it is essential in case of an emergency. a driver may need to call emergency services or a family member urgently. in such situations, using a cell phone can prove to be lifesaving. in addition, using gps navigation on a cell phone has become a common practice, especially for individuals who drive in unfamiliar areas. banning the use of cell phones while driving would make it challenging for drivers to navigate through new places, increasing the chances of accidents. on the other hand, those against using cell phones while driving argue that it is a significant distraction that diverts the drivers attention from the road. using a cell phone while driving, even for a split second, can lead to a fatal accident. studies have shown that drivers using cell phones are more likely to be involved in accidents than those who do not. additionally, the use of handsfree devices is also distracting and does not eliminate the risks associated with using a cell phone while driving. in my opinion, drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle. while using a cell phone in case of an emergency may seem necessary, there are alternative ways to seek help. drivers can pull over to the side of the road to make emergency calls or ask a passenger to do so. additionally, drivers can use traditional maps or seek directions before starting the journey to avoid using a cell phone while driving. in conclusion, while using a cell phone while driving may seem necessary in certain situations, the risks associated with it far outweigh any benefits. the use of cell phones while driving should be banned to ensure the safety of all individuals on the road. it is our responsibility as citizens to prioritize safety over convenience.
