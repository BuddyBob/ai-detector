technology has become a major part of modern society. it has revolutionized the way people live and work, and it has made many aspects of life easier. as technology advances, more and more time, money, and energy is being put into developing new and more elaborate technology. however, while developing new technology can bring great benefits, it can also be wasteful. in this essay, i will argue that society should focus more on maximizing the use of existing technology for the immediate benefit of its citizens, rather than devoting too much time, money, and energy to developing new and more elaborate technology. first, it is important to note that new technology can be expensive. not only do companies have to invest in research and development, but they also have to pay for the materials and labor needed to produce the technology. additionally, developing new technology can be very timeconsuming, with the process often taking years to complete. this means that the money and energy spent on developing new technology could be put to better use elsewhere. second, existing technology can be used in more effective and efficient ways. for example, many companies are using artificial intelligence and machine learning to automate mundane tasks and streamline their operations. this not only saves time and money, but it also reduces the need for human labor. additionally, existing technology can be used to improve existing services, such as healthcare and education. by utilizing existing technology, these services can become more efficient and more accessible, improving the lives of citizens. third, investing in existing technology can have a more immediate impact on society. for example, investing in renewable energy sources can help reduce air pollution and improve public health. additionally, investing in efficient transportation networks can help reduce traffic congestion and improve commute times. by investing in existing technology, society can quickly see the results and experience the benefits. finally, it is important to remember that technology can be a doubleedged sword. while new and more elaborate technology can bring great benefits, it can also be used as a tool of oppression. by focusing on maximizing the use of existing technology, society can ensure that the technology is used for the benefit of all citizens. in conclusion, it is clear that society should focus more on maximizing the use of existing technology for the immediate benefit of its citizens. not only can this help save time and money, but it can also have a more immediate and direct impact on society. by investing in existing technology, society can ensure that the technology is used for the benefit of all citizens.
