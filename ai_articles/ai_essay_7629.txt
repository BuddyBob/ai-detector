institutions. as such, the public funding of the arts can help to lift people out of poverty, particularly those in underserved communities. moreover, the public funding of the arts is necessary for diversity of cultures that exists in the world. that being said, i understand the concern that public resources should be directed towards more practical needs such as alleviating hunger and poverty. however, i believe the public funding of the arts can actually help to address these issues. by providing opportunities for creative expression and economic growth, public money can help to improve the quality of life for those in need. furthermore, public resources can be used to help fund organizations and institutions that are dedicated to social justice and the alleviation of poverty. in conclusion, while the public funding of the arts may appear inappropriate or even cruel when people in a society are suffering from hunger or poverty, i strongly believe that public resources should
