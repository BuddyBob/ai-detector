today the majority of humans own and operate cell phones on a daily basis. while cell phones have become integrated into many aspects of modern life, they should be banned from use by drivers for the following reasons cell phone use while driving can be dangerously distracting. texting, talking, scrolling social media or otherwise engaging with a phone takes the drivers eyes off the road for extended periods of time. numerous studies have shown that drivers engaged with phones have slower reaction times, miss traffic signs and do not see potential hazards as quickly as drivers who are fully focused on operating the vehicle. this kind of avoidable distraction has contributed to many traffic accidents and even deaths over the years. engaging with a phone not only draws visual attention away from the road, but also cognitive attention. operating a vehicle safely requires full awareness of everything happening around one  nearby cars, pedestrians, traffic lights and signs, road conditions. a driver busy talking or texting cannot give the task of driving their undivided focus. this decreases their ability to drive responsibly and predictably, increasing risks for themselves and others. while handsfree options allow for calling without physically touching a phone, they do not solve the cognitive distraction problem. studies have not found handsfree phone use to be significantly safer than handheld use. the brainpower used to converse or listen can still take the place of, and delay, reactions needed for safe vehicle operation. fully banning all cell phone use, including handsfree, is warranted on public safety grounds. obeying traffic laws is paramount to ensuring orderly and accidentfree flow of vehicles. allowing unrestricted cell phone use would undermine this. a full ban provides a clear boundary and enforcement mechanism, sending the message that driving requires singleminded focus on the road. alternatives like only banning texting still allow dangerous forms of distraction. overall, the risks of permitting any phone use behind the wheel far outweigh any perceived benefits. for the safety of all citizens who share the road, cell phones should remain prohibited for drivers.
