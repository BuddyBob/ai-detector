the advent of online school has ushered in a new era for young scholars. the potential benefits of online school are manifoldit can make higher education more accessible to students with disabilities or illnesses, provide a more flexible learning environment for students, and open up opportunities for them to gain employment. on the other hand, some drawbacks of online school should not be overlooked. the prevalence of technology in every home is not a given, thus access to computers and the internet can be quite a challenge for many students, particularly in some socioeconomic communities. nevertheless, the potential for online school to help both those with disabilities or illness and the economy, should not be discounted. with the right technological infrastructure and support, online school could become an option for all school districts, allowing students to access educational opportunities that would otherwise be unavailable.
