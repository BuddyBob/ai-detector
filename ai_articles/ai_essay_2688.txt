when considering the pros and cons of a school day one hour and thirty minutes longer, there are several different points to consider. on the one hand, adding an hour and a half to the school day would give students more time to cover material in their classes, which could benefit their grades and increase their opportunities for success. additionally, it could provide more time for students to complete afterschool activities such as sports and arts programs, as well as give students extra time to complete their homework or participate in extra study sessions. on the other hand, a longer school day could mean an overall decrease in the amount of free time students have to themselves. this could lead to unnecessary stress, as students feel overwhelmed by the amount of work they have to complete in a day. additionally, it could also lead to exhaustion, as students can become worn down by having less time to rest and recharge. in conclusion, there are both positive and negative aspects to consider when contemplating whether or not to change the length of the school day. ultimately, it is important to carefully think through and weigh these different factors in order to make an informed and beneficial decision.
