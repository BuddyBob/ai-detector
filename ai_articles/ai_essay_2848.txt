selfsufficiency is a key component of personal growth and development, allowing one to make their own decisions and be more responsible for their actions. developing the ability to think independently and trust ones own judgement can provide greater control over ones life. however, seeking guidance from experts can be beneficial for understanding complex situations as it can provide helpful advice and support. this external help can help to develop skills and provide valuable guidance. ultimately, it is a matter of personal preference and the individuals individual needs should be taken into consideration to decide the most beneficial option. making ones own decisions can lead to a greater sense of independence but relying on external help can be just as beneficial in certain circumstances. both approaches have their advantages and disadvantages and the decision should take into account the implications for personal growth and development.
