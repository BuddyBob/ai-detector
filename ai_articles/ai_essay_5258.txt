i disagree that work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. while it is true that the pace of work has increased and work hours may have become longer, there are many new options that have opened up, providing employees with the ability to work in a more relaxed and enjoyable way than ever before. the option of remote work has grown significantly over the past decade, allowing more people to work from home or other locations that are more enjoyable and stressfree than a traditional office. this means more time to relax and enjoy leisure activities while still achieving the same work output. additionally, many companies now offer flexible work hours and the option to adjust hours depending on the individuals needs. the availability of technology has also helped make work less stressful and more leisurely. automation and cloudbased systems have taken away much of the manual labor associated with certain tasks, leaving more time for leisure activities. technology also enables employees to take time off without losing productivity. for example, employees can now keep in touch with colleagues and clients online, reducing the need to be in the office all the time. in addition, there are now more opportunities for employees to engage in leisure activities during work hours. for example, many companies offer employee wellbeing initiatives, such as fitness classes or meditation sessions, during the workday. this gives employees a dedicated time to relax and recharge, reducing the stress of the workday. overall, i believe that work can be just as leisurely now as it was in the past. with the advent of remote work, technology, and wellbeing initiatives, there are more opportunities than ever before to work in a stressfree environment while still enjoying leisure activities.
