high school students should not be required to take music, art, and drama classes because these subjects are often seen as secondary branches of a students educational career. it is impossible to deny the importance of these subjectsstudents should have the opportunity to explore their creative passionsbut for many students, their primary focus is on more academic classes. often, in order to make room for these subjects, students need to sacrifice other classes, such as math, science and language, which are essential for preparing students for college. by not requiring these creative classes, students can dedicate more time to the core subjects that are necessary for succeeding in the long term. furthermore, the benefit of enrolling in music, art, and drama classes is largely individual. while some students may gain tremendous value from these classes, others may not find the same utility in them. this makes it difficult to justify a blanket requirement across the entire student body as it would not be a beneficial use of time for everyone. if the classes are optional, students can choose to take them if they think it suits their individual interests, and teachers are able to create classes tailored to their students needs. in conclusion, music, art, and drama classes should not be required for high school students. this would ensure that students have the freedom to choose the classes that are best suited to their individual interests, and make room for the academic classes that are essential for college preparation.
