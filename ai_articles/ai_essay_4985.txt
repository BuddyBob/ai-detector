fame is a doubleedged sword. while it brings immense popularity, wealth, and success, it also comes with a price. famous people are under constant scrutiny of the media, paparazzi, and fans. some people believe that famous people are treated unfairly by the media and should be given more privacy, while others argue that this is the price of their fame. in my opinion, both views have their merit, and it depends on the situation. on the one hand, famous people are human beings, and they deserve privacy and respect like anyone else. the media often invades their personal lives, making it difficult for them to lead a normal life. for instance, the media often publishes pictures of celebrities on holiday, revealing their private moments. this is an intrusion of their privacy and can be distressing for them. they also face constant scrutiny with regards to their personal relationships, which can cause immense stress and pressure. for example, when princess diana was alive, she was relentlessly chased by the paparazzi, causing her immense stress and eventually leading to her tragic death. in such cases, the media should be more considerate and give celebrities their space. on the other hand, fame comes with a certain level of responsibility and scrutiny. famous people are role models for millions of people, and they have a duty to maintain a certain level of decorum and professionalism. they should be accountable for their actions, and the media has a right to report on it. for instance, if a celebrity is caught driving under the influence of alcohol, it is in the public interest to report it. similarly, if a celebrity has been accused of sexual harassment, the media has a responsibility to investigate and report on it. in such cases, privacy cannot be used as a shield to escape accountability. in conclusion, the treatment of famous people by the media should be based on a casebycase basis. while celebrities deserve privacy and respect like everyone else, they also have a duty to be accountable for their actions. the media should strike a balance between reporting news and respecting the privacy of celebrities. ultimately, fame is a choice, and those
