ralph waldo emerson is well known for his musing that a foolish consistency is the hobgoblin of little minds. to him, it meant that life should be an everevolving journey of discovery rather than settling for what one already knows. many people take this statement as an invitation to push past their comfort zone and take a step into the unknown. although this message may be motivating, there are a few who disagree with emersons viewpoint. it can be argued that people can still grow and develop without trying something new. taking time to reflect on the lessons already learned can lead to personal growth that foundations have been laid for. a person can also develop new skills within an already familiar subject, such as furthering the understanding of mathematics. despite this, attempting something beyond what one has already mastered can be incredibly rewarding. by pushing beyond preconceived boundaries, people can open themselves up to fresh creative ideas and make incredible breakthroughs. in addition to this, people may develop transferable skills that can be applied to a variety of contexts. finally, mastering something new may give a person the inspiration and confidence to attempt further feats. overall, emersons idea of venturing beyond ones comfort zone holds considerable merit. pushing boundaries and attempting something new can often lead to phenomenal results, both personally and professionally.
