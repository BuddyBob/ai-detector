as the cost of education continues to rise, more and more students are taking on substantial amounts of debt to pursue their academic interests. therefore, it is vital for educational institutions to ensure that students choose fields of study in which they are likely to succeed. while some may argue that students have the right to pursue any field they choose, regardless of their chances of success, i believe that educational institutions have a responsibility to dissuade students from pursuing fields of study in which they are unlikely to succeed. firstly, it is not in the best interest of students to pursue fields of study in which they are unlikely to succeed. for example, if a student has always dreamed of becoming a doctor, but lacks the necessary scientific aptitude, attempting to pursue a career in medicine may lead to frustration, disappointment, and ultimately, failure. moreover, the student may end up accumulating a significant amount of debt, which they may struggle to pay off later in life. secondly, when students pursue fields of study in which they are unlikely to succeed, they may be taking up spots that could be better utilized by more qualified candidates. for instance, if a student who is not mathematically inclined decides to pursue a degree in engineering, they may end up occupying a seat that a more qualified candidate could have filled. this could ultimately lead to a shortage of skilled workers in certain fields, which could negatively impact society as a whole. lastly, educational institutions have a responsibility to ensure that their graduates are competitive in the job market. if students are not dissuaded from pursuing fields of study in which they are unlikely to succeed, they may end up with degrees that are not marketable. for example, a student who lacks artistic talent but pursues a degree in fine arts may find it difficult to find employment after graduation. this could lead to a situation where graduates are burdened with debt but do not have the skills necessary to secure a wellpaying job. in conclusion, while students have the right to pursue any field of study they choose, it is the responsibility of educational institutions to dissuade students from pursuing fields in which they are unlikely to succeed. this is important for the wellbeing of the student, the economy, and society as a whole. by encouraging students to pursue fields in which they are likely to excel, educational institutions can help ensure that students are prepared for the job market and can contribute to society in a meaningful way.
