english is a language that is widely used around the world, and it is becoming increasingly important for chinese learners to master it. while some people think that reading in english should be given priority, others think that speaking is more important. in my opinion, speaking is the most important skill of english for chinese learners. first of all, speaking is the most direct way to communicate with other english speakers. in order to communicate effectively with people from other countries, chinese learners need to be able to express their ideas in english. without a good command of spoken english, it is impossible to have a meaningful conversation. furthermore, speaking can help learners to improve their listening skills. by listening to native speakers, learners can become more familiar with the intonation and pronunciation of english. apart from that, speaking can also help chinese learners to become more confident in using english. by speaking english, learners can gain a better understanding of the language and how to use it. they can also get immediate feedback from native speakers, which can help them to identify their mistakes and improve their english. moreover, speaking can help learners to develop their critical thinking skills. by engaging in conversations with native speakers, learners can learn to think quickly and express their ideas clearly in english. in conclusion, speaking is the most important skill of english for chinese learners. it is the most direct way to communicate with native speakers, and it can also help learners to become more confident in using english. moreover, speaking can help learners to develop their critical thinking skills. therefore, i believe that chinese learners should prioritize speaking over reading when learning english.
