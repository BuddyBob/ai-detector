education is a critical component of success in todays society. in the united states, education is particularly important for disadvantaged individuals who may not have access to resources that other people do. to ensure everyone has an equal opportunity for success and upward mobility, it is necessary to provide quality educational experiences to all citizens regardless of socioeconomic background. this essay will discuss why personalized learning through technologybased systems should be adopted as the primary method of delivering education in the us public school system. when considering how best to deliver instruction, educators must consider which approach provides students with the most effective way of learning given their individual needs and interests. personalized learning plays on this idea by allowing students control over what they learn and how they learn it based on their own preferences and abilities. by utilizing technologydriven strategies such as online courses and virtual classrooms, personalized learning allows students more autonomy when deciding how to engage with materials and pursue their academic goals. additionally, due to its flexible nature, personalized learning can accommodate different student needs better than traditional models that rely heavily on set curriculums or rigid classroom schedules. a number of studies have also shown that tailored educational approaches lead better outcomes than nonpersonalized ones according to a  study published by stanford universitys center for education policy analysis, students from lowincome backgrounds improved more when enrolled in nontraditional schools offering highly individualized instruction than when placed into traditional classes where teachers followed set curriculum. fullest potential because these methods allow them greater access points for understanding complex material rather than simply relying on memorization alone  something many disadvantaged students are
