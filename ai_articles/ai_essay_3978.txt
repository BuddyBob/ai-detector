emerson once said, life is a journey, not a destination. this statement speaks to the idea that success is not the end goal, but rather the byproduct of the journey. practice and experience are essential components in this journey. practice helps to ingrain the skills and learning necessary to become successful. as one practices and starts to gain experience, the skills and knowledge become second nature and can serve as a guide in the journey. additionally, as one gains more experience, any mistakes seem less disastrous and can provide further lessons to build upon. practicing and having more experience helps to give the necessary tools and confidence to pursue and attain success. ultimately, this cumulative effort of practice and experience helps to empower and drive ones journey.
