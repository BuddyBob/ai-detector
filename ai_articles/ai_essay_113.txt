impacting the car industry. however, this shift can also create opportunities for new transportation options and services, such as carsharing programs and public transit. in conclusion, limiting car usage has several advantages for individuals and communities. it helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions, create more livable and sustainable communities, improve air quality, decrease congestion,
