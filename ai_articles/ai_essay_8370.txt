in my student experiences, i often used the memory to learn the fact, in the case of the lesson with a lot of dates, names or formulas but ive always had the fundamental idea that studying without understanding principles, concepts that are at the basis of the facts could be an unuseful work. i agree with this statement. i think that students need to have a basic understanding of what they are learning in order to be able to apply it to real life situations. if they dont have this understanding, then they will not know what to do when they encounter a situation that is different from the one they have been taught. this is why i believe that the most important part of a lesson is to teach students how to use the information they learn in a way that they can use it in their everyday lives.
