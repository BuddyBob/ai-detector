the health of the environment is a topic that has been gaining traction in recent years due to its everincreasing importance. this essay will focus on the effects of pollution on ecosystems and human health, as well as potential solutions to this major global issue. pollution occurs when hazardous materials are released into our air, land and water systems. it affects both humans and other living organisms in a variety of ways depending on the type of pollutant being released. for example, greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide trap heat in earths atmosphere, leading to an increase in global temperatures thus threatening entire species with extinction from climate change. additionally, chemicals used for industrial processes can enter rivers or groundwater which contaminates drinking water supplies and harms wildlife who might drink it or live near contaminated areas. the destruction that these pollutants cause can be seen everywhere from dead fish washing ashore on beaches due to oil spills to smog blocking out city skylines due to high levels of air pollution. humans have not escaped unscathed either exposure to environmental contaminants has been linked by many studies such as those conducted by harvard t h chan school of public healthto numerous diseases including respiratory illnesses like asthma, endocrine disruptors related cancers such as breast cancer, neurological disorders such as parkinsons disease , birth defects caused by toxic substances present during pregnancy etcetera making it one of the most prominent public health problems today . fortunately there are several effective measures we can take together towards reducing pollution levels . firstly disposable plastics should be banned or at least replaced with biodegradable alternatives wherever possible . secondly industries must invest more resources into developing ecofriendly production techniques especially those involving non renewable energy sources like coal which generate significant amounts carbon dioxide emissions . thirdly governments should provide incentives for businesses using green practices through fiscal policies . finally individuals can contribute their own part by
