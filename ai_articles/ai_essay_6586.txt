there is no denying that facts are important. they provide the foundation upon which students can build their understanding of ideas and concepts. however, facts alone are not enough. in order for students to truly understand, they need to be able to apply what they have learned to new situations. they need to be able to see the connections between ideas and concepts. for example, consider the fact that the earth revolves around the sun. this is an important fact to know. but it is not enough for students to simply memorize this fact. they need to understand why it is true. they need to be able to explain how the earths rotation and revolution create day and night. they need to be able to apply this knowledge to other planets in our solar system. facts are important, but they are not the only thing that students need to learn. ideas and concepts are just as important, if not more so.
