since time immemorial, people have quested to express their ideas and follow their instincts in order to perpetuate free expression and create a more just world. being deprived of the power to choose and speak for oneself can stifle societal progress, suppress individual freedoms and lead people to abandon their efforts to make a difference. freedom of speech is one of the most basic human rights. it allows individuals to express their opinions, explore new ideas, and defend their beliefs without fear of persecution. when authorities refuse to allow people to freely express their thoughts, opinions, and ideas, it leads to the suppression of their right to be heard. this situation lines up with the ideal of a totalitarian regime, where the ruling class has unlimited power. it is incredibly important for people to be allowed to follow their instincts and ideas, as well as to question the ideas of the ruling class. this provides the opportunity for a more equal society, where everyone is given the chance to contribute to the decisionmaking process and challenge the status quo. without the freedom to think and express ones ideas, it would be almost impossible to achieve true justice and freedom.
