many people become overwhelmed when they have a lofty goal in mind  whether it be a dream job, a lifestyle change, or an entirely new career. setting the aim too high can backfire, leading to discouragement and even failure. instead of attempting to reach the top in one large leap, it is beneficial to set the aim slightly lower and take achievable steps forward. this strategy helps to build character, create a roadmap for success, and instill a sense of hope and accomplishment. additionally, taking small risks can help to spur innovation and growth in ones life. it is important to remember, though, that risks should not be taken at the expense of ones safety and wellbeing. instead, small risks should be balanced against both shortterm and longterm goals, and should create positive opportunities that lead up the ladder of success. ultimately, it is important to think before taking any kind of action. whether it is setting the aim high or low, weighing the risks and benefits of any endeavour is key to achieving success. this thought process can help to ensure that ones hard work and dedication are directed towards realistic goals and have the best possible outcome.
