the impact of technology on the modern education system has been notable. in recent decades, technological tools and resources have become increasingly integrated into classrooms around the world. these advancements present both advantages and disadvantages to students as they pursue their educational goals. one major advantage of technology in education is its ability to provide access for a diverse range of learners. classroom resources such as online textbooks, websites, smartboards, and other digital learning materials can be used by educators to engage students from different backgrounds and knowledge levels in meaningful activities during class time . additionally, these technologies enable more efficient communication between teachers and their students for instance email or messaging applications can be utilized for increased collaboration outside the classroom setting. on the other hand, there are some drawbacks associated with an overlydigitized approach to education that should not be discounted . for example , when using devices such as tablets or laptops during instruction , distraction becomes easier than ever before due to the proliferation of internet access within schools . also , while it provides convenient access to uptodate information relevant topics being discussed in class , this type of reliance on informational sources found online may discourage critical thinking skills among younger generations who may tend towards simply accepting data presented without question. finally , many low income families lack access necessary computers or tablets needed for successful completion assignments which could potentially create inequities amongst student populations across schools districts . . overall , while incorporating technology into classroom lessons certainly offers certain benefits including improved engagement with content material among all types learners  it also presents potential difficulties related distractions  privacy concerns that need addressed by school administrators prior implementation new initiatives involving digital devices . through proper use high quality educational software
