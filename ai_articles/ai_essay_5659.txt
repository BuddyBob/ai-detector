there are advantages and disadvantages to both paper and electronic cards. i believe that, overall, electronic cards are the better choice. one advantage of paper cards is that they can be handwritten, which gives the recipient a feeling of importance and personal connection. however, most electronic cards can also be handwritten, so this is not really an advantage. another advantage of paper cards is that they can be displayed on a shelf or in a photo album. however, electronic cards can also be saved and displayed in this way. the main advantage of electronic cards is that they are easier and cheaper to send. you can send an electronic card to dozens of people at once, while it would be very expensive to send paper cards to that many people. additionally, electronic cards can be sent at any time, while paper cards must be sent in the mail, which takes longer.
