not all heroes wear capes, but they can be found in the most unexpected places. the subject of this essay is an unsung hero a teacher. teachers are by and large underappreciated for their hard work and dedication to educating our future generations. despite often long hours and low pay, teachers continue to work tirelessly every day with a smile on their face as they provide invaluable knowledge to those coming up behind them. the role of a teacher goes far beyond just standing at the head of the classroom lecturing each day. they mold young minds into readyfortheworld citizens who have been given the tools necessary for success in life through education and somewhat tough love when needed  which can even mean being understanding during difficult times like teenage angst or parental issues that may arise from home lives outside of school walls. a good teacher will not only educate but also demonstrate how lessons learned in class apply directly to situations we experience out in real life afterwards  giving us skills that help us navigate any terrain successfully! and it takes strength internally and externally deal with rebellious students while still remaining patient enough so that everyone wins at the end of the day. beyond guidance within classrooms, there are other areas where teachers prove themselves valuable assets  including extracurricular activities such as sports teams, clubs  organizations, student councils etc here they show great leadership qualities which carryover into many aspects within our society today like business management  team projects further down the road after graduation has come  gone. not just one type either but rather all types working together towards achieving common goals! even more impressive is when these same faculty members volunteer their time outside normal hours doing things like coaching or sponsoring special events or fundraisers for important causes so that currentfuture generations may benefit from them too! this level of commitment serves as proof that not only do teachers care about their students academically but holistically throughout every aspect imaginable within our everevolving world
