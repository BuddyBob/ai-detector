the use of cellphones in classrooms can have both positive and negative effects on student performance. on the one hand, it can allow students to quickly access information and stay connected with their peers to facilitate group work. additionally, the use of an educational app or online platform can help improve engagement in the classroom. on the other hand, if not properly regulated, the use of cellphones can lead to distractions and can prevent better concentration on the lesson. furthermore, the proliferation of smartphones can lead to issues of cyberbullying and phubbing, which can negatively impact student mood and attitude. in sum, while cellphones can be beneficial in terms of access to information and resources, it is important that clear rules and proper regulation are implemented in order to achieve an optimal learning environment.
