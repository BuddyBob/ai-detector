there are a few reasons why electronic cards may not be as popular as paper cards. first, electronic cards may not be as visible. when someone hands you an electronic card, you do not have to see the card to know it is a card. also, electronic cards do not have the same feel as paper cards. paper cards are delicate and can get creased if you use them too much. electronic cards are harder to damage and may not have as many creases. finally, electronic cards do not have the same functionality. paper cards can be used as thankyou cards, to make donations, or to print out your order. electronic cards can only be used as yes or no answers. this is not as useful as a facetoface interaction.
