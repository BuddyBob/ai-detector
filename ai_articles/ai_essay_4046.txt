the use of technology in everyday life has the potential to create a variety of risks for children. addictions to screens, loss of physical sight from staring at devices, a lack of communication skills, and the danger of being influenced by bad content can all be potential concerns. as parents, it is important to limit these risks by creating and enforcing healthy habits for their children. parents should limit screen time, encourage outdoor activities, and keep an open dialogue with their children to promote meaningful connections in their interactions. overall, building a safe and healthy relationship with technology starts with the parents and is essential for the wellbeing of future generations.
