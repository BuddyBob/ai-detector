the study of ancient greek philosophy has had a lasting and profound impact on our modern world. from the works of aristotle to socrates, their writings have shaped our view of human behavior, ethics, morality and even science. while they may be thousands of years old, their words still ring true in todays society. in ethics by aristotle we are presented with an exploration into what it means to live a good life how we should treat one another ethically based on logical reasoning rather than blindly following tradition or religious decree. he outlines three types of lives that someone can strive for happiness through acquiring wealth the life of pleasure, moral excellence the life according to reason and political achievement the practical life. these concepts still remain relevant in modern times as many people weigh up which type of lifestyle would bring them the most satisfaction  whether thats through money and possessions or moral integrity and social responsibility. socrates also left his mark on history when he wrote about the importance of critical questioning as well as selfreflection  something that has become increasingly prevalent in our postmodern culture where knowledge is gaining more weight than faith. his enquirybased learning model encouraged students to test assumptions instead settling for accepted truths without question  something which remains at the heart education today from primary school all way to college level universities. additionally, ancient greek philosophies were one key pioneers in scientific inquiry laying down some foundations for mathematical principles such as geometry, trigonometry, astronomy etc., which are fundamental components not just within physics but everyday activities like mapping out roads or planning building projects etc. this revolutionary approach towards understanding the natural world paved way for further discoveries throughout history leading us right up until this day where scientists continue exploring every corner reality around us  often guided by similar lines thinking found during ancient greece period over  thousand years ago!. it is clear then that without these early philosophical teachings there would be no foundation upon which much current thought stands
