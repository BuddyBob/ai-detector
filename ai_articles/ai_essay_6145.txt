i wholeheartedly agree with the best way to travel being in a group led by a tour guide. there are so many benefits to traveling with a tour guide. first and foremost, a tour guide is an expert on the destination you are visiting. they will be able to provide you with insider tips and advice that you would never be able to find on your own. they will also be able to show you around the city or town in a way that is both informative and entertaining. additionally, a tour guide can help you make the most of your trip by providing you with transportation, food, and accommodation recommendations. finally, a tour guide is a great source of support during your travels. they can provide you with emotional support if you are feeling overwhelmed or scared, and they can also provide you with practical advice if you are having trouble getting around or finding your way. there are simply no negatives to traveling with a tour guide, and i believe that everyone should experience a guided tour at least once in their lifetime.
