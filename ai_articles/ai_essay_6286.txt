there is no doubt that advertising can make products seem much better than they really are. after all, that is the whole point of advertising  to make us want to buy a product. however, i do not agree that this is always the case. sometimes, advertising can be truthful and accurate. for example, a car commercial might show a car driving down a beautiful mountain road, with the sun shining and the wind in your hair. this is a realistic portrayal of what driving a car can be like. it is not an exaggeration or a distortion of the truth. at other times, advertising can be misleading. for example, a shampoo commercial might show a woman with long, luxurious hair, when in reality the shampoo might not be able to produce those results. this is an exaggeration and a distortion of the truth. overall, i think it is fair to say that advertising can make products seem much better than they really are. however, this is not always the case, and sometimes advertising can be truthful and accurate.
