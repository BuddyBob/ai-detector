success in school requires dedication and organization. begin by creating a detailed schedule of when assignments are due and set aside specific times for studying. then, make sure to follow through with this schedule everyday. to make sure everything is completed and turned in on time, break large tasks into smaller, achievable goals and make a plan for when each of these smaller goals should be completed. additionally, be respectful to teachers and ask questions when you are unsure. making sure to prioritize school and taking full advantage of teachers resources provides a strong foundation for a successful academic experience.
