the mystery of the face on mars while some believe the face on mars is evidence of alien life, a closer examination reveals it is simply a natural landform. there are several reasons to think the face is not an artificial structure. for one, the face lacks distinguishing features one would expect of a constructed object. it resembles a human face in only a vague, superficial way. a real face would have clearly defined eyes, nose, mouth and other elements in proper proportion. yet the faces features are ambiguous and could represent many different patterns just as easily as a face. its appearance is too irregular to signify intentional design. additionally, geological phenomena can account for the faces formation. the area contains deposits of lightertoned ash and rocks that have eroded over eons into quirky shapes resembling faces or animals. other landforms in the region have similarly strange outlines due simply to natural weathering. most conclusively, higher resolution images from newer mars orbiter missions reveal the supposed face is actually a naturally shaped hill whose texture is accentuated by shades of erosion. at close inspection, its features dissolve into nondescript slopes and crevices. if aliens had carved it, we would see evidence of artifice even in the clearest photographs  yet all signs point to a random act of nature. in the end, while the prospect of discovering ancient aliens is exciting, the preponderance of evidence demonstrates the face on mars is a case of pareidolia rather than a monument left by extraterrestrial beings. unless new facts emerge, scientists are right to dismiss it as a trick of light and shadow on an ordinary martian mesa. sometimes a face is just a face  or rather, just a hill. a more dispassionate examination of the data supports the view that this celebrated oddity is a purely natural phenomenon.
