when it comes to deciding whether to pursue extracurricular activities, students consider a variety of factors. the availability of activities is an important consideration, as is the cost associated with the activity. for students with tight budgets, the cost can be a deterrent, especially if the cost of supplies and equipment for the activity is high. additionally, students may also factor in the amount of time involved in the activity, as many are already juggling schoolwork with extracurriculars. lastly, students might also take into account their own goals  whether or not they are passionate about the activity, whether they can gain practical experience or develop skills  when choosing which activities to pursue.
