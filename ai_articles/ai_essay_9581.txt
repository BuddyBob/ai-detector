the practice of yoga has become increasingly popular over the last several decades, and for good reason. as a holistic physical and mental discipline, it can be used to both relax and invigorate the body reduce stress, anxiety, and depression increase strength, flexibility, and endurance improve overall health cultivate mindfulness foster creativity enhance relationships with oneself and others as well as help people face challenges in their life more effectively. with all these benefits making up its repertoire  its no wonder why so many people are drawn towards this ancient practice. when looking at yoga through a modern lens  specifically when using it to nurture ones own wellbeing  there are various approaches that one can take. one common approach is hatha yoga an umbrella term which encompasses all styles of physical yoga practices that involve postures asanas linked together with breath work pranayama. emphasis is placed on developing physical awareness while connecting with inner stillness simultaneously. this makes hatha yoga an ideal choice for those who wish to explore their relationship between bodybreathmind while experiencing the calming effects associated with vigorous movement meditation. another type of approach found within traditional yogic philosophy includes bhakti yoga a path devoted to cultivating love by dedicating oneself wholeheartedly to any form of devotion or spiritual service. it involves engaging in rituals such as chanting mantrashymns  prayers as well as offering ones actions in service to something greater than themself like humanity or nature itself  also known as karma yoga . this particular branch seeks out emotional healing through conscious acts rather than relying solely on bodily posture mastery like done within hatha. in conclusion , although two vastly different approaches exist within traditional yogic philosophy , each serve practical purposes depending upon what individual goals someone may have set for themselves . whether practicing hatha or bhakti , taking time out from daily life activities can prove beneficial if done diligently
