i believe that it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than it is for them to learn facts. when students understand the concepts they are learning, they are better able to apply them in their lives. for example, i have learned about the stock market and how it works. i would not have been able to do this if i did not understand the concepts. another example is the history of the united states. i would not have been able to learn this if i did not understand the concepts behind it. concepts like government, history, and the revolutionary war would have been much more difficult for me if i did not know them. there are many other examples. i think that understanding concepts is the most important thing for students to do in order to learn information.
