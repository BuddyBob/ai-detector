minerals like magnesium and potassium than their nonorganic counterparts do thanks to improved soil fertility techniques used during cultivation. thus consuming organic vegetables helps ensure optimal nutrient levels for optimal health benefits!. on top of providing nutritional benefits for our bodies, eating organic also has positive impacts on the environment around us. it reduces carbon dioxide emissions into the atmosphere since chemical fertilizers are not used when growing these types of crops making them better for air quality overall. furthermore it helps conserve water resources
