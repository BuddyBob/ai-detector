the absence of choice is a phenomenon that, while rare, is not impossible to find in certain circumstances. indeed, while the vast majority of people living in a modern, democratic society can make their own decisions and enjoy a relative level of autonomy, there are some situations in which choice is absent. to begin with, it is important to acknowledge that there are certain times in a persons life when choice is lacking. for example, young children do not have the same level of choice as adults, as they are not yet able to understand the consequences of their actions and are still dependant on their parents or guardians. similarly, in cases of severe poverty, individuals may not have the means to make certain choices, such as whether they can afford a better education or sufficient housing. furthermore, in certain oppressive regimes, individuals do not have the same access to choice as they do in a free, democratic society. for example, in countries under dictatorial rule, individuals may be put in prison or even killed if they speak out against the government. this lack of choice is also reflected in aspects of everyday life, as citizens are often limited in what jobs they can choose, what entertainment they can access, and what type of education they can pursue. in these circumstances, the lack of choice can be truly overwhelming and oppressive. finally, in some cases, choice is often absent due to external circumstances, such as the death of a close family member or a natural disaster. in these situations, individuals may feel powerless and unable to control their lives or make any sort of decision. in conclusion, the absence of choice is a rare but real occurrence. while most people living in a modern, democratic society are fortunate enough to be able to make their own decisions and enjoy a relative level of autonomy, there are certain situations in which choice is absent. moreover, in oppressive regimes, individuals may not have the same access to choice, while in other cases, choice may be taken away due to external circumstances, such as death or a natural disaster. therefore, it is important to recognize the rare but real absence of choice in certain circumstances.
