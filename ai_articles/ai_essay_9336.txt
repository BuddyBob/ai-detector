however, with this widespread effect comes potential risks that are often overlooked. it is important to be aware of these risks in order to enjoy a safe and secure experience when making purchases through ecommerce sites. the first major risk associated with ecommerce is identity theft or fraud. as customers input personal information such as credit card details onto a website, there exists the possibility that their data could be accessed by malicious third parties who may steal money from bank accounts or use stolen identities for other fraudulent activities. to protect against this, customers should ensure they only make purchases on websites which have strong security measures in place such as encryption software or firewalls. additionally, they should never provide any financial details over email or unsecured channels like public wifi networks as this can leave them open to interception by criminals looking to gain access to valuable personal information. another issue with online shopping involves the quality of goods purchased through ecommerce sites. due to the lack of physical contact between customer and seller before purchase, there remains no guarantee that items delivered will match those advertised on site thus increasing potential for disappointment upon arrival at home due to discrepancies between description and reality in terms of size, color etc. in order to reduce these chances it can be helpful if buyers are familiar with return policies prior purchase so that unsatisfactory products may be exchanged without hassle later down the line
