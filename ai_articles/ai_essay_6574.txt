there are two schools of thought when it comes to education one that prioritizes memorization of facts, and one that emphasizes understanding ideas and concepts. i believe that it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than it is for them to learn facts. one reason for this is that understanding ideas and concepts allows students to see how those ideas fit together. when students are able to see the big picture, they are better able to apply what they have learned to new situations. for example, a student who understands the concept of photosynthesis will be able to apply that knowledge to a plant they have never seen before. a student who has memorized the steps of photosynthesis, on the other hand, will only be able to apply that knowledge to the specific plant they memorized. another reason it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts is that understanding is more likely to lead to longterm retention. a student who understands a concept is more likely to remember itsix months or a year later than a student who has simply memorized a fact. this is because understanding requires students to make connections between ideas, and those connections are more likely to stick in students minds. of course, there is a place for memorization in education. students need to memorize some facts in order to be able to apply them later. but i believe that the emphasis should be on understanding ideas and concepts. when students understand the big picture, they are better able to apply what they have learned to new situations.
