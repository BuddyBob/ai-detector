solar energy has been growing in popularity lately as an alternative form of energy. with many countries investing heavily into research and development, it is becoming a viable option for meeting global needs. in the united states, solar panels are now being installed on residential homes to provide clean and renewable energy that can help lower electricity bills. but what exactly is solar energy and how does it work?. solar energy is derived from the suns radiation. solar cells, also known as photovoltaic pv cells, capture this solar power and convert it into usable electricity by way of direct current dc. as sunlight strikes pv cells they produce electrons which move through metal contacts placed at opposite sides of the cell. this process produces an electric field across the cell that generates dc electrical power which can then be converted using special inverters to alternating current ac used in homes and businesses today. the advantages of using solar energy in place of more traditional forms are vast most notably its abundance and ability to reduce emissions from nonrenewable sources such as coal or gas. solar photovoltaics require very little maintenancethey dont need fuel nor do they emit any pollutionmaking them sustainable even over long periods of time unlike other forms like nuclear or hydroelectricity plants with their large environmental footprints due to necessary construction or destruction to build or upgrade infrastructure respectively. additionally, there are no limitations when installing these systems since they use available natural resources rather than requiring large centralized facilities built far away from populated areas consuming fossil fuels while emitting hazardous chemicals into the environment along with green house gasses like co contributing directly to climate change effects worldwide such as extreme weather events including floods or droughts leading to food shortages impacting communities both locally an internationally placing significant economic burdens on nations worldwide making sustainability a priority especially with regards renewable energies like solar pv installations offering stability once operational practically indefinitely delivering clean electricity reliably over time reducing negative externalities associated with traditional methods cutting costs
