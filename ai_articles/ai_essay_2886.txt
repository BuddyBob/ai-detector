ralph waldo emerson once said, what lies behind us and what lies before us are tiny matters compared to what lies within us. emersons words provide the backbone to the idea that mastering something important is essential in order to succeed in life. oftentimes, it can be overwhelming by the amount of advice one may receive from friends, family, and colleagues in regards to what direction life should take. instead of focusing on someone elses opinion, it is important to focus on ones true passions and dreams. success can be achieved by pursuing something that sparks joy. take for example the success story of mark zuckerberg, founder of facebook, who pursued his passion in computer science and declined a lucrative job at microsoft. he followed his dream and his success is now admired by people all around the world. as the saying goes, a dream doesnt become reality through magic it takes sweat, determination and hard work. unfortunately, some people may try to bring us down and discourage us from pursuing our dreams. negative people will likely stand in our way and with the right amount of resilience and focus, it is possible to push past the doubts and move forward. though unsolicited advice is often difficult to process, it is important to remember to take something away from it that is valuable. constructive criticism can be used to help one reach their goals. not all advice will be of use, but when taken with an open heart, it can help foster optimistic mindsets and create an attitude of resilience in the face of obstacles. it is also known that positive words have a powerful impact on people. whether it is something said in a rolemodel, movie quote, or from a stranger, even the slightest remark can greatly affect someones perception of success. little words of encouragement can act as boosters to get one back to their feet and back on track. ultimately, what matters most is to pursure ones own dreams and find a way to make them a reality.
