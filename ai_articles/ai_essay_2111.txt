working in a group can be more advantageous than working alone in many ways. by collaborating together, group members can share ideas, thoughts, and resources to create more comprehensive and creative solutions that may be less achievable individually. working in a group can also include bouncing ideas off one another so members can identify any potential issues and provide helpful feedback and direction. furthermore, group members are often more motivated and energized to work together, making the task more enjoyable than working alone. lastly, shared accountability ensures that tasks are completed in a timely and effective manner, leading to better results than what could be achieved alone.
