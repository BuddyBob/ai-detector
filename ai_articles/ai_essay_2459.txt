living in a world where people try to force others to conform can be incredibly detrimental to our wellbeing, both mentally and physically. by being pressured into conforming, we risk sacrificing our individual sense of identity and originality for the sake of fitting in among everyone else. we may try to emulate the behaviors and looks that those around us have adopted, and in doing so, we may be blind to our own carefully crafted uniqueness. we may also become lost in a sea of sameness, where it seems as though everyone experiences the same scenarios, and the same trials and tribulations. by refusing to highlight our individual preferences, beliefs and even our quirks, we may end up leaving ourselves isolated and disconnected from other people. it is important to remember that by embracing our distinctive characteristics, we are able to form individual opinions and interpretations of the world, and this can lead to better understanding of ourselves, our peers and the world as a whole. finally, there is an element of danger in trying to fit into a group if it involves participating in activities that deviate from our personal moral and ethical standards. we risk subscrib ing to some potentially harmful ideas which can, in the long run, prove extremely detrimental for us. in conclusion, living in a world where people try to force others to conform often leads to very negative outcomes. in order to stay true to ourselves, we must evaluate the decisions we make in regards to conforming to the behavior of those around us and make sure that our individual values are not compromised in the process.
