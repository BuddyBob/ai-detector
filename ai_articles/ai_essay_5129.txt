english is a global language, and it is essential to learn it in todays world. chinese learners of english face the challenge of mastering the languages various skills, such as reading, writing, speaking, and listening. however, there is a debate about which skill is more important for chinese learners. some people argue that reading is more critical, while others believe that speaking is more important. in my opinion, both skills are equally important, but it depends on the learners goals and needs. firstly, reading is an essential skill for chinese learners of english. it helps them to improve their vocabulary, grammar, and comprehension skills. reading english books, newspapers, and articles can expose learners to new words and phrases, which they can use in their writing and speaking. moreover, reading can help learners to understand the structure and syntax of english sentences, which is crucial for writing and speaking. for example, if a chinese learner wants to study in an englishspeaking country, they need to have excellent reading skills to understand academic texts and materials. on the other hand, speaking is also a crucial skill for chinese learners of english. it enables them to communicate effectively with native speakers and other english learners. speaking english fluently and accurately can help learners to build confidence and improve their social and professional interactions. for instance, if a chinese learner wants to work in an international company, they need to have excellent speaking skills to communicate with colleagues and clients from different countries. in conclusion, both reading and speaking skills are essential for chinese learners of english. however, the priority should depend on the learners goals and needs. if a learner wants to improve their academic skills, reading should be given priority. if a learner wants to improve their social and professional interactions, speaking should be given priority. therefore, it is crucial to identify the learners goals and needs before deciding which skill to prioritize.
