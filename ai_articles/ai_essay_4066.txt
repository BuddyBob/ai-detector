recent studies have been conducted to research the impact of device usage on students academic performance. the presence of devices in the classroom can be a source of distraction, leading to decreased academic performance. schools around the world have instituted policies regarding the use of devices in the classroom, with varying levels of success. there are several strategies for mitigating the negative effects of device usage in the classroom, such as discouragement of use during instruction or implementation of specific software and tools for educational purposes. these strategies are meant to harness the potential of technology while limiting the interference of digital distraction.
