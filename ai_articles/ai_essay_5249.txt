there are a number of factors that contribute to the rising stress levels that people experience in the workplace. for one, the increase in competition means that employees are under more pressure to perform at their best. in addition, the everchanging nature of the workplace means that employees have to constantly adapt to new situations and technologies. and finally, the globalization of the economy has made it easier for employers tooutsource jobs to other countries, which can lead to job insecurity. while it is true that work can be more stressful than in the past, i would argue that it is also more leisurely. this is because of the increase in automation and the rise of the gig economy. with automation, many of the tasks that used to be timeconsuming and stressful are now done by machines. and with the gig economy, people have more flexibility when it comes to choosing when and where they work. so while work may be more stressfultoday, it is also more leisurely.
