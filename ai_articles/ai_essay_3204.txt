the work ethics of some of the worlds greatest athletes have a direct effect on their success. larry bird, michael jordan, lebron james, and walter payton have exemplified how hard work plays a significant role in reaching success. each of these basketball players have made history through their hard work and drive, from larry birds intense competitiveness and understanding of the game, to michael jordans relentless practice routines and will to win any way he could, to lebron jamess focused mentality and ability to push himself, and walter paytons relentless work ethic and drive. comparatively, someone who is idle or expects things to come to them is likely not to reach the same level of success as those who put in the effort. working hard requires concentration, dedication, and a willingness to put yourself in uncomfortable situations to reach your goals. when one puts in the hard work, they can expect to be rewarded with tangible results correlating to their efforts. it is important to note that the work ethics of these athletes are not only applicable to sports, but also to life as a whole. when one works hard, they can almost always expect to be rewarded.
