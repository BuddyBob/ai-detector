selfreliance and making independent decisions are essential keys to personal growth. individuals should strive to become selfsufficient and learn to make decisions on their own. for example, when making a major life decision such as buying a house, getting married, or dropping out of school, individuals should rely on themselves rather than asking for help or advice from someone else, as it allows for greater personal growth and selfefficacy. asking for someone elses opinion when making a decision can sometimes be detrimental, since it allows for external influence, thus limiting an individuals independence and autonomy. therefore, it is important to practice selfreliance and develop the capacity to make independent decisions in order to achieve personal growth.
