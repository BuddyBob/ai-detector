first impressions are incredibly important in the formation of relationships and opinions of people and events. they can shape the way we view the people, places, and cultures around us. throughout my life, i have both experienced and seen first impressions that are hard to overcome. for example, i remember going to a new school and having my first impressions of the other students being based on what they wore, the things they said, and their general attitude towards the other students. sadly, these initial, often inaccurate, impressions would remain with me for some time. incidentally, this is backed up by research which suggests that first impressions are made quickly, often in less than a few seconds, and are difficult to later change. its even been shown that first impressions are made primarily using visual cues such as clothing, hairstyles, body language, and facial expressions. while some first impressions are accurate in predicting how people will behave, many times they are not, which is why its so important to be aware of them. by being aware of first impressions, we can make sure that we dont unnecessarily limit our relationships and interactions with other people based on these first impressions. its also important to remember that first impressions are not always accurate, and making snap judgments about someone could lead to misdirected opinions and misunderstandings of the person in question. in essence, first impressions are a powerful tool that can shape our opinions of an individual or situation, but its important to remember that these first impressions can be wrong.
