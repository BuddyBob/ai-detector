the internet has revolutionized the way we consume our entertainment, with streaming services offering a nearly limitless library of content for users to enjoy. but few can match the sheer breadth and depth of anime on offer. amongst this vast library of stories and characters lies one classic series that stands out in particular neon genesis evangelion. created by hideaki anno in , neon genesis evangelion was one of the first major successes of japanese animation also known as anime. spanning  episodes and two movies, it tells the story of a ragtag group of teenagers who must pilot giant robots called evas against mysterious enemies intent on destroying humanity. it quickly gained notoriety for having complex themes dealing with religion, philosophy and human psychology something which had rarely been seen before in anime. the shows unique blend of action, drama and psychological exploration made it an instant classic amongst fans worldwide its popularity only increasing over time due to its endless rewatchability factor  even  years later!. at its core however is a larger message that speaks to viewers across all ages  a message about acceptance among those who feel different or ostracized from society due to their differences. to this day evangelion remains highly influential both inside and outside japan inspiring countless creators around the world through its bold narrative choices, imagery  music composition as well as its overall style  tone. despite being created during the height of japans economic bubble era, it continues to remain relevant today due to how skillfully it illuminates issues related to not only young people but all humans alike trying their best despite struggling with mental health or societal pressures. updated translations coupled with new merchandise opportunities have allowed old fans  newcomers alike to experience one genredefining masterpiece like never before  making neon genesis evangelion timeless yet ever
