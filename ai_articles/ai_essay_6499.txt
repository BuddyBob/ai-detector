most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this statement for a few reasons. first, many advertisements use photoshop to make the product look better. they might make the colors brighter, the features more pronounced, or the size larger. second, advertisements often use celebrities or models to sell the product. these people are usually considered to be very attractive, so viewers associate the product with the celebritys good looks. finally, many advertisements use catchy phrases or jingles to make the product more memorable. these techniques all make the product seem better than it might actually be. of course, not all advertisements use these techniques. some ads simply show the product being used in a normal way. but even these ads can be misleading, because they often dont show the negative aspects of the product. for example, a car advertisement might show the car driving on a beautiful, winding road. but the ad doesnt show you what its like to be stuck in traffic in that car. in conclusion, i believe that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. this is because they use techniques like photoshop, celebrity endorsements, and catchy phrases.
