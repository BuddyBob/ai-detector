there is no one definitive answer to the question of whether the best way to travel is in a group led by a tour guide. some factors to consider include the cost of the tour, the size of the group, the destination, and the level of independence desired by the traveler. on the one hand, a guided tour can be a great way to see a new destination without having to worry about logistics. a good tour guide will be knowledgeable about the history and culture of the area and will be able to provide interesting insights into the sights you are seeing. a tour group will also be able to take advantage of group discounts on transportation and admission to attractions. on the other hand, guided tours can be expensive, and the larger the group, the higher the cost per person. if you are looking for more independence, a guided tour may not be the best option. you will be limited in what you can see and do, and you will have to follow the schedule of the tour.
