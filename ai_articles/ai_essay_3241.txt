starting a career at a young age can be both beneficial and detrimental to the individual. students who have taken career classes at a young age have had mixed experiences, although some have reaped great success. however, embarking on a career too soon can possibly backfire, with students quitting courses and wasting time on work that may not benefit them in the long run. therefore, it is important for students to think carefully before diving headlong into their chosen career. it is wise to consider the best practices for those considering starting a career at an early age. adequate preparation and research, understanding of relevant skills and qualifications, and finding balanced mentors are all critical elements that can help someone to plan out and thrive in their early career. ultimately, embarking on a career at a young age can bring many rewards, but has potential risks which must be taken into account.
