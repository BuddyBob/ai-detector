when faced with an important decision, it can be helpful to get perspectives from different people. each person sees the world through their own unique lens and life experiences. by talking to more than one person about a problem or choice, you gain access to a wider range of ideas, information, and ways of thinking about the situation. different people may notice or emphasize different parts of the decision. for example, if youre deciding between two jobs, one friend may focus on benefits like pay and location while another remembers how much they enjoyed the work environment at one of the companies. getting different viewpoints helps ensure you consider all relevant factors rather than just the ones that initially come to mind. it can expose angles you hadnt thought of before. people also have different strengths and areas of expertise. one friend might understand the technical elements of a decision better while another gives especially useful advice about emotional or relational aspects. combining specialized knowledge or skills helps give you a more wellrounded understanding of the issues involved in each choice. importantly, weighing multiple opinions decreases the chance youll overlook important downsides or disadvantages. when relying only on your own judgment, its easy to let your personal preferences or biases shape how you see things. discussing options with others helps counterbalance this by bringing in assessment less influenced by your own perspective. disagreements between advicegivers may also highlight uncertainties, weaknesses, or alternative ways of looking at the decision that youll want to seriously consider. in the end, the decision is yours to make. but taking the time to solicit advice from more than one trusted source helps arm you with fuller information from a diversity of life experiences and viewpoints. this comprehensive analysis allows you to make wiser, bettersupported choices that you feel confident about after hearing what others have to say. seeking multiple opinions primes you to look at decisions from multiple angles to find the optimal solution.
