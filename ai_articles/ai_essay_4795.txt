there is no right answer when it comes to whether children should be taught to compete or cooperate  it depends on the childs individual personality and the situation at hand. however, i believe that, in general, children should be taught to cooperate. one reason why children should be taught to cooperate is that it builds teamwork and community spirit. when children learn to work together towards a common goal, they develop a sense of camaraderie and mutual respect. this can carry over into their adult lives, making them more likely to work collaboratively with others and to be supportive of their communities. another reason why cooperation is important is that it teaches children how to compromise and to be flexible. when children are forced to cooperate with one another, they often have to make concessions and find solutions that work for everyone. this is an essential life skill, as it helps to resolve conflicts and to get things done in a collaborative manner. finally, i believe that cooperation is important for children because it teaches them how to be social and to interact with others. often, the best way to learn how to communicate with someone is to actually talk to them, and this is what cooperation allows for. children who learn to cooperate at a young age are more likely to have healthy social relationships as adults. while it is true that competition can teach children some valuable skills, such as how to win and lose gracefully, i believe that cooperation is a more important life skill. in my opinion, children should be taught to cooperate in order to build teamwork, community spirit, and social skills.
