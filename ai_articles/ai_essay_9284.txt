while the internet has been a blessing to many, it can also be an incredible distraction from the things in life that really matter. this is especially true for students who are constantly bombarded with notifications and news stories each time they open their browser window. as a result of this constant barrage of digital noise, many young people have found themselves forgetting about their studies when really these should come first. one way to combat this problem is through mindful use of technology. for example, setting limits on screen time or taking regular breaks away from electronics can help reduce the amount of distractions we let take hold over our lives. its important to remember that we dont need to check our emails all day long  stepping away and tackling assignments one at a time will often be more productive in the long run than attempting multitasking all day while staring at your computer screen!. another useful approach that may help some students focus better is organizing their workspace around them before beginning any tasks. having everything neatly organized gives off an air of productivity, meaning you are less likely to get sidetracked by random items as you work through your tasks for the day or week ahead. additionally, try using calming music such as classical pieces or nature sounds if that helps clear your headspace and put you in a positive mindset ready for studytime!. ultimately, its up to us how much attention we give our electronic devices during school hours but there shouldnt be any shame if taking occasional breaks proves beneficial towards completing projects faster and better quality results overall. keeping track of what works best for ourselves  whether its stricter rules around usage times, organization hacks or background audio  can make all the difference between success and failure when dealing with online distractions during studying sessions!
