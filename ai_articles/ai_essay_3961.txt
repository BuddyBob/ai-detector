for much of the th century, technology slowly evolved to become an integral part of our lives. we soon saw that the advancements of technology had a profound impact on the way we communicate. from text messaging to social media, technology has greatly increased our ability to interact with others. one of the positives of technology is that it has allowed us to connect with people who we would not have been able to do without it. by using digital media, we can confidently express our true selves without the fear of judgement or rejection from those disconnecting with us in real life. this has opened up the possibilities to form relationships with people thousands of miles away, and it has allowed us to stay in touch with those who may not be as close to us. however, there can be potential negative implications, such as limiting reallife interactions and creating a false sense of connection or isolation. to prevent the potential downsides, it is important to maintain physical interaction by talking to people in person, meeting up for coffee, or attending events. technology should be viewed as a tool to enhance our lives and not replace reallife interactions.
