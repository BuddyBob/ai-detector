young people are undoubtedly essential resources to any country. they are the future of their nation, and their contributions can make a significant difference in the development of their country. however, it is unfortunate that many governments often overlook some of the problems that young people face in running the country. in this essay, i will argue that i agree with this statement. firstly, young people often face challenges in the education system. many young students struggle with the quality of education they receive, and the resources available to them. in some situations, schools do not have enough teachers or educational materials to ensure that all students receive a quality education. governments need to recognize the need to invest more in education infrastructure and resources to provide young people with the tools they need to succeed. secondly, young people often face unemployment, underemployment, or job insecurity. this issue is particularly prevalent in developing countries. the youth unemployment rate in many countries is higher than the national average, and this can lead to a sense of hopelessness and frustration among young people. governments need to focus on creating more job opportunities for young people, investing in training programs and vocational education, and incentivizing small businesses to hire young people. thirdly, many young people are struggling with mental health issues. the pressures of modern life, including the need to succeed academically, socially, and economically, can be overwhelming for some young people. governments must recognize the need to invest in mental health services and support programs for young people, including access to counseling and therapy. in conclusion, young people are vital resources to their country, and governments need to recognize and address the challenges they face. by investing in education, job opportunities, and mental health services, governments can support the development of their young people and create a brighter future for their country.
