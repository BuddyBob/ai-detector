high school students face the important challenge of selecting and committing to a career at a young age. schools can help students understand the importance of committing to a career by providing handson job activities and internships, as well as career guidance. committing to a career helps students prepare for their adult life by promoting a sense of purpose, identity and structure. setting specific goals within their chosen career and metrics to measure progress can help students stay focused on their chosen path. it is also important to give students enough time to consider different career options and make an ideal career choice. taking the time to research opportunities, rediscover personal talents and passions, and exploring potential paths can be of great benefit in the long run.
