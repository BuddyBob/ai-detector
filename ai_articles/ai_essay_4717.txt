there are two different opinions about learning. some people believe that learning is something that happens to you, and that once you have learned something, you cant learn anything else. this is not true. you can learn new things all the time. its just that some things are easier to learn than others. for example, learning how to play an instrument is easier than learning to drive a car, but its not as easy as learning a new language. another opinion is that you learn by doing. if you do something every day, then you will get better at it. however, if you only do it once a week or once every two weeks, it will take you a long time to get good at that thing. the same is true for learning languages. learning a language takes a lot of time and effort, so you need to make sure you are doing it enough to be able to speak it fluently.
