i think it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than it is for them to learn facts. i believe this because when students understand an idea, they can use that concept to solve problems or understand new information. furthermore, facts can be forgotten, but concepts can be retained and applied over and over again. one example of this is understanding math concepts. many students struggle with basic math concepts like addition and subtraction, but after they understand the underlying principles, they can apply those concepts to more complex problems. another example is understanding history concepts. when students understand the major events and figures in history, they can better understand current events. in conclusion, i believe it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than it is to learn facts. this is because when students understand an idea, they can better understand and retain new information, as well as apply concepts to solve problems.
