nowadays, work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. the main reasons are the following first, the pace of work has accelerated. in the past, people had more time to complete their tasks. nowadays, however, deadlines are shorter and there is less time to finish everything. this can lead to a lot of stress. second, the expectations at work have increased. in the past, employers were more lenient and understanding. nowadays, however, they expect employees to be perfect and to always meet deadlines. this can be very stressful. third, the competition at work has increased. in the past, there were fewer people competing for the same job. nowadays, however, there are many people competing for the same position. this can be very stressful. fourth, the workload has increased. in the past, people had to complete fewer tasks at work. nowadays, however, the workload has increased and people have to complete more tasks. this can be very stressful. overall, work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. this can be a problem for many people because it can lead to a lot of stress.
