nowadays, computer games are very popular among children and teenagers. they play them at home, in school, in internet cafes. some people think that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual as well as psychological development. therefore, they suggest that effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing them. i agree with this opinion to some extent. on the one hand, computer games have some disadvantages. first of all, they can have a bad effect on childrens health. for example, if children spend a lot of time sitting in front of the computer, they can have problems with their eyesight. secondly, computer games can make children more aggressive. they can start to think that it is normal to resolve problems with the help of violence. thirdly, computer games can make children less social. they can start to spend less time with their friends and family and more time alone, playing computer games. on the other hand, computer games have some advantages. first of all, they can help children develop their logical thinking. secondly, computer games can help children learn new information. for example, they can learn about different countries and cultures. thirdly, computer games can help children relax and have fun. in conclusion, i would say that computer games have both positive and negative effects on children. however, i think that the negative effects are more important than the positive ones. therefore, i believe that effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing computer games.
