writing a professional statement or story is a crucial skill in todays world. not only in the professional arena, but in everyday life too. we often find ourselves in situations where writing in a professional manner is important to get ahead such as submitting a job application, completing an academic assignment or entering a competition. take for example, carrie, a university student looking to boost her chances of landing a summer internship. an essential element of her application is her professional statement. she knows it must be concise and free of errors. achieving the latter comes with painstaking checks and edits, she uses quotations marks to highlight her points too. after multiple revisions she is confident in her professional statement and is sure it stands out amongst her peers. the conflict in this story surrounds carries fixation on creating an impeccable statement. she must review it more times than she ever imagined, ultimately taking up more time than she had anticipated. however, the hard work she put into her statement pays off. she gets accepted into the internship at her dream company and is ready to start her new adventure. this story illustrates the importance of writing a professional statement or story if we want to stand out, we must invest our time and effort into it. just as carrie did, we can doublecheck our statements to ensure accuracy of facts, use quotations marks to make our points pop and create a professional title and cover. if were diligent and invest the necessary effort, professional success is right around the corner.
