intro in todays world, many people use cell phones as a part of everyday life. but is it safe to use cell phones while driving? this is a topic of much debate. in this essay, i will discuss my opinion on whether drivers should be able to use cell phones while operating a vehicle. first paragraph i think drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity while driving. cell phones can distract the driver, and that could lead to an accident. it only takes a split second of being distracted to get into an accident. even just answering a call or sending a text can be dangerous as it causes the driver lose focus. it is better to be safe than sorry. second paragraph some people argue that handsfree devices are safer to use while driving, but i disagree. using bluetooth or speakerphone can still be distracting. the driver has to take their attention off the road and focus on the call instead. this can be just as dangerous as using a handheld device. so i think its better to avoid using any device while driving. conclusion in conclusion, i believe that using cell phones while driving should be prohibited. the risks are too high to justify it. even a small distraction can cause a terrible accident, so why take the chance? the best way to keep yourself and others safe is to focus on the road and avoid any distractions. it is important to remember that a cell phone call or text message can always wait until you are not behind the wheel of a vehicle.
