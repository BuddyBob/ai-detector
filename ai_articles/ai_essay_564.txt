global fight against climate change. moreover, this idea is being adopted not just in developed countries but also in developing nations. in bogota, colombia, a day without cars aims to underline the public health by reducing respiratory issues linked to poor air quality. notably, there seems to be a latent shift in the american lifestyle, which enjoys a notorious reputation for being carcentric.
