nowadays, work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. the main reason is that the competition is more fierce than ever. with the development of technology and economy, the world has become more and more connected. as a result, the competition in every field is getting more and more intense. for example, in the field of education, there are more and more people who want to get into top universities. in the job market, there are more and more people who are vying for a limited number of jobs. the competition is so fierce that people have to work harder and harder just to keep up. another reason why work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past is that the pace of work has become faster. with the advent of the internet and the globalization of the economy, the pace of work has become faster and faster. people have to work longer hours just to keep up. as a result, they have less and less time for leisure and relaxation. so, work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. the main reasons are the fierce competition and the fast pace of work.
