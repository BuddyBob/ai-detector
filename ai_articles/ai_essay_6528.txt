the number of cars in use has been increasing steadily for the past few decades. however, there are a number of factors that suggest that this trend will not continue in the future. first, the cost of ownership is rising. the price of gasoline, insurance, and maintenance is becoming increasingly expensive. this is making it difficult for people to afford to own and operate a car. second, the population is becoming more urban. more people are living in cities, where they have access to public transportation and can walk or bike to work or school. this means that they dont need to own a car. finally, the technology of cars is changing. electric cars are becoming more popular, and selfdriving cars are being developed. these new technologies will make it easier for people to get around without owning a car. in conclusion, there are several reasons to believe that the number of cars in use will decrease in the next twenty years. this would have a number of positive effects, such as reducing traffic congestion and pollution.
