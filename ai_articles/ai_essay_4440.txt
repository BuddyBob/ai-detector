i believe that the university education has multiple functions, the most important of which is to educate students about the world around them. too often, we learn about the world through what we are told rather than what we see. the university educates students in the sciences, humanities, and social sciences so that they can be wellrounded citizens. they learn about the world around them so that they can form their own opinions and be able to critically discuss different topics. the university also educates students in the areas of business, law, and medicine. these areas of study prepare students for careers in which they can make a difference in the world. by learning about the world and the different careers that are available to them, students are better equipped to make the most of their education and pursue their dreams.
