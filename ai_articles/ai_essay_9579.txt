living in the modern world, there is a lot of pressure for us to constantly stay connected. we are expected to be available by phone or on social media  and this can have some major effects on our mental health. one way that people attempt to deal with all the stress of staying connected is through digital minimalism  a practice which encourages cutting down on unnecessary technology use and focusing instead on real life experiences such as outdoor activities and meaningful relationships. digital minimalism can help reduce stress levels caused from constantly being connected online. it allows people to break away from their screens, set boundaries around their usage, appreciate offline moments more deeply, and create space for other activities which fulfill them in ways that tech cannot. for instance, going outside into nature or connecting with friends over coffee instead of endlessly scrolling through instagram stories helps us gain perspective about what really matters in life and gives us more control over how we spend our time rather than just getting sucked into the constant need to check emails or update statuses without any real purpose behind it. one key element of digital minimalism is setting limits around your own device usage  this could mean turning off notifications during specific hours each day so youre not distracted while doing something else putting devices away after certain points in the evening so you get enough sleep or deleting apps which sap too much energy out of you but dont bring anything positive back like games. having these kinds of rules helps ensure that tech doesnt take up too much focus in our lives because at times its easy for addictive apps like social media sites to consume huge amounts of time if we dont pay
