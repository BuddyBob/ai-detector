drivers should be allowed to use them in any capacity while operating a vehicle. in this essay, i will argue that drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any capacity while driving, and why its important for road safety. firstly, using cell phones while driving is a major distraction. according to the national highway traffic safety administration nhtsa, distracted driving was responsible for , deaths in the united states in . cell phone use while driving is a significant contributor to this problem, as it takes the drivers attention away from the road and their road. even talking on the phone can be risky, as it can cause the driver to miss important visual and auditory cues. secondly, using cell phones while driving impairs a drivers reaction time. when a texting may not have enough time to brake or swerve to avoid a collision. in addition, using cell phones while driving can decrease a drivers situational awareness, making them less likely to anticipate potential hazards and react accordingly. thirdly, there are safety concerns regarding the physical aspects of using cell phones while driving. for example, if a driver is holding their phone while driving, they may not have both hands on the wheel in case of an emergency. additionally, if a driver is using their phones gps, they may be looking at their phone instead of the road, handsfree devices can be dangerous. a study by the university of utah found that drivers who used handsfree devices were just as impaired as those who held their phones while talking. this is because the conversation itself, rather than the physical act of holding the phone, is what causes the distraction. furthermore, there are legal and ethical considerations to take into account. in many countries, including the united states, it is illegal to use a handheld phone while driving. this is because lawmakers have recognized the dangers of cell phone use while driving and have taken steps to prevent it. additionally, it is ethically irresponsible to put others at risk by using a cell phone while driving. drivers have a responsibility to ensure the safety of themselves and others on the road, and using a cell phone while driving goes against that responsibility. in conclusion, drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle. the evidence considerations to take into account. by banning cell phone use while driving, we can make our roads safer and reduce the risk of accidents.
