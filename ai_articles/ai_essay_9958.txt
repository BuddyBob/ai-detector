the effects of a poor diet on physical health are often underestimated. while it is easy to think that partaking in an unhealthy diet may only lead to minor inconveniences such as weight gain, the reality is far more dire. eating a diet low in essential nutrients can cause serious damage to ones longterm health, leading to chronic illnesses and drastically decreasing the quality of life. poor diets typically contain large amounts of processed foods loaded with additives and preservatives rather than nutrientdense options like fruits and vegetables. these kinds of food have also been scientifically linked to inflammation which can further contribute to many other underlying diseases or conditions such as diabetes, cancer, heart disease, high blood pressure, stroke and obesity. not only do these meals lack important vitamins and minerals but they are also often high in trans fats which are known for raising bad cholesterol levels thus increasing the risk for developing cardiovascular diseases even further. aside from the potential internal damages caused by eating poorly there can be some pretty observable changes in our physical appearance when we dont get enough essential nutrients from our diets. inadequate nutrition has been linked with dull skin complexion along with dry patches due to vitamin deficiencies while wrinkles tend to appear earlier on those who do not consume enough antioxidants found within certain types of produce like green leafy vegetables or berries. hair loss or thinning hair is another common symptom associated with a nutrient deficient diet  this occurs because our bodies cannot generate proteins needed for healthy locks without adequate amounts of protein consumed regularly through dietary sources such as fish or poultry products. overall, it is evident that having an unhealthy diet does more than just add inches onto your waist size it carries severe risks for both short term physical issues such as breakouts all the way up until longterm ones heart problems. taking measures towards ensuring you maintain a balanced lifestyle including paying attention what kind ingredients you put into your body will ensure that your mental well being remains intact
