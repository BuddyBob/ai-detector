english is a language that is widely used around the world, and it is becoming increasingly important for chinese learners to master it. when it comes to deciding which skill of english is more important for chinese learners, there is a debate between those who think that reading should be given priority and those who think speaking should be given priority. in my opinion, speaking should be given priority over reading for chinese learners. first of all, speaking english is essential for chinese learners to communicate with native english speakers. in todays globalized world, it is becoming increasingly common for chinese people to travel abroad or work with people from other countries. in such situations, being able to communicate in english is essential. furthermore, speaking english is also important for chinese learners to participate in international conferences and seminars. being able to express their ideas and opinions in english is essential for chinese learners to make their voices heard in the international arena. secondly, speaking english is also important for chinese learners to develop their confidence. learning to speak english can be a daunting task, but once chinese learners have mastered the basics, they can gain a great sense of accomplishment. this can help to boost their confidence and selfesteem, which can be beneficial in other areas of their lives. finally, speaking english can also help chinese learners to develop their critical thinking skills. when speaking english, chinese learners must think quickly and come up with the right words to express their ideas. this can help to develop their problemsolving skills and their ability to think on their feet. in conclusion, i believe that speaking should be given priority over reading for chinese learners. speaking english is essential for chinese learners to communicate with native english speakers, develop their confidence, and hone their critical thinking skills.
