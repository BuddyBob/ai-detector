beauty pageants have long been a popular form of entertainment, but there is much debate over their purpose and value in our society. beauty pageants can provide an opportunity to celebrate physical characteristics such as beauty, poise, grace and confidence while considering the culture and values that are represented by the contestants. however, they also risk objectifying women and promoting unattainable standards of beauty that are unhealthy for body image. the positives of beauty pageants should not be ignored. by highlighting qualities like intelligence, confidence, charisma and kindness during competitions it encourages young girls to develop these traits in themselves too  providing them with skills that would otherwise take years to acquire. pageant winners become role models for other young girls who may look up to them and strive to achieve similar accomplishments in life. on the flip side though it is important to consider the issues associated with beauty pageants like issues surrounding gender identity, race diversity or size acceptance which place heavy emphasis on conforming within certain parameters set by external forces outside their control . this could lead young children towards developing low selfesteem or unrealistic expectations about their bodies from an early age. furthermore if only one type of ideal woman was being displayed at all times then this could negatively impact upon those who do not fit into this narrow definition due to criteria such as age or skin colour etc., leading some people feeling marginalized or excluded from participating altogether thus reinforcing various stereotypes about different types of people including gender roles imposed on both men  women which can perpetuate inequality between genders. ultimately whether we view beauty pageants positively or negatively depends on how they are used although they can be entertaining when done responsibly they run the risk of propagating ideas about what constitutes perfection that dont always reflect reality nor benefit all members of society equally. it is essential therefore that any potential negative impacts should be highlighted where possible so as not create damaging impressions on impressionable minds!
