as these buildings have already been constructed, there is no need to use additional resources and energy in order to build a new structure. this helps to reduce the amount of energy and resources that are wasted on new construction, thereby reducing the environmental impact of development. furthermore, preserving these buildings can help to ensure that the local wildlife is not disturbed or displaced by the construction of a new building. in conclusion, preserving historic buildings should be given priority over modern development, as it offers a myriad of benefits to society. not only is important that we consider the potential benefits of preserving our historic buildings and make an informed decision that is in the best interests of society.
