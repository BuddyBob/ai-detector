the rise of the sharing economy has been a major talking point over recent years, with companies such as airbnb and uber becoming household names. while there are certainly benefits to this new wave of businesses, it is important that we take a closer look at how such companies affect those who live in areas where these services are most popular. in urban centers like new york city, san francisco, and london, the effects have been especially noticeable due to high demand for rental properties from tourists using sites like airbnb. the influx of people into these cities seeking shortterm rentals has resulted in rising housing costs which many locals simply cannot afford. this lack of affordable housing means that nontourist residents often must move further away from city centers or out of their hometowns altogether just to find an apartment they can afford on their salary alone. in addition, landlords may be more likely to rent out their units through airbnb instead of offering longterm leases because it offers them a steady income without having to commit to one tenant for too long  leaving locals even less options for securing reasonable accommodations in desirable areas and forcing rents skyward as competition increases. the same problem applies when looking at transportation services such as uber or lyft while providing much needed additional jobs for local drivers, these ride share programs also reduce public transportation use by encouraging commuters opting not drive themselves and thus lowering government revenue but still relying on personal transport  creating overcrowding streets and increasing air pollution levels . additionally, since drivers working for ride share apps dont get any benefits like health insurance, many can barely support themselves financially  leading some countries already implementing regulations aimed at improving working conditions within the industry so individuals receive fair paychecks regardless if they are full time or part time employees. . . though theres no doubt that the sharing economy brings advantages along with its drawbacks it is essential that policy makers take into account all sides when evaluating whether this model should be encouraged or not before
