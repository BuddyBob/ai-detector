the increase of the school day by an extra hour and a half to a students day would have the potential to have a substantial effect on the physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing of students, teachers, and bus drivers. to start with physical health, with more time at school every day, students would be more prone to fatigue, leading to an overall decrease in physical activity. for teachers and bus drivers, the strain on them for the increase in time and effort they would need to expend working would become more and more taxing. in terms of mental health and happiness, the students would be more under pressure to cope with more time in the classroom. this could lead to higher levels of stress and anxiety as students would have to manage even tighter schedules. for teachers, the same can be said, as they would find themselves with greater workloads and more homework to check. bus drivers, too, would find it more difficult to meet the extra demands of their job. finally, the afterschool program could also be affected heavily by the extra hour and a half school day. there would likely need to be either cutbacks in the program or changes on how the program is conducted. this could lead to a decrease in morale and enthusiasm among students, leading to an overall decrease in quality of programs. in conclusion, the addition of an extra hour and a half to the school day could have the potential to adversely affect the physical, mental, and emotional health of students, teachers, and bus drivers. additionally, the afterschool program could become more difficult to maintain and lead to decreased motivation for those involved.
