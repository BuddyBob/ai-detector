dear senator, i am writing to you today to argue that the united states should change from the current electoral college system to electing the president by popular vote. while the electoral college system aims to give all states equal representation in presidential elections, i believe the popular vote upholds the democratic principle of one person, one vote in a more direct way. one argument made in favor of keeping the electoral college is that it encourages candidates to build broader coalitions and encourages twoparty politics. however, this system can result in a president being elected despite losing the nationwide popular vote. in fact, this has happened twice in the last  years. many argue this is undemocratic and undermines the principle of majority rule. supporters of the popular vote counter that every vote for president would be equal, and election outcomes would align more closely with the will of the people nationwide. it could also be claimed that small states might feel ignored in elections without the electoral college system. however, i do not think candidates would ignore small states, as every vote would still count toward the national popular vote total. while candidates may focus more on highly populated areas, they would still campaign across many states to earn votes from a wide range of americans. under the current system, candidates sometimes ignore certain states altogether since they are not considered competitive in that states electoral votes. a national popular vote may encourage higher voter turnout as more citizens votes directly impact the outcome. in conclusion, while the electoral college system aimed to create a more balanced representation of states, it can result in an undemocratic outcome where the candidate who loses the popular vote may still become president. election by popular vote upholds the principle that every vote carries equal weight in selecting our elected officials. for these reasons, i believe the united states should replace the electoral college with a national popular vote system for presidential elections. thank you for your consideration of this important issue.
