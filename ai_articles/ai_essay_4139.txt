one important reason students should take four years of high school is because they will have more time to learn. during this extra year, students can take more classes and gain a deeper understanding of the topics they are studying. with the extra year in school, students also have the opportunity to take electives, which can help them find out what interests them as they prepare for their future careers. moreover, having an extra year in school also provides students with more freedom and flexibility. studies have shown that extended summer vacations and evening time with family and friends can have positive effects on students mental health and help them develop life skills. with the extra year of high school, students will have more time to do the things they want to do, while still continuing to pursue their education. in conclusion, four years of high school are beneficial for students as they can have more time to learn, enjoy more diverse classes and electives, and have more time to enjoy the different aspects of life they wish to explore. taking four years of high school can be beneficial to students in the long run, as it will give them the opportunity to truly focus on their education and become better prepared for their future paths.
