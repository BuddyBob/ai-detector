graduating high school in three years rather than the traditional fouryear period can be incredibly beneficial in terms of saving time and money. unfortunately, this accelerated timeline can come at the cost of overexerting the student. staying on top of daily workload and taking multiple and more difficult courses per year can lead to exhaustion and decreased academic performance. furthermore, when attempting to graduate early, students may be tempted to allocate more of their time and energy towards classes that they are good at and neglect other courses, leading to an imbalance in their educational experience. if a student chooses to forgo a summer break in order to keep on track for graduating early, they risk running themselves too thin and may become burnt out. all things considered, trying to graduate in three years instead of four can be advantageous in some circumstances, however, it is important for the student to consider the consequences that come with it.
