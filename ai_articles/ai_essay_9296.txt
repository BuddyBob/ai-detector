the struggle of staying motivated and encouraged while studying for the mcat is a challenge that many medical school hopefuls face. despite the pervasive notion that taking the mcat is an allornothing endeavor, there are several effective strategies you can use to maintain your motivation throughout this important process. first and foremost, its essential to break down your goals into smaller chunks when studying for such an intimidating test. having a clear plan in place can make it easier to focus on small steps toward success rather than overwhelming yourself with the overall goal of scoring well on exam day. for example, set aside time each week for dedicated study sessions focused around specific topics or sections or create daily lists of tasks which need to be completed within a certain amount of time so youre held accountable for making sure those tasks get done even if they seem tedious or unimportant at first glance. additionally, having someone check in with you regularly whether through text messages or video calls can help keep you honest about how much work youre actually doing towards achieving your goals keeping someone informed also helps avoid procrastination since theyll know if progress isnt being made!. . another great way to stay motivated during mcat prep is by utilizing online resources available through various websites like khan academy and crash course chemistry. these sources have been curated specifically so students preparing for the exam have easy access to high quality content written by experts who understand exactly what will be tested on the day of exams watching these lectures not only ensures knowledge retention but also allows busy students more freedom from setting up their own study schedules as many come with premade materials meant as supplements to traditional course books used in preparation. studying smarter rather than harder will pay off dividends in terms of both increased motivation levels and improved scores!. finally, dont forget that adequate selfcare plays an integral role when it comes preparing for any type of standardized tests like the mc
