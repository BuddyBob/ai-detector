there are two schools of thought when it comes to animals some people believe that animals should be treated as pets, while others believe that animals are resources of food and clothing. i believe that animals should be treated as resources of food and clothing. here are three reasons why. first, animals are a renewable resource. unlike fossil fuels, which are finite and will eventually run out, animals can be bred and slaughtered for food and clothing on an ongoing basis. second, animals are a more efficient use of resources than crops. it takes less land, water, and feed to raise animals for food than it does to grow crops. and third, animals provide a more complete source of nutrition than plants. meat, for example, is a good source of protein, while vegetables are not. that said, i do not believe that animals should be treated inhumanely. they should be given proper care and housing, and they should not be subjected to unnecessary pain and suffering. but i do believe that animals are a valuable resource that should be used for food and clothing.
