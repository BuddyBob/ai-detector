advertising is a form of persuasion. it takes a lot of time and effort to make a product look good in an advertisement, and sometimes it is not true. one example is the infamous buy now, pay later offer. this is a true offer when the customer is paying upfront, but it is not always the case when the customer is buying a product. sometimes the customer is paying in installments, which makes the offer less desirable. another example is the  legendary  products. these products are often overrated and not actually that great. sometimes people believe because the product is legendary that it must be good. this is not always the case. sometimes a product is overpriced, and the advertisement does not tell the whole story. for example, a product called lysol is often overpriced because it is a brand name. however, the product is actually effective and popular.
