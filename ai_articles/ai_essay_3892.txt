partnerships between schools and companies have many benefits for students. not only are students exposed to different work environments and taught the necessary job skills, but they are able to acquire an understanding of what job requirements are expected. these partnerships are essential for providing students with a wellrounded view of the workplace. by gaining experience in the corporate setting, students are better prepared for their future careers. they gain a sense of professionalism and confidence in their abilities, as well as an understanding of the requirements needed to succeed in a given position. furthermore, these partnerships can often lead to internships and job opportunities as students build relationships with professionals. all of these advantages can be realized through partnerships between schools and companies, providing students with an invaluable opportunity to prepare for their future.
