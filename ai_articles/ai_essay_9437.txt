the world of competitive cheerleading is one that has become increasingly popular over the past few years. from its roots as a sideline activity at sporting events to the fullfledged sport it is today, competitive cheerleading has come a long way in terms of athleticism and skill level. it takes hard work and dedication to become an elite competitive cheerleader and those who do succeed must have an incredible amount of determination and passion for their art form. competitive cheerleaders train both on routines involving stunting as well as tumbling skills such as roundoffs or handsprings. practices often involve hours spent perfecting precise motions with fellow teammates so they can all move on beat together while executing each stunt perfectly. in order to compete, teams must be able to perform strong stunts which require more than just strength but also balance, timing, flexibility, agility and synchronization between each member of the team. ultimately whether a routine succeeds or fails depends not only on individual performance but how well everyone works together towards achieving their goal success in competition requires teamwork above all else!. when competing against other squads at local competitions or national championships, teams are scored based on difficulty level displayed during their performance along with execution points awarded by judges for any errors made throughout the routine. judges also rate teams based on creativity within choreography used in the routine along with overall showmanship throughout performances that leave crowds cheering wildly from start to finish! the best part about participating in competitive cheerleading is having fun doing what you love most  showcasing your talents alongside other athletes who share similar goals when striving for excellence!. . competitive cheerleading is something that should be admired due not only its athletic capabilities but also because it offers young people opportunities like no other sport does  allowing them to build confidence through pushing themselves outside their comfort zone developing leadership skills among peers which carry into life off the mat making lifelong friendships through working together towards common goals learning commitment through dedicating countless hours training
