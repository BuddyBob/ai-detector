there is no doubt that work has become more stressful and less leisurely in recent years. there are a number of reasons for this, but one of the most significant is the increase in work hours. in the past, people could take a few days off per week to relax and enjoy themselves. now, many people work all day and sometimes into the night, which has a negative impact on both their mental and physical health. additionally, the work schedule has changed so that people are often working for long periods of time without any time off. this can lead to a lack of rest and a lack of opportunity to relax. finally, the work environment has become more competitive and competitive salaries have made it difficult to achieve a good worklife balance.
