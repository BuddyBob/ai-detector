there is no denying that computer games are popular among children. however, i do not agree that they have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual and psychological development. on the physical level, computer games can help children improve their handeye coordination and problemsolving skills. for instance, the popular game angry birds requires players to use their fingers to flick birds across the screen to destroy green pigs. this game helps children improve their handeye coordination as they need to hit the targets accurately. in addition, the game also requires players to come up with strategies to achieve their objectives, which helps them improve their problemsolving skills. on the intellectual level, computer games can help children learn new information and improve their cognitive skills. for example, the game civilization allows players to build their own civilizations from scratch. this game helps children learn about different civilizations and their cultures. in addition, the game also requires players to make strategic decisions, which helps improve their cognitive skills. on the psychological level, computer games can help children relieve stress and anxiety. for instance, the game minecraft allows players to build their own worlds using different blocks. this game helps children relieve stress as they can escape from the real world and build their own worlds. in addition, the game also allows children to express their creativity, which helps them reduce their anxiety. in conclusion, i do not agree that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual and psychological development. on the contrary, i believe that computer games can help children improve their physical, intellectual and psychological skills.
