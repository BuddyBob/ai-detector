first impressions are very important as they shape an idea of who someone is and who they are likely to be in the future. i have experienced this firsthand during my first year of university. as i walked into my first class, i was met with skeptical glares and glances from some of my classmates. i immediately felt out of place and my discomfort was evident in my interactions with them. as time went on, my classmates started to warm up to me and show interest in getting to know me, but the initial impression i made made it more difficult to build a connection with them. if i had made a more positive first impression, i strongly believe that the rest of the semester would have gone much smoother.
