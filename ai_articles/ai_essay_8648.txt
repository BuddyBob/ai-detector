the study of the psychology of grief is a complex and often overlooked field. grief can take many forms, with no two people experiencing it in exactly the same way. it is an emotion that affects all aspects of life, from physical health to psychological wellbeing. as such, understanding griefits causes and effectsis important for providing support to individuals dealing with this difficult experience. grief has been studied as a condition since antiquity however, most contemporary models approach it from an evolutionary perspective. according to these theories, grieving helps us cope with loss by allowing us to adapt our behavior accordingly and move forward in life without being overwhelmed by negative emotions such as sadness or guilt associated with bereavement. in modern times, various therapeutic techniques have developed which are now used to help those who are struggling with grief work through their feelings in a healthy manner. these include but are not limited to cognitive behavioral therapy cbt, psychotherapy, mindfulnessbased stress reduction mbsr practices and expressive therapies such as art or music therapy. the process of mourning goes beyond merely recognizing ones feelings about death rather, it involves actively engaging in activities that honor the deceased persons memory while also learning how best to navigate life after them being gone. this could mean partaking in rituals involving photos or objects related to the deceased individual or engaging in meaningful conversations about their impact on your life and those close to you shared memories together etcetera . additionally , undertaking selfcare activities like journaling , participating in group counseling sessions , practicing relaxation methods etcetera can be beneficial when trying to cope with grief . overall , there is no one right way for each individual person deals with grief  different strategies may work better depending on what feels best suited for someones particular situation . that said regardless of whatever approach taken however always seek professional assistance if symptoms persist over time leading towards feeling completely
