group travel has many benefits that cant be matched by traveling alone. group travel allows for a more immersive experience, as you can get to know the people in your group better. this can lead to better conversations and connections, which can create lifelong memories. additionally, group travel can be more affordable than traveling solo. tours are often cheaper when booked in advance, and group discounts are often available. however, there are also some drawbacks to traveling with a group. first, group travel can be less personal. you may not get as much oneonone time with the tour guide as you would if you were traveling alone. additionally, group travel can be less comfortable. large groups can berowdy and noisy, and it can be difficult to get a good nights sleep. finally, group travel can be less safe. if there is a problem on the tour, such as a accident, it can be more difficult for members of the group to get help.
