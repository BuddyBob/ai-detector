throughout the years, technology has had a significant impact on how people communicate. text messaging and online communication have grown in popularity over the past decade or so, becoming the primary means of communication for many individuals. this is particularly true among younger generations who are more comfortable using technology to express themselves than using other forms of traditional media such as phone calls or facetoface interactions. despite its convenience, text messaging does not come without its risks and drawbacks. when communicating via text message, it can be difficult to express emotions accurately due to lack of physical cues and vocal tones that are present during other forms of communication, leading to misunderstandings between parties involved in a conversation. additionally, since conversations happen quickly when texting with short messages being sent back and forth within minutes rather than hours or days like through email exchanges, there is often an increased potential for misinterpretation which can lead to conflict resolution issues if left unresolved. furthermore, relying on text messages too heavily could make it hard for some individuals to engage in meaningful conversations with others which require more thoughtful responses that take into account multiple perspectives from different points of view. studies have also shown that excessive use of digital devices including smartphones may be linked to reduced levels of empathy as well as higher rates depression among users  both important skills needed for successful social relationships. ultimately then while text messaging certainly has its advantages given how convenient it can be as a form of instant communication across long distances care must still be taken by users when relying on this mode exclusively so as not cause any negative impacts on their personal relationships  whether professional or otherwise  due to miscommunications resulting from misunderstanding one anothers intentions behind individual messages sent out via this medium .
