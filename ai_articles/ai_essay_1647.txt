drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle cell phones have become ubiquitous in todays world, with the majority of people owning and using them on a daily basis. while cell phones provide convenience and connectivity, their use behind the wheel poses significant risks. for this reason, drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity when operating a motor vehicle. there are several reasons why cell phone use, even handsfree, significantly increases accident risk. firstly, any cell phone conversation requires the driver to divide their attention between driving and communicating. numerous studies have shown that even handsfree conversations force the brain to multitask in a way that degrades driving performance. talking on the phone makes a drivers reactions slower, impairs their ability to divide attention, and decreases their overall awareness of their surroundings. additionally, reading or sending any type of text message is prohibitively dangerous, as it forces the driver to take their eyes completely off the road for significant periods of time. texting while driving makes an accident twentythree times more likely. the distraction of even a brief text means a drivers eyes are off the road for around five seconds  the equivalent of driving the length of a football field blindfolded at  miles per hour. some argue that a total ban infringes on personal freedom and responsibility. however, the risks of cell phone use behind the wheel endanger more than just the user. allowing any cell phone use allows for detrimental distractions that split the drivers attention and put all other motorists and pedestrians at risk of injury or death. with the splitsecond reactions often required for safe driving, complete focus on the road is necessary to prevent accidents. in conclusion, while cell phones have become an integral part of modern life, their use presents an unacceptable hazard when operating heavy machinery like motor vehicles. for the safety of all road users, the complete ban of cell phone use is the only prudent policy. lives depend on drivers keeping their full attention on the task of safely navigating traffic.
