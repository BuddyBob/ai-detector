projects can be discussed and debated openly. these conversations can help shape policy decisions on issues ranging from zoning laws to public works projectsallowing locals with firsthand knowledge of their environment to provide valuable input into the process. additionally, these events often serve as educational opportunities, providing information about how regulations could impact local
