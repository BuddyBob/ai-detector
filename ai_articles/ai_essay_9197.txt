the impact of social media on education. in recent years, social media has become an integral part of many peoples lives. it has allowed us to connect with our friends and family members from around the world, share news stories and experiences, and  most notably  access vast amounts of information in a matter of seconds. as modern technology continues to expand its reach into every corner of daily life, one area that is increasingly being impacted by the advent of social media is education. at first glance it may seem that there are few benefits for students using social media in the classroom however, research suggests otherwise. for example, utilizing platforms such as twitter can be a great way for teachers to communicate with their students outside of class time. students can access updates about assignments or upcoming tests and also ask questions if they need clarification on something discussed in class. in addition to providing useful resources for learning, some studies have even found that simply having access to these channels can increase student engagement levels as well as overall academic performance. . social media can also provide an opportunity for collaboration between peers who might not otherwise interact due to different classes or timetables throughout the school day. platforms like facebook allow students within the same college or university program to discuss topics related directly back to their coursework and help each other understand concepts more thoroughly than if they were only relying on lectures provided by professors alone. the ability to work together virtually helps build relationships between classmates which could lead them down new paths when pursuing independent research projects during higher level courses later on in their studies. not all educational implications are positive ones however there are potential risks associated with too much reliance upon digital technology while learning as well
