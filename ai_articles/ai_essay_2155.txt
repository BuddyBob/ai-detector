as students begin to develop a sense of individual identity, the career paths they pursue change drastically. elementary school students are typically unaware of the concept of a longterm job and tend to approach career choices from a more whimsical perspective. on the other hand, middle school students start to recognize the consequences of their decisions and may begin to commit to a career path. this age group may also begin to explore the creation of a resume and the development of professional skills. unfortunately, many adults may view teenagers as too inexperienced or immature to decide on a career. however, this belief fails to acknowledge the reality of the modern world in which teenagers can commit to longterm goals earlier on. young people can demonstrate the breadth of their experience and knowledge which shows that not all adolescents are the same. even with the ambition and drive to pursue a career, teenagers still face unique challenges. they may not have the educational background or internships necessary to enter professional fields. moreover, due to their age, there are limits to the degree of responsibility they can take on. ultimately, committing to a career at a young age requires the willingness to learn and the patience to maneuver through an everchanging landscape.
