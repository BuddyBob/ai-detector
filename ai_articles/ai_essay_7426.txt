tourism has been a vital source of income for many countries around the world. however, with the advent of television and worldwide computer connections, people can now become familiar with a great many places that they have never visited. this has led to a debate about whether tourism will become obsolete. while some argue that tourism will soon become obsolete, i disagree with this statement. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my view. firstly, television and computer connections cannot replace the experience of traveling to a new place. when we travel, we get to experience the culture, food, language, and lifestyle of the people in that particular place. these experiences are not just limited to what we see on television or the internet. for instance, one cannot experience the beauty of the eiffel tower or the grandeur of the taj mahal by just browsing through pictures on the internet. the experience of being there in person is unique and cannot be replicated through a screen. secondly, tourism brings economic benefits to the local community. many countries rely on tourism as a significant source of income. according to the world travel and tourism council, the travel and tourism industry contributes . to the global gdp and supports  million jobs worldwide. tourism creates jobs in various sectors such as hospitality, transportation, and entertainment. these jobs provide a livelihood for many people and contribute to the economic development of the region. moreover, tourism helps in the preservation of historical and cultural landmarks. when people visit historic sites or cultural landmarks, they understand the importance of preserving these sites for future generations. as a result, governments and local communities invest in the preservation of these sites to attract tourists. for example, the angkor wat temple complex in cambodia attracts millions of tourists each year, which has led to the preservation of the site. finally, traveling helps in personal development. when we travel to a new place, we get to step out of our comfort zone and experience new things. this helps us in broadening our perspectives and understanding different cultures. we learn to appreciate and respect different ways of life. traveling also helps in reducing stress and improving mental health. in conclusion, while television and computer connections have made it easier for people to explore new places from the comfort of their homes, tourism will not become obsolete. the experience of traveling to a new place cannot be replicated through a screen. tourism brings economic benefits to the local community, helps in the preservation of historical and cultural landmarks, and helps in personal development. therefore, it is safe to say that tourism will continue to be an essential part of our lives.
