in todays society, it is essential for children to learn how to compete. in a world where jobs are scarce and the economy is struggling, competition is necessary in order to succeed. however, cooperation is also important and should be taught to children as well. competition is important because it teaches children how to be assertive and how to stand up for themselves. it also teaches them how to set goals and how to achieve them. competition can be healthy if it is managed correctly. it can motivate children to do their best and to strive for excellence. cooperation, on the other hand, is also important. it teaches children how to work together towards a common goal. it also teaches them how to compromise and how to resolve conflicts. cooperation is essential in both the workplace and in personal relationships. both competition and cooperation are important. they are both necessary skills that children need to learn in order to be successful in life.
