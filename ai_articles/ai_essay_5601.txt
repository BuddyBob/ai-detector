in todays society, degree certificates have become an important factor in making career choices. however, some people argue that excessive emphasis on degrees is leading young people astray. i completely agree with this argument because a degree can only reflect ones academic achievements, not all the skills essential to a successful career. to begin with, a degree certificate primarily measures ones academic skills and knowledge. however, the job market today requires many other skills, such as communication, teamwork, problemsolving, and time management, which are not necessarily taught in classrooms. for example, a manager needs to have excellent leadership skills, which cannot be acquired solely through academic activities. therefore, if young people focus too much on earning degrees, they may neglect other essential skills that are crucial for their career development. moreover, the job market is highly competitive, and employers are increasingly looking for candidates who have practical skills and experience. a degree may provide a good foundation, but it is not enough to guarantee success in the workplace. for instance, a graduate with a degree in computer science may need to learn additional programming languages to be more competitive. in such cases, the degree certificate becomes less important, and practical skills and experience become more valuable. furthermore, it is important to note that not all degrees have the same value in the job market. some degrees may be more relevant to certain industries, while others may not be as relevant. therefore, putting too much emphasis on a degree certificate may mislead young people into thinking that all degrees are equally valuable, which is not the case. in conclusion, while degree certificates are important, they are not the only factor that determines success in the workplace. young people need to focus on developing practical skills, gaining experience, and acquiring knowledge that is relevant to their chosen careers. by doing so, they will be better equipped to succeed in the job market, regardless of whether they have a degree certificate or not.
