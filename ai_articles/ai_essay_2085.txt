studies have shown that using examples in communication can be an effective tool to influence peoples behavior. research from a  study by cialdini and goldstein showed that consumers were more likely to sign up for energy conservation programs after seeing an example of someone similar to them who had already adopted such a program. this illustrates the power of social proof and how the actions of certain individuals can have a ripple effect and motivate someone else to follow an example. other studies have also shown that simply providing an example of the desired behavior can be effective in influencing peoples action. it is important, however, to make sure that the example is relatable and relevant in order to maximize its impact.
