ralph waldo emerson once said, that which we persist in doing becomes easier, not that the nature of the task has changed, but our ability to do has increased. his statement rings true in my own experience of learning to play a musical instrument and a sport. setting goals and challenging myself to reach them meant spending more and more time practicing, pushing myself beyond the limits i knew i was capable of. i experienced the joy of growth, because i could feel my abilities increasing as i continued to work hard. but growth can be difficult. it often required patience, dedication, and perseverance in the face of frustration when i did not achieve my goals or faced setbacks. its not easy to keep up motivation and enthusiasm for something that can seem insurmountable. but the payoff of success feels all the greater when, with the help of dedication and perseverance, i am able to push myself and find new heights of excellence.
