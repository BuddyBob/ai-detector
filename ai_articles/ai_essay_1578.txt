seeking multiple opinions when making an important choice or decision can significantly help someone make a better, more wellinformed choice. there are several key reasons why getting perspectives from more than one person is advantageous. first, different people will have had different experiences that could provide useful insights. no one person has all the answers or has considered every angle of a situation. by talking to more than one person, you gain the collective knowledge, observations, and lessons learned from their varied backgrounds. different life paths lead to distinct problemsolving approaches and perspectives on issues. pooling opinions exponentially increases the database to draw solutions and guidance from. secondly, separate people can point out blind spots or oversights. when deeply immersed in our own thoughts, it can be easy to fixate only on a few dominant ideas while overlooking other feasible or preferable options. fresh eyes that arent fully immersed can more readily spot holes in reasoning, lies of which we arent aware, or inconvenient truths wed rather not see. outside viewpoints challenge preconceived notions and force us to defend or reform initial stances. a third reason is that separate people tend to focus on different important factors. while one advisor zeroes in on practical considerations, another stresses emotional readiness. a third emphasizes longterm implications rather than just shortterm benefits. by gaining a diversity of emphases, one obtains a rounded, multifaceted perspective accounting for an array of crucial variables. a choice or plan that satisfies different evaluation criteria from multiple trusted counselors is likely wellsupported and prudent. in summary, seeking the advice of more than one person provides access to a much larger pool of knowledge, experiences, and ways of thinking. it helps compensate for individual blind spots while exposing us to important considerations we might otherwise ignore. synthesizing varied viewpoints results in choices bolstered by more information from more angles  radically improving outcomes. overall, considering multiple opinions greatly enhances ones ability to make sound, carefully deliberated decisions.
