it is debatable whether or not famous people should be given more privacy from the media. while some people argue that it is the price of fame, others assert that famous people should be treated more fairly. in my opinion, famous people should be treated reasonably and be given more privacy from the media. firstly, celebrities are individuals, and no one should expect them to give up their privacy as a matter of course. society has every right to be informed about the activities of celebrities but to invade their privacy is unjustifiable. for example, some paparazzi have been known to follow celebrities around, taking pictures of them without their consent. it is difficult for a famous person to have a normal life because they are constantly being watched and documented by the media which can be very intrusive. secondly, famous people can suffer from anxiety and depression from the lack of privacy. all people need a certain amount of privacy to balance their lives. when celebrities lack privacy, it can be difficult for them to relax and be able to form meaningful relationships with others. their stress and anxiety can lead to poorer mental health. not being able to have a private life can take a toll on the persons wellbeing. finally, celebrities should be given more privacy as they have a right to their own lives. they should be allowed to live their lives however they want without having to worry about being hounded by the media. everyone deserves to have some control over who can access their personal lives. in conclusion, famous people should be treated reasonably and be given more privacy from the media. this would allow them to have control over their private lives, lead healthier lives, and reduce their stress and anxiety. it is only fair for celebrities to be given the same basic rights to privacy as anyone else.
