the effects of social media on mental health. in the age of digital media, social networks have become a pervasive part of our lives. most people use platforms such as twitter, instagram and facebook to stay connected with family and friends or to share pictures or thoughts. while there are many benefits to using these online sources  like staying connected with distant loved ones and having access to global conversations  it is important for everyone to be aware that overuse can have serious implications for mental health. . studies suggest that increased use of social media can lead to poorer sleep quality, decreased selfesteem, depression and anxiety in users. this correlation between mental health struggles and heavy usage has been linked particularly with comparison behaviors when we constantly compare ourselves against those around us on social media sites, we often feel inadequate or worried about not fitting in. long hours scrolling through images can also make one feel isolated from their reallife relationships which may feed into feelings of loneliness or even lead someone down a path toward addiction or isolation from reality altogether. . it is essential that we manage our level of engagement with online communities so as not to damage our own mental wellbeing being mindful about how long you are spending online each day is an important step towards regaining control over your life balance. creating time limits for yourself should help ensure that you do not get lost among the pixels likewise setting boundaries between personal versus professional accounts and keeping them separate will limit your exposure only to relevant information rather than allowing your eyes wander endlessly through an ever growing stream of posts unrelated directly related topics. additionally prioritizing meaningful activities away from screens  like exercise, talking facetoface with friends and family members outside the virtual world  will help create a more balanced too much screen time while still retaining all the many advantages offered by digital resources
