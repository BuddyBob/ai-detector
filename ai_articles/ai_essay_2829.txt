a policy allowing students to use their cellphones during lunch and free time has both benefits and drawbacks. on the positive side, it can be an effective way to help students stay connected to family and friends, especially for those who live far away or dont have access to other forms of communication. additionally, it can be a great tool for students to access educational resources, such as audio podcasts, news articles, and educational videos, during lunch or other free time. on the negative side, being able to access social media or play games during lunch can be a distraction from the purpose of taking a break from the school day. additionally, unstructured access to technology can led to an increase in cyberbullying or unwise internet use. it is important for schools to set rules around the use of cellphones and have a strict policy of consequences for inappropriate behavior.
