in todays world, as we move more towards a digital era, we are often advised by environmentalists to use electronic cards instead of paper cards for holiday greetings. while electronic cards have their advantages, some people argue that they do not have the same flavor of paper cards and do not display the same function. in this essay, i will argue that while electronic cards do have their advantages, paper cards are still the better option for holiday greetings. firstly, paper cards are more personal and have a personal touch. the texture of paper, the design of cards, and the handwritten message make it more personal and meaningful. in contrast, electronic cards lack that personal touch, and the message can often seem generic and insincere, especially when sent in bulk. secondly, paper cards are more memorable. they can be displayed on shelves or mantelpieces, creating a festive atmosphere in the house. electronic cards, on the other hand, are quickly forgotten and often get lost in a sea of emails and social media notifications. thirdly, paper cards are environmentally friendly. while electronic cards save paper, they require energy to produce and consume electricity. additionally, they contribute to ewaste, which is a growing environmental issue. in contrast, paper cards can be recycled or even upcycled into other creative projects. that being said, there are some advantages to electronic cards. they are fast, easy, and can be sent to a large group of people instantly. they are also costeffective, as there are no printing and postage costs involved. in conclusion, while electronic cards do have their advantages, paper cards are still the better option for holiday greetings. they are more personal, memorable, and environmentally friendly. moreover, they can be kept as a keepsake, something that electronic cards cannot offer. therefore, it is important to strike a balance between the two, considering both the advantages and disadvantages, and choose the best option depending on the situation.
