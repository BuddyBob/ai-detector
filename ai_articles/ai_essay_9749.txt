on top of gaining these useful survival tactics you learn about yourselfdiscovering strengths you never knew you possessed when faced with challenging tasks like those presented throughout your journey at campgrounds around the world!. . . finally ,camping encourages creative expression whether its painting landscapes inspired by mother nature herself or producing music using natural instruments found nearby  people often tap into their inner artist hidden behind layers upon layers of technology they face each day they take off into woods . you never know what kind masterpieces will be made until given this unique opportunity ! not only does this benefit oneself but also those they share these achievements with resulting in both laughter and awe within seconds !.
