in recent years, there has been an increase in the popularity of farming as a way to produce both food and other materials. the process of growing ones own crops and animals for eating or selling is known as smallscale farming and offers numerous benefits compared to traditional methods. this essay will discuss why smallscale farming is becoming increasingly preferred over industrial agriculture. first off, smallscale farming allows people to have control over their food sources from production through harvesting. by controlling what goes into the soil in terms of fertilizers, pesticides and other chemicals, farmers can create healthier foods that are higher in nutrition than whats commonly found on store shelves. additionally, by limiting their scope of operations to only one farm instead of hundreds or thousands like large corporate farms do, they can maintain greater control over production costs while still producing quality foods that are free from contamination due to inappropriate chemical use or overcrowding due to lack of space. furthermore, by focusing on smaller plots rather than vast acreages like many commercial farms require, farmers can maximize land usage with sustainable practices such as crop rotation which helps ensure soil fertility throughout the season while minimizing overall water consumption since each plot doesnt need its own irrigation system like larger ones do. not only does small scale farming provide fresh healthy foods but also it fosters community engagement between growers and consumers alike as well as providing a more meaningful lifestyle for those who chose this vocation when compared with factory jobs that offer no sense of connection with nature nor satisfaction created from seeing tangible results from hard work put forth during each harvest season . for instance not only does consuming locally grown fruits and vegetables result in fewer emissions due transportation fees but this purchasing choice encourages local economic growth since these products generate revenue within communities rather than being sent away benefiting foreign economies instead . as far as employment opportunities go working at ones own farm provides job security along with a sense flexibility when wanting time off without having fear about potentially losing wages needed for essential household expenses
