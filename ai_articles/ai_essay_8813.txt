the benefits of a vegan diet a vegan diet is one which excludes all animal products including meat, dairy, eggs and honey. it may seem like an extreme lifestyle choice but there are many benefits to adopting a vegan diet that make it worthwhile. firstly, research has found that following a vegan diet can result in substantial health improvements such as reducing the risk of heart disease, diabetes and obesity. animal proteins contain unhealthy saturated fats which can contribute to high cholesterol levels whereas plantbased proteins provide much healthier sources of energy. as well as this, eating plenty of fruit and vegetables increases your intake of crucial vitamins and minerals needed for optimal health whilst cutting out processed foods helps maintain healthy blood sugar levels. furthermore, vegans typically eat more fibre than nonvegans which aids digestion and prevents constipation  something everyone should aim for!. following a vegan diet also provides numerous environmental advantages animal agriculture is responsible for huge amounts of water consumption after all the crops required to feed them have been taken into account so by not eating any animal products you will be helping reduce water waste significantly! additionally, methane gas from cows contributes greatly to global warming due to its ability to trap heat in the atmosphere so if everyone opted for a plant based alternative instead then we could potentially help slow down climate change considerably in future years!. finally, choosing to follow a vegan diet supports ethical values too animals used for food production often suffer mistreatment at commercial farms with little regard given towards their wellbeing or quality of life before they are slaughtered  something no morally conscious person would want on their conscience when deciding what type of food they buyconsume. . overall its clear that transitioning onto a vegan lifestyle has wide ranging positive implications both physically  ethically speaking and if done properly e.g ensuring vitamin b supplementation then it may just be worth considering trying yourself today!
