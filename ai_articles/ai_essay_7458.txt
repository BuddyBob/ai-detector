in recent years, advancements in technology have revolutionized teaching methods in schools. video, computers, and the internet have all been hailed as tools that can enhance learning and help students achieve success in the classroom. however, there is growing concern that these technologies may be doing more harm than good. in my opinion, these innovations can be beneficial if used properly, but they can also be a distraction from real learning if not managed correctly. to start with, video can be a powerful tool for education when it is used properly. for example, educational videos can be an effective way of supplementing classroom instruction. they can help students visualize complex concepts and processes that would otherwise be difficult to understand. additionally, videos can be used to provide a visual record of classroom lectures and discussions, which can be especially useful for students who miss class due to illness or other reasons. however, the overreliance on videos can become a distraction. videos can be used as a substitute for direct interaction between teachers and students. this is a problem because true learning requires active engagement and interaction. students need to be challenged to think critically, ask questions, and make connections between ideas. if students are simply watching videos, they may not be getting the kind of intellectual stimulation they need to truly learn and retain information. similarly, computers can be a valuable tool for education, especially when it comes to research and data analysis. with the internet, students have access to vast amounts of information on virtually any topic. this can be incredibly helpful when conducting research for a project or paper. in addition, computers can be used to create interactive learning experiences that engage students in new and innovative ways. however, like videos, computers can also become a major distraction. when students are using computers, there is always the temptation to check social media, play games, or browse the internet for noneducational purposes. this can be a major problem if it interferes with the students ability to focus and learn. moreover, the excessive use of computers has been linked to a range of negative outcomes, including decreased attention span, decreased social skills, and increased obesity. finally, the internet itself can be both a valuable resource and a major distraction. on the one hand, it provides students with access to vast amounts of information that would be impossible to find otherwise. on the other hand, the internet is filled with distractions that can prevent students from focusing on their studies. social media, online shopping, and other distractions can easily take up large amounts of time that should be spent on studying. in conclusion, while video, computers, and the internet can all be valuable tools for education, they must be used carefully and in moderation. when these technologies are used properly, they can help students learn and achieve success in the classroom. however, when they are overused or misused, they can become a major distraction that prevents students from engaging in true learning. ultimately, it is up to teachers and administrators to ensure that these technologies are being used effectively and appropriately, so that students can get the most out of their education.
