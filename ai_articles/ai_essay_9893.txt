organic farming is a type of agricultural production that focuses on growing crops and raising livestock without the use of synthetic fertilizers, pesticides, or other artificial inputs. it is touted by some environmentalists as an environmentallyfriendly alternative to conventional farming practices due in large part to its focus on using natural methods for crop and soil fertility management. organic farming also has several potential benefits for farmers, consumers, and ecosystems alike. for farmers engaging in organic agriculture, there are numerous financial benefits they can experience over time. since synthetic fertilizers and pesticides are eliminated from their production process, it means less money spent annually on purchasing these materials which afford them more flexibility when it comes to budgeting decisions throughout the year. additionally, certain organic products often fetch higher prices than those produced through traditional methods meaning even greater returns for farmers who specialize in this style of agriculture production. furthermore, since organic farms tend to be more laborintensive than those operating with conventional methods organic farmers may require fewer numbers of employees thus saving additional costs associated with having a staff such as providing health insurance coverage or paying out overtime wages. on the consumer side of things
