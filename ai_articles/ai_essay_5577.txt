there is no doubt that degree certificates are becoming increasingly important in our society. many employers look for candidates with a graduate degree and promotions are often based on this criterion. while it is true that a degree certificate reflects only ones academic achievements, i believe that it is not the only ability essential for a successful career. there are many other qualities that are important in the workplace, such as teamwork skills, communication abilities, and critical thinking skills. in fact, many employers now place greater emphasis on these qualities than on academic qualifications. for example, i know a woman who is a highly successful lawyer, and she has only a bachelors degree. there are many other examples of people who have achieved success without a graduate degree. bill gates, the founder of microsoft, never completed his college education. so i believe that it is wrong to say that a degree certificate is the only ability essential for a successful career. there are many other qualities that are important, such as creativity, determination, and risktaking ability.
