i remember one of my friends who recently decided to take online classes. she was so excited about all the flexibility and convenience it offered her. but as time passed, she found it difficult to stick to a schedule and stay on top of all her assignments. one day, i checked in on her progress and saw that she had fallen behind on more than half of her courses. she was overwhelmed by all the assignments she had to complete, so she started procrastinating and ended up missing important deadlines. it was a real eyeopener for me. i quickly realized why its so important for students to come to school instead of taking online classes. when youre in a physical classroom, you cant procrastinate as easily. plus, theres less chance for distractions since youre surrounded by your peers and teachers. additionally, coming to school holds you more responsible for your work since youre in a learning environment surrounded by other students. it puts you in a situation where you have to take your studies seriously. with online classes, its hard to maintain that sense of responsibility. school may seem daunting at times, but its ultimately the best way to prepare yourself for the future. it teaches you important lessons in responsibility, organization, and selfdisciplineall skills that will be beneficial in the long run. so if youre ever considering taking online classes over going to school, always remember the experience of my friendit may be more convenient in the shortterm, but its not worth it in the long run.
