when peoples ask for advices, they somtimes talk to more than one person. this is a good idea because it can help them make a better choise. for example, if someone is trying to decide which college to go to, they might talk to their guidance counselor, their parents, and a few friends who are already in college. each person can give them different perspectives and information that can help them make a better decision. the guidance counselor can give them information about the different colleges and what they offer. they can also tell them about the requirements for each college and what classes they need to take. this can help the person narrow down their choices and figure out which college is the best fit for them. their parents can give them a different perspective. they might have experience with college themselves and can tell their child about what it was like for them. they can also give them advice on how to balance school and other responsibilities. this can be helpful because parents often have a lot of experience and can give their child good advice. friends who are already in college can give them another perspective. they can tell them about what its like to be in college and what they wish they had known before they started. they can also give them advice on how to navigate the college campus and how to handle the workload. this can be really helpful because friends who are already in college can give them realworld advice that they might not get from a guidance counselor or their parents. in conclusion, seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because it gives them different perspectives and information that they might not have thought of otherwise. its always a good idea to talk to more than one person when trying to make a big decision.
