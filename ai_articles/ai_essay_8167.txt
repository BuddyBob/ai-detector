in recent years, the degree certificate has become a common criterion for accessing the job market, or for promotion within the organization. people often believe that having a degree certificate can guarantee their success in their career. nonetheless, i disagree with this opinion for several reasons. to begin with, many people have succeeded in their careers without a degree certificate. for example, steve jobs, the founder of apple, did not complete college. he dropped out at the age of  to develop his own business. he was able to achieve great success in his life and career due to his intelligence and creativity. this example clearly shows that possessing a degree certificate is not the only way to succeed. additionally, people tend to forget that not everyone is suitable to receive a degree certificate. it is true that the degree certificate is the proof of the individuals level of knowledge, but it cannot measure the creativity, the ability to take risks, or the level of commitment that the individual has. such qualities are essential for achieving longterm success in a career. finally, too much emphasis on obtaining a degree certificate can lead to neglecting the importance of other skills. for example, many young people are focused on getting a degree and neglecting the development of their social and interpersonal skills. these skills are essential for their success, as they may one day need to interact with colleagues, customers or business partners. in conclusion, i do not agree that a degree certificate is the only way to ensure success in career. people should instead focus on developing a range of skills, such as creativity, communication and risktaking, in order to succeed in their lives.
