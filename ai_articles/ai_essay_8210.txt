when asked about the unceasing uproar involving the distinction between boys and girls education, traditional africans give their answer by using a simple but obscure comparison in an ancient proverb. the proverb is if you teach a man to fish, he will catch more fish than if he taught a woman to do the same. this proverb has been used by africans for centuries to explain the difference between the two types of education. it is said that if the man is taught the art of fishing, the fish will come to him. if the woman is educated to be a fisherman, she will be able to take care of her family, her community, and her country. in other words, if we teach our children to learn, they will learn better than we can teach them. the same is true for our countrymen and women. we must educate them in order for them to become better citizens and citizens of the world.
