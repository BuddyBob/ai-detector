as is know to all that the population of the cities is increasing quickly. it is estimated that by the end of this century there will be more people living in cities than there are in rural areas. this is a serious problem for the future of our planet. in order to solve this problem, it is necessary to move people out of cities and into smaller towns and villages, where they can live in peace and harmony with nature and each other. in this way, the problems of traffic congestion, pollution, overcrowding and the like can be eliminated. the population problem can also be reduced by increasing the birth rate in urban areas and by encouraging people to have more children than they are able to care for, thus reducing the number of people who are forced to live on the streets and in the slums. as a result of these measures, we can expect to see an improvement in our quality of life, as well as a reduction in pollution and a decrease in crime. we will also have a better standard of living than we have today, since we will no longer have to work so hard just to keep our heads above water, and will have time to devote to our families, our friends and our communities.
