dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the electoral college system for electing the president of the united states should be kept as is, rather than changing to a popular vote system. there are several reasons why retaining the electoral college is the better choice. one advantage of the electoral college is that it encourages candidates to build broader geographical coalitions. with a popular vote, candidates could focus only on densely populated urban areas for votes. the current system encourages candidates to appeal to voters in different regions of the country. it prevents candidates from concentrating all of their efforts in a few major population centers and ignoring the rest of the nation. however, some argue that a popular vote would be more democratic since it would allocate the presidency to whichever candidate receives the most individual votes nationwide. while a national popular vote seems simpler, switching to this system could diminish the influence of smaller states in presidential elections. candidates may feel they can win the presidency without needing the support of less populous rural states. this could reduce voter turnout in those states. additionally, switching to a popular vote could undermine the federal character of the existing electoral college system. the current process gives citizens in each state some influence over the national outcome of presidential elections, based on their states electoral votes. a national popular vote may concentrate power in a few densely populated areas. in conclusion, while the electoral college system is imperfect, changing to a nationwide popular vote could have undesirable consequences. on balance, retaining the electoral college achieves the goal of encouraging geographically broad support and respects the federal nature of the u.s. system of government. for these reasons, i believe the electoral college should remain in place. please consider supporting keeping our current system when this issue is debated. thank you for your consideration. sincerely, your name
