qualifications at all. furthermore, having an advanced level of knowledge from universitylevel study means employers can trust that an individual has the necessary skillset for the role they are applying for. but academic achievement isnt just about getting good grades and passing exams  it also involves gaining important life experiences along the way. during college and university years students learn not only how to research topics and write essays but also how acquire vital interpersonal skills such as communication, collaboration, problem solving and team working which are essential for succeeding both professionally and personally. attending lectures on a wide range of subjects gives one insights into different ways of thinking one could question their assumptions about certain topics or even rekindle old passions through newly discovered interests. in addition, universities create networks between likeminded individuals joining clubs or societies provides
