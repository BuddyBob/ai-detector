arise such as depression or anxiety this will help you recognize when to take action in order to address these feelings before they become too overwhelming. additionally, consider activities that bring you joy such as journaling or spending time with friends  doing things that make us happy helps reduce stress levels and boost moods!. its also incredibly important to practice selfcare
