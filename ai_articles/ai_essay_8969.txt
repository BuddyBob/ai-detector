gardening is an activity that many people find enjoyable and rewarding. from people who just want to spruce up their yards, to those looking for a way to make extra money through gardening, there are numerous benefits of this hobby. gardening can increase physical health by providing exercise in the fresh air, as well as mental health through reducing stress and anxiety. it also has ecological significance  gardens can provide habitat for wildlife and help sequester carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. for those new to gardening or experienced growers looking for something new, growing vegetables can be extremely exciting and satisfying experience. the possibilities of what you can grow are endless tomatoes, lettuce, carrots  whatever your favorite food might be! growing your own vegetables ensures you know exactly where its coming from  no more worrying about pesticides or chemicals used in largescale farming operations. and better yet any excess produce can easily become part of delicious meals at home with friends and family!. in addition to growing vegetables other plants like flowers add beauty while encouraging beneficial insects such as bees into the garden space. planting a variety of species provides nectar sources throughout different months of the year allowing pollinators access when they need it most during their lifecycle including overwintering habitats found in trees and shrubs which provide protection form winter temperatures along with access to water when other plants may have dried out due summer heat waves. the practice of gardening offers numerous rewards both tangible food! but also intangible physical  mental wellbeing. whether planting edible crops or colorful flowers, diving into a project such as this allows us time outdoors away from screens fostering a connection back with nature that we all crave whether we realize it or not . so why not give it a try?
