progress and development of the academic field. in the social realm, idealistic movements such as civil rights, environmentalism, and feminism have brought about significant change. these movements were not motivated by practicality but rather by an idealistic view of a more just and equitable society. for example, the civil rights movement in the united states was not a pragmatic solution to the problem of racial discrimination, but rather an idealistic pursuit of equality and justice. similarly, the environmental movement was not driven by practical concerns, but rather by a desire to protect the planet for future generations. in the business world, taking risks and pursuing idealistic goals can lead to significant success. some of the most successful companies in the world, such discrimination, but rather an idealistic pursuit of a more just and equitable society. similarly, the fight for marriage equality in the united states was not driven by practical concerns, but rather
