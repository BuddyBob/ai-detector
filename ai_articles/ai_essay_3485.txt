first impressions are powerful and have a lasting effect they can shape opinions and perceptions, and are especially difficult to change. studies have found that overall, first impressions tend to be positive, yet there can be exceptions where they are determined to be negative. moreover, it can be extremely difficult to shift this initial opinion, as firmly crafted perceptions are hard to move from one extreme to the other. even when someones thoughts on a subject are initially negative, it is possible to change them through open discussion and open mindedness. for example, if a person has a preconceived notion about a particular restaurants cuisine, providing a positive or enlightening experience allows for a shift in their opinion. therefore, even when a first impression is impossible to change, there is potential for it to be turned into a more positive outlook.
