as china continues to grow as a global power, the importance of english proficiency for chinese citizens has become increasingly recognized. however, there is a debate over which skill of english is more important for chinese learners reading or speaking. in my opinion, while reading is undoubtedly an essential skill, speaking is ultimately more critical for chinese learners. firstly, the ability to speak english is essential for effective communication. many chinese learners are able to read and write english at a high level, but struggle to speak fluently. this can cause problems in both personal and professional settings, as clear communication is crucial for success. for example, a chinese businessperson negotiating with an englishspeaking partner would need to be able to communicate effectively in order to reach an agreement. furthermore, speaking english can help chinese speakers to form connections with people from other cultures, which can be incredibly valuable in todays globalized world. secondly, speaking develops other important skills such as confidence, critical thinking, and creativity. when speaking in english, chinese learners must think on their feet, responding to questions or comments in realtime. this requires critical thinking skills to assess the situation and come up with an appropriate response. additionally, speaking develops creativity as learners must find ways to express their ideas and emotions in a different language, often using idiomatic expressions and metaphors. this can be a fun and rewarding process, helping learners to develop their own unique voice in english. of course, reading is still an important skill for chinese learners, as it allows them to access a vast array of knowledge and information in english. however, i believe that speaking should be given priority, as it is the skill that will enable chinese learners to truly engage with englishspeaking cultures and communities. by speaking english fluently, chinese learners can unlock a world of opportunities and enrich their personal and professional lives. in conclusion, while both reading and speaking are important skills for chinese learners of english, speaking should be given priority. speaking enables effective communication, develops critical thinking and creativity, and facilitates connections with people from other cultures. by prioritizing speaking, chinese learners can unlock a world of opportunities and enrich their lives both personally and professionally.
