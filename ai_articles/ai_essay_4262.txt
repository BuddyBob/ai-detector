extracurricular activities can provide an opportunity for students to explore their passions, engage in hobbies, and build relationships outside of the classroom. however, when students academic performance falls short, its important for schools to step in and carefully control their involvement in these activities. at the end of the day, school should always remain a students top priority. if a student is not prioritizing their education, this can lead to longterm consequences that can severely hurt their future. for this reason, schools should pay extra attention to students who are struggling academically, and where necessary, step in and limit their extracurricular activities. this can help to ensure that the student has enough time to focus on their school work and use the necessary resources to raise their grades. while this may seem drastic, it is important to remember that this step can encourage students to focus on their academics and prioritize schoolwork over leisure activities. not only can this help to keep the student on track to achieving their goals, but can also foster discipline and instill a healthy work ethic. ultimately, controlling students participation in extracurricular activities when they are not doing well in school can provide an important reminder that school should remain the top priority  even during a time of exploration and growth. it may be difficult for students to appreciate this lesson at the time, but it is invaluable in terms of preparing them for the future.
