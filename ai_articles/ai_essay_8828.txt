as a child, i was always captivated by the beauty of sunsets. it seemed like no matter where i looked, i could find beautiful hues of purple and orange streaking across the sky. as an adult, my fascination with these spectacular displays has only grown stronger. in particular, there is nothing quite like witnessing a ferry sunset on the san francisco bay. when viewed from the pier near fishermans wharf in san francisco, california, one can truly appreciate just how magnificent this natural phenomenon really is. the rolling waves glisten as they splash against the piers while snowwhite gulls fly through air that glows golden from reflecting off the setting suns rays. the sky itself paints a breathtaking picture of color  deep blues are chased away by oranges and purples as twilight slowly falls over town its like something out of a dream! if you time your visit right the time varies depending on season, you can also enjoy watching sailboats float lazily along in silhouette before them  adding to overall tranquility of such an experience. however what strikes me even more than these aesthetic aspects are emotional ones simply sitting back and allowing yourself to relax amidst natures beauty reveals so much about our own significance in comparison to this vast world we inhabit. at such moments we cant help but feel small yet unified with all that surrounds us  ultimately giving rise to feelings of reverence for both the landscape around us and ourselves within it  allowing us greater insight into our place here and now throughout lifes journey. . in conclusion, experiencing a ferry sunset on san francisco bay must be experienced at least once in lifetime  there is not another sight quite so aweinspiringly beautiful or emotion charged anywhere else in my opinion!
