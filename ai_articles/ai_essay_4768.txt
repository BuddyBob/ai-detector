i believe that children should be taught to cooperate, as this is the most beneficial way to raise them. cooperation is key to success in life, and it is essential that children learn how to work together as a team. competition can be very harmful to children, as it can lead to feelings of jealousy, anger, and frustration. these emotions can have a negative impact on a childs development, as they can lead to problems such as aggression, anxiety, and depression. one example of how competition can be harmful to children is when children are forced to compete in school. if a child is struggling in school, for example, and is being placed in a competitive class, this can be very challenging. the child may feel overwhelmed and stressed, which can have a negative impact on their academic performance. in addition, competition can lead to rivalry between students, which can be harmful as it can lead to conflict and hostility. on the other hand, teaching children to cooperate can have a number of benefits. for example, cooperation can lead to a sense of teamwork and responsibility. it can also help children learn how to problem solve. furthermore, cooperation can lead to a sense of fairness and justice, as everyone involved in the process is treated fairly. finally, cooperation can lead to a sense of satisfaction, as individuals are able to achieve their goals together. overall, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. this is the best way to raise them, as it will lead to positive outcomes such as success in school, teamwork skills, and a sense of satisfaction.
