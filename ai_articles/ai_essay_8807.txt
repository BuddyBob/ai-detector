or poor nutrition from unhealthy snacking while playing. muscles in arms and hands can also become strained after long hours of repetitively using controllers or joysticks. the blue light emitted by screens can affect sleep patterns if used close to bedtime, leading to fatigue during the day which may impact performance at school or work. from a social perspective, spending too much time with electronic devices could lead to isolation from friends and family since these activities are mostly done alone instead of engaging in more interactive experiences with others facetoface . it is important for young people especially during their formative years to interact and build relationships outside the virtual world so that they develop better communication skills necessary for life interactions later on in adulthood. educationally speaking, overly indulging in this type of entertainment can negatively effect academic performance by distracting children from completing homework assignments or studying for tests due its immersive nature that encourages longer periods than necessary when allotted times has expired. furthermore it could lead them down paths not conducive towards learning such as online gambling websites or other inappropriate content when safety measures arent taken seriously enough e.g parental controls activated etc. . . . in conclusion it is clear
