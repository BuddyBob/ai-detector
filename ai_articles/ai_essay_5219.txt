work has always been a source of stress for people. however, in the past, work was often seen as a way to provide for ones family and provide a sense of purpose. today, many people view work as a source of stress and a way to make money that they can use to buy things they want. one reason many people view work as a source of stress is because of the amount of time that people are required to spend at work. in the past, people often worked a nine to five job and had time to spend with their families after work. today, many people work long hours and have little time to spend with their families. this is especially true for people who work in the fast food industry or other jobs that require them to be on their feet for hours at a time. another reason many people view work as a source of stress is because of the amount of pressure that they are under at work. in the past, people often worked in jobs that were not as demanding as the jobs that people work today. today, many people are required to work long hours and meet high standards. this pressure can be difficult to handle, especially for people who are not used to it. finally, many people view work as a source of stress because of the amount of noise that is present at work. in the past, people often worked in quiet offices. today, many offices are filled with loud music and people talking. this noise can be difficult to deal with, especially for people who are sensitive to noise. all of these reasons make work a source of stress for many people. however, the amount of stress that people experience at work can vary depending on the individual. some people may find the pressure and noise to be difficult to deal with, but others may find them to be manageable.
