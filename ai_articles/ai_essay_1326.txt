in this day of age, most people own and use cell phones on a regular basis. but, should people be allowed to use their phones while driving? my opinion is that drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any way while driving, as it distracts them from the road and can be dangerous. firstly, using cell phones while driving diverts a persons attention from the road, and that can lead to accidents. when someone texts or talks on the phone while driving, they are concentrating on their conversation rather than the road, which can be very dangerous for the driver and other people on the road. it is very difficult to focus on both a conversation and driving safely at the same time, and its not worth putting peoples lives in danger just for the sake of a phone call or text message. secondly, it is not just the person driving who can be affected by using a cell phone behind the wheel. other people on the road can also be at risk because they might not be able to react fast enough to avoid an accident if another driver is distracted by their phone. this puts both the driver and the people around them at risk, and its just not worth it. in conclusion, cell phones have become an essential part of our daily lives, but using them while driving can put us and others in danger. it is crucial that we prioritize safety over convenience and avoid using phones when on the road. therefore, the government should pass laws that prohibit drivers from using their phones when driving to ensure that everyone is safe on the road.
