there are two main schools of thought when it comes to how animals should be treated those who believe that animals are resources to be used for food and clothing, and those who believe that animals should be treated as pets. i believe that animals should be treated as resources, for the following reasons first, using animals for food and clothing is more efficient than treating them as pets. if all of the worlds . billion people were to keep just one pet dog, that would require the use of over a billion acres of land  an area the size of mexico. by contrast, if all of those people were to consume meat and other animal products, that would require only a fraction of the land. second, using animals for food and clothing is more humane than treating them as pets. pets are often kept in cramped, unsanitary conditions, while animals raised for food are typically given more space and better care. in addition, pets are often euthanized when they become sick or too old, while animals raised for food are typically slaughtered in a humane way. third, using animals for food and clothing is more sustainable than treating them as pets. it takes many resources  water, food, land, energy  to care for a pet, while animals raised for food can be used to produce those same resources. for example, a cow can be used to produce milk, meat, and leather, while a dog can only produce milk if its a female and feces. fourth, using animals for food and clothing is more ethical than treating them as pets. pets are often bred for the sole purpose of being sold as pets, while animals raised for food are typically bred for their meat or milk. in addition, animals raised for food are typically killed in a way that is quick and painless, while pets often suffer from neglect and abuse. i believe that animals should be treated as resources, for the reasons listed above. i understand that some people may disagree with me, but i believe that this is the most humane and sustainable way to treat them.
