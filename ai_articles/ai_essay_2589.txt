thomas jefferson once famously declared that nothing can stop the man with the right mental attitude from achieving his goal nothing on earth can help the man with the wrong mental attitude. his words have a powerful resonance in our present day, for it is clear that those who act on their dreams and goals can achieve far more than those who do not. to put it simply having an active attitude of determination and ambition can truly bring success. when examining individuals who actively strive to reach their ambitions, the key component is the clear focus on their objectives. they will create tangible plans, allowing them to establish deadlines and strides in reaching their goals. to illustrate this idea, we can consider the example of steve jobs an ambitious man who sought to revolutionize the world of technology. he did not simply wait for his goals to come to fruition instead, he was actively involved in every step of the creation process, from indepth market research to detailed product testing and design. in contrast, there are those who fail to reach their dreams because they do not strive to actively pursue their goals. those who do not have a clear focus do not tend to be willing to make sacrifices or take risks to push themselves further. for example, many aspiring entrepreneurs will not risk their financial security in order to invest in their ideas, as they are not motivated to work towards their dreams. in conclusion, it is undeniably true that individuals who have an active ambition to strive towards their goals will become more successful than those who do not, as stated by thomas jefferson. achieving ones ambitions does not come easy, and for those willing to work hard, the rewards can be great.
