emotionally and mentally. this essay will focus on one particular aspect of social media  its ability to allow people to compare themselves to others  and examine why it can be damaging for users selfesteem. as humans, it is in our nature to compare ourselves with other individuals in order to establish a sense of identity comparisons are made so that we can feel better or worse about ourselves based on how we stack up against those around us. social media has taken this process online by providing platforms where anyone can post pictures of their lifestyles or accomplishments while simultaneously seeing what everyone else is doing. this comparison then leads people down a rabbit hole where they seek validation from others through likes or comments if someone does not receive enough attention there is often feelings of disappointment or resentment towards those who do get more recognition than them. at first glance these comparisons might seem harmless but the truth is that they often lead to unhealthy behaviours such as low selfesteem because people are constantly being reminded of their flaws compared to those around them  even though many images posted on social media may not be accurate representations as many users manipulate their photos using filtersediting techniques before posting them publicly for all see  thereby creating unattainable ideals
