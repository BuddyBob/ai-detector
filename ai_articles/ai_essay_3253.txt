working in a group has many advantages compared to working alone. one benefit of working in a group is that it allows multiple perspectives and ideas to be discussed, compared, and explored. with a variety of different ideas being discussed, more comprehensive solutions can be developed. having peers to motivate and support each other can also be beneficial to the progress of a project or task. for example, if one person is stuck on a certain task, their peers can help them brainstorm and come up with a solution. however, working in a group can also be a challenge. team dynamics and disharmony can impede the progress of the task or project. it can also be hard to remain focused when others are discussing topics unrelated to the task at hand. working alone can help to prevent this distraction. additionally, everyone is often expected to contribute in a group setting, while working alone can allow an individual to work at their own pace or within a comfortable space. however, if support or advice is necessary, it can be difficult to obtain when working alone.
