completing a full four years of high school before moving on to college can help students build essential skills and gain knowledge for their future. for instance, skills such as better time management, organization, writing, and study techniques can be developed over the four year period. this can help students apply these skills to their college courses, eventually translating into better grades and success. on the contrary, leaving high school one year earlier than traditional can be challenging. without the full fouryear curriculum, students may lack the exposure to higher level classes needed to perform well in college. additionally, early graduation can sometimes hinder selfdevelopment, as students may miss out on important milestones that could contribute to maturity level and development. for example, one student left high school early in order to take a dualenrollment course at a local college. by not experiencing a full four years of high school, the student lacked the maturity level and knowledge needed to succeed in college academics. as a result, her grades began to suffer. it is clear that while leaving high school early can be appealing to some, it is important to consider the effects it may have on a students future. taking the four year high school experience can help them prepare for the rigors of college by developing crucial skills and knowledge, while also allowing them to build maturity and grow into adulthood.
