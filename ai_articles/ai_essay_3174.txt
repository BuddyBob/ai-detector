selfimprovement is an important and necessary part of life. it can be hard to know where to start, especially when it comes to finding the best approach. while there is no onesizefits all answer, two of the most popular methods are improvisation and following a script when trying to be someone. these two methods have their own set of benefits and drawbacks, and understanding them can help us decide which is best for our own individual needs. improvising when trying to be someone is a great way to achieve goals on the spot. it can help you think on your feet and allow you to be more creative in your approach and better adjust to unpredictable situations. a great example of this would be a public speaker. the ability to think quickly and remain strategic in ones approach can be helpful in persuading an audience and having a greater impact. however, there is also the risk that if we do not know our subject matter well enough, the speech can go off the rails and lead to results that are less than ideal. on the other hand, following a script can be very helpful for a number of situations. when someone is stuck in a rut, or trying something entirely new, following a stepbystep process can help them establish a foundation and develop their skills over time. this can be especially beneficial in contexts such as business or entrepreneuring, as if the individual is properly prepared, this can allow them to better anticipate obstacles and make better decisions in the long run. of course, with this method, there is the risk of becoming overly reliant on a script and becoming dependent on it, leading to a lack of creative thinking. in conclusion, both improvisation and following a script can be valuable and helpful tools when pursuing selfimprovement. its important to consider the context and end goals in order to determine which method best fits the individual, as well as how effective it is over time.
