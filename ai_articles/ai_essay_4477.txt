it is common for people to think that the primary purpose of university education is to prepare students for employment. while i agree that employment is one important outcome of university education, i believe that there are other important functions of university education as well. first, university education helps students develop important skills that are not necessarily related to employment. for example, university education helps students learn how to think critically, how to communicate effectively, and how to work in teams. these are all important skills that can help students in their personal lives as well as in their professional lives. second, university education helps students develop a broader perspective on the world. through their coursework, students are exposed to new ideas and new ways of thinking about the world. this can help them to become more openminded and tolerant of others. finally, university education can help students to find their passion in life. many students go to university not knowing what they want to do with their lives. but through their experiences at university, they discover new interests and talents that they never knew they had. in conclusion, while employment is one important outcome of university education, there are other important outcomes as well. university education helps students develop important skills, a broader perspective, and a passion for life.
