practices. as factories produce more products at a faster rate, we are also releasing more pollutants into our atmosphere and depleting natural resources faster than ever before. this has had direct consequences for both public health and the global environment. among these consequences is an increased risk for air pollution due to factory emissions. as industries emit large amounts of toxic gases like carbon dioxide co, nitrogen oxide nox, sulfur oxide sox among others, these pollutants become trapped in our atmosphere causing smog and acid rain which can lead to respiratory illnesses such as asthma or lung cancer when inhaled over time. in addition to this, air pollution also contributes significantly to climate change by trapping heat within earths atmosphere leading to higher temperatures globally as well as extreme weather events like floods, droughts etc., thus making it essential that we find ways to reduce emissions from industry if we wish to avoid devastating environmental outcomes in the future. apart from air pollution, industrialization impacts our water sources too due to its reliance on heavy machinery and equipment which require coolants like oil or
