the idea of a fourday work week with ten hour days is gaining traction in the business world  and for good reason. many studies suggest that this type of schedule can maximize productivity and energy levels while reducing fatigue and increasing employee satisfaction. in terms of energy use, the compact time frame of this type of arrangement means that less energy is consumed overall. in addition, employees often report feeling more motivated and refreshed due to the additional day off. this can increase productivity, as employees have more time to rest and recharge. while the five day work week is the more traditional option, the four day work week with ten hour days has the potential to bring benefits to both the employer and the employee.
