taking classes online offers students many advantages as opposed to attending physical classes in school. for students who are not fond of attending class in person, online classes provide the benefit of being able to learn from the comfort of their own homes. students who take online classes can also work at their own pace, since they are not limited to prescribed class times. furthermore, classes are available on their own schedule and assignments can even be due after midnight, meaning snow days and other external factors that would otherwise cause school closure have no effect. taking classes online provides students with a convenient option to work with their own schedule and complete work in a more comfortable environment. therefore, it is fair to conclude that online classes have clear advantages that benefit students in their learning process.
