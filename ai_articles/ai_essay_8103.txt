in todays world, competition is considered a crucial aspect of success. however, when it comes to children, the debate is whether they should be taught to compete or cooperate. in my opinion, children should be taught to cooperate rather than compete. this essay will explain why i believe so. to begin with, cooperation is a fundamental skill that children need to learn to succeed in life. in the real world, success is not just about individual achievement but also about working together with others. in a team, each member has a unique set of skills and knowledge that they can contribute to achieve a common goal. therefore, children who learn to cooperate are more likely to be successful in their personal and professional lives. moreover, cooperation teaches children important values such as empathy, respect, and responsibility. when children learn to work together, they learn to understand and appreciate the differences in others. they also learn to respect the opinions and ideas of others, which is essential for building healthy relationships. additionally, cooperation teaches children to take responsibility for their actions and to be accountable for their mistakes. these values are crucial for building a strong character and becoming a responsible citizen. on the other hand, competition can have negative effects on childrens development. when children are taught to compete, they may become overly focused on winning and may develop a fear of failure. this can lead to anxiety, stress, and low selfesteem. moreover, competition can create a hostile environment where children may feel isolated and unsupported. this can have a detrimental effect on their mental health and wellbeing. in conclusion, i strongly believe that children should be taught to cooperate rather than compete. cooperation teaches children important skills and values that are essential for success in life. moreover, it promotes a positive and supportive environment that fosters healthy relationships and mental wellbeing. therefore, parents and educators should focus on teaching children to work together and to appreciate the value of teamwork.
