there are pros and cons to both learning to compete and cooperating. on one hand, learning to compete can help children learn how to be successful in life. this can come in handy when they are facing challenges or when they are trying to achieve something. competition can also spur them on to do their best and to push themselves to their limits. on the other hand, learning to cooperate can help children learn how to work together and to be helpful to others. this can be important in their lives, both in terms of their social lives and their careers. cooperation can also lead to lasting friendships. ultimately, it is important for children to learn how to compete and cooperate in a way that is healthy for them. it is important to find a balance that works for them.
