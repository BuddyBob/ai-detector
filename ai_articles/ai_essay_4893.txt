competition and cooperation are two basic concepts which share the human communication circle. competition is the process of trying to outdo the other person in some way. for example, if you and your friend are playing a game of chess, you are competing with each other to see who can make the best move. cooperation is a process in which two or more people work together to achieve a common goal. if two people want to build a house together, they might cooperate to make sure that the house is built in a way that is safe and comfortable for both of them.
