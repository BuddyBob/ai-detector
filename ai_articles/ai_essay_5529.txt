there is no doubt that we have become a throwaway society. we are constantly bombarded with ads telling us to buy the latest gadget, and then to upgrade to the latest model a few months later. we buy clothes that we only wear a few times before they go out of fashion. and we buy food that comes in packaging that we then have to throw away. the problem is that we are generating more waste than we can deal with. we are filling up our landfills and our oceans with plastic rubbish. and it is having a devastating effect on the environment. wildlife is being killed by ingesting plastic or getting tangled up in it. and the chemicals from the plastic are leaching into the soil and water, and making their way into the food chain. we need to find ways to reduce the amount of waste we generate. we need to recycle more, and we need to buy less stuff. we need to think about the environment before we buy something, and ask ourselves whether we really need it. only by changing our throwaway habits, will we be able to protect our planet for future generations.
