studies have consistently shown that young people enjoy life more than older people do. numerous reasons for this abound, but one of the most evident is that young people are not burdened with the weight of life experiences and baggage that many older people are. they are still growing and learning, and have not yet attained the level of complacency or entitlement that often comes with age. additionally, young people often have more energy and enthusiasm for life than older people do. they have not yet been beaten down by the trials and tribulations of life, and are therefore more likely to seize the opportunities that life presents them. this can be seen in everything from their attitudes and behavior to their interests and hobbies. in short, young people generally enjoy life more than older people do, and there are a number of reasons for this.
