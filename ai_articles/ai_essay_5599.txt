degree certificates are widely recognized as a measure of ones academic achievements and are often used as a criterion for promotion in many institutions. however, if this tendency goes to the extreme, young people may be misled into believing that a degree certificate is the only factor that determines their success in their career. to begin with, a degree certificate is only a reflection of ones academic achievements. it does not necessarily mean that the holder of the certificate is competent in the field of study. for instance, a student may have obtained a degree in engineering but may not have the necessary skills to work in the engineering field. therefore, relying solely on a degree certificate to determine ones promotion may not be the best approach. in addition, a degree certificate does not reflect other abilities essential for successful career. for example, a person may have excellent communication skills, problemsolving skills, and a strong work ethic, but may not have a degree certificate. such abilities are essential for success in any career, yet they are not reflected in a degree certificate. therefore, relying solely on a degree certificate to determine ones promotion may be unfair to those who possess such abilities but do not have a degree certificate. finally, a degree certificate does not guarantee success in ones career. it is only one of the many factors that determine ones success. other factors such as experience, attitude, and networking skills are also important. therefore, relying solely on a degree certificate to determine ones promotion may be misleading. in conclusion, if the tendency to rely solely on a degree certificate to determine ones promotion goes to the extreme, young people may be misled into believing that a degree certificate is the only factor that determines their success in their career. therefore, it is important to consider other factors such as experience, attitude, and networking skills when determining ones promotion.
