when it comes to being resilient and striving towards your goals, ralph waldo emerson had some wise words. he once said our greatest glory is not in never failing, but in rising up every time we fail. we can draw inspiration from these words and use them as motivation when we face obstacles or setbacks on our path to success. for me, resilience has been key to achieving my dreams. despite family members discouraging me, i set my sights on a career in the arts. but the journey wasnt easy  i had to work hard, face rejection and put in extra hours outside of my day job. however, i kept going and never gave up. in time, i finally got my foot in the door and realized my goals. resilience involves maintaining a positive attitude even when the going gets tough. henry ward beecher believed that, one unerring mark of the love of truth is not entertaining any proposition with greater assurance than the proofs it is built upon will warrant. this teaches us to be openminded and mindful of the circumstances and situations that we face  while remaining resilient and focused on our goals. through resilience, we can persevere and remain strong, even when presented with adversity. as les brown reminds us, one of the greatest gifts you can give to yourself is the gift of resilience. the ability to bounce back and rise above whatever challenges life brings. true resilience involves focusing on the end result and never giving up  no matter how long the journey may be. at the end of the day, resilience is a valuable tool that can help us to reach any goal with optimism and courage. it may not be easy but, as emerson said, what lies behind us and what lies before us are small matters compared to what lies within us. we should remember this and be resilient in the pursuit of our dreams.
