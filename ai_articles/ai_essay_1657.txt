drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while driving cell phone use while driving has become increasingly common in todays world. however, using a cell phone in any way when operating a vehicle poses serious risks and should be prohibited. there are several key reasons why cell phone use should not be permitted when driving. first and foremost, cell phone use is enormously distracting for drivers. whether dialing a number, checking messages or social media, having a conversation, or other cell phone activities, the drivers attention is diverted from the critical task of operating the vehicle safely down the road. even handsfree calling requires the drivers mental attention, as they focus on the conversation instead of their surroundings. numerous studies have shown that cell phone use while driving can reduce the amount of brain activity associated with driving by almost . this level of distraction significantly increases the risk of accidents. second, the distracted driving caused by cell phone use slows drivers reaction times. it takes longer for drivers to notice hazards and respond appropriately when preoccupied with their phones. slower reaction times can transform even minor situations into collisions. research shows cell phone use makes a drivers braking reaction as impaired as driving drunk. at highway speeds, the extra halfsecond to full second of slowed reaction time from phone distraction can mean the difference between avoiding an accident and crashing. third, cell phone use presents a manual distraction as drivers manipulate their phones. even purportedly handsfree options require some physical interaction to activate or adjust settings. the manual behavior distracts a drivers hands from the controls of the vehicle. texting, in particular, involves prolonged visual attention away from the road that can last several seconds at a time  an eternity when driving. taking their eyes and hands off the road for any period endangers both the distracted driver as well as other motorists and pedestrians sharing the roadway. in summary, cell phone use imposes an unacceptable safety risk to drivers and others due to the severe visual, manual, and cognitive distractions it introduces into the critical task of driving. the proliferation of phones does not lessen this reality. for the safety of all road users, drivers must keep their full attention dedicated to the complex task of navigating vehicles, unimpeded by cell phone interactions of any kind. a total ban on cell phone use is the only reasonable policy that adequately protects life and property on our roadways.
