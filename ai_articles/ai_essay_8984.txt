the importance of early childhood education is often overlooked in todays society. its a shame, as the years between birth and age five are some of the most formative ones for any human being. its at this time that children learn to be curious and explore their environment, develop social skills, acquire basic academic knowledge like letters and numbers, build motor skills, gain confidence  all important traits for success later on in life. early childhood education isnt just about teaching kids how to read or count either it also involves helping them understand their emotions by providing them with coping mechanisms such as practice expressing themselves through art or playing with toys under adult guidance. teachers who work in early learning centers are key players in aiding childrens development during these vital few years they must operate well within emotional intelligence guidelines when managing student behavior so that no one feels wronged or unfairly treated. studies have shown that those who engage in quality early childhood education programs are more likely to succeed academically than those without one. additionally, many experts agree that students involved with prek learning tend to show increased capabilities across various intellectual domains involving language development, mathematics skills acquisition and problem solving strategies compared to those not enrolled in educational settings prior to kindergarten entry. its essential for parents everywhere to recognize the value of proper early childhood education before starting primary school particularly if there is a lack of resources available at home due to family poverty levels or language barriers  as both sets of circumstances can easily impact a childs ability and chances for success once heshe enters formal schooling environments down the line. early investment makes all the difference!
