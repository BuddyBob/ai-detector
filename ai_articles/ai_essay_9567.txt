the world of competitive gaming has created an entirely new generation of athletes. people who might not have had the opportunity to compete in traditional sports can now make a name for themselves as professional gamers. with organized leagues and tournaments, players can show off their skills and prove their mettle on the virtual battlefields. many people question whether or not these video game players should be considered actual athletes, but proponents argue that it isnt about what kind of sport you play its about how hard you work at it and how dedicated you are to becoming better. just like conventional athletes, professional gamers must practice day in and day out so they dont lose their edge during competition. they must stay ahead of the curve when it comes to strategy, knowing when to attack or defend against particular opponents. most importantly, they need to develop mental fortitude so they will remain calm under pressure while still playing with precision accuracy no matter what happens during heated moments within games. competitive gaming is even starting to gain traction outside its own community too many companies are taking notice by investing in progaming teams which compete regularly at various events across the globe for prize money and glory. this acceptance from major corporations such as microsoft only further legitimizes this profession among mainstream audiences who may have viewed it skeptically before now understanding that there is more than meets the eye when concerning serious esports competitors  these players put in just as much hard work if not more than any other athlete bound for stardom yet still require respect from all sides if progress is going to be made moving forward into the future for this rapidly growing industry of professional gaming culture .
