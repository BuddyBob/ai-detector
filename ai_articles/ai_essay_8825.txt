they say the memories we make when travelling are some of the most important in our lives. its no wonder then that so many people around the world strive to have unique and interesting experiences while on holiday, seeking out new sights, sounds and tastes. a great way to do this is by visiting countries with rich cultures that differ significantly from your own  it can be a truly eyeopening experience! one such country would be thailand its vibrant atmosphere, amazing cuisine and stunning scenery will surely captivate anyone who visits. the first thing youll notice about thailand is the warm hospitality extended by its locals. everywhere you go, youll find friendly people willing to help guide you through their culture and share tales of their homeland with eager ears. the second thing youll likely notice is how varied it is  from bustling cities like bangkok filled with street markets selling all kinds of wares to more rural areas where traditional cooking methods remain an integral part of life. all throughout, theres plenty to explore!. when it comes time for food, thai cuisine will not disappoint popular dishes include tom yum soup made with lemongrass and chilli paste as well as pad thai noodles served alongside prawns or chicken in a sweet sauce. and dont forget about desserts like mango sticky rice or coconut ice cream  perfect treats after a long day exploring!. adventure seekers will also find themselves spoilt for choice in thailand trekking through lush forests teeming with wildlife before cooling off at hidden waterfalls may just become your new favourite activity here! for those looking for something slightly less strenuous but equally rewarding and instagrammable!, why not try making lanterns from paper before releasing them into the night sky?
