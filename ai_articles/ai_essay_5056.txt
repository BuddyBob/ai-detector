computer games have become ubiquitous among children, who are attracted to the colourful graphics, immersive storylines, and addictive gameplays. some people have argued that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual, and psychological development. thus, they believe that effective measures must be taken to prevent children from playing them. in my opinion, this belief is unfounded. firstly, computer games can be beneficial for childrens physical development. many action, sports, and adventure games incorporate physical exercise into the gameplays. for example, many games such as dance dance revolution, wii sports and ring fit adventure require players to mimic reallife exercises, such as jumping, running and stretching. as a result, these games can encourage children to become more physically active, helping them to improve their physical health. secondly, computer games are also beneficial for childrens intellectual development. in educational games, children can learn a variety of topics, such as mathematics, science, geography and history, which can greatly enhance their academic performance. moreover, strategy and puzzle games can help children develop problemsolving abilities and logical thinking. for instance, the popular game, minecraft, provides a sandboxlike environment where children can build their own virtual worlds, which can help them to hone their creativity and innovation. thirdly, computer games can help to relieve stress and anxiety for children. during a stressful day, playing computer games can provide children with a sense of pleasure and relaxation. as a result, this can reduce the effects of psychological problems such as depression and mood swings. in conclusion, computer games can be beneficial for childrens physical, intellectual and psychological development. in my opinion, instead of preventing children from playing computer games, effective measures should be taken to help them to choose suitable and safe games to play.
