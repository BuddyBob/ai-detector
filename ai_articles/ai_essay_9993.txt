when it comes to the topic of organic food, many people view it as a healthier alternative to traditional methods of farming and producing food. indeed, there is an abundance of evidence that suggests eating organic foods has numerous beneficial effects on our health. however, whats often overlooked in this conversation are the environmental benefits associated with choosing organic over conventional goods. organic farming relies heavily on natural forms of pest control and nutrient replenishment in contrast to chemical pesticides which can damage both soil composition and ecosystems. organic agriculture works alongside nature rather than against it by focusing on maintaining biodiversity within our agricultural systems. in addition, organically grown produce requires less energy for production due to its lack of dependence on synthetic fertilizers or herbicides reducing greenhouse gases and other forms pollutants emitted from machines used in conventional farming processes like tilling and harvesting. . the process responsible for transporting food from farmtotable also takes advantage when switching from conventional to organic foods since the former usually involves greater distances traveled before reaching the consumer while local or regional farms selling organics are more commonplace in certain areas  further decreasing transport related emissions and pollution levels overall. with all this considered, transitioning toward buying onlymostly organic products not only helps us as individuals but contributes positively towards eliminating some of the adverse impacts modern day agriculture poses on our planets fragile environment too!
