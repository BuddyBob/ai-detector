its easy to take for granted the power of technology in our daytoday lives. we use it for communication, entertainment, and even shopping. but seldom do we think about how much impact technology has had on art, specifically visual art. over the last few decades, digital artwork has been revolutionizing the way people create pieces that stand out from others. from film production to painting, digital tools have become a crucial part of any artists arsenal  allowing them unprecedented freedom when creating their own works of art. take computer animation as an example there was once a time where animating characters took weeks or months now it can be completed with relative ease using software like adobe animate or autodesk maya. not only does this reduce cost and labour involved in making animated films but also allows artists to experiment more freely with different techniques like hand drawn elements integrated into d models or highly stylized animations made entirely within a computer graphics program . this level of experimentation used to require expensive equipment or worse  hours upon hours of manual labor but with modern technology these ideas can easily come alive! it gives creators room to push boundaries and work faster than ever before  leading us down roads less travelled by traditional animators who are limited by physical materials when working on their projects. technology has enabled such creative breakthroughs not just in animation but across so many different mediums painters can simulate lighting conditions right on their tablet devices using applications like sketchable while sculptors have access to powerful d printing machines which allow them recreate intricate shapes without having to carve each piece individually by hand!
