starting from a young age, we are often taught that failure is not an option, and that trying and eventually failing is considered a sign of defeat. however, i believe that failure can actually help one be successful. as winston churchill famously said, success is the ability to go from failure to failure without losing enthusiasm. failure can make one stronger, in both a mental and physical sense. it shows you that you cant rely on external factors to get the outcome you desire. it also forces you to dig deep within and find strength you were unaware of before. this is a crucial trait for anyone wanting to truly become successful. failure also has another, more immediate, benefit. it teaches you not to make the same mistake again. it helps you assess the situation more deeply, understand what went wrong and how you can adjust your behavior and strategies in the future. through this you gain more knowledge and learn to become more mindful when deciding how to act or go about achieving your goals. finally, failure can help you find the right path to success. this is because no successful person ever got what they wanted on the first try. success is built upon a series of trial and error, so through failure we can assess and understand the roadblocks, and, with perseverance and determination, create a plan or approach that will eventually get us to where we desire to go. in conclusion, failure should not be seen as the end, but rather a part of the process to success. through failure we can become stronger, learn from our mistakes and find the right path to success. as winston churchill so wisely said, success is the ability to go from failure to failure without losing enthusiasm.
