there is no single answer to the question of which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. it depends on the individual learners needs and goals. however, i believe that speaking is more important than reading for most learners. there are several reasons why speaking is more important than reading. first, speaking is essential for communication. unless a learner can speak english fluently, he or she will not be able to communicate with other english speakers. second, speaking is a more natural way to learn a language. when a learner speaks english, he or she is able to hear the language spoken by native speakers and learn the correct pronunciation. third, speaking is more fun than reading. it is more enjoyable to speak with others than to read a book. that said, i do not believe that reading is unimportant. reading is a good way to improve a learners vocabulary and understanding of english. however, i believe that speaking is more important for most learners because it is essential for communication and it is more fun and natural than reading.
