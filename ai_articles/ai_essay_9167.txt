a wealth of knowledge that goes beyond traditional textbooks. with this new tool comes many benefits as well as potential drawbacks and issues that must be considered when assessing its impact on educational effectiveness. one major advantage that computers bring into the classroom is increased engagement from students. in comparison to traditional lectures or reading assignments, incorporating digital media such as videos or animations in class presentations can keep students interested longer and help make material more memorable studies have shown higher retention rates when multimedia elements are included than through strictly textbased instruction. additionally, providing relevant applications such as software simulations helps students visualize abstract concepts which can further increase participation levels within classes by making complex ideas easier for all learners regardless of their background knowledge or academic ability. furthermore, using online resources like khan academy allows educators to supplement existing curricula with courses tailored specifically towards the needs of individual learners so they can get the most out of their studies
