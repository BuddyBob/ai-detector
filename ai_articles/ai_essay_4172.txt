the importance of being successful in life versus being a failure depends on how an individual defines success. success lies in achieving and maintaining it, while failure involves giving up or not being able to meet the standards of success. diligence, hard work, and dedication are the most important qualities needed in order to be successful. to prevent failing in life, one must set realistic goals and make sure to create a plan to achieve it. even after failing, one can capitalize on past mistakes and become successful again by learning from them. however, staying successful all the time comes with its own set of challenges. it can be difficult to remain humble and maintain that same level of success. additionally, one should be aware that complacency can lead to a downward spiral of failure. therefore, to remain successful, it is important to remain aware of ones surroundings and adjust according to new challenges.
