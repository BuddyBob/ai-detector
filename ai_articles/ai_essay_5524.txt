there is no doubt that our society has become increasingly wasteful in recent years. we are producing more and more garbage, and much of it is plastic bags and other types of packaging. this is a major problem, because plastic bags and other types of packaging are not biodegradable and can take hundreds of years to decompose. there are several reasons why this has happened. firstly, the use of plastic bags is incredibly convenient. they are lightweight and can be easily carried around, which makes them perfect for carrying groceries or other items. secondly, many stores and businesses give away free plastic bags, which encourages people to use them. and finally, many people simply do not know how to recycle plastic bags and other types of packaging. as a result of all of this, we are filling up our environment with plastic bags and rubbish that we cannot fully dispose of. this is a major problem, because it is harming our planet and causing serious environmental damage. there are several things that we can do to address this problem. firstly, we need to start using less plastic bags. we can do this by bringing our own reusable bags when we go shopping, or by refusing to take a plastic bag when we dont need one. secondly, we need to start recycling plastic bags and other types of packaging. this can be done by taking them to a local recycling centre, or by using a recycling bin at home. and finally, we need to educate ourselves and our children about the importance of recycling and reducing waste. in conclusion, i believe that we have developed into a throwaway society, and this is having a negative impact on our environment. we need to start using less plastic bags and recycling more plastic bags and other types of packaging. by doing this, we can help to protect our planet and ensure a sustainable future for our children.
