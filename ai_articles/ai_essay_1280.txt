seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because it gives them different perspectives and insights. when you ask only one person for advice, they can only offer their own opinion based on their own experiences and biases. but if you ask multiple people, you can get a more wellrounded view of the situation and consider different options. for example, lets say youre trying to decide what sport to play in the upcoming season. if you only ask your best friend who loves basketball, they might tell you to play basketball too. but if you also ask your cousin who is a soccer player and your neighbor who does track and field, they might suggest those sports instead. then you can think about what you like and what youre good at, and make a more informed decision. in addition, seeking multiple opinions can also help you avoid making a bad choice. if everyone you ask agrees on something, its probably a good idea. but if there are conflicting opinions, you might need to do more research or think harder about whats right for you. overall, asking multiple people for advice can help you make a better choice and feel more confident in your decision.
