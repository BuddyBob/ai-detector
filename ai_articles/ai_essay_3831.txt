chinese culture has a long history of confucian values, which emphasize education and selfimprovement through study and contemplation. this is in stark contrast to the philosophy of ralph waldo emerson, who values selfreliance, independence, and intuition. to the chinese, education and tradition are extremely important, and students are taught to learn and grow from the wisdom of their elders. additionally, chinese families and communities place a high value on respect and loyalty. for emerson, on the other hand, selfimprovement is achieved not through studying conventional wisdom, but rather through individualism, openminded exploration, and creativity. thus, there is a distinctly different outlook between the two philosophies, with chinese learning focusing on respect for tradition, while emersons is centered on the exploration of ideas.
