seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because considering different perspectives allows you to get a more wellrounded view of the situation. when you only talk to one person, you only get their viewpoint and advice. but different people look at things in different ways and have had different life experiences. getting advice from more than one person opens you up to new ideas and information you may not have thought of on your own. one persons advice alone could potentially be flawed or biased in some way without their realizing it. but when you average out the input of multiple individuals, their collective biases are less likely to mislead you. you can look for common themes in what different advisors tell you to identify what is most objectively true. talking to more friends, family members, teachers or others wellsuited to offer guidance also increases the chances something important wont be overlooked. one person may forget to mention a key factor, but others are likely to catch what gets missed. considering a variety of opinions also helps ensure a choice feels right from multiple angles rather than possibly being influenced too heavily by just one persons potentially limited viewpoint. ultimately, weighing various advice streams gives a choicemaker the most wellrounded set of pros and cons to contemplate before deciding. it helps reduce random errors and brings the best collective wisdom to bear. seeking multiple opinions, then, is statistically more likely to lead to a choice someone feels confident and comfortable with in the long run.
