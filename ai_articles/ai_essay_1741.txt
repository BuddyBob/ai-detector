while cell phones have become an integral part of modern life, using a cell phone while driving can seriously endanger safety on the road. drivers should not be able to use cell phones in any capacity when operating a vehicle. there are a few key reasons why cell phone use while driving should be prohibited. first and foremost, distracting activities like talking or texting on a phone take a drivers eyes off the road and mind off their main task  safely navigating the vehicle. numerous studies have shown that even handsfree calling can impair reaction times and decision making while driving. when every second counts to avoid an accident, any distraction from focus on ones surroundings poses an unacceptable risk. secondly, cell phone use while driving puts not only the user but others on the road in danger. one wrong move such as veering into another lane or running a red light due to distraction could have catastrophic consequences and seriously injure or kill innocent people. as operators of heavy machinery that can cause harm, drivers have a responsibility to maintain fully attention on the task of driving at all times. finally, prohibiting cell phone use except in emergency situations establishes a clear standard that helps change driver behavior for the better. with cell phone use now banned in many places, drivers learn not to rely on their phones when navigating traffic. they can focus fully on arrival safely without endangering lives. while access to communication technologies is important, it should never come at the cost of human life and safety on public roads. in emergencies like responding to breakdowns or accidents, phones may be used, but for general use they have no place in the drivers seat. in conclusion, any kind of cell phone use provides too much of a distraction from the critical task of driving. lives are at stake with each vehicle on the road. to protect all citizens from potential harm, drivers should not have access to cell phone functions of any kind except in real emergencies. public safety must take priority over any convenience cell phones provide, and a total ban offers the safest standard for our roads.
