in todays fastpaced world, young people are often criticized for not giving enough time to helping their communities. while it is true that some young people may not be actively involved in community service, it is unfair to generalize and say that all young people are indifferent to the needs of their communities. in this essay, i will argue that young people are indeed contributing to their communities in various ways. firstly, young people are often involved in social media campaigns and online activism. with the advent of social media, it has become easier for young people to connect with likeminded individuals and raise awareness about social issues. for instance, the metoo movement, which started as a social media campaign, has brought attention to the issue of sexual harassment and assault. similarly, young people are using social media to raise awareness about climate change, mental health, and other important issues. while this may not involve physical volunteering, it is still a form of community service that can have a significant impact. secondly, young people are often involved in extracurricular activities that benefit their communities. for instance, many high school students participate in volunteer clubs that organize events such as food drives, blood drives, and fundraisers for local charities. additionally, many young people are involved in sports teams, music groups, and other organizations that give back to their communities. these activities not only benefit the community but also teach young people important skills such as leadership, teamwork, and communication. finally, young people are often involved in political activism and advocacy. many young people are passionate about issues such as gun control, lgbtq rights, and racial justice, and they are using their voices to advocate for change. for instance, after the parkland shooting in , many young people organized marches and protests to demand stricter gun control laws. similarly, young people are using their voices to advocate for racial justice and equality, as seen in the black lives matter movement. while this may not involve physical volunteering, it is still a form of community service that can have a significant impact. in conclusion, while it is true that some young people may not be actively involved in community service, it is unfair to generalize and say that all young people are indifferent to the needs of their communities. young people are contributing to their communities in various ways, including social media campaigns, extracurricular activities, and political activism. as a society, we should encourage and support young people in their efforts to make a positive impact on their communities.
