i agree that we have developed into a throwaway society. every day, we generate huge amounts of waste, much of which is nonbiodegradable. this means that it will stay in the environment for centuries, polluting the land and water and harming wildlife. one of the biggest culprits is plastic. we use it for everything, from packaging our food to carrying our shopping. and when were finished with it, we just throw it away. but plastic takes hundreds of years to break down, so its accumulating in our environment. its estimated that there are now . trillion pieces of plastic in the worlds oceans, harming marine life and polluting the water. we need to find ways to reduce our reliance on plastic and other disposable items. we can start by recycling more, and using reusable bags and containers. we also need to change our attitudes towards waste. we need to see it as a valuable resource that can be reused or recycled, rather than something to be thrown away. if we dont make these changes, we will continue to damage our environment and leave a legacy of pollution for future generations.
