success is not final, failure is not fatal it is the courage to continue that counts, said winston churchill. reflecting on the importance of failure on the path to success, it is an integral role and often presents learning opportunities when things dont go the way they were planned. with each failure, people gain insight into what went wrong and can learn how to improve the situation next time. not only that, but failing gives us the chance to build underlying strength, resilience and perseverance that help propel us on our journey towards success. not only can failure bring about success, but it often makes us better human beings, with strong character traits that help us succeed in the long run.
