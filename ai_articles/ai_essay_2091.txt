a positive attitude plays a crucial role in achieving success in life. it can increase the chances of making a good impression in job interviews and can also help demonstrate the positive traits that come from a persons upbringing and education. furthermore, having a good attitude can be directly linked to better academic achievement. although attitudes can be shaped by environment and experiences, they are also internalized and focused upon by the individual. consequently, attitudes cannot be completely avoided, however the benefits of having a positive attitude are far greater than those of a bad attitude. those who choose to have a good attitude are more likely to reach their goals and attain success in life.
