committing to a career at a young age has many advantages. for one, those who are younger often have the capacity to learn faster, meaning that they can get ahead with their career preparations sooner. the mental and knowledge preparation that is a part of committing to a career at a young age is also one of its benefits, as they will have the time to explore different career options and become properly informed of what the job will entail. on the other hand, students who dont want to commit to choosing a career at a young age may find themselves with a disadvantage later on without having the time to find out what career they would ideally like, they may find themselves accepting jobs they dont want out of desperation. by committing to a career at a young age, students can ensure that they get the most out of their education and embark on the career path they have chosen with confidence.
