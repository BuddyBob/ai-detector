it is important to keep in mind that failure is a stepping stone on the path to success. countless successful people have experienced setbacks and yet persevered towards their goals. it is this resilience, recognition, and acknowledgement of ones failures that helps to instill confidence and strength. with each failure, comes the opportunity to learn, to identify what could have been done differently, and how it might be achieved in the future. having small successes along the journey also helps to provide momentum. success does not have to come in grandiose gestures in order to be counted. acknowledging progress in small steps in the right direction can help to build more confidence to eventually reach larger successes. although mistakes can seem daunting and sometimes a source of shame, it is important to remember that success is rarely achieved without failure. it is important to remember that failure should not be seen as a sign of weakness but rather an opportunity to grow and learn. it is only with this mindset that one can truly hone in on what is necessary to reach the ultimate goal.
