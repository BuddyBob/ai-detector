there is no denying that travelling in a group led by a tour guide has its advantages. for one, it takes away the hassle of having to plan and map out your route as the tour guide would have already done that for you. furthermore, being in a group usually costs less than travelling solo as tour companies are able to negotiate better rates with service providers such as hotels and restaurants. finally, travelling with a tour guide means that you would always have someone to ask for help or advice, whether it is about the best place to eat or how to get to your next destination. however, there are also some disadvantages to travelling in a group led by a tour guide. for instance, you would have less freedom and flexibility to do things at your own pace. this is especially true if you are travelling with a large group where everyone would have to stick to the schedule set by the tour guide. additionally, you might not get to see and experience everything that a place has to offer if you are following a set itinerary. overall, i believe that whether or not travelling in a group led by a tour guide is the best way to travel depends on the individual. some people might prefer the convenience and safety of travelling in a group, while others might prefer the flexibility and freedom of travelling solo.
