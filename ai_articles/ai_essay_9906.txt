few people can argue against the fact that technology has drastically improved the way we live. in todays world, it is almost impossible to imagine life without computers and electronic devices. with this in mind, it is worth considering how technology has impacted one very specific aspect of our lives language learning. for centuries, traditional methods have been used by language learners to become proficient in a new tonguewhether through reading textbooks, immersing oneself in native culture or undertaking an intensive period of study abroad. while these traditional methods are still effective for some individuals, there is no doubt that the introduction of technological advancements has significantly enhanced the ease and speed with which modernday students acquire a foreign language. in recent years, interactive websites such as duolingo have emerged as an invaluable tool for those wanting to learn a second or third language quickly and effectively from their own homes or anywhere else. by utilizing sophisticated algorithms coupled with userfriendly visual interfaces such as word matching games and virtual flashcards, duolingo provides users with exercises tailored specifically towards their individual needs while tracking progress along the way. not only does this make the process more fun but also helps build confidence when speaking aloud due to its rewarding feedback system  both verbally and visually  each time something is learned correctly. beyond online tools like duolingo there are now countless mobile applications available on smartphones too  many of which use similar techniques albeit on smaller scales  so even brief moments during commutes can be put towards developing linguistic proficiency if need be!. as well as providing more conventional instruction based around grammar rules and vocabulary lists they offer activities such as pronunciation drills where users record themselves speaking certain phrases then receive corrective feedback from automated voice recognition software systems all helping them perfect their accentuation over time via repeated practice sessions. . all things considered then its clear that nowhere near enough credit can be given to what modern day technology offers us when learning languages  enabling greater flexibility
