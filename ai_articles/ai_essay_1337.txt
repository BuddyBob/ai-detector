seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because different people have different perspectives and experiences. for example, if im deciding which sport to join and i ask my friends who play basketball, they might all suggest that i join the basketball team since thats what they know and enjoy. but if i also ask my cousin who plays soccer, he might tell me about the benefits of playing soccer and give me a different perspective to consider. by gathering multiple opinions, i get a more wellrounded view of the options and can weigh the pros and cons better. it also helps me avoid making a decision based solely on one persons opinion or bias. for instance, if my best friend really wants me to join the drama club with her, she might unintentionally pressure me into it without considering my interests or strengths. however, if i ask my drama teacher and my parents for their thoughts as well, i can get a clearer picture of whether drama is truly the right choice for me. in summary, seeking multiple opinions helps me make a better choice by providing diverse perspectives, preventing bias, and allowing me to weigh my options more thoroughly.
