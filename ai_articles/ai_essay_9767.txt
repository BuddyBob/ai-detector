our health and the planets health. they also reduce energy consumption associated with shipping foods from rural areas or overseas sources, resulting in fewer emissions that contribute to global warming. additionally, compared to traditional farming methods, where land has been cleared of vegetation and fertilizers are used on large monocultures a single crop planted over a large area, urban gardens require less space and make use of natural resources such as composting waste materials into nutrientrich soil amendments that benefit plants without relying on chemical fertilizers or pesticides. another major benefit is how it can help bring communities togetherurban gardening encourages individuals who wouldnt otherwise interact with each other because theyre strangers sharing
