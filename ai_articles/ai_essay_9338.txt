office or hospital setting. health practitioners can also use remote monitoring devices that provide realtime data about vital signs like blood pressure or heart rate over long distances which helps with diagnosis and follow up care for their patients. the development of telemedicine has revolutionized the way healthcare is provided in many ways it increases access to specialists who may not be
