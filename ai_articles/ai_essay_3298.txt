imagination has incredible power and can often be far more important than knowledge. it has the power to innovate, to create, and to improve. take the example of the wright brothers. knowing the principles of aerodynamic flight, would not have been enough to make them the first to fly in a heavierthanair craft. it was their imagination, their belief that they could create something that had never been created before, that allowed them to succeed. the same is true today. having knowledge is important, but its our imagination that often makes the biggest difference. projects that challenge us to think about different ways of doing something, and to come up with innovative approaches, often benefit more from imaginative thinking than from a deeper understanding of the subject matter. by applying our imaginations, we can take something that might seem impossible, and make it a reality. imagination can also be a highly useful tool in our work and personal lives. it can help us generate ideas for dealing with problems, for creating new avenues of income, and for finding more efficient and effective ways of doing almost anything we undertake. it can also be used to help us work though challenging feelings such as fear, anxiety, and even depression. by using imaginative exercises, we can work through our emotions in a healthy, constructive way. imagination can be invaluable when combined with knowledge, but it can also be an indispensable tool on its own. it can help us dream up new possibilities, forge new pathways, and lead us to places we never thought possible. that is why imagination can often be more important than knowledge.
