excerpt ralph waldo emerson is a renowned philosopher and poet whose works are still quoted today. one of his most renowned quotes is, growth is the only evidence of life. in this quote, emerson explores the importance of growth in our lives. growth, in its many forms, is essential to living a fulfilling life. it can be seen through the physical, mental, and emotional changes that people experience throughout their life. but more than just being a natural part of life, growth is also something that requires dedication and effort to attain. people must be actively involved in the process of personal development. whether through intellectual pursuits, creative expression, or forging meaningful connections, people must seek out opportunities to grow and be open to the change that comes along with it. by doing this, they will be more able to use the growth that they encounter to become the best version of themselves.
