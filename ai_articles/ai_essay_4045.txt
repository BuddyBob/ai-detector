growing up, i was taught that adhering to the paths of others was the only sure way to achieve success. i did what i was told in order to fit in, to appease authority figures and to repay kindness and debts. over time, i became used to the idea that i had to do what was expected of me  but i was gradually losing my own sense of identity and purpose. realizing that i needed to find a balance, i began searching for ways to differentiate between external and internal pressures. dr. seusss words of wisdom have been a great source of comfort and guidance as i strive for a life of authenticity. he famously wrote, today you are you, that is truer than true. there is no one alive who is youer than you. this quote reminds me that there is nothing wrong with embracing different paths and opportunities, as long as i am true to myself.
