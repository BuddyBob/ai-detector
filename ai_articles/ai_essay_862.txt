maintaining this balance, the electoral college system provides adequate representation for citizens in both small and large states. lastly, the electoral college system eliminates the need for runoff elections, which can be timeconsuming, costly, and complex. runoff elections often occur when no candidate receives a majority of the votes cast, leading to prolonged uncertainty and potential divisiveness. the electoral college, on the other hand, nearly always produces a clear winner, preventing the need for an additional round of voting. while there are arguments against the electoral
