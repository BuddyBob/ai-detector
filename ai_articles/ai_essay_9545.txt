as technology advances and becomes more commonplace, examining the implications of increased automation on our job market is an important topic to consider. automation has been making its presence felt in many industries in recent years by replacing human labour with machines. this has caused disruption for workers who used to be employed by these industries, as well as those still working in them. the most obvious effect of automation on our job market is a decrease in employment opportunities for humans. machines are often able to complete tasks faster, more accurately and consistently than their human counterparts this means that businesses can reduce cost by investing in automated processes rather than hiring people to do the same work. as automated processes become increasingly sophisticated, it is likely that these effects will only increase over time, leading to fewer jobs available for people. additionally, some jobs may never be replaced entirely but are instead adapted so they require less human input through the use of ai or other new technologies  even if a human role exists within the process there will be fewer positions needed compared with before automation was introduced. this decrease of employment opportunities has widereaching implications beyond just those individual employees affected directly entire communities can suffer when local economies suffer due to lack of job opportunities or skillsets becoming obsolete because of technological advancement wages may also drop overall as employers no longer have to compete for skilled labor unless they pay competitive wages since machines dont need salaries or benefits finally businesses themselves will face increasing pressures from competition who have taken advantage of new technologies which make them more efficient leading to potential losses if businesses cannot keep up technologically speaking and remain competitive enough without cutting employee wages too much which could hurt morale further contributing towards workplace problems such as absenteeism etc . . while there are certainly negatives associated with increased automation on our job market we must recognize that there can also be positive impacts resulting from this shift too such as improved safety no longer needing dangerous manual labor , greater precision accurate data gathering, higher quality productsservices
