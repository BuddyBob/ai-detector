the power of music cannot be underestimated. from calming a restless mind to inspiring creativity, it can have a profound effect on individuals and societies alike. it has the capacity to bind people together or divide them apart. music is often seen as an integral part of any culture, with particular songs being linked strongly to certain countries or eras in history. indeed, many nations even use music as a form of patriotism for example, god save the queen is still sung proudly by british citizens today despite its age and longstanding family ties within the uk monarchy. in recent years, there has been an increasing trend towards using music as therapy for various mental health issues such as depression and anxiety disorders. there are numerous studies that support this concept one study found that listening to classical mozart pieces resulted in significantly reduced stress levels among participants compared to those who listened only to silence during the same period of time . additionally, musical intervention has also been used successfully in treating conditions such as ptsd  and chronic pain , providing potential relief where traditional treatments have failed in some cases. music itself can take many forms  from pop hits on radio stations across the world right through to folk tunes which are specific only to certain regions or cultures  creating endless possibilities for those looking for something new or different when it comes their own therapy sessions. furthermore, due its relative affordability compared other forms of psychotherapy  such as cognitive behavioral therapy cbt  more people are now able incorporate musical elements into their treatment plans than ever before . its clear then that music should not be dismissed simply because it doesnt conform easily with scientific approaches favoured by many professionals today instead we should embrace its unique powers and consider how best we might use them when helping others gain control over their lives once again. music may never completely replace existing therapies but if nothing else it serves as reminder that sometimes all you need is few minutes away from reality
