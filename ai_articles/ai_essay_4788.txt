there are many different opinions on whether children should be taught to compete or cooperate. some people believe that it is important for children to learn how to compete in order to be successful in life. others believe that it is more important for children to learn how to cooperate in order to get along with others. i believe that both of these skills are important, and that children should be taught to do both. competing can be important in order to achieve success in life. if a child is able to compete and win, it can give them a feeling of accomplishment and boost their confidence. this can be helpful in many different areas of life, such as in academics, athletics, and business. however, competition can also have negative effects. if a child is always competing and never cooperating, they may become aggressive and arrogant. they may also have difficulty making friends, and may be less likely to succeed in relationships. cooperation is important for many reasons. first, it is necessary in order to get along with others. if a child can cooperate with others, they will be more likely to have successful relationships. second, cooperation is necessary for teamwork. if a child can work well with others as part of a team, they will be more likely to be successful in life. third, cooperation can help a child learn to compromise. if a child can learn to compromise, they will be less likely to have conflict with others. in conclusion, i believe that children should be taught to compete and cooperate. competition can help a child achieve success in life, but it is also important to learn how to cooperate. cooperation is necessary for getting along with others, working well in a team, and compromising.
