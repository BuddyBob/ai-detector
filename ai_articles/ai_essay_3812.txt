knowledge is far more important than imagination as it lays the foundation for progress and innovation. having knowledge enables one to apply creative and innovative thought to craft solutions to existing problems, as well as to explore new avenues of research and discovery. without knowledge, imagination is severely limited in its scope and impact. citing historical examples of great fiction from jules vernes science fiction stories to modern movies and television series, most of these creative works are rooted in some kind of sciencebased knowledge of the physical world. without an understanding of the physics of space travel, the imaginative worlds created would lack any kind of reality or believability. knowledge provides us with the framework and insight to apply our imagination productively, turning dream worlds into reality. without knowledge, imagination can only take us so far.
