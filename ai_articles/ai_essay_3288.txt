unfortunately, first impressions dont always go well. however, research shows that open minded people and the willingness to give second chances can often help to change a bad first impression. as an example, consider the story of an american investor who visited a foreign company with which she was looking to establish a business relationship. during the visit, the investor mispronounced one of the companys names, which caused many of the members of the staff to become offended. despite the unfavorable impression, the companys director chose to show understanding for the innocent mistake and offered the investor the benefit of a second chance. the directors openmindedness about the mispronunciation signaled to the investor that her careless error would not interfere with the potential business relationship. ultimately, the investor and the foreign company were able to develop a longterm, successful partnership. another example is a young, highachieving student who was applying to college. during his initial interview, the student made several mistakes and created a chaotic atmosphere. not only did the student misspell words during his presentation, but he was not able to recall important details from his past experiences. however, the interviewer simply reminded the student to relax and view the exercise as an informal conversation. from that point on, the student was able to loosen up and give the interviewer the information and anecdotes necessary to create an accurate and impressive impression. it is clear that openmindedness and second chances offer the opportunity to change a bad first impression. the examples of the investor who was able to create a successful business relationship and the student who was given the chance to improve his college interview both demonstrate the power of a second chance. with the right attitude and a little understanding, many unfavorable first impressions can transition into favorable ones.
