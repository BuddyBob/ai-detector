first impressions are powerful. they can shape our behavior towards a person in an often unintentional or unconscious manner. its amazing how one look or response in a meeting can make our opinion of a person. but whats even more special is that sometimes, these impressions are so powerful that no matter what a person does after, the first impression remains unchanged. this can be both a blessing and a curse. on the one hand, first impressions may help us pick up on traits of a person we may have otherwise overlooked. on the other hand, it may be difficult to sort out our own preconceptions and remain openminded. thats why its important to remember that first impressions dont always tell the full story. despite this, its important to accept people for who they are and respect them, rather than falling back on our own preconceptions.
