success is a subjective term that has different meanings for each individual. society has its own definition of success, which is often linked to external rewards such as wealth, fame, and power. however, i believe that true success can only be measured in relation to the goals one sets for oneself. in this essay, i will explain why i agree with this statement and provide specific reasons and examples to support my argument. to begin with, success is a personal journey that is unique to each individual. what may be considered successful for one person may not be the same for another. for instance, a person may set a goal to become a doctor or lawyer, while another person may aspire to be a musician or artist. both of these people are pursuing success, but in completely different fields. therefore, external rewards such as money or status cannot be used to measure the success of these individuals. moreover, external rewards often come with their own set of problems. for example, a person who achieves fame and fortune may find that it brings more stress and pressure than happiness. they may feel trapped in the public eye and unable to live a normal life. similarly, a person who has a highpaying job may feel unfulfilled if they are not pursuing their true passions. these external rewards may provide temporary happiness, but they do not guarantee longterm fulfillment. on the other hand, setting and achieving personal goals can bring a sense of purpose and fulfillment that cannot be found in external rewards. when a person sets a goal for themselves, they are often driven by their own passions and desires. achieving that goal can be incredibly rewarding and can provide a sense of accomplishment that cannot be found in wealth or fame. for example, a person who sets a goal to run a marathon may feel a tremendous sense of pride and accomplishment when they cross the finish line. this feeling of success is not dependent on external rewards but rather on the effort and dedication put into achieving the goal. furthermore, the pursuit of personal goals can lead to personal growth and development. when a person sets a goal for themselves, they often have to learn new skills and overcome obstacles to achieve it. this process can be challenging but ultimately rewarding. for example, a person who sets a goal to learn a new language may struggle with the nuances of grammar and vocabulary. however, when they finally become fluent in that language, they will have not only achieved their goal but also gained a new skill that can benefit them in many aspects of their life. this kind of personal growth cannot be measured by external rewards but is instead a testament to the individuals perseverance and tenacity. in conclusion, societys external rewards are no measure of true success. true success can only be measured in relation to the goals one sets for oneself. external rewards may bring temporary happiness, but they do not guarantee true fulfillment. pursuing personal goals, on the other hand, can bring a sense of purpose and fulfillment that cannot be found in external rewards. moreover, the pursuit of personal goals can lead to personal growth and development, which is a testament to the individuals perseverance and tenacity. therefore, i firmly believe that true success can only be measured in the context of personal goals and aspirations.
