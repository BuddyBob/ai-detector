drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while driving in todays technology driven world, cell phones have become virtually indispensable. however, the widespread use of cell phones while driving has become a major safety concern and should be banned. there are several key reasons for this first and foremost, cell phone use significantly increases distractions behind the wheel. whether dialing a number, texting, or browsing the internet, these activities take a drivers eyes off the road for prolonged periods. numerous studies have shown that even handsfree use of a cell phone leads to lapses in attention and slower reaction times similar to drunken driving. the brain can only focus on one task at a time, so any phone conversation splits driving performance. second, cell phone use is a direct factor in an alarming number of crashes each year. statistical data reveals cell phone interactions as a primary cause of thousands of accidents causing injuries and deaths that could have been avoided. texting poses one of the greatest risks, as it requires taking both hands off the wheel and eyes off the road. but even merely talking or listening to a call increases the likelihood of collision. finally, prohibiting cell phone use establishes a clear standard and protects all motorists. if permitted, exceptions and ambiguities around handsfree devices will remain problematic and unenforceable. driving is a complex task that requires full attention to myriad variables like traffic conditions and road signs. shifting even part of ones focus to a phone deprives them of the undivided concentration necessary to drive responsibly and react effectively in emergencies. in summary, the dangers of cell phone distracted driving far outweigh any perceived convenience or necessity. a total ban provides the simplest and most effective policy to curb this entirely preventable epidemic and save lives on our roads. the small sacrifice of not using a phone will have large benefits for public safety. for these compelling reasons, drivers should not be permitted cell phone use of any kind while operating a vehicle.
