the importance of physical activity in improving mental health is often overlooked. while the link between physical and mental wellbeing has been scientifically established, many people do not realize that engaging in regular exercise can have a significant impact on their moods and overall quality of life. from reducing stress to helping treat depression, the benefits of physical activity are numerous and should not be taken lightly. physical activity releases endorphins, which are hormones responsible for making us feel good naturally. when we engage in any type of moderate to vigorous exercise our bodies release these chemicals into our blood stream which makes us feel happier than before we began exercising. additionally, even low levels of aerobic exercise can reduce symptoms associated with anxiety such as muscle tension and racing thoughts by providing an outlet for pent up energy or emotions that could otherwise lead to negative feelings or behavior patterns. exercise also helps improve sleep quality since it tires out both your body and mind through the day allowing for better rest at night this further contributes to improved mental health because deep restful sleep is essential for cognitive processing functions like memory recall or problem solving skills needed for daily activities. furthermore, being physically active is important because it encourages social interaction when done regularly with others involved in similar pursuits as yourself such as members from a running club. having positive relationships with others provides more opportunities to express oneself while feeling safe doing so  something critical during times where depression strikes hardest  leading to greater selfesteem over time due an individuals ability to form closer relationships built on trust rather than fear or doubt previously experienced internally alone without external support systems functioning properly around them. ultimately, participating in regular physical exercises improves ones mental health significantly if done consistently over time it reduces stress levels while increasing happiness upon releasing endorphins into the body naturally along with improving social networks through joining clubs like running clubs as mentioned earlier all while providing an outlet whereby individuals can get away from worries faced everyday making them healthier overall mentally speaking if exercised
