when people ask for advice, they sometimes talk to more than one person because seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice. by talking to multiple people, they can get different perspectives and ideas that they may not have thought of before. for example, if someone is trying to decide what college to attend, they might talk to their guidance counselor, a family member, and a friend who is currently attending college. the guidance counselor might provide information about the colleges academic programs, the family member might offer advice based on their own college experience, and the friend might share their personal experiences and give insights into the colleges social scene. by considering all of these different opinions, the person can make a more informed decision about which college to choose. additionally, seeking multiple opinions can help someone feel more confident in their decision because they have considered different perspectives and made a choice based on a variety of factors.
