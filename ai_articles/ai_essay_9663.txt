music has been a major part of human life since the dawn of civilization. for as long as people have been living and breathing, music has played an important role in every culture around the world. even today, it is hard to imagine our lives without music, whether we enjoy listening to it or playing it ourselves. the potential for making music has always existed however, throughout history musical instruments were developed which allowed humanity to create more complex and vibrant sounds with greater control than before. from primitive percussion instruments made from natural materials such as wood or bone to modern day digital keyboards and synthesizers capable of creating any sound imaginable through electronic manipulation  countless varieties of musical instruments are available worldwide today. beyond what can be created by musicians with their own hands, technology now allows us to access vast libraries of recorded music across all genres from classical symphonies to cutting edge pop songs. this provides even those who cannot play an instrument the opportunity to appreciate a wide range of styles from different cultures and eras at the touch of a button  something that was previously impossible for anyone not able to physically attend live performances in person!. at its core, music remains one thing a form of expression that speaks volumes about its performers. it is no surprise then that millions upon millions continue to turn toward this art form time after time when seeking refuge from everyday stresses or simply looking for inspiration in their own creative pursuits. whether used therapeutically or recreationally, few things come close when comparing how universal yet personal this medium can be!
