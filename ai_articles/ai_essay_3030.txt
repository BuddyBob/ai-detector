my personal experience with selfreliance relates to my college decision. at the beginning of my senior year in high school, i had to decide where i wanted to go to college. i wanted to make the decision on my own, without relying too much on expert advice. after spending months doing my own research, i ultimately decided to attend my dream school. it should not be understated how important selfreliance is when making important decisions. if i had relied too heavily on the advice of others, i would have had to take their opinions into account and could have ended up attending a college that was not originally my first choice. selfreliance gave me the confidence and drive to make the best decision for myself. at the same time, it is important to be aware of the pros and cons of making decisions independently versus relying on expert advice. it is often beneficial to seek expert opinions when making decisions, as they may be able to provide insights and resources that i may not have considered on my own. overall, it is important for individuals to research and consider the facts before making important decisions, and to weigh the pros and cons between selfreliance and seeking expert advice. my personal experience in relation to selfreliance is evidence of how it can play an important role in life decisions.
