the traditional chinese culture regards cancer as a very serious disease. families usually keep this information from patients until the very end, when they are either too sick or have passed away. this practice is often seen as disrespectful and can cause immense emotional distress for patients and their families. however, this traditional approach to cancer care is not always in the best interests of patients. for example, withholding information can lead to incorrect diagnosis and treatment, which can be more harmful than helpful. furthermore, patients who are not given the full picture of their disease may feel overwhelmed and helpless, which can further damage their morale. as modernization advances, it is important that the chinese culture changes its approach to cancer care. patients should be given the full picture of their disease as soon as possible so that they can make informed decisions about their treatment. additionally, families should be more open about their loved ones diagnosis and should not try to keep the information from them. this will help to ensure that patients receive the best possible care and are able to cope with their illness in a healthy way.
