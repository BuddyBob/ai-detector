there is no definitive answer as to which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. however, i believe that speaking is more important, for the following reasons first, speaking allows learners to use the language for communication. this is essential for learning a language, as it allows learners to use the language in a realworld context. speaking also helps learners to improve their pronunciation and fluency. second, speaking helps learners to develop their language skills. speaking involves using all four language skills  listening, speaking, reading and writing. by practicing these skills, learners will become more proficient in the language. third, speaking is more fun than reading. when learners are engaged in a conversation, they are more likely to enjoy the experience and learn more effectively. reading can be a bit boring, especially for beginners. finally, speaking is a great way to make friends and learn about other cultures. by speaking with native english speakers, learners can learn about the culture and customs of englishspeaking countries. they can also make friends with people from all over the world. in conclusion, while reading is an important skill, speaking is more important for chinese learners. speaking allows learners to use the language for communication, develop their language skills, and learn about other cultures.
