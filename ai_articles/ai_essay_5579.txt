nowadays, people have paid more and more attention to degree certificates. for example, in many institutions, ones promotion is primarily decided by whether one has obtained a graduate degree or not. a growing number of critics say that if this tendency goes to the extreme, young people may be misled. a degree certificate can reflect only ones academic achievements but not all abilities essential for successful career. i agree with this point of view. there are several reasons why a degree certificate may not be a good indicator of ones ability to have a successful career. first of all, a degree certificate only reflects ones academic achievements, which may not be relevant to the job that one is seeking. for example, a degree in engineering may not be helpful if one wants to become a journalist. secondly, obtaining a degree often requires a lot of time and money, and not everyone is able to do so. this means that some people may be very talented but may not have a degree certificate, which may hinder their career prospects. finally, having a degree certificate does not necessarily mean that one is able to use it in a practical way. for example, a person may have a degree in mathematics but may not be able to use it in the real world because he or she does not have any work experience. all these factors show that a degree certificate is not always a good indicator of ones ability to have a successful career. there are many other factors, such as ones work experience, skills and talents, that are also important for career success. therefore, young people should not be misled into thinking that a degree certificate is the only thing that matters. they should instead focus on developing all the skills and abilities that are necessary for a successful career.
