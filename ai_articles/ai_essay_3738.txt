technology impacts the lives of people around the world in countless ways, both positive and negative. in positive terms, technology can be a lifeline in emergency situations, allowing those in need to get the help they need quickly. it can also help to shape attitudes, providing access to information the average person would not have had in years past. on the downside, too much reliance on the virtual world can create an imbalance in peoples lives, causing them to prioritize spending time online over spending time in the physical world. people can become so accustomed to virtual connection that they may struggle to form meaningful relationships with people in their own community. ultimately, technology can be a force for good, provided it is used responsibly and in moderation.
