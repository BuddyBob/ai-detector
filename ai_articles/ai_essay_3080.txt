at our school, we have developed a mentorship program that pairs older students with younger ones. through this program, older students can help younger students get situated for the school year, helping them identify their classes and assisting them in completing their assignments. this program provides a great opportunity for older students to develop their leadership skills, while the younger students benefit from the support and guidance of their peers. we believe that fostering these kinds of supportive relationships can have a positive impact on both students school experiences.
