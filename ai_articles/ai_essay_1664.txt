limiting car usage has several benefits for both individuals and society as a whole. one of the major advantages is improved air quality and environmental health. as passage  notes, vehicles are a leading source of air pollution in many cities. they emit a variety of harmful gases and particles that can cause respiratory problems like asthma. by driving less and using alternatives like public transportation, biking, or walking, cities would see lowered emissions and clearer air. this would significantly boost public health outcomes over time. a second advantage outlined in passage  is reduced traffic congestion. traffic jams waste everyones time and cause unnecessary stress. they also release more pollutants into the busy city air. if fewer private vehicles clogged up roads and more people switched to other modes, there would be less congestion for those who still need to drive. commutes and goods delivery could be more efficient. people may also save money on gas by avoiding slow traffic. public transportation could move more reliably too with less competition on the streets. passage  discusses the economic and infrastructure benefits. parking spaces and roads are extremely expensive for cities to build and maintain. with fewer cars needing accommodations, municipalities could allocate those funds elsewhere to improve transit, bike lanes, or public services. individuals may save substantial costs in the long run without owning a private vehicle for every trip. financial resources are better spent on more sustainable options. in conclusion, limiting car usage through behavioral changes, urban design, and policy would lead to an array of ecological, social, and economic advantages according to the sources. improved public health and reduced congestion are compelling outcomes. by raising awareness of these benefits, more citizens may be motivated to adopt multimodal transportation habits for the betterment of communities overall. a shift away from complete car dependence can deliver positive results if implemented thoughtfully and progressively over time.
