after school programs have become increasingly popular for both students and teachers. not only do they provide students and teachers with the opportunity to learn new material, but they also allow for the chance to gain better grades on quizzes and tests. these programs offer the ability for both students and teachers to apply the knowledge they have gained throughout the school day, something that is essential for career and academic success. through after school programs, students and teachers have the chance to volunteer to help others understand or finish tasks, which not only reinforces their knowledge but also promotes camaraderie and teambuilding skills. having an after school program provides crucial insight into realworld scenarios and fosters students extracurricular abilities. it presents students with essential leadership and problemsolving skills that help them excel beyond a learning environment. through creative, handson learning, students stay ahead in their classes and gain a better understanding of their overall academic performance. for teachers, after school programs can provide unique perspectives on certain topics that can be applied in their classroom materials. overall, having an after school program is a beneficial endeavor for both students and teachers. it encourages knowledge application, promotes career and academic success, and allows students and teachers the opportunity to cultivate skills they may not possess in the classroom setting.
