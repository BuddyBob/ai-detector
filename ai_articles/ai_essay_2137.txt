staying out of trouble in school is essential to making sure youre able to stay in school and graduate on time. there are many ways to stay on the right side of the law and out of trouble while in school. here are three of the most important first, choose your friends wisely. its no secret that the people around you have an influence on your behavior. having friends who are successful academically and stay out of trouble can help you stay focused on doing the same. second, set a good example for your peers. showing leadership in the classroom or hallways can have a positive impact on other students and help them stay out of trouble. be a role model and mentor to your peers and youll set a good example of what it looks like to be successful in school. finally, establish positive relationships with school staff. the more positively your teachers and other educators view you, the less likely it is that you will get in trouble. seek out the help of your teachers and school administrators when facing challenges and youll be further away from potential legal issues. overall, staying out of trouble in school involves making good decisions, setting a positive example, and forming positive relationships. with the right approach, you can ensure that your school experience is trouble free.
