advocates of outdoor activities argue that there are countless physical and mental health benefits to be found in taking part in activities such as riding a bicycle, playing sports, or going for a walk. studies show that active outdoor exercise releases endorphins, improving our mood and reducing feelings of depression. additionally, regular cardiovascular activities such as cycling and jogging can reduce the risk of both heart disease and diabetes. even something as simple as a brisk walk can help reduce stress and promote better sleep. ultimately, outdoor activities can help us lead healthier, happier lives.
