my friend amanda decided to take the challenge of completing her high school studies in three years. on one hand, she was able to get a head start on her college education and saved a significant amount of money. she didnt have to pay the costly fees associated with four years of high school. on the other hand, she felt as though she was lacking preparation for some of her college classes because she had to cover a lot of material in a shorter period of time. she reported feeling organized and focused, but it was a lot of work to finish on time. i also talked to another friend, roger, who took the threeyear high school program. he reported having to sacrifice his social life and extracurricular activities to keep up with his studies. additionally, he shared that it felt like a big transition from high school to college, having to jump right into rigorous college courses. it appears that the threeyear high school program can be a successful option for some students, but it is not without drawbacks. it requires a great deal of planning, focus, and dedication to finish the program successfully.
