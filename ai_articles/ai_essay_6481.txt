most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this statement for a few reasons. first, many advertisements use attractive models to sell products. the models are usually airbrushed or have other editing done to them to make them look perfect. this gives people the false impression that if they buy the product, they will look like the model. second, advertisements often use catchy phrases to make people want to buy the product. for example, a shampoo commercial might say for shiny, healthy hair. this makes people think that if they use the shampoo, their hair will be shiny and healthy. however, the shampoo might not actually make any difference in the quality of hair. finally, many advertisements make products seem like they are a necessity. for example, a commercial might say you need this product to be happy. this is not true, but it makes people think they need to buy the product to be happy. in conclusion, i believe that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. this is because they use attractive models, catchy phrases, and make products seem like a necessity.
