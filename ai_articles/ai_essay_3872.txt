working with a group can offer a number of benefits. sharing the workload can make for a more efficient use of time and resources, as well as providing multiple perspectives and increasing creativity. working as part of a team can also lead to better communication and collaboration skills. on the other hand, there are also advantages to working alone. without the need to coordinate schedules or consider the needs of others, you have the freedom to organize and structure time in whatever way you feel is most productive for you. you are able to devote full attention and concentration to your task until it is completed, leading to better quality of work. additionally, working alone can often provide a greater sense of autonomy and creativity.
