dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the united states should change from the current electoral college system to instead elect the president by a national popular vote. while the electoral college system has its defenders, i believe a popular vote would be fairer and more democratic. some argue that the electoral college system encourages candidates to build broader coalitions and appeal to a wide range of states. however, in recent elections we have seen candidates focus their efforts only in key swing states that could tip the electoral vote, largely ignoring the vast majority of states. this discourages voter turnout, as millions feel their votes do not truly matter in noncompetitive states. a popular vote would make every american citizens vote equally important, wherever they live, and encourage higher participation across the country. critics of the popular vote point out that candidates might then focus only on densely populated urban areas. however, in todays society americans are less regionally isolated, and campaign messages can reach rural areas costeffectively through television and internet. a national campaign aimed at winning the popular vote would still require appealing to a diverse range of demographic and geographic groups. it is also argued that the electoral college guards against instability by usually producing a clear winner. however, it nearly failed to do so in the very close  election, and narrow nationwide margins could still lead to doubts about legitimacy. a popular vote would replace disputed statebystate outcomes with a clear national result. while a candidate could still win without a majority, the minimum plurality needed would likely be larger than the electoral vote can provide. in summary, while the electoral college system aims to achieve representative and stable government, in practice it discourages voter participation and national consensusbuilding. changing to a popular vote would make every citizens voice equal and compel candidates to appeal to americans across geographic and demographic divides. for these reasons, i ask that you support electoral reform and consider citizens preference for a national popular vote.
