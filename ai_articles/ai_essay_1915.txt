seeking multiple opinions when making an important decision or asking for advice can help someone make a better choice for a few key reasons first, getting perspectives from different people exposes you to a wider range of ideas, opinions and potential options. no single person can be expected to think of everything or anticipate how all factors might impact a decision. hearing multiple points of view helps ensure more angles are considered. secondly, other people often notice things that you do not. when close to a decision ourselves, we can become biased or stuck only thinking about it one way. fresh eyes catching something we have overlooked can be valuable. others may also weigh various tradeoffs differently than you would naturally. this brings a more wellrounded analysis of costs and benefits. importantly, crosschecking opinions also helps verify the reasoning or logic. if very different people reviewing the same situation arrive at a similar recommendation, that advice is stronger. on the other hand, conflicting opinions could reveal weaknesses, assumptions or uncertainties in the decision that require more careful thought. weighing diverse viewpoints against one another helps separate the most wellsupported or sensible advice from counsel that is flawed or based on inadequate information. finally, polling family, friends or colleagues disperses responsibility for the outcome. if you solely follow one persons guidance and it turns out poorly, you may regret not getting another perspective. but incorporating multiple recommendations into your decision makes it more of a group process, where responsibility is shared if things do not work out ideally. this can give you greater peace of mind in implementing your choice. in summary, seeking the advice of more than just one individual enables you to make choices from a place of greater insight, assurance and confidence. considering a variety of perspectives helps guarantee a more thoroughly deliberated decision based on the strongest rationales. for significant decisions, this multipronged advice approach often leads to optimal outcomes.
