the immense popularity of the outdoor sport rock climbing has grown exponentially in recent years, and its many benefits are becoming more widely known. for those who take part in this exciting activity, it provides an opportunity to challenge physical ability while taking in gorgeous natural landscapes. in addition to providing a unique adrenaline thrill, there are numerous mental and physical health benefits associated with rock climbing as well. one of the primary reasons behind the surge in popularity is that rock climbing offers such an enjoyable way for people to get exercise. while engaging core muscles along with arms and legs, climbers can easily burn upwards of  calories per hour depending upon their weight and speed at which they climb. as such, its a great cardiovascular workout that helps keep participants leaner than some other sports activities which may require less effort but have higher risk factors for injury due to overexertion like running or biking on pavement surfaces. rock climbing also serves as a very effective form of stress relief since it requires intense focus throughout each route taken up the wall or cliff face being climbed  allowing thoughts and worries from everyday life outside the experience temporarily drift away while completely focused on completing each move successfully and safely. this allows individuals who engage regularly in this pursuit feel refreshed after every outing ready to tackle whatever challenges await them back home or work once again feeling calm yet energized by what was accomplished outdoors during their sessions. for those looking for a fun way to stay fit and healthy both mentally and physically  rock climbing should certainly be considered! not only does it provide amazing views from atop cliffs overlooking beautiful countryside settings  but anyone willing enough will gain confidence through conquering feats deemed impossible when first attempting these routes originally set forth by experienced professionals. with practice comes progress which opens up further opportunities available within this dynamic sport making it something truly worth giving a try if one desires change combined with selfimprovement along lifes journey!
