when it comes to english learning, there is no onesizefitsall solution. in fact, relying too heavily on electronic dictionaries edictionaries can have more disadvantages than advantages. first and foremost, edictionaries can actually impair our spelling ability. when we rely on edictionaries to spell words, we often forget how to spell words correctly the oldfashioned way. this becomes especially problematic when we are required to spell words in a test or in a writing assignment. second, edictionaries can be a waste of time and resources. when we use edictionaries, we are not actually learning english. we are simply copying information from a computer screen. in fact, many students find that they are unable to remember the definitions from edictionaries once they have stopped using them. finally, edictionaries can have a negative impact on our overall english skills. when we rely on edictionaries, we are not practicing speaking and writing english. we are instead relying on a machine to do it for us. this can lead to a decrease in our fluency and accuracy when we are speaking and writing english.
