drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle cell phone use has become prevalent in todays world, with most people using their phones on a daily basis. however, cell phone use should not be permitted for drivers. operating a vehicle requires the operators full attention and anything that distracts from focusing on driving poses great risks. there are several reasons why cell phone use, in any form, should be prohibited for drivers safety is the primary concern. numerous studies have shown that cell phone use while driving can impair a driver as much as being intoxicated. answering calls, checking messages or social media, even handsfree, takes the drivers attention away from the road. this distraction increases the likelihood of accidents. in fact, statistics show that cell phone use is a leading factor in car accidents globally. risking lives by any type of cell phone use is simply not worth it. beyond safety, cell phone use also affects driving skills. talking or texting on the phone prevents drivers from executing driving tasks well. it delays reaction times, affects driving judgment and increases small errors that may result in bigger mishaps over time. fully concentrating on the road is needed to drive defensively and respond to any developing hazards properly. cell phone engagements undermine competent driving. enforcement is also easier without ambiguity. banning all cell phone use leaves no room for debate over what is or isnt allowed. law enforcement does not need to determine if the driver was engaged in a call or texting. even a straightforward ban is challenging to implement but clarity eases the job of patrollers. complex rules fuel confusion and noncompliance. in todays world where mobility is emphasized, road safety should be the nonnegotiable priority. distracted driving poses a threat, especially from cell phone use. while phones enhance our lives, they endanger others if used while driving. for the protection of all road users, cell phones should remain out of reach and out of use for drivers. lives depend on keeping full attention on the activity of operating a vehicle. a total cell phone ban is therefore justified.
