winston churchill once said, success is not final, failure is not fatal it is the courage to continue that counts. his words are filled with truth and wisdom that can be seen in all aspects of our lives. success is rarely just a matter of having a good plan and getting it perfectly right the first time it often involves going through failure as part of the process. no matter what the setback, having the determination to carry on without allowing yourself to lose enthusiasm and focus can be the key to eventual success. when dealing with failure, its important to take the time to learn from it  the mistakes that have been made, the actions that can be improved, the goals that were too ambitious. having that kind of perspective, and an understanding that setbacks can provide valuable information, can be hugely beneficial in developing an approach that will bring eventual success. the causes of failure are numerous and varied, from external factors such as marketplace or social pressures to individual skills or capabilities. whatever the cause, success can only be achieved through understanding, embracing and learning from these failures. no one should expect success to be easy to come by and, in this age of intense competition, resilience and perseverance are more important than ever. ultimately, as winston churchill so famously observed, it is your willingness to continue that counts. with courage and determination, success can be achieved despite any and all setbacks that may come along the way.
