modern students often debate the merits of classes in the arts being required versus being elective. while some argue that classes in the arts should not be required, as they do not provide students with practical skills for future employment, others maintain that the arts can provide academic and personal growth, expanding students worldviews. from my own experience, taking classes in the arts has provided me with a better appreciation for the creative process and non linear modes of thinking. it has also developed my skills in collaboration, as much of the artistic process can be a shared effort. in addition, i have found that the classes in the arts have provided a necessary balance between academic and creative pursuits throughout my schooling. from the experiences of my peers and other sources, i have seen the positive impact that classes in the arts can have on developing a students empathy, selfexpression, and respect for the perspectives of others. for these reasons, i believe that classes in the arts have significant value and should be required for all students in order to build a wellrounded educational experience. when students are exposed to the creativity and unique perspectives that classes in the arts provide, it can help them to foster imagination, explore new ideas, and learn the capacities for excellence.
