group work offers several key advantages over working on an individual level. for one, it provides an opportunity for students to learn to appreciate the diverse perspectives and expertise of their peers, which can be an invaluable asset in the process of brainstorming and problem solving strategies. group work also provides a heightened level of accountability since students are more likely to take ownership of their contributions when working with others. furthermore, the emphasis on respect and teamwork encourages students to develop interpersonal skills and learn to collaborate effectively in order to foster successful outcomes. additionally, group work can be an effective way to divide up work, maximize efficiency, and help students gain a better understanding of topics through the process of peer teaching. while group work is not without its drawbacks, when facilitated properly, it can be a powerful tool to help students achieve academic success.
