extending the school day carries both benefits and drawbacks. on the one hand, students will have more time for learning core material and exploring areas of special interest. however, this will only be possible if students are able to sustain concentration for a longer period of time. some research suggests that students may become bored and distracted, leading to lower academic performance. on the other hand, implementing a four day school week could also provide more learning time without pushing students past their mental limits. officials must weigh these factors carefully when deciding which option is best suited to their school system.
