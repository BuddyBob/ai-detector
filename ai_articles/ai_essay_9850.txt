the world of college athletics has seen incredible growth and change in recent years. from the influx of money to the increase in media coverage, there is no doubt that college sports have become a major part of the american landscape. despite this, few people truly understand how these changes affect student athletes. one area where college athletes feel an immediate impact is academics. with large athletic programs come increased expectations for performance both on and off the field. student athletes are under considerable pressure to maintain high grades while also attending practices and competing on game day  all while trying to balance a social life as well. unfortunately, professors may not always recognize or understand why athletes fail to turn in homework or perform poorly on tests they simply see poor results without understanding whats causing them. this can lead to feelings of frustration and alienation among studentathletes who find themselves struggling with their academic workloads due to time constraints as well as mental exhaustion from playing a sport at a high level day after day. another area where studentathletes often feel impacted by college sports is finances since many scholarships only cover tuition costs if any. although most schools provide meals specifically for their athletes, other expenses like food outside of meal times usually fall upon the athletes own funds which must be carefully managed alongside tuition payments and travel expenses when away games are required . this can make it difficult for students who do not come from wealthy families or those receiving minimal financial aid from institutions themselves  sometimes forcing them into debt just so they can remain eligible for playability standards set by ncaa regulations. its easy to see how this situation could start affecting players physical health if proper nutrition isnt followed because healthy eating habits require resources some players cannot access easily due to cost barriers alone!. finally, many studentathletes struggle with long term goals once their collegiate careers end since professional contracts arent guaranteed nor available
