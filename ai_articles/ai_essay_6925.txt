helps individuals to grow and learn. when we step out of our comfort zone, we challenge ourselves to learn new skills and acquire new knowledge. for instance, a person who has always worked in a also help them to overcome their fear and build confidence in their abilities. in conclusion, i strongly agree that successful people try new things and take risks. trying new things and taking risks
