studying online or at home and attending a traditional school both have their advantages and disadvantages. for those studying online or at home, the lack of a structured learning environment can make it easy to become distracted, resulting in potentially lower learning outcomes due to the lack of accountability. at the same time, studying online or at home provides more flexibility, allowing students to set their own schedule and work in their own space. in contrast, attending a traditional school involves inperson instruction from an experienced teacher and allows students to gain handson experience. in terms of academic performance, students studying from home will have to learn to manage their time and become selfmotivated, in order to be successful in their courses. this can be an advantage or disadvantage depending on each student. with regard to standardized testing, such as the sol, those who have taken classes from home or online may do well, if they have taken courses that have prepared them. finally, there is the impact on social interaction that needs to be taken into account. attending a traditional school may give students more opportunities to interact with other students and form meaningful relationships. conversely, those who take classes from home or online may experience a lack of meaningful social interaction, but may still be able to connect with classmates and teachers online.
