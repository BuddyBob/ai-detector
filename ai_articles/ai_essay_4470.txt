there is no one answer to the question of what universities are for. some people believe that the main purpose of universities is to prepare students for employment, while others believe that they serve other functions as well. i believe that universities have both purposes, and that both are important. employment is an important outcome of a university education, and i believe that universities should do everything they can to help their students find jobs. however, i also believe that universities have an important role in teaching students how to think critically and analytically. these skills are important not just for getting a job, but for living a full and productive life. a university education should also provide students with a sense of community and a chance to meet people from all walks of life. ultimately, i believe that the purpose of a university education depends on the individual. some people may only want to attend university so that they can get a job, while others may see it as an opportunity to learn new things and explore new ideas. both of these are important, and universities should strive to provide both types of education.
