drivers should not use cell phones while operating a vehicle in todays world, cell phones have become ubiquitous. however, operating a motor vehicle requires the drivers full attention. allowing cell phone use while driving puts both the driver and other road users at serious risk of harm. for this reason, drivers should not be permitted to use cell phones in any capacity when controlling a moving vehicle. there are several reasons why cell phone use is unsafe for drivers. first and foremost, cell phones are an extreme distraction from the task of driving. whether talking, texting, or using apps, a cell phone draws the drivers eyes and mind away from the road. numerous studies have shown that even handsfree phone conversations significantly impair drivers reaction times and concentration levels. any distraction from focusing fully on the road can have dangerous or even deadly consequences in emergency situations like unexpected maneuvers by other vehicles. secondly, cell phones enable multitasking that is simply not possible when driving safely. texting, scrolling social media, or accessing the internet all require visual attention that should be on the road, not a phone screen. texting is especially risky, as it involves taking ones eyes completely off the road for periods of time. many distractions pile up when a person tries to juggle phone use and driving simultaneously. finally, reactions and decision making are slowed when the brains processing power is split between driving and communicating via phone. operating motor vehicles at speed requires making splitsecond judgements based on road conditions, signs, and other vehicles movements. any degradation of reaction time or cognitive process from cell phone distraction presents a hazard. numerous collisions have resulted from drivers focused on phones rather than the road ahead. in conclusion, allowing any cell phone use while driving compromises safety and puts lives at risk, both of the driver and others sharing the road. for the welfare of all, the most prudent policy is to ban all nonemergency cell phone activity by motorists when a vehicle is in motion. drivers undivided attention should be on the critical task of navigating traffic and arriving safely at destinations.
