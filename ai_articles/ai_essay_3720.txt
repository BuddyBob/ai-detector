advice is a part of life and from many different sources. it often starts from our parents, who offer us guidance as we grow up and experience the world around us. teachers also provide advice, not only on our studies but also in terms of personal development and decision making. friends also offer advice, not only for social situations but also on big decisions. other people, such as neighbors or acquaintances, can also provide us with advice, though it may be harder to take from someone we dont know well. in general, the advice that we receive from our parents and teachers is usually more valuable. while advice from friends may be wellintentioned, some of it may be biased by their personal experiences and decisions. these sources provide a more objective view that can help us make wise decisions that we may not have considered. they may also have more life experience to draw from when offering advice. ultimately, it is up to us to decide which advice to take, and which to leave behind.
