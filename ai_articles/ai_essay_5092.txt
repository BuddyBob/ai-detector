there is no one answer to the question of which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. it depends on the individual and their specific needs. however, i believe that speaking is more important than reading for most learners. there are several reasons why speaking is more important than reading. firstly, spoken english is more natural and fluid than written english. when we speak, we can use facial expressions and gestures to help communicate our ideas, which is not possible when we are reading. secondly, spoken english is more interactive than written english. in a conversation, we can respond to the other persons comments and questions, which helps us to learn more about the language. finally, spoken english is more useful in everyday life than written english. we can use spoken english to communicate with people from all over the world, whereas written english is mainly used for academic purposes. of course, reading is also an important skill for learners of english. it helps us to improve our vocabulary and understanding of the language. however, i believe that speaking is more important than reading, because it is more natural and useful in everyday life.
