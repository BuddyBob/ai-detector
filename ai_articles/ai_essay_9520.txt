in todays world, it can be hard to find time for selfcare. with work, family obligations and social media competing for a persons attention, it is important to remember the importance of taking care of ones own mental and physical health. selfcare doesnt have to mean going out on expensive spa trips or making radical lifestyle changes  small things like getting enough sleep, eating healthy meals and even setting aside regular reading time can make all the difference when it comes to feeling contented and relaxed in life. one way that i incorporate selfcare into my daily routine is by meditating each morning before beginning my day. starting with just two minutes of breathing exercises helps me maintain focus throughout the day while allowing me to take some much needed me time first thing in the morning. during this brief moment of peace i often reflect on what is really important in my life  relationships with friends and family, healthful habits such as exercise, career goals etc. not only does this help me get better organized for success but it also allows me to appreciate any successes that have been achieved recently like completing a project or reaching a new milestone. another aspect of selfcare that i like is disconnecting from technology at least once a week. this means turning off all devices including tv for an hour or two each week so that there are no distractions from enjoying quality moments alone or with loved ones doing something fun together like playing board games or simply talking about our hopes and dreams over dinner. taking regular breaks away from screens not only helps reduce stress levels but also helps boost creativity while giving people more energy throughout their days!. lastly, another practice i use regularly when looking after myself is getting outdoors whenever possible  nothing quite beats being surrounded by nature!
