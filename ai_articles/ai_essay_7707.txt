advancements in various fields, it has also resulted in a lack of accessibility to the general public. i strongly agree that until scholars can reach a wider audience, their ideas will have little use. one reason why scholars need to reach a wider audience is that their research can have a significant impact on society. however, if their work is only accessible to a select few individuals, then medical journals, then the general public may never know about it. in order to make a real difference, scholars must be able to disseminate their ideas to a wider audience. another reason why scholars need to reach a wider audience is that it can lead to greater collaboration and interdisciplinary work. when scholars work in silos, they miss out on opportunities to learn from others in different fields. however, if they are able to share their ideas with a wider audience, they may be able to connect with individuals from other disciplines who can provide valuable insights and perspectives. this can lead to new collaborations and interdisciplinary work that may not have been possible otherwise. furthermore, reaching a wider audience can also help scholars receive funding and support for their research. if their work is only known to a small group of individuals, then it may be difficult for them to secure the necessary resources to continue their research. however, if they are able to gain a wider audience, they may be more likely to attract the attention of funders and supporters who see the value in their work. while it is true that some scholars may argue that their conclusion, the specialization of academic disciplines has resulted in a lack of accessibility to the general public, which limits the usefulness of scholars ideas. it is important for scholars to research. by doing so, scholars can contribute to the greater good and make a real difference in the world.
