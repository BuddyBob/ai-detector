there is no doubt that work today is more demanding than it was in the past. with the advent of technology, the workplace has become more fastpaced and competitive. in addition, the global economy has made it necessary for workers to be able to adapt to change quickly. however, i would not say that work is necessarily more stressful than it was in the past. while there are certainly more demands, there are also more opportunities to take breaks and enjoy leisure activities. for example, many workers now have the option to work from home, which can be a great way to reduce stress levels. in addition, there are more opportunities for vacation and flexible work hours. overall, i believe that the amount of stress in the workplace depends on the individual. some people thrive in a fastpaced environment, while others find it to be overwhelming. it is important to find a job that suits your personality and lifestyle.
