there are pros and cons to both paper and electronic cards, but ultimately, i believe that electronic cards are the better choice. paper cards have a personal feel to them that electronic cards cannot replicate. you can hold the card in your hand, feel the paper, and see your handwriting. this is a personal connection that cannot be replicated electronically. additionally, paper cards often have beautiful designs and can be customized in a variety of ways. however, electronic cards have several advantages over paper cards. first, they are easier and faster to send. you can send an electronic card in a matter of seconds, while it may take longer to find and mail a paper card. additionally, electronic cards are often less expensive than paper cards. you can send as many electronic cards as you want for free, while paper cards often have a postage cost. finally, electronic cards are more environmentally friendly. paper cards require resources to produce and often end up in the landfill after they are used. electronic cards do not require any resources to produce and can be recycled when they are no longer needed. overall, i believe that electronic cards are the better choice for holiday greetings. they are faster, less expensive, and more environmentally friendly than paper cards.
