there is no doubt that computer games are very popular among children. however, there are some people who think that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual as well as psychological development. therefore, they suggest that effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing them. i agree with the latter view for the following reasons. first of all, computer games are very addictive. once children start playing them, they can hardly stop. as a result, they spend less time on their studies and physical activities, which is not good for their overall development. secondly, computer games are usually very violent. children who play such games tend to be more aggressive and even violent in real life. thirdly, computer games are a waste of time. instead of spending their time in productive activities, children waste their time in playing computer games. however, there are also some positive effects of computer games on children. for instance, computer games help children develop their problemsolving skills. in addition, computer games also help children develop their handeye coordination. in conclusion, i believe that the negative effects of computer games on childrens development outweigh the positive effects. therefore, effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing them.
