degree certificates have become increasingly important in many institutions, and this trend has been met with criticism from some people who argue that it may lead young people astray. while i agree certificates can also be used to show that one has the necessary skills to succeed in a particular field. for instance, a degree in engineering can be used to demonstrate that one has the technical knowledge and expertise to work in the engineering industry. at the same time, it is important to recognize that degree certificates are not the only measure of success. while they can be used to demonstrate ones academic achievements, they do not necessarily reflect other important qualities such as creativity, problemsolving skills, and interpersonal skills. these qualities are essential for success in many fields, and employers should take them into consideration when making hiring decisions. additionally, it is important to recognize that some people may have the necessary skills and knowledge to succeed in a particular field, but may not have the necessary degree certificate. in such cases, employers should look beyond the degree certificate and consider the individuals other
