the impact of technology on education has been profound in the st century. in particular, the rise of mobile devices and increased access to the internet has revolutionized how students learn. by harnessing convenient tools like tablets and smartphones, users are now able to access vast amounts of information from anywhere at any time. educational apps provide an unprecedented level of convenience for those seeking knowledge, giving them instant access to educational materials from the comfort of their own home or even while onthego. however, there is a dark side to this technological advancement as well that should not be overlooked. increased screen time can affect physical health issues such as dry eyes, headaches, neck and back pain due to poor posture when using devices for extended periods of time without breaks. additionally, mental health problems including anxiety have also been linked with too much tech usage which could potentially lead to a decline in grades if left unchecked by parents and educators alike. given these potential risks associated with excessive use of digital media it is important that we find strategies which promote healthy technology use among students so they may reap all the benefits without suffering any drawbacks such as those mentioned previously. efforts should be taken towards educating children about safe online practices such as avoiding clicking malicious links or participating in unsafe activities over social media networks while still encouraging developmentally appropriate uses for educational purposes during established class times. furthermore schools should develop policies concerning student device usage during school hours so teachers can ensure proper use in order for pupils to benefit from emerging technologies instead being hindered by its usage guidelines and expectations need to be established early on before habits become ingrained making them difficult if not impossible change
