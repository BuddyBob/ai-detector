the products benefits. therefore, i agree that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. one reason why advertisements tend to exaggerate the benefits of products is do this is by making bold claims about the products benefits, even if they are not entirely true. for example, a weight loss supplement may claim to help people lose  pounds in a week, even though
