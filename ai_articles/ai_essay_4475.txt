there are different opinions about what universities should do. some people think that universities should focus on preparing students for employment. others think that universities have other functions. i agree that universities have other functions. universities provide students with an education that they cannot get anywhere else. at universities, students learn how to think critically and how to find and use information. they also learn how to work in teams and how to communicate effectively. these are skills that students need in order to be successful in the workplace. in addition to providing students with an education, universities also conduct research. this research helps to solve problems that we face in society. it also helps to create new products and services. without research, we would not have many of the things that we take for granted, such as computers, cell phones, and the internet. so, i believe that universities have more than one function. they provide students with an education and they conduct research. these two functions are important for the advancement of society.
