when it comes to achieving our goals, there are two paths we can take actively working towards our goals or simply sitting and waiting to see what happens. as thomas jefferson noted, im a great believer in luck, and i find the harder i work the more i have of it. his quote highlights the idea that hard work and determination can lead to success, which is the ultimate benefit of taking the active approach. on the other hand, adopting a wait and see strategy can also be a valid approach. as the ancient proverb says, luck is when preparation meets opportunity. while it is possible to reap the rewards of good fortune, being prepared beforehand will increase the chances that success will come when the right opportunity arises. ultimately, both paths have their advantages  by actively pursuing our goals, we may gain valuable skills and experiences. on the other hand, waiting can lead to surprise successes and offer more flexibility in our approach. thus it is up to each individual to decide which approach to take.
