there are a number of reasons why people might prefer to send paper cards over electronic cards for holiday greetings. firstly, electronic cards can often seem cold and impersonal, particularly if they are sent as a mass message. paper cards, on the other hand, are often seen as more personal and warm, as they are often sent as a single card to a specific person. secondly, electronic cards can often be difficult to personalize, whereas paper cards can be easily customized with a personal message, photo, or drawing. finally, electronic cards can be easily forgotten or lost, whereas paper cards are more likely to be kept and remembered.
