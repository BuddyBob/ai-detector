seeking advice from multiple people when facing an important decision can provide significant benefits. by getting different perspectives on a situation, you gain a more wellrounded understanding of your options and their pros and cons. no single person has a monopoly on good advice, and different people will approach problems from unique angles. each person you consult brings their own life experiences, wisdom, values and frame of reference. what seems like an obvious choice to one may not be so clear cut when viewed through another lens. discussing a dilemma with friends, family members, colleagues, mentors or experts in relevant fields exposes you to diverse viewpoints you may not have considered on your own. this helps ensure you dont miss important factors or make assumptions without realizing. relatedly, multiple viewpoints reduces the risk of being unduly swayed by a biased or inaccurate opinion. if different people draw similar conclusions after hearing all sides, their collective judgment is likely to be sound. but if viewpoints are mixed, it suggests complexities you need to carefully weigh yourself. discussing tradeoffs with others also pushes you to think through choices in a more methodical way, clarifying your own priorities in the process. finally, gaining confidence in a decision through crossvalidation can empower better followthrough. by understanding how multiple trusted individuals analyzed a problem, you feel more invested in and committed to the eventual solution. this commitment strengthens your resolve to implement the choice fully and reduce second guessing later on. overall, considering the expertise and experiences of various sources helps people make carefully deliberated choices they feel at peace with over the long run.
