there is a great deal of debate surrounding which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. some people think that we should give priority to reading in english, while others think speaking is more important. i believe that both skills are important and should be given equal attention. reading is a fundamental skill that helps develop a strong foundation in the english language. it allows learners to increase their vocabulary and learn new grammar rules. furthermore, reading comprehension skills are essential in order to be able to communicate effectively in english. speaking, on the other hand, is also a very important skill. it allows learners to practice their pronunciation and learn how to construct sentences. in addition, speaking also helps learners to develop their confidence in using the english language. both skills are important and should be given equal attention. however, i believe that the priority should be given to reading. this is because reading is a more passive activity that does not require as much effort as speaking. as such, it is more suitable for beginners who are still learning the basics of the english language. once they have a strong foundation in reading, they can then start to focus on speaking.
