there is no single answer to the question of whether the best way to travel is in a group led by a tour guide. some factors to consider include the cost of the tour, the size of the group, and the destination. for example, a tour guide can be a great way to see a new city or country. guides know the best places to go and can give you a historical perspective on the sights you see. they can also help you navigate public transportation and find the best restaurants and hotels in the area. however, group tours can also be expensive. if you are on a tight budget, it might be cheaper to travel on your own. additionally, group tours can be large, which can be overwhelming or uncomfortable if you are not used to being around a lot of people. ultimately, the best way to travel depends on the individual and the specific circumstances. some people prefer the structure and convenience of group tours, while others prefer to explore on their own.
