there are a few schools of thought in regards to whether children should learn to compete or cooperate. some believe that children should learn to compete in order to better themselves and others believe that children should be taught to cooperate in order to create a more cohesive society. i believe that children should learn to do both. learning to compete can be beneficial to children in a number of ways. for one, it can teach them how to set and achieve goals. if a child knows that they have to compete in order to win, they will be more likely to set their sights high and work hard to achieve them. additionally, competition can teach children how to deal with disappointment and how to handle winning and losing gracefully. losing is a part of life and its important for children to learn how to deal with it early on. however, i believe that cooperation is just as important as competition. in our society, we have to work together in order to get things done. if everyone is always trying to oneup each other, its going to be difficult to get anything accomplished. cooperation can also teach children the value of teamwork and how to compromise. these are skills that they will need in their future careers and in their personal lives. so, while i think that competition has its place, i believe that cooperation is just as important. i think children should learn to do both.
