in the modern age, technology has had a simultaneously beneficial and damaging effect on social relations. social media platforms have provided opportunities to stay connected with acquaintances and make new friends, while at the same time making it easier to live alone and engage in cyberbullying. this is especially the case for young people, who are heavily influenced by the media they are exposed to in daytoday life. while technology can be used as a tool for selfexpression and productivity, there are also serious risks associated with the misuse of this power. parents have a responsibility to ensure their children are developing essential social skills, such as communication and problemsolving, and to limit the use of technology in order to benefit their children in the long run.
