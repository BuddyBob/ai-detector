i wholeheartedly agree with those who believe that children should be taught to cooperate, as opposed to compete. there are many reasons why, and i will list a few here. first and foremost, competition can be very harmful to children. it can cause them to feel insecure and stressed, which can have a negative impact on their overall development. it can also lead to them becoming bullies or competitive rivals, which is not healthy for them or anyone else. second, competition can actually lead to a lack of creativity and innovation. when people are constantly trying to outdo each other, they tend to stop thinking outside the box and coming up with new ideas. this can have a negative impact on their career prospects and overall ability to succeed in life. third, competition can actually lead to a lack of cooperation. when people are focused on winning, they may not be as willing to help out their fellow humans. this can lead to problems in the community, as well as problems for the individual. finally, competition can be very destructive to relationships. when people are constantly trying to outdo each other, they often end up getting angry and hurt. this can have a negative impact on their relationships with family and friends, as well as their relationships with other people in the community. all of these reasons make it clear why it is important for children to learn to cooperate, rather than compete.
