though first impressions can tell you a lot about a person, they have the potential to be misleading. i remember when i first met my best friend. i had heard rumors that she was a wild party girl, so i expected her to be loud and obnoxious when i met her. however, she was actually quite shy and sweet. after spending more time with her and getting to know her, i found out that she was actually more of a homebody than a party person. though first impressions can heavily influence the opinion you form of a person, i believe that an opinion can change as you get to know someone better. when i got to know my best friend better, i was able to see her true personality and appreciate her kindness and intelligence. however, first impressions are usually hard to change. once someone forms an opinion of another person, it is difficult to persuade them to think differently. no matter how many facts a person may be presented with, first impressions are often the most powerful influence.
