in todays increasingly globalized world, the consequences of failing to educate are more expensive than ever before. a lack of education can lead to a number of problems, including unemployment, poverty, and crime. unemployment is one of the most common consequences of a lack of education. in many countries, jobs that once did not require a college degree now do. this is especially true in the united states, where the majority of jobs now require some form of postsecondary education. without a college degree, it is very difficult to find a goodpaying job. poverty is another common consequence of a lack of education. education is one of the most important factors in determining income. in general, the more educated a person is, the higher their income will be. this is especially true in developing countries, where a lack of education can prevent people from getting jobs that would lift them out of poverty. crime is another consequence of a lack of education. education can provide people with the skills and knowledge they need to get good jobs. without these skills and knowledge, people are more likely to turn to crime. this is especially true in areas where there are few jobs available. the consequences of a lack of education are clear. education is essential for getting a good job, lifting people out of poverty, and reducing crime. these consequences are more expensive than ever before, making education more important than ever.
