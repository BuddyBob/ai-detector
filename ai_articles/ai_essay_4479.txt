there is a growing trend in many countries for students to attend university with the primary goal of securing employment after graduation. while it is certainly true that a university education can lead to better job prospects, i believe that there are other functions that university serves. firstly, university provides an opportunity for young people to learn about a variety of different subjects in depth. this can be of great value in itself, regardless of whether or not it leads to a specific career. secondly, university is a time for young people to socialize and make friends. this is important for both personal development and for building professional networks. finally, university can be a stepping stone to further study, either at a postgraduate level or in a specific field. in conclusion, while securing employment may be a primary concern for some university students, i believe that there are other important functions that university serves.
