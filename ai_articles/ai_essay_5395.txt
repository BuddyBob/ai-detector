nowadays, electronic dictionaries edictionaries have been increasingly popular among students. however, there is a growing concern among teachers that the overuse of edictionaries might have more disadvantages than advantages for english learning. this is because, while edictionaries are very convenient and useful tools, they can also lead to the deterioration of our spelling ability. there are several reasons why reliance on edictionaries may have a negative effect on our spelling skills. firstly, because edictionaries are so easy to use, students may be tempted to use them for every word they encounter, instead of trying to spell the word correctly from memory. secondly, the definitions and examples provided by edictionaries can often lead to confusion and incorrect spellings. finally, the use of edictionaries can also encourage students to use shortcuts and informal spellings, which can be difficult to unlearn. all of these factors suggest that teachers should discourage the overuse of edictionaries, and instead encourage their students to rely on their own memory and common sense when it comes to spelling. this is not to say that edictionaries should never be used  they can be very helpful tools  but they should not be used to the exclusion of other methods of learning spellings. in conclusion, while edictionaries are a valuable resource, they should be used with caution, and should not be relied on to the detriment of other learning methods.
