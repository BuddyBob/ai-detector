when a student chooses to attend classes from home, they can reap a variety of benefits. these benefits can range from more leisure time, the opportunity to sleep longer, and the elimination of a daily commute. for example, consider a student who is attending classes from home due to the current pandemic. by staying home, they are able to sleep in longer and eliminate the need to take a bus or walk to and from school. with more time to sleep, a student can stay healthier with sufficient rest, and more time for exercise and healthy meals. additionally, with the distractions of school removed, the student has an improved focus and can better plan their time. of course, there are some drawbacks to attending classes from home, like the potential for distractions from family and friends as well as feelings of isolation from classmates. but with the right balance, a student can make the most of online classes and find success.
