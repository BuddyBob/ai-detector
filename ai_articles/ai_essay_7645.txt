i agree with the notion that it is the educators duty to determine the curriculum and the students duty to study what is presented to them. while i understand the need to give students some autonomy and freedom in what they study to keep them engaged, there are certain core topics and themes of formal education that should be decided by educators. it is ultimately the educators job to ensure that students are receiving the best possible education, and they are the most qualified to determine what should and should not be included in the curriculum. one of the primary reasons why educators should determine the curriculum is that they are knowledgeable about which topics are essential for students to learn. while students may have ideas about what interests them, these interests may not always align with the knowledge necessary for students to succeed in their future endeavors. for instance, a student may be interested in studying the art of filmmaking, but this may not be beneficial for their development outside of the classroom. alternatively, educators can determine which academic and professional topics are necessary for students to learn, such as mathematics, science, history, and literature. these topics are essential for students to succeed in postsecondary education and future careers. another reason why educators should determine the curriculum is that they are best suited to balance structure and freedom. while students should have some autonomy in what they study, there should also be a certain level of structure to ensure that students are learning the skills and topics necessary for their development. educators have the knowledge and experience to determine the right balance between structure and freedom. for instance, a teacher may allow students to pick a few topics to write about for their research paper, but still provide a specific list of topics from which students may choose. this allows students to explore their interests while also ensuring that they are learning the necessary information. finally, educators should determine the curriculum because they understand the importance of challenging students. while it may be tempting for students to only learn what interests them, such an approach can result in students not being adequately challenged. educators are uniquely positioned to understand which topics are challenging, yet not too difficult for students to understand. for instance, a math teacher may introduce advanced topics such as calculus if they believe the students are capable of understanding them. this allows students to be challenged while also ensuring that they are learning the necessary information. in conclusion, i believe that it is the educators duty to determine the curriculum and the students duty to study what is presented to them. this allows educators to select topics and themes that are necessary for students to learn, while also allowing them the flexibility to explore their interests. moreover, educators understand the importance of challenging students without overwhelming them, which is something that only they can do. for these reasons, it is essential that educators determine the curriculum.
