first impressions are often seen as set in stone but, in reality, they can be changed over time. people frequently jump to conclusions without getting to know an individual, something we need to work to break away from. as each person may have a unique story to tell, it is important to remember that we all have the potential to change our image in the eyes of others. one example of how first impressions can be changed is seen between longterm coworkers. although they may initially form a certain opinion of one another, this image can become more positive over time as they get to know each other. this occurs naturally as the conversations become more involved and there is an increased understanding of each individuals ideas, strengths, and values. another example of first impressions changing is reflected in friendship circles. initially, one may not think somebody is interesting or suitable to become a friend. but, after conversing and speaking with them, one may become increasingly drawn to them and so the opinion is altered. the ability to change ones initial image is something that needs to be appreciated more. this does not mean people should be afraid to judge somebody at first, but, just that we should all try to dig deeper and get to know one another on a deeper level before we make our final judgment. it is a good thing to have flexibility and an open mind, and to let someones personality come through before forming a lasting opinion.
