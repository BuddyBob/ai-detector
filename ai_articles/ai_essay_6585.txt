i agree that it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than to learn facts. here are three reasons why first, understanding ideas and concepts helps students see how things are connected. for example, if a student understands the concept of photosynthesis, they can see how it relates to the water cycle, the food chain, and the climate. second, understanding ideas and concepts helps students remember information. if students just memorize facts, they are likely to forget them. but if they understand the concepts behind the facts, they are more likely to remember them. third, understanding ideas and concepts helps students apply what they have learned. if students can understand the concepts, they can figure out how to use them in realworld situations. for all of these reasons, i believe that it is more important for students to understand ideas and concepts than to learn facts.
