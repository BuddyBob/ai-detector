there are a variety of opinions on what universities are meant to do  some think they are solely meant to prepare students for employment, while others believe that they have other functions as well. i believe that universities do have a dual purpose  to prepare students for the workforce, and to provide them with a wellrounded education. employers increasingly want employees who have university degrees, so it is important for students to have a strong academic foundation. a university education can provide students with the skills and knowledge they need to be successful in the workforce. for example, a university degree can teach students how to think critically, how to solve problems, and how to work in a team. however, a university education is about more than just preparing students for employment. it is also about giving students the opportunity to explore different subjects and to learn about different cultures. a university degree can open up new doors for students, and it can help them to achieve their goals and dreams. in conclusion, i believe that universities have both a vocational and a nonvocational function. they provide students with the skills and knowledge they need to be successful in the workforce, but they also give students the opportunity to explore different subjects and to learn about different cultures. i think this is an important part of a university education, and it is something that should not be overlooked.
