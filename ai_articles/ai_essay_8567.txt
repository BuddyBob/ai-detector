it is no secret that technology has drastically changed the way we work and live. with the introduction of computers and smartphones, many everyday tasks have become easier than ever before. however, this surge in technological advances has also created a new problem digital overload. digital overload occurs when people are overwhelmed by too much information or data streaming through their devices on a daily basis. this can lead to an increase in stress levels and difficulty focusing, as well as a host of physical symptoms such as headaches, fatigue, insomnia, and muscle tension. in todays world it is increasingly important for individuals to learn how to manage their digital life so they dont succumb to the illeffects caused by digital overload. one effective way of managing your online presence is by setting boundaries around your use of technology. for instance, create specific times during the day where you completely power down all electronic devices for thirty minutes or more including phones, tablets, laptops etc., focus on calming activities such as reading or meditating instead this will give your mind time to relax from the constant bombardment of information coming at it through various screens throughout the day. . another strategy which can help reduce stress associated with digital overload is digital decluttering  which means going through all your emails regularly and deleting unimportant ones while unsubscribing from any irrelevant newsletters cluttering up our inboxes deactivating social media accounts you havent used in months or even years! and generally cutting out anything that does not add value or meaning into our lives but rather just adds noise and distraction making us feel overwhelmed with information without actually providing useful content we need at that moment in time. finally remember that being mindful about how we interact with technology is key  avoid multitasking whenever possible since research shows that constantly switching between tasks reduces productivity substantially use helpful apps like freedomwhich allow users to block certain websites while working thus reducing distractions away from essential tasks helping saving valuable time  set goals
