cell phones have become a hot topic when it comes to the debate about whether they should be allowed in the classroom. from a students perspective, having access to a cell phone during class may seem convenient, but others argue that cell phone use can be a distraction and negatively affect their academic performance. while some believe that students should be trusted with selfcontrol when it comes to personal phone use, others argue that even small distractions can add up and hamper learning. while there can be benefits to having access to a cell phone in class, as well as potential drawbacks, it is ultimately up to the educational institution to decide how and when cell phones should be given access.
