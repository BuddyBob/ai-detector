sportsmanship is a vital part of every sport, and its something that can make or break an athletes career. it involves displaying respect for the game, your opponents, oneself and others involved in the competition. to be successful in sports requires more than just physical ability mental toughness and emotional stability are also needed. by having good sportsmanship habits well established before any competition begins, athletes will help ensure their success both on the field as well as off. showing respect to opponents is one of the most important aspects of good sportsmanship. this includes treating them fairly during play no matter who wins or loses each round or match. congratulate them after matches even if you win so they know that their efforts were valued and appreciated despite not winning themselves. respect should also be given to referees by following their decisions without arguing or complaining about calls made during the game  whether theyre right or wrong. finally, recognizing when another team has achieved victory with grace instead of gloating shows true class among competitors regardless of rank within a league structure. having strong selfcontrol throughout sporting events means controlling emotions such as anger or frustration rather than letting them get out of hand during gameplay which often leads to unnecessary mistakes being made due to lack of focus on strategy and skill abilities instead focused solely on beating your opponent at all costs  this goes against everything that makes up great sportsmanship! controlling emotions also extends offfield into postgame interviews where comments made can heavily influence public opinion towards yourself as an athlete  so take care to stay professional in what you say at all times!. finally, demonstrating good sportsmanship means taking responsibility for any action taken during competitive situations no matter how small it may seem at first glance apologize whenever necessary even though it may hurt pride but doing this helps build strong relationships between players and teams which encourages healthy rivalries while still respecting one anothers strengths  weaknesses alike making everybody better overall
