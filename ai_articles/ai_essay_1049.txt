the facial action coding system is a new technology that can read peoples emotions. it can tell if you are happy, sad, or even bored. some people think it would be cool to use this technology in a classroom to see how students are feeling. but i dont think thats a good idea. first of all, its just too creepy. i dont want a computer watching my face all the time, trying to figure out how im feeling. its like big brother is watching me, and thats just not cool. second, its not like its going to help us learn better. i mean, if im bored, im bored. theres not much the computer can do about it. and if im happy, well, thats great, but its not like its going to make me learn more. third, what if the computer gets it wrong? like, what if it thinks im mad when im really just concentrating? that could be really embarrassing. last, this technology is expensive. schools dont have enough money as it is, and now they want to waste it on some fancy computer program? thats just not right. so, in conclusion, using the facial action coding system in a classroom is not a good idea. its creepy, it wont help us learn better, it could get it wrong, and its a waste of money. lets just stick to learning the oldfashioned way, with a teacher who can actually see and understand our emotions.
