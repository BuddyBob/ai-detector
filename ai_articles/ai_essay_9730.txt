the history of roller derby is an intriguing and unique one. although the sport first started in the early s, it has evolved into a fullfledged competitive event enjoyed by both men and women all over the world. initially created as a form of entertainment for carnivals, roller derby quickly became popular with audiences due to its highspeed action and lively atmosphere. roller derby has been through many changes over the years, adapting to cultural trends while staying true to its original roots. in , leo seltzer sparked a revival with his traveling show transcontinental roller derby which toured cities throughout america. at this time, rules were less formal and matches were more geared toward entertaining spectators than providing real competition between teams. these games featured live music and theatrics that added excitement for viewers but lacked structure or purpose within each match itself. in , sports promoter jerry seltzer took control of his fathers business after leo passed away and began developing modern roller derby rules that emphasized aggressive skating tactics such as blocking opponents from passing or jostling them off track  these aspects are still present today in various forms throughout different leagues around the globe! the s saw another surge in popularity thanks to tv coverage however, this was short lived due to economic troubles facing promoters who could no longer afford large venues or lucrative contracts with networks like abc sports network which had previously broadcasted televised bouts. as it stands now there are several professional leagues competing regularly including roller derby league rdl, womens flat track derby association wftda north american roller championships narch etc each league has its own unique set of rules which competitors must abide by during their events  however they all follow basic guidelines regarding safety equipment worn while playing along with standard game lengthsformats stipulated by international governing bodies such as world skate international federation of roller skating ifrs. .
