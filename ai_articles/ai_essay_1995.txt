when discussing the pros and cons of changing the school menu for the benefit of student health and academic performance, it is important to consider both sides. on one hand, providing students with healthy meals provides the essential nutrients and energy needed to focus in class and refrain from getting sick. studies have shown that students who regularly eat healthful school lunches tend to have higher grade point averages and miss fewer days of school. for example, a study conducted at a high school in california found that students who ate school lunches had higher grades than those who brought their own. on the other hand, changing the school menu may present more challenges than benefits. as schools transition to healthier options, students may not take the change positively, leading to lack of interest in the meals and potential food waste. additionally, some students may require additional dietary accommodations that may not be available in the new menu. furthermore, although healthier options can be more expensive, school budgets are limited. having to pay for these meals could place an additional burden on students and families. ultimately, the key is providing students with healthy options that are both delicious and affordable.
