the best way to tackle any problem or situation is to make it as simple as possible. many people believe that complexity and size are the keys to success, but it takes real effort and courage to take possible. the most successful products and services are those that are easy to use so that the customer can quickly and efficiently find what they are looking for. for example, apple inc. has been streamlining processes and eliminating waste. this approach has been used to great effect by many successful companies, such as toyota, who have been able to maintain their high levels of production while also reducing costs. in addition, simplicity is essential when it comes to communication. it is important that messages are concise and clear so that they can be quickly and easily understood.
