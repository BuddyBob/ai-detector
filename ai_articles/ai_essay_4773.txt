there is no one answer to the question of whether children should learn to compete or cooperate. some people feel that competition is a natural and necessary part of life, while others believe that cooperation is key to a successful society. i believe that both competition and cooperation have their place in a childs education. competition can teach children many important life skills. it can teach them how to set goals, work hard, and be successful. these skills can be useful in many areas of life, from academics to work to relationships. cooperation can also be valuable, teaching children how to work together and get along with others. this is important in both personal and professional relationships. i think it is important for children to learn both competition and cooperation. competition can teach them how to be successful and achieve their goals. cooperation can teach them how to get along with others and work together. these skills are important in both the personal and professional world.
