cellphones have become an increasing part of everyday life, and classrooms are no exception. schools are beginning to embrace this technology for its potential to encourage learning and more personalized instruction. not only are schools allowing students to bring mobile devices to use during class, but certain apps have been developed to enhance teaching and improve student results. while there are potential issues that come with allowing cellphones in the classroom, if done properly, they can be an invaluable learning tool. as such, schools must establish established guidelines for acceptable use as well as safety measures, such as time and data restrictions. in addition, cellphones can be used in the case of family emergencies, such as when a student needs to leave school early or attend an appointment. furthermore, schools can provide information on how to contact the school in emergencies as well as ways parents can be informed should emergency arise.
