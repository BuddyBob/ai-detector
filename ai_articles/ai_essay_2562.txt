ralph waldo emerson was an american philosopher renowned for his writings on individualism and selfreliance. he believed that personal growth and development could only take place when individuals take responsibility for their own lives and eschew conformity to societal norms. this philosophy can be applied to mastering any skill or trying something new. it encourages us to take ownership of our own development, symbolically take the helm of our own lives, and stay the course of relentless selfimprovement. in todays world, emersons philosophy is just as relevant as ever. in an era of  connectivity and distractions, it is harder than ever to stay focused and determined when trying something new or mastering a skill. emersons idea of selfreliance provides foundation for anyone to stay steadfast and committed to their path and personal growth.
