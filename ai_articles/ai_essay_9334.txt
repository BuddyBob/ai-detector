fossil fuels have been a major source of energy for centuries, but it is becoming increasingly clear that the continued use of such resources is unsustainable and detrimental to our environment. while fossil fuels are relatively inexpensive and easily accessible, their longterm effects on our planet make them an unsuitable option for powering the future. the impacts of burning fossil fuels are far reachingfrom air pollution to climate change. when coal, oil, or natural gas are burned for energy production, they release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. this results in changes in global temperatures as more heat becomes trapped on earth than can escape back out into space. additionally, when these pollutants enter the air we breathe they contribute to smog and poor air quality which leads to health issues including respiratory diseases like asthma and bronchitis as well as cancerous tumors among many other illnesses associated with breathing polluted air. in addition to its negative environmental impact, using finite fossil fuel resources also has economic consequences that must be considered when discussing potential alternatives. once supplies run out there will no longer be an available resource to meet demands which could negatively affect economies around the world through higher prices or limited availability of necessary goods and services that rely on energy production from fossil fuels. it is clear then that we cannot continue relying on this outdated technology if we want our future generations to live healthy lives in a sustainable environment with adequate access to essential services at reasonable costs. fortunately there are now multiple options available that offer clean renewable sources of energy such as solar power or wind turbines which can replace traditional methods powered by nonrenewable sources used throughout history . these new technologies allow us not only generate electricity without producing harmful emissions but also create jobs related maintenance operations , construction projects , research initiatives , etc., furthering economic growth while providing green alternatives for meeting global needs . investing in these types of renewable energies allows us reduce reliance on polluting practices while helping mitigate some effects caused by climate change
