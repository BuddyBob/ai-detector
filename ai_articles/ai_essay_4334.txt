one of the biggest issues to be considered when changing the summer break to june and august and eliminating spring break is the effect on students and teachers. for students, this means that they would lose out on a week off that they usually look forward to. the loss of spring break could lead to higher levels of stress and frustration, especially among those who have been looking forward to vacations or time off from school work. teachers could be put under increased pressure as they would have to ensure that students meet their yearlong educational goals without the extra week in the spring. to try and make up for the lost spring break, schools could introduce activities that simulate a break in the middle of the year. one idea could be a week of fun activities that take place at the end of the school year, with events like organized trips, workshops, and games. another solution would be for schools to offer extended summer breaks, where students could take a longer break in between june and august. this could help to make up for the lost spring break and enable students to have time off from their studies.
