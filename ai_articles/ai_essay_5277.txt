in todays fastpaced world, people are often heard complaining about the stress and workload they have to bear in their jobs. while it is true that work has become more demanding, i believe that it is not entirely accurate to say that it has become less leisurely than in the past. in this essay, i will explain why i disagree with this statement. firstly, technology has made work easier and more efficient. in the past, people had to do everything manually, which was timeconsuming and tiring. for example, accountants had to do all their calculations by hand, which could take hours or even days. nowadays, they can use computers and software to do the same work in a matter of minutes. this has not only reduced the workload but has also made work less stressful. secondly, there are more opportunities for career growth and development today than in the past. companies today offer various training programs, workshops, and seminars to help employees improve their skills and advance their careers. this was not the case in the past, where people had limited opportunities for career growth. with more opportunities for growth and development, employees are more motivated and engaged in their work, making it less stressful. lastly, worklife balance has become a significant concern for employers today. companies are now offering flexible working hours, remote work options, and paid time off to help employees maintain a healthy worklife balance. this was not the case in the past, where work was considered the top priority, and employees had to work long hours without any breaks. with a better worklife balance, employees are more productive and less stressed. in conclusion, while it is true that work has become more demanding, i disagree with the statement that it has become less leisurely than in the past. technology has made work easier and more efficient, there are more opportunities for career growth and development, and worklife balance has become a significant concern for employers today. all these factors have contributed to making work less stressful and more enjoyable.
