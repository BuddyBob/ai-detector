when it comes to achieving success in life, school, work and sports, there is an old adage that you can go from failure to failure without loss of enthusiasm. this means that it is possible to be resilient to failure and to continuously persist in the pursuit of our goals. enthusiasm is often overlooked as a vital ingredient to our success, but it can be a powerful motivator. it can give us the hope and courage to continue on our journey, despite the hardships and setbacks that we may face. moreover, enthusiasm is contagious. if we can tap into this positivity and spread it to those around us, we can help to inspire and motivate them to do the same. by never allowing ourselves to be discouraged by failure and instead projecting enthusiasm and optimism, we can find the strength and motivation to keep pressing forward on our path to success.
