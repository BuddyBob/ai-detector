art has been used throughout history as a way to express feelings, tell stories, and to create something beautiful. more recently, using art for relaxation has become a popular activity used to help reduce stress levels and provide an outlet for creativity. different forms of art can provide different levels of relaxation, including painting, drawing, building, and sculpting. painting and drawing are popular activities for relaxation because they allow you to fully express yourself without needing any other supplies. building, meanwhile, provides an environment for creativity and focus, helping you lose yourself in the process rather than worrying about everyday pressures. sculpting is known to even reduce levels of cortisol  the stress hormone  as well as providing a unique outlet for different perspectives and emotions. overall, art can provide tremendous benefits as an activity, whether youre using it to express yourself or to reduce stress. by exploring different forms of art, you can open up a whole new world of creativity and use it to your emotional benefit.
