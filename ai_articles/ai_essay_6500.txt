in todays world, its hard to find an advertisement that doesnt make a product seem better than it really is. every company wants you to believe that their product is the best on the market, and theyll do whatever it takes to make you think that. whether its using attractive models in their ads, making the product look bigger than it is, or using editing techniques to make it look perfect, companies will go to great lengths to make their products look as appealing as possible. and its not just television and print ads that do this. even companies that use online advertising have gotten in on the act. youve probably seen ads that pop up on websites that show you a product that you were just looking at on another site. and these ads are usually carefully targeted to show you products that youre interested in, or that are similar to ones that youve bought in the past. so why do companies do this? well, its simple really. they want to sell more products. and the more appealing they can make their products look, the more likely people are to buy them. so, do i agree that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are? yes, i do. and i think that its something that we as consumers need to be aware of. we need to remember that we cant believe everything we see in an ad, and that sometimes the products are not as great as they seem.
