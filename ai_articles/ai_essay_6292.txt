there is no doubt that advertisements often make products seem far better than they really are. for one, advertisers have a vested interest in making their products look as appealing as possible after all, the more people who buy a product, the more money they make. additionally, advertisers often use exaggerated or even false claims to make their products seem more desirable. for example, a shampoo commercial might claim that using the product will make your hair  times softer. in reality, the shampoo might only make your hair a tiny bit softer than it would be if you didnt use it at all. another example is a car commercial that might show a car driving through a beautiful, lush forest, with the narrator saying that the car is perfect for offroad adventures. in reality, the car might not be very good at driving on rough terrain. there are a few reasons why people might still buy products even though they know that the advertisements are exaggerating or lying. for one, people might be in a hurry and not have time to do a lot of research into the product. additionally, people might not be very good at critical thinking, and so they might not be able to see through the exaggerated or false claims in the advertisement. finally, people might simply not care if the product is actually as good as the advertisement says it is they might just want the product because it looks cool or because they think it will make them happier.
