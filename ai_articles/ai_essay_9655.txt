when it comes to aesthetics, there is no denying that the modern art movement of abstract expressionism has had a huge impact on how people view and appreciate visual works. abstract expressionism was an influential postworld war ii style of painting characterized by gestural brushstrokes and nonrepresentational imagery. the movement began in the s with jackson pollock, willem de kooning, mark rothko, franz kline and others who sought to move away from traditional representational styles and instead focus on expressing emotion through formless shapes and colors. abstract expressionist paintings are often seen as chaotic yet meaningful representations of our innermost feelings  a way to express thoughts or emotions without speaking them out loud. to create these works, artists typically used bold colors and dramatic brushstrokes that spanned across large canvases. through this powerful mark making technique they could convey a sense of energy or tension in their compositions which often contained ambiguous forms such as amorphous shapes or fragmented figures. as viewers we can interpret these pieces through our own personal experiences while still appreciating them for their beauty regardless of what story they might tell us about ourselves or the world around us. the legacy left behind by abstract expressionist painters continues to be felt today in the work of contemporary artists who take inspiration from its groundbreaking techniques while adding their own unique style into the mix. whether youre an artist looking for creative stimulation or someone just interested in learning more about this fascinating period of art history, exploring abstract expressionism can provide you with plenty of insight into how one singular style can have lasting effects on culture over time
