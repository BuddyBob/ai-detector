there is no doubt that education is a lifelong process. every day we learn new things and we apply what we have learned in our daily lives. however, there are some people who believe that formal education is only necessary during our school years. in my opinion, both formal and informal education are important and play different roles in our lives. formal education is important because it provides us with the essential skills and knowledge that we need in order to function in society. without formal education, we would not be able to read, write, or do basic math. formal education also teaches us about history, science, and other subjects that we need to know in order to be informed citizens. informal education is also important because it helps us to learn about the world around us. we learn about the world through our interactions with other people, by reading books and magazines, and by watching television and movies. informal education can also be very useful in our professional lives, as it can help us to learn new skills and to keep up with changes in our field. both formal and informal education are important, and both play a role in our lives. formal education is necessary in order to function in society, while informal education helps us to learn about the world around us.
