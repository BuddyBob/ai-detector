ways. through team building activities within practices and games, young athletes learn how to build trust between teammates while also learning valuable skills such as working together towards a common goal. this allows them to foster strong relationships with their peers based on respect and understanding, which will carry into other interpersonal relationships throughout their lives. the sport display higher levels of psychological resilience when faced with stressful situations compared to those who do not take part in any form of sporting activity. additionally, research has demonstrated increased feelings of empowerment following involvement in competitive sport due to enhanced social support systems amongst participants which provides individuals with motivation outside
