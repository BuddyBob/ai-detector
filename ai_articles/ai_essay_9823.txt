connect with remote doctors in order to get professional advice without having to physically visit a doctors office or hospital  another example of how technology is transforming healthcare delivery systems around the world. telemedicine allows people who are unable to travel due to disability lack access because they live in rural areas or simply cannot find enough available appointments with
