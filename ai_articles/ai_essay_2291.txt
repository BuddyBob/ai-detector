the world is a complex place and, oftentimes, there is no better guide through life than a loving parent or guardian. throughout our lives, as we experience the highs and lows of life, wisdom and experience from those who have gone before us can be invaluable. for this reason, it is important to obtain wisdom and experience from a parent or guardian. for the younger generation, the guidance of a parent or guardian can help them make wise decisions, especially during times of adversity. with practical wisdom and life experience, these mentors can help advise through difficult decisionsfrom the small but significant ones in teenage life such as career choices, to the bigger life decisions such as sorting out relationships and financial management. finally, as children become adults, having someone with experience to act as a sounding board to discuss career opportunities, sports, social situations, and relationships, can be key to making wise choices. a parent or guardian who is wise and experienced can help a young adult tap into their strengths and teach them how to find and develop wisdom. to sum up, wisdom and experience from a parent or guardian can be invaluable. while a young person might be more independent, those with parents or guardians to help guide them on their journey will have a much greater chance of success and fulfillment in life.
