limited car usage can provide significant benefits for communities and the environment. according to multiple sources, reducing reliance on personal vehicles can help address serious issues like pollution, traffic congestion, and climate change. air pollution from traffic emissions poses major health risks. as passage  notes, vehicles are a leading source of particulate matter and nitrogen oxides which contribute to respiratory illnesses. limiting personal car trips would decrease these harmful pollutants released into the atmosphere. public transit produces significantly fewer emissions per passenger than private cars. transitioning some drivers to buses or trains could thus help cities attain cleaner air. traffic congestion not only wastes peoples time but also fuel as cars sit idling. passage  refers to how a growing number of vehicles has led to more time stuck in congestion. if a portion of commuters shifted to alternative modes of transport like cycling, walking or public transit, there would be fewer cars competing for road space during peak hours. this could reduce vehicular bottlenecks and free up traffic flow. with less congestion, fuel costs from idling would also decline, providing an economic benefit. additionally, restricting car usage would lower greenhouse gas emissions from the transportation sector according to passage . as one of the fastest growing sources of climate pollution, curbing automobile dependence can make an impact in mitigating the carbon footprint. lowemission public transit, walking and cycling can replace shortdistance car trips which significantly damage the environment. transitioning to more sustainable modes will support efforts to curb global warming for future generations. in summary, limiting car usage through promotion of public transit and active mobility brings multiple advantages by reducing air and noise pollution, traffic congestion, and emissions responsible for the climate crisis. a balanced multimodal transportation system with fewer personal vehicles can offer communities cleaner air and a more efficient movement of people and goods.
