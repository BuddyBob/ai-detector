in my opinion, extending the school day by one and a half hours is a bad idea. the additional time would place an undue burden on students, drivers, and teachers. for students, the extra time spent in the classroom would leave less time for homework, activities, and family time all of which contribute to physical and mental health. for drivers, it would mean more kids on the road during rush hour, potentially leading to dangerous situations and delays. finally, for teachers, it would mean longer days with more lesson plans to create and teach. all in all, i think that extending the school day is not a good idea. it could lead to dangerous and negative situations for students, drivers, and teachers alike. there are better ways to improve education that dont rely on cutting into the precious time students have for other activities.
