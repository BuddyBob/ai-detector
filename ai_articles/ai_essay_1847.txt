seeking multiple opinions can help someone make a better choice because it allows them to get different perspectives on their decision or problem. here are a few reasons why different people have different experiences and points of view. no single person can anticipate all the dimensions of a complex choice or situation. by talking to more than one person, you gain insight into aspects that any individual advisor may overlook. you get a more wellrounded sense of the issues and factors at play. people sometimes give biased advice unintentionally based on their own preferences, prejudices or circumstances. getting a second or third viewpoint helps balance out such biases by introducing alternative ways of thinking about the choice that the other advisors may not have mentioned. it helps ensure a more objective analysis. different advisors are likely to have complementary strengths and weaknesses in terms of expertise, judgment and field of knowledge. while one advisor provides a useful risk analysis, another helps clarify priorities and values. their combined insights offer a more comprehensive set of guidance than any single person could provide alone. no one approach is perfect. multiple advisory dialogues allow the person seeking advice to analyze recommendations critically and choose the elements from different advisors that seem most prudent and applicable to their unique situation. crosschecking input promotes more careful consideration and a more confident decision. in summary, a diversity of counsel enhances the decisionmaking process by exposing the question and potential answers to a multiplicity of informed viewpoints. it ensures a more wellrounded examination of options and outcomes before making an important choice. the old proverb is correct two heads are better than one.
