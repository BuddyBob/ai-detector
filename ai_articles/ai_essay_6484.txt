most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this statement for a few reasons. first, many advertisements use attractive models to sell products. the models are usually airbrushed to look perfect, which gives the viewer an unrealistic expectation of how the product will actually look on them. second, advertisers often use persuasive language to make their products seem essential. they might use phrases like you deserve this or treat yourself to make viewers feel like they need the product in order to be happy. finally, ads often use beforeandafter photos to show how much a product has improved someones life. however, these photos are often heavily edited or even fake. in reality, the product probably wont have such a dramatic effect. overall, i think its important to be skeptical of advertisements. just because a product is being advertised doesnt mean its actually a good product. its important to do your own research before buying anything, and to remember that the people in ads are not always representative of the average person.
