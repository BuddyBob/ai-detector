through any environment with speed and efficiency by utilizing running, jumping, climbing, vaulting and other forms of movement. it is often referred to as an art or practice of selfdevelopment its practitioners use it to overcome obstacles both physically and mentally while pursuing personal growth. at first glance parkour might appear to be just another extreme sport due to its focus on acrobatics but there are actually many more layers than one observes at surface level. it encompasses much more than simply performing tricks for spectacleit encourages critical thinking about how best to navigate efficiently from point a to point b using the most efficient route possible while avoiding injury or harm. this involves problem solving skills in order identify the path that offers optimum safety along with maximum speed which is why it can be applied not only for fitness purposes but also for practical uses such as emergency situations like firefighting or rescue operations where time could mean life or death. beyond its practical applications lies an even deeper purpose learning how our own body works and understanding how we interact with our environment  something
