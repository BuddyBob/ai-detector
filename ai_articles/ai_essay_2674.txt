considering ones first impression when making decisions has its advantages and disadvantages. on one hand, we may be able to gain a quicker and informed understanding of a person or situation. on the other, our assumptions may be limited or misguided, preventing us from making truly objective decisions. alternatively, when we wait to form a more complete opinion, there is the danger of procrastinating and making decisions too late. still, this tactic gives us the opportunity to make informed decisions that take into account all information gathered over time. ultimately, ones approach will depend on the situation at hand and the desired outcome.
