schools should not be yearround for many reasons, the foremost of which has to do with the importance of summertime for students. summertime is a time for students to destress and take a break from the rigor of regular school life. it gives them the opportunity to learn and explore outside of the classroom in ways traditional schooling cannot. moreover, research shows that longer hours of schooling actually decrease education quality. when students are in school for too long, the quality and quantity of learning decreases. by taking regular summer breaks, students are able to come into the new school year fresher and more motivated. summer breaks also allow students to spend more time with their family and friends, which can be especially beneficial for teenage development. finally, longer school years are expensive and can put incredible strain on school systems. yearround schools require additional funds for staffing, utilities, and resources. it can often be too costly for schools with already strained budgets. in contrast, summertime can be a muchneeded break for the school system and for administrators. in summary, summertime is a vital part of the school year for students, and schools should not be yearround. summer offers students a chance to rest and explore, the quality of their learning is improved, and it can be more costeffective for school systems.
