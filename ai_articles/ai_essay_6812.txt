in todays fastpaced world, young people are often criticized for not giving enough time to their communities. while some may argue that this is true, i believe that young people are actively involved in community service and are making a positive impact in their communities. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my opinion. firstly, young people are actively involved in community service through various organizations and initiatives. for instance, many schools and universities require students to complete a certain number of community service hours as part of their curriculum. this not only helps students gain valuable experience but also encourages them to give back to their communities. moreover, there are numerous youthled organizations that focus on community service, such as the national honor society and key club. these organizations provide young people with opportunities to volunteer and make a difference in their communities. secondly, young people are using social media to raise awareness and promote community service. with the rise of social media platforms like instagram and twitter, young people are using these platforms to spread awareness about various social issues and encourage others to get involved in community service. for example, the trashtagchallenge, which went viral on social media, encouraged people to clean up litter in their communities and share their efforts online. this challenge was started by a young person and gained widespread attention, inspiring many others to get involved in community service. lastly, young people are taking initiative and starting their own community service projects. for instance, a group of high school students in my community started a food drive to help families in need during the covid pandemic. they created a social media page to promote their project and collected donations from their peers and local businesses. their efforts were successful, and they were able to provide food to many families in need. this is just one example of how young people are taking initiative and making a positive impact in their communities. in conclusion, i disagree with the statement that young people do not give enough time to helping their communities. young people are actively involved in community service through various organizations and initiatives, using social media to raise awareness and promote community service, and taking initiative and starting their own community service projects. it is important to recognize and support the efforts of young people in making a positive impact in their communities.
