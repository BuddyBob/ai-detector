the world of painting is one full of beauty, emotion and power. while many painters choose to explore abstract concepts in their works, landscape paintings have a unique appeal that brings nature into art. one of the most famous landscape painters was american master thomas cole, who is best known for his series the course of empire from . this body of work explored mankinds relationship with nature over time through five distinct scenes  desolation, consummation, destruction, chaos and restoration  all depicting an imagined view from catskill mountains along the hudson river valley where cole lived and worked during his lifetime. thomas coles fascination with capturing the beauty and power of natural scenery in paint first began when he emigrated to america at age eighteen after studying art in england for three years prior. his style combined romanticism with classical elements often emphasizing bold colors while also exploring light and shadow as they interacted with natures organic forms such as mountains or trees. in addition to being influenced by these artistic styles, cole had a deep appreciation for literature which can be seen within his compositions as well detailed metaphors are woven throughout landscapes like rolling hills representing powerful historical events or figures guiding viewers through various eras. coles dedication to capturing nature on canvas earned him critical acclaim from both european and american audiences alike however it was the course of empire that cemented his legacy forever among artists . as a response to increased industrialization occurring around him at the time , this project allowed him to step away from more traditional portraiture pieces commonly seen up until then , but still deliver impactful messages about man versus nature . with each scene brimming with vivid details , viewers experience a journey across centuries starting off peaceful before progressing towards destruction only return back again eventually once balance has been restored . unsurprisingly , its influence can still be felt today within modern day works referencing topics such as climate change or political unrest using similar tropes found originally in thomas coles wild
