of incorporating technology into math curriculum. while there is much debate surrounding the integration of tech into standardsbased learning environments, one thing is clear done properly, it can have a major impact on how students approach their mathematics studies. when integrating technology into math instruction, one important factor to consider is how it can be used to facilitate problem solving and critical thinking. for example, online software such as mathletics allows users to solve problems more quickly than traditional methods by providing instant feedback on progress made during practice exercises. this type of interactive platform also helps students identify areas in which they need additional assistance so that they may gain a better understanding of difficult concepts before moving onto new ones. additionally, digital resources such as geogebra give learners access to powerful tools for visualizing conditions and exploring relationships between different variables  helping them develop a deeper understanding of mathematical principles through experimentation and exploration rather than memorization alone. next up is using tech to differentiate instruction  allowing teachers to tailor lessons according individual student needs while maintaining high expectations across all lesson plans. take khan academy as an example here learners are able
