when it comes to learning english, many people believe that it is important to focus on both reading and speaking skills. there are many reasons why this is the case. first of all, reading is essential for understanding english grammar and vocabulary. if you cant read english, you wont be able to learn the language effectively. second, speaking is the best way to practice your english skills. you can practice your pronunciation and fluency by speaking with a native english speaker. finally, speaking is a great way to make new friends and build relationships. if you want to improve your english skills, you need to put in the effort and practice both reading and speaking.
