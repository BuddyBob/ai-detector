there are two schools of thought when it comes to teaching children how to interact with others  teaching them to compete or to cooperate. those who believe in teaching children to compete think that it will help them in life, as they will have to compete for jobs, for example. those who believe in teaching children to cooperate think that it will help them get along better with others and will make them more successful in life. i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. one reason for this is that competition can often be harmful to children. it can make them feel bad about themselves if they lose, and it can also lead to rivalry and bullying. cooperation, on the other hand, can help children feel good about themselves and can help them learn how to get along with others. another reason why i believe that children should be taught to cooperate is that it can help them in life. cooperation is essential in the workplace, and it can also help children in their personal relationships. learning to cooperate at a young age can help children develop strong relationships and can help them be successful in life. in conclusion, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate, not compete. cooperation can help children feel good about themselves, get along with others, and be successful in life.
