success is a subjective term that means different things to different people. however, one thing that is common among successful people is their willingness to try new things and take risks. i strongly agree with the statement that successful people try new things and take risks rather than only doing what they already know how to do well. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my argument. firstly, trying new things and taking risks is essential for personal growth and development. when we step out of our comfort zone and try something new, we challenge ourselves to learn and grow. we acquire new skills and knowledge, which can be beneficial in both our personal and professional lives. for instance, a person who has always been interested in photography but has never tried it may discover a hidden talent and turn it into a successful career. similarly, a person who has always been afraid of public speaking may overcome their fear by taking a public speaking course and become a confident speaker. secondly, trying new things and taking risks is crucial for innovation and progress. without taking risks, we would never have seen the invention of the light bulb, the telephone, or the internet. successful people are not afraid to take risks and try new things because they understand that failure is a part of the process. they learn from their mistakes and use them as stepping stones to success. for example, steve jobs, the cofounder of apple, took a risk by introducing the iphone, which was a revolutionary product at the time. today, the iphone is one of the most successful products in the world, and apple is one of the most valuable companies. lastly, trying new things and taking risks is essential for achieving our goals and dreams. if we only do what we already know how to do well, we limit ourselves and our potential. successful people set ambitious goals and take calculated risks to achieve them. they are not afraid of failure or setbacks because they understand that they are part of the journey. for instance, serena williams, one of the greatest tennis players of all time, did not become successful overnight. she had to work hard, take risks, and overcome obstacles to achieve her goals. she did not let setbacks and failures discourage her but used them as motivation to keep pushing forward. in conclusion, trying new things and taking risks is a crucial component of success. it is essential for personal growth and development, innovation and progress, and achieving our goals and dreams. successful people understand that failure is a part of the process and use it as a learning opportunity. therefore, we should all strive to step out of our comfort zones, try new things, and take calculated risks to achieve success in our lives.
