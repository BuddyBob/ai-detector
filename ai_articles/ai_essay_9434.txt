the changing landscape of education during the covid pandemic has had a dramatic impact on students and teachers alike. schools around the world have been forced to switch to virtual learning, forcing everyone involved to adapt in new ways while still trying to maintain educational standards. this essay will explore how this shift has impacted student engagement and teacher preparedness, and look at potential solutions for these issues going forward. one major issue that has arisen due to this sudden transition is reduced student engagement. without being able to interact with their peers in person or benefit from direct instruction, many students are struggling with motivation when it comes to completing online tasks. in addition, there is often confusion about expectations surrounding work completion which can lead some students to procrastinate or give up entirely. as a result, teachers need additional strategies in place that ensure all of their pupils remain engaged even when they are miles away from each other. another challenge brought about by the pandemic is teacher unpreparedness for virtual learning environments. many instructors were not provided proper training on how best utilize digital tools such as zoom or google classroom prior to having classes move online thus leaving them feeling overwhelmed and underprepared come time for teaching remotely . it can also be difficult for teachers who are used physical teaching methods such as handson activities or group discussions adjust in order accommodate an internet platform where they may lack visuals cues necessary for understanding whats happening during class time . additionally , if instructors dont feel adequately prepared then it can cause frustration among both themselves and their students which further reduces motivation levels overall . . so what solutions should be considered moving forward?
