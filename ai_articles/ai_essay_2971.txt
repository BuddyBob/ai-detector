asking multiple people for advice can be a beneficial decisionmaking strategy. by doing this, one can gain perspectives from a more diverse range of sources, allowing for a more balanced and informed decision. this could lead to a better choice than what might be derived from a single opinion. furthermore, by listening to a variety of counsel, one may be exposed to new ideas and solutions to a problem, reaffirming convictions or allowing for a more personalized solution. this method can also provide an individual with a more indepth understanding of the situation, as each party usually provides their personal perspective that can contextually provide more information. in addition, this individual can learn how to analyze a situation from multiple points of view, allowing this person to make more informed choices beyond the dilemma in question. overall, seeking advice from different people can be an invaluable asset that can help someone greatly in life, both in the shortterm and longterm. with a broad range of experiences and perspectives, gathering advice from multiple people is a tool with great potential to help someone make more thoughtful and sound decisions that can have a lasting and positive impact.
