the rise of distant learning has had a massive impact on students in recent decades. with the increasing influence of technology on todays generation, students are now able to access educational content and resources from virtually anywhere, taking advantage of opportunities that werent available before. video conferencing has enabled teachers to have facetoface interactions with their students even at a distance, providing that important personal connection that can often be missing from online courses. on top of this, students have the potential to benefit socially and educationally from distant learning, with virtual classes being a great way for people from different cultures, countries and backgrounds to connect and learn. as remote working and online education continues to increase, the potential benefits of distant learning for students only looks set to grow.
