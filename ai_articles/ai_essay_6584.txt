there is no denying that facts are important. they provide the foundation upon which concepts and ideas are built. however, facts alone are not enough. in order for students to truly understand, they need to be able to see how those facts fit together to form a larger picture. think about it this way a student who memorizes a list of countries and their capitals may be able to recite them back to you, but do they really understand what theyre saying? on the other hand, a student who understands the concept of geography will be able to tell you not only where a country is located, but also what its climate is like, what its people are like, and how it compares to other countries. facts are important, but they are only a starting point. it is concepts and ideas that students need to really understand in order to be successful.
