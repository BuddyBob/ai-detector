there is no question that education is expensive. the cost of tuition, room and board, and other associated expenses can be prohibitive for many families. however, the consequences of a failure to educate are even more expensive. in an increasingly globalized world, a lack of education can lead to a lifetime of poverty and insecurity. the most obvious consequence of a failure to educate is a lack of economic opportunity. without an education, it is very difficult to find good jobs that offer livable wages and benefits. this can lead to a cycle of poverty that is difficult to break out of. in addition, a lack of education can also lead to poor health outcomes. studies have shown that people with higher levels of education are more likely to have better health outcomes. they are also more likely to have access to better healthcare. the costs of a failure to educate are clear. however, the benefits of an education are even more important. an education opens the door to economic opportunity and a better quality of life. it is an investment that is worth making.
