dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the united states should change from the current electoral college system to instead elect the president by popular vote. while the electoral college was developed with the aim of giving smaller states more influence, i believe the popular vote system would be more democratic and align better with the principles of one person, one vote. one argument made in favor of keeping the electoral college is that it encourages candidates to build broader coalitions and campaign in more states. however, this claim does not seem to hold up in practice. as we have seen in recent elections, candidates tend to focus their efforts only on swing states that could vote either way, largely ignoring the vast majority of states that strongly favor one party. this disadvantages voters in all but a handful of closely divided states. a popular vote system would encourage candidates to appeal to a broader range of americans across the entire country. it is also argued that without the electoral college, candidates might ignore lowpopulation states. however, i do not find this persuasive because candidates already heavily concentrate on a few swing states under our current system. a national popular vote would not change the incentive structure as much as claimed. furthermore, concerns about less attention paid to small states could be at least partially addressed through other reforms like public campaign financing rather than maintaining a system that can result in a president being elected despite losing the national popular vote. in conclusion, while the aims of the electoral college were understandable at the time of its founding, today it promotes an unfair dynamic where presidential elections are decided by only a small fraction of states. a national popular vote would make every american citizens vote truly equal and foster greater grassroots voter participation. it is time for our elections to be decided directly by the will of the people rather than an indirect system that can undermine majority rule. for these reasons, i believe we should change to electing the president by direct popular vote.
