the modern worlds obsession with technology is pervasive and everchanging. whether it be through the use of smartphones, computers or television, people are constantly connected to a variety of technological platforms that can serve almost any purpose they could desire. in particular, the rise of online streaming services such as netflix and hulu has revolutionized how we watch television shows and movies in our homes. although these streaming services offer an abundance of entertainment options for viewers to enjoy, they also have their drawbacks which should not be ignored. one of the primary issues with using online streaming services is their ability to limit consumer choice. both netflix and hulu restrict their content selection based on licensing agreements between them and major production companies like nbc universal or disneyabc television group. this means that the majority of popular films that you would expect to see available at your local movie store may never appear on a streaming service unless some type of agreement is made between them regarding distribution rights  something many organizations are unlikely to do due to financial concerns. additionally, even if certain shows become available for consumption via one particular platform there will always be regional restrictions preventing users from other countries from accessing this same content thus making it incompletely visible across global markets . furthermore when compared to other media outlets such as cable tv networks or even physical video distributors i.e blockbuster, streaming services lack certain features which could benefit consumers immensely  features like rewind capabilities, parental controls to prevent children from watching inappropriate material or multiple device support allowing customers access content simultaneously across different screens without additional cost being incurred by each user. all these factors taken together make relying solely on appbased providers somewhat unsatisfactory when looking for varied forms entertainment  especially when considering its simplified yet heavily edited framework being used as base foundation all this new media experience we now call streaming. . in conclusion while internet based viewing has certainly become more accessible than ever before thanks largely in part towards apps like netflix  hulu
