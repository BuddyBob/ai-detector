the afterschool homework club is a popular idea among elementary and middle school students, as it provides an additional support system to help them understand and complete class assignments. on the one hand, it can be beneficial for students who need additional help beyond what their teacher can provide in the classroom. a homework club can also keep students off the streets after school hours and provide them with a safe place to work on their assignments. on the other hand, there are potential drawbacks to having an afterschool homework club. many students may use it as a way to procrastinate and avoid doing their homework. additionally, its difficult for teachers to monitor the students progress in the club and ensure that they are on track. it can also be expensive to run and may not be accessible to all students due to the cost or other factors. therefore, there are both pros and cons to having an afterschool homework club. ultimately, it is up to educators to decide if the benefits outweigh the potential drawbacks and create a homework club that both meets the needs of their students and fits within their budgets.
