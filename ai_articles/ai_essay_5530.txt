there is no doubt that we have become a throwaway society. we are constantly bombarded with advertising telling us to buy the latest gadget, the newest fashion, or the latest musthave item. and when we tire of these things, we simply throw them away and buy something new. this culture of disposability has had a devastating effect on the environment. each year, we generate millions of tonnes of waste, much of it plastic. this plastic takes hundreds of years to break down, and in the meantime it pollutes our land and waterways. animals often mistake it for food and ingest it, with fatal consequences. what can we do to change this culture of disposability? one way is to simply consume less. we can choose to buy items that are made to last, and repair them when they break instead of throwing them away. we can also support businesses that are committed to sustainable practices. ultimately, it is up to each of us to decide whether we want to continue contributing to the throwaway society, or whether we want to make a change.
