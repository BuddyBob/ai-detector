this summer, join us for a week of fun activities, games, sports, donation drives, and picnics at a national park near you. on each of the seven days, we will host different activities for people and animals in need, such as a volleyball tournament, a minigolf tournament, a k run, book drives, pet adoption events, and more. all are welcome to attend, and we encourage community involvement in the week of events. end the week with a celebratory party with music, food, and a chance to connect with your fellow parkgoers. lets be the change and create a brighter future for people and animals alike.
