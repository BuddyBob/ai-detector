distance learning has opened the door to educational opportunities for students who may not have had the chance to attend a traditional school due to financial or other limitations. through online courses, students are able to connect with instructors and classmates from around the world, and have access to a variety of resources. in many cases, distance learning can be completed at a greatly reduced cost, making it an attainable form of education for many. additionally, distance learning provides students with the flexibility to access educational materials on their own time, allowing them to prioritize their studies alongside other life commitments. when compared to traditional methods of education, distance learning shows great promise for those who may not have had otherwise been able to participate.
