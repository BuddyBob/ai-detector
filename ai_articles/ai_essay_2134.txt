jamaica is an island full of exciting and diverse attractions. from lush rainforest hikes to pristine beaches, there is something for everyone to enjoy. if youre an outdoor enthusiast, outdoor activities like ziplining, snorkeling, and rafting are popular. visit the famous dunns river falls to swim in its natural pools, and explore the countrys many stunning caves. for a unique experience, explore jamaicas hidden coves in a kayak or standup paddleboard. cuisine in jamaica is an exciting combination of influences from the caribbean and african flavors to british and spanish influences. dishes like jerk chicken, festival cake, and curried goat can be found in the traditional markets or on the street. kaye, saltfish and bammy, and ackee and saltfish are also popular dishes. in addition to all the activities and food available in jamaica, the country is home to some of the most diverse and exciting species of wildlife. monkeys, birds, and dolphins can be seen while exploring the mangroves and other secrets of jamaicas surrounding waters. stalk the majestic green iguana and explore jamaicas famously rich coral reefs. from the seas to the woods, jamaica is home to some of the worlds most incredible wildlife.
