in todays fastpaced world, young people are often criticized for not giving enough time to their communities. while it is true that some young people are not as involved in their communities as they could be, i do not agree that this is a widespread problem. in fact, many young people are actively engaged in their communities and are making a significant difference in the lives of those around them. firstly, volunteering is becoming increasingly popular among young people. many high schools and universities require students to complete a certain number of volunteer hours in order to graduate. this has led to a rise in the number of young people who are actively involved in their communities. for example, i have a friend who volunteered at a local food bank every saturday for a year. she found the experience to be incredibly rewarding and plans to continue volunteering even after she graduates. secondly, social media has made it easier than ever for young people to organize and mobilize their communities. platforms like facebook, twitter, and instagram allow young people to connect with others who share their interests and passions. this has led to the creation of many grassroots movements and campaigns, such as the metoo movement and the march for our lives. these movements have had a significant impact on society, and they were all started and led by young people. furthermore, young people are often at the forefront of social justice and environmental causes. they are passionate about making the world a better place, and they are not afraid to speak out against injustice. for example, greta thunberg, a yearold swedish climate activist, has inspired millions of young people around the world to take action on climate change. she has spoken at the united nations and has been nominated for the nobel peace prize. in conclusion, while there may be some young people who do not give enough time to helping their communities, i do not believe that this is a widespread problem. many young people are actively engaged in their communities and are making a significant difference in the lives of those around them. whether it is through volunteering, social media activism, or advocacy for social justice and environmental causes, young people are proving that they are capable of effecting positive change in the world.
