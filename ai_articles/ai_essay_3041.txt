first impressions can be a valuable insight into someones life  by observing how someone interacts within a space, their general demeanor and attitude, and how they present themselves, one can immediately begin to guess at their personality, their approach to life, and even their living circumstances. such an impression of another person could provide an understanding of how they think, act, and are overall comfortable with themselves and the idea of responsibility  all invaluable information that can be gleaned solely through the first glimpse of the person in question. taking those observations and truly understanding them can be the first step in forming a meaningful connection with someone.
