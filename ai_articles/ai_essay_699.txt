the system discourages runoff elections, reducing further intricacy in the already complex election process. apart from practical reasons, the electoral college helps ensure that the chosen president has appeal across various regions and caters to the interests of diverse populations . that said, i acknowledge the disaster factor argument posed by bradford plumer  that electors may occasionally go
