have given up their cars. streets are completely carfree, except for the main thoroughfare where the tram runs. car ownership is allowed, but parking is limited and expensive. as a result,  of vaubans passengers to encourage greener alternatives. the concept of a carfree day has also gained popularity in cities like bogota, colombia. this day promotes alternative transportation methods such as hiking, biking, skating, or taking buses. violators are fined to ensure compliance. the program has been successful in reducing traffic congestion and promoting a healthier environment. other cities have even adopted this idea and implemented their own carfree days. these examples show that limiting car usage has numerous advantages. firstly, it helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution. cars are a major contributor to these problems, and by finding alternative transportation methods, we can make significant progress in combatting climate change. secondly, limiting car usage reduces congestion in cities, leading to less traffic and improved traffic flow. this ultimately saves time for commuters and reduces frustration. finally, promoting alternative transportation methods not only benefits the environment but also encourages a more active and healthier lifestyle. in conclusion, there are clear advantages to limiting car usage. we can see from examples around the world that lowcar or carfree living leads to reduced greenhouse gas emissions, improved air quality, reduced congestion, and a healthier lifestyle. it is important for us to adopt these ideas and
