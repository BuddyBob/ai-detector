in his famous essay, ralph waldo emerson wrote, in this world, be yourself. this statement speaks to the power of selfconfidence and the freedom that comes from being your own unique self. when you are confident in who you are and what you stand for, your path to success can be unstoppable. patience and hard work are essential in reaching success but it is also vital to have a variety of skills. by experimenting, you can learn which skills are needed for your success. accomplishment accompanies success and it is important to savor that feeling in order to stay motivated. following emersons advice of being yourself can lead to greatness. no matter your journey on the way to success, remembering his suggestion can help guide you onto the right path.
