mentoring programs that pair older and younger students come with many benefits. firstly, older students can use their knowledge to help their younger colleagues, as they will likely have more experience and insights to share. for example, an older student may be able to provide an inside view of a college or offer tips on applying for scholarships. in addition, the younger students can act as mentors of their own in terms of their grasp of technology, familiarity with digital media, and the latest trends in youth culture. finally, the overall collaboration between the two ages can bridge the gap in knowledge and experience, creating an open and symbiotic atmosphere of learning.
