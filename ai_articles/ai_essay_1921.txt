drivers should not use cell phones while operating a vehicle many people have become heavily dependent on their cell phones in daily life. however, using a cell phone while driving poses grave risks that endanger lives. drivers should not be permitted to use cell phones in any capacity when operating a motor vehicle. there are several reasons why cell phone use behind the wheel is dangerous and should be prohibited. firstly, both handsfree and handheld cell phone use requires the drivers visual, manual and cognitive attention away from the task of driving. even brief glances away from the road to check a phone can cause accidents as conditions on the road may change rapidly. numerous studies have shown that cell phone use while driving leads to slower reaction times, failure to notice road signs and increased chances of rearending another vehicle. secondly, cell phone conversations distract the mind from focusing fully on driving. important details like traffic signals, pedestrians or other cars can be missed as the drivers attention waverles between the call and their surroundings. some research even indicates that driving while engaged in a phone conversation impairs driving ability as much as driving drunk or tired. distracted driving due to phones increases the risk of collisions by up to four times. finally, as cell phone addiction grows in society, many compulsively check their phones for messages, alerts or notifications even at red lights. this can lead to becoming distracted again immediately after the light turns green. there have been instances where drivers missed the light change altogether while preoccupied with their phones. ultimately, any cell phone distraction while driving may result in a tragedy like loss of life in an accident. in conclusion, allowing any cell phone use behind the wheel poses unacceptable risks to public safety on roads. drivers need their undivided attention to awareness and control of changing traffic scenarios. a total ban on cell phone handling and conversations is necessary to curb distracted driving and reduce accident rates. lives depend on drivers operating vehicles responsibly without distractions. for this reason, cell phones should not be used in any form by drivers.
