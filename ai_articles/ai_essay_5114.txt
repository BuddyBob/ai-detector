english is an important language for chinese learners, and there is much debate over which skill should be given priority. some people argue that reading should be given priority, while others argue that speaking should be given priority. in my opinion, speaking should be given priority over reading. first of all, speaking is a more practical skill than reading. in todays world, english is the language of international communication. therefore, it is important for chinese learners to be able to communicate in english. being able to speak english fluently will enable them to communicate with people from other countries, and this is a very practical skill. reading, on the other hand, is not as practical as speaking. secondly, speaking is a more interactive skill than reading. when speaking, learners can interact with others and get immediate feedback. this helps them to improve their speaking skills quickly. reading, on the other hand, is a solitary activity. learners can read books and articles, but they cannot get immediate feedback. this makes it harder for them to improve their reading skills. finally, speaking is a more enjoyable skill than reading. when speaking, learners can have conversations with others and express their thoughts and feelings. this is much more enjoyable than reading, which can be a dull and tedious activity. in conclusion, speaking should be given priority over reading for chinese learners. speaking is a more practical, interactive, and enjoyable skill than reading, and it is important for learners to be able to communicate in english. therefore, speaking should be given priority over reading.
