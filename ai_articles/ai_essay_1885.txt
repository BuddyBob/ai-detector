dear senator, i am writing to you today to express my strong support for changing our presidential election system to a popular vote. as you may know, currently we use the electoral college system, where each state is allocated electors based on their representation in congress. however, i believe this system is outdated and does not accurately reflect the will of the american people. one of the main issues with the electoral college is that it can result in a president being elected despite losing the nationwide popular vote. this has occurred a few times in our nations history, most recently in  and . according to federalist paper no. , the electoral college was meant to promote the choice of a president who receives the majority of support from the american public. yet in recent elections, the opposite has occurred. this undermines the principle of majority rule that is crucial to our democratic system. supporters of the electoral college argue that it preserves the federal character of our system and gives a voice to smaller states. however, directly electing the president by popular vote could still acknowledge every vote equally without depending on the statebystate outcome. it would not eliminate the role of states in elections. states would still determine how electoral votes are allocated and could opt to divide votes proportionally rather than using a winner take all method. additionally, evidence suggests that switching to a popular vote may increase voter turnout nationwide. more citizens may be inspired to vote if they feel their ballots have an equal chance of influencing the outcome rather than feeling like their states are already decided. this could have the effect of making our elections more representative of the entire population. in conclusion, i believe the time has come to update how we select the most important office in our country. direct nationwide election by popular vote is a fairer system that is more aligned with democratic principles of equality and majority rule. it deserves serious consideration as an alternative to the current electoral college method. i urge you to support any legislation proposing this change. thank you for your consideration. please let me know if you have any other questions.
