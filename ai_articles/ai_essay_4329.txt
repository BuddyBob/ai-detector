when people are not accepted for who they truly are, it has a devastating impact on interpersonal relationships and professional success. even though we may be able to connect with others superficially, without the ability to be our true selves, we cannot form meaningful relationships or grow in our career. my own experience shows that when i have hidden my true identity, my relationships have been superficial and i have been unable to reach my full potential in the workplace. similarly, ralph waldo emersons famous quote to be yourself in a world that is constantly trying to make you something else is the greatest accomplishment acknowledges the struggle many of us face when we are unable to be accepted for who we truly are. it is clear that authentic acceptance of our true selves is fundamental to healthy relationships and professional success.
