in recent decades, the global media landscape has experienced a rapid shift with the emergence of digital platforms. the advantages and disadvantages of this development for independent artistes have been heavily debated by proponents and opponents alike. this essay will explore how digital media is affecting independent music producers in terms of access to resources, audiences, autonomy and creativity. firstly, it is widely accepted that digital media offers unprecedented access to resources which are beneficial to independent artists. through these platforms they can easily create their music on an individual basis without having to invest in expensive equipment or rent a studio space. additionally, they also benefit from easier distribution channels modern streaming services allow them to reach large global audiences with relative ease compared to traditional methods such as vinyls or cds sales where limited capacity and cost implications were always restrictive factors for smaller acts. on the other hand there can be drawbacks associated with using digital mediums  particularly when considering autonomy over ones work. once content is uploaded online it can quickly become part of larger corporate entities through reposting, remixing etc., often resulting in loss of control over copyrights or even artistic integrity if not protected properly . this means that some creators may struggle financially due to inability to capitalise on their hard work especially true for those who lack representation from major labels or publishing companies who provide legal protection against such dilemmas more effectively than any singular artiste could manage themselves. moreover certain sound engineering processes like compression have become so standardised across
