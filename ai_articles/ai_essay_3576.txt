technology has become an integral part of our lives, more so in the past few decades, and has made many aspects of our lives simpler and more efficient. however, while technology can be incredibly beneficial, there are also several ways in which it can have a negative impact on us and our society. one of the main issues that comes with increased technology usage is the health implications it can bring. over the years, studies have linked prolonged exposure to screens with a range of health issues, including poor sleep, fatigue, eye strain, headaches, and more. prolonged screen time can also cause people to become more sedentary, leading to adverse effects on their physical health. another way technology can have a negative impact on people is through distraction. throughout the day, most of us are surrounded by notifications, alerts, and messages. this constant influx of information can be overwhelming and make it difficult to stay focused on a single task. finally, technology can also have a negative social impact. people are often so focused on their digital devices that they neglect facetoface interactions. this can lead to feelings of disconnect, loneliness, and isolation, especially among young people. in addition, the rise of digital media has had a detrimental effect on our selfesteem. with so many idealized images and videos online, its easy for people to become disillusioned and lose confidence in themselves. its important to remember that while technology has its benefits, it can also have serious drawbacks. its important to be mindful of our usage, take regular breaks away from digital screens, and maintain a healthy balance between tech and reallife interactions.
