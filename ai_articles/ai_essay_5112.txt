there is no doubt that reading and speaking are both important skills in english. however, i believe that speaking is more important for chinese learners. here are the reasons why. to begin with, speaking is more essential in daily life. no matter where we go, we need to communicate with others. for example, when we go shopping, we need to ask the price of the goods when we take a bus, we need to tell the driver our destination. if we cannot speak, we will have great difficulty in our daily life. in addition, speaking can help us improve our other skills in english. for instance, if we want to improve our listening, we can practice by talking to native speakers. if we want to improve our writing, we can practice by dictating our writing to native speakers. last but not least, speaking is more important than reading in terms of job opportunities. in todays globalized world, many jobs require employees to communicate with people from other countries. for example, a salesperson needs to make presentations to clients a customer service representative needs to handle phone calls from customers. if we cannot speak english fluently, we will have little chance of getting these jobs. in conclusion, speaking is more important than reading for chinese learners. speaking can help us in our daily life, improve our other skills, and get better job opportunities.
