the renowned essayist and poet ralph waldo emerson perhaps best summed up the importance of pushing ourselves beyond our boundaries with his quote unless you try to do something beyond what you have already done. this sentiment resonates deeply with many of us, as it draws attention to our potential for growth. there are three key reasons why this quote is so valid and inspiring. firstly, as we try something outside of our comfort zone, it allows us to discover new skills, knowledge and passions. for example, if someone has traditionally worked in finance, they might find that they enjoy and have an aptitude for sales or marketing. secondly, by expanding our repertoire of abilities, it opens up more opportunities for advancement or career changes, meaning our future prospects are increased. for instance, if someone has a passion for counselling, they may find that their experience in business could help them to secure the role. lastly, pushing ourselves enables us to build our selfreliance and selfbelief. taking on a challenge or exploring a new area can help us to trust in our abilities and be more confident in difficult situations. trying something beyond what we have already done can have a profound impact on our lives. not only can our careers be boosted, but we also gain a greater understanding of our capabilities and resources. going beyond our limits instils courage and resolve in us and helps us to move forward into our future with more purpose and resilience.
