medical research and techniques to provide the best care for their patients. secondly, the world is constantly changing, and individuals need to keep up with these changes to remain relevant. knowledge and skills it is also about personal growth and development. education helps individuals to develop critical thinking skills, creativity, and problemsolving abilities. these skills are
