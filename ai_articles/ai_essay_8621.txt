additionally, opting for vintage finds  either online or at thrift stores  allows you save money while shopping responsibly too. . furthermore, people should look into extending the lifecycle of their clothes through simple methods such as mending rips and tears instead throwing them away immediately when theyre no longer on trend or fashionable enough anymore  thereby reducing unnecessary waste further down the line. similarly washing clothes less frequently will keep them looking good while also saving energy used during production processes like dyeing and bleaching fabric fibers
