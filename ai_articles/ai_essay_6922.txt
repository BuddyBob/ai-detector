success is a subjective term that means different things to different people. however, one thing that is common among successful people is their willingness to try new things and take risks. i strongly agree with the statement that successful people try new things and take risks rather than only doing what they already know how to do well. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my opinion. firstly, trying new things and taking risks helps individuals to grow and learn. when we step out of our comfort zone and try something new, we are forced to learn new skills and acquire new knowledge. this not only helps us to become more knowledgeable but also makes us more adaptable and versatile. for instance, if a person who has always worked in the it industry decides to start a restaurant, they will have to learn about food, cooking, and customer service. this will broaden their horizons and make them more wellrounded individuals. secondly, taking risks can lead to great rewards. many successful people have achieved their success by taking calculated risks. for example, steve jobs, the cofounder of apple, took a risk by introducing the ipod, a product that was not in demand at the time. however, this risk paid off, and the ipod became a huge success, leading to the creation of the iphone and ipad. similarly, j. k. rowling, the author of the harry potter series, took a risk by writing a book that was initially rejected by several publishers. however, she persisted, and the book eventually became a bestseller, leading to the creation of a successful franchise. thirdly, trying new things and taking risks can lead to personal growth and fulfillment. when we challenge ourselves and achieve something that we never thought was possible, it gives us a sense of accomplishment and satisfaction. this, in turn, boosts our confidence and selfesteem. for example, if a person who has always been afraid of public speaking takes a risk and gives a speech, they may feel nervous at first, but once they have successfully delivered the speech, they will feel a sense of pride and accomplishment. in conclusion, trying new things and taking risks is an essential part of achieving success. it helps individuals to grow and learn, can lead to great rewards, and can lead to personal growth and fulfillment. therefore, i strongly agree with the statement that successful people try new things and take risks rather than only doing what they already know how to do well.
