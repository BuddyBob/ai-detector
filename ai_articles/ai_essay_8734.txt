differently than without music. music provides an outlet for expression that is often inaccessible through regular communication methods. from singing songs to banging on drums or playing instruments such as guitars and keyboards, musical activities help autistic individuals communicate better by allowing them to express themselves freely without relying solely on words or gestures. music helps facilitate social interaction between autistic individuals and those around them by providing an avenue of understanding others perspectives which may otherwise be difficult for an individual with asd to comprehend. furthermore, it allows autistic individuals to build upon strengths rather than focusing on weaknesses by using sound as a meaningful form of communication that does not rely heavily
