when it comes to computer games, opinions vary greatly on their effects on children. some people think that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual, and psychological development. others believe that the benefits of playing computer games outweigh the drawbacks. personally, i believe that computer games can have both positive and negative effects on children. some of the benefits of playing computer games include improved handeye coordination, problemsolving skills, strategic thinking, and teamwork. additionally, computer games can help children learn about other cultures and worlds. however, computer games can also have negative effects. for example, they can lead to obesity if children are playing them for hours on end. additionally, computer games can be addictive and lead to social isolation. in conclusion, i think that it is important for parents to monitor their childrens computer game playing. they should ensure that their children are getting enough exercise and are socializing with other children. additionally, parents should be aware of the negative effects that computer games can have so that they can take steps to prevent them.
