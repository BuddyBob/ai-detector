there are many reasons to support the idea that it is better to have broad knowledge of many academic subjects than to specialize in one specific subject. first, having a broad knowledge base helps individuals to better understand the world around them. specializing in one area can lead to a myopic view of the world, and an inability to see connections between different disciplines. second, individuals with a broad knowledge base are better able to adapt to change. those who specialize in one area may find it difficult to change their focus if their area of expertise becomes obsolete. finally, individuals with a broad knowledge base tend to be more wellrounded and interesting people. they are able to hold stimulating conversations on a variety of topics and can better relate to others. there are, of course, some advantages to specializing in one area. individuals who specialize in one area can become experts in their field and can contribute significantly to their field of study. however, the advantages of having a broad knowledge base far outweigh the advantages of specializing in one area.
