selfish or apathetic towards their communities. there are countless examples of young people who are actively engaged in volunteer work and social activism, often using their unique skills and sustainable farming. others are dedicated to improving access to education and healthcare for marginalized populations, volunteering at schools or clinics and raising awareness about social inequality. moreover, it is important to recognize that the concept of community service has evolved in recent years, with many young people finding creative and innovative ways to give back that go organizations that address specific community needs. in this sense, young people are demonstrating a willingness and ability to contribute to their communities in ways that are meaningful and
