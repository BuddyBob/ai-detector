there are a number of reasons why i believe that young people enjoy life more than older people do. for one, younger people are generally more physically active than older people, and thus have more energy to enjoy life. they can also be more easily amused than older people, since they have not yet experienced as much of life and thus have not yet become jaded. in addition, younger people often have more social energy than older people, as they are still in the process of making friends and exploring romantic relationships. of course, there are exceptions to every rule. some older people are more physically active than some younger people, and some younger people are more jaded than some older people. however, on the whole, i believe that young people enjoy life more than older people do.
