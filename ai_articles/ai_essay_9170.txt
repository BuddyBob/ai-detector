the development of renewable energy sources has been a topic debated among many for years. with the growing concern over climate change and its effects, the importance of finding alternative energy sources to reduce our reliance on fossil fuels is undeniable. although much progress has been made in recent years to expand the use of renewable energy, a more concerted effort must be taken if we are to truly reverse the effects of global warming. renewable energy comes from natural resources that can be replenished naturally such as sunlight, wind, rain, tides and geothermal heat. these sources can provide us with clean and efficient electricity without releasing any harmful carbon dioxide emissions into our atmosphere as do traditional fossil fuelbased power plants. in addition to reducing co emissions thereby helping reduce climate change, utilizing renewable energies also helps preserve natures finite resources by eliminating dependence on coal or oil reserves which will eventually run out anyway!. one major obstacle preventing greater penetration of green energies into everyday life is cost currently renewable energy remains significantly more expensive than traditional forms like coal or natural gas because solar panels and other components required are still quite costly compared to those used in conventional technologies. there have however been some government initiatives aimed at addressing this problem by providing subsidies for customers installing these systems in their homes or businesses programs such as net metering also exist where utilities pay people back for excess electricity they generate using renewables so that it can then be sold back onto the gridallowing savings even beyond what was previously thought possible!. in order to increase public awareness about green technologies and incentivize further investmentadoption legislation should focus not only on subsidizing products but also educating consumers about why certain choices such as investing in renewables make long term economic sense too  especially when you factor environmental benefits included within them! ultimately though governments neednt bear full responsibility alone either companies themselves must take part through corporate social responsibility campaigns that showcase their commitment towards creating sustainable business models going forwards  pushing innovation
