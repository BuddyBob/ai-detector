most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this statement for a few reasons. for one, many advertisements use models or actors to sell their products. they are usually young, attractive, and in great shape. they make the products look good by using them in a way that makes them look appealing. for example, a model using a new type of toothpaste may be shown brushing her teeth in a way that makes the toothpaste look like it will make your teeth whiter. however, the model may have had her teeth professionally whitened before the advertisement was filmed. in reality, the toothpaste may not make your teeth as white as the models. another reason why advertisements make products seem better than they really are is because they often use editing techniques to make the products look better. for example, an advertisement for a new type of shampoo may show the shampoo making the persons hair look shiny and soft. however, the shampoo may actually make the persons hair look greasy and oily. lastly, many advertisements use persuasive language to make products seem better than they really are. they may use words like amazing, revolutionary, or miraculous to describe the product. they may also use exaggerated claims, such as this shampoo will make your hair grow  times faster. in reality, the shampoo may not make your hair grow any faster. advertisements can be misleading. they often use models, editing techniques, and persuasive language to make products seem better than they really are.
