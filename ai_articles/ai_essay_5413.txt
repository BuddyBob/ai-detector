the overuse of electronic dictionaries edictionaries will have both advantages and disadvantages for english learning. there are strong arguments both for and against the overuse of edictionaries. on the one hand, some people argue that the overuse of edictionaries can be beneficial for english learning. firstly, edictionaries are efficient and convenient. students can access them anytime and anywhere, making english learning much more convenient. for example, instead of carrying around bulky and heavy paper dictionaries, students can easily look up the words and their definitions with an edictionary. secondly, edictionaries contain more information than paper dictionaries, such as example sentences and audio recordings. students can learn not only the meaning of the words but also how to pronounce them and use them in contexts. on the other hand, there is some evidence to suggest that the overuse of edictionaries can be detrimental to english learning. for example, it can lead to a deterioration of students spelling skill. since they can easily look up the correct spelling, they may become reliant on edictionaries and forget to spell words correctly. furthermore, the overuse of edictionaries can also lead to students not being able to remember the words they have looked up. without the context of use, it is easy for students to forget the words, leaving them with an empty understanding. in conclusion, the overuse of edictionaries can both benefit and harm english learning. it is important to use edictionaries as an aid and not a crutch in english learning. students should not rely solely on edictionaries, but should also utilize other learning techniques such as memorizing and context building. in this way, they can maximize the advantages of the edictionaries while minimizing the disadvantages.
