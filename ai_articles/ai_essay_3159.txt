research has shown that playing sports can have numerous educational and social benefits to individuals and teams. studies have shown that when children are regularly playing sports, their academic performance tends to improve as well. it has been theorized that taking part in physical activity leads to better concentration and a higher ability to focus on studies. moreover, playing sports can lead to increased selfesteem and better communication and teamwork with peers, as sports activities bring together different people to collaborate to reach the same goal. teamwork is a key concept in competitive sports, as it provides support for players while enabling them to learn how to cooperate and make decisions together. through teamwork, sports teams are able to bond and build trust, allowing them to work together more effectively on and off the field. statistics have proven that teams that emphasize team work tend to have greater success. for example, the  super bowl champions, the philadelphia eagles, practiced rigorous team building activities in order to enhance their performance on the field. these activities, such as developing a team motto, wearing matching uniforms and motivating each other with teamwork slogans, arguably contributed to the teams success. similarly, olympic sprinter usain bolt attributed much of his success and multiple gold medal wins to his strong team work approach.
