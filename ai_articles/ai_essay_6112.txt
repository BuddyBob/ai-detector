i generally agree that advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. one reason for this is that ads are designed to create a desired reaction in the viewer. for example, an ad for a car might feature a beautiful sunset, making the viewer want to buy a car to take advantage of the view. ads often neglect to mention the downsides of the product, such as the high price tag or the difficulty of maintaining the car. another reason ads make products seem better is that they often feature celebrities or other wellknown people. this makes the product seem more prestigious and desirable, which can lead people to buy it even if they may not actually need it. for example, ads for expensive cars might feature celebrities like george clooney or brad pitt, making the car seem like a musthave item. finally, ads often use catchy slogans to make the product seem more appealing. these slogans may be misleading, as they may not accurately reflect the features of the product. for example, the slogan got milk? weve got cheese! may make the viewer think that the product has cheese in it, when in fact it may just have milk.
