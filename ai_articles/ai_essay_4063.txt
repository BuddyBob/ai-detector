selfreliance is an important concept that helps define success. being selfreliant means having the ability to become selfsufficient and providing for ones own needs without relying on others. in order to become more selfreliant, individuals can focus on learning new skills, developing budgeting habits, and becoming more independent. many famous people have used selfreliance to become successful, such as oprah winfrey and elon musk. not putting an importance on selfreliance can have unfavorable consequences such as wasting money, engaging in poor behavior, and general feelings of helplessness. fortunately, investing in selfreliance can have positive results, such as developing confidence and skills that attract success and opportunities in life.
