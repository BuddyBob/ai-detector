the internet has revolutionized the way we communicate and interact with one another, creating a global platform for ideas to be shared by everyone from every corner of the world. however, it has also brought about some unintended consequences cyberbullying is one such unfortunate reality that many people have had to face due in part to the anonymity and reach the internet provides. cyberbullying consists of using technology and social media platforms as a means of deliberately intimidating or harassing others online by sending threatening messages or posting embarrassing content without their consent. though cyberbullying can happen anywhere onlinein chat rooms, on forums, over emailsocial media is often used as a weapon against those who are deemed vulnerable targets. sadly, teens are most commonly targeted because they are still developing emotionally and tend to be more exposed in terms of their digital presence than adults may be aware of. it can lead to serious issues such as anxiety, depression, low selfesteem and even suicidal thoughts if left unchecked. as concerned parents or mentors it is important that we take proactive steps in order to protect our children from these malicious acts of cyberbullying by first educating them about its dangers so that they know what constitutes an abusive message versus playful banter between friends online  its important for them to understand when certain language crosses into unacceptable territories like racism or sexism which should never be tolerated regardless of context. additionally monitoring your childs social media accounts with permission helps ensure they remain safe while also allowing you detect any instances where they become victims themselves  this includes both direct messages received from other users but also comments made on postspictures uploaded onto their account which could potentially create an antagonistic environment for them among peers e.g., making fun out someones appearance.
