the emergence of the internet has irrevocably changed the way we consume, share and create media. from streaming services to ebooks to interactive digital art, its clear that our modern landscape is one driven by digital content. this essay will explore how this transformation impacts independent publishing and artwork in particular. independent publishers have seen much success due to the growth of online platforms such as kindle direct publishing, which allow them to reach larger audiences with relative ease while keeping their creative control intact. with minimal investment required beyond their original work and some basic formatting knowledge, authors can upload manuscripts directly onto popular websites like amazon or smashwords without ever needing a publishers help. this not only gives creatives more freedom over their work but also helps eliminate any gatekeepers who may be biased against certain genres or styles of writing. as a result, selfpublished works now represent a growing portion of available literature according to recent statistics from bowker market research llc., more than  million titles are estimated to have been released in  alone!. digital artwork has
