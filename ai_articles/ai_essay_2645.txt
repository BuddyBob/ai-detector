my summer project revolves around discovering ways to treat an open wound without the use of pharmaceuticals or medical treatments. i believe that by researching natural remedies, such as utilizing the healing properties found in certain plants, we can reduce our reliance on antibiotics. this project could be used in everyday life. for example, a family member or friend that has suffered an open wound could treat it using the natural remedies researched in this project. the remedies could also be useful in developing nations, where medical treatments and pharmaceuticals are often difficult to come by. in addition, the natural remedies could be useful in a natural disaster situation, when access to medical care may be limited. overall, this project is about finding safe and noninvasive treatments for open wounds and reducing our reliance on antibiotics.
