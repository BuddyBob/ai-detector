dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the united states should change from the current electoral college system to instead elect the president by a popular vote of the american people. while the electoral college was established by the founders with the aim of giving less populous states more influence, there are good reasons to move to a popular vote system. one argument against keeping the electoral college is that it can result in a president being elected despite losing the nationwide popular vote. this has occurred a few times in history, most recently in  and . some argue this is undemocratic and undermines the principle of one person, one vote. supporters counter that the system was designed to give smaller states more influence, but in todays highly polarized political climate, i believe a popular vote would make the process feel more fair and democratic to more americans. a second argument is that the current winnertakeall approach used by most states encourages negative campaigning targeting only a handful of swing states. candidates have less incentive to address issues important to voters in noncompetitive states. a national popular vote may encourage candidates to campaign on issues that appeal to a broader range of citizens across many states. it could lead to campaigns discussing pressing challenges facing our entire nation. those in favor of keeping the electoral college point out that it has worked reasonably well for over  years. however, as the population has increasingly congregated in major cities and metropolitan areas, the system does not evenly weigh the interests of rural and urban voters. a popular vote could help realign incentives to value all americans more equally. in conclusion, while the electoral college system aimed to give smaller states influence, i believe a popular vote aligns better with democratic principles and could encourage candidates to discuss issues relevant to more of the nation as a whole. for these reasons, i ask that you support changing to a national popular vote for electing the president. thank you for your consideration. sincerely, your name
