true selfesteem comes from achievement  the knowledge that one has changed the world in some tangible way, even if only in a small corner of it. while lofty goals are laudable, even smaller accomplishments can be empowering and show a person the value of their contribution. building a strong work ethic, taking on responsibility, and working towards goals can all contribute to the development of selfesteem. praising great students in the learning environment can help foster a positive learning environment and encourage others to reach for their own goals. praising students for their accomplishments creates a positive feedback loop, creating an outlook that emphasizes effort and hard work rather than perfectionism. it can help students take pride in their work and contribute to the larger culture of achievement. by recognizing great student work, teachers can provide a source of inspiration to the students they are teaching. in conclusion, the source of true selfesteem is achieved through tangible accomplishments that recognize and honor a persons hard work. praising great students can create a healthy, motivational environment that encourages others to reach for their goals. by celebrating small triumphs and successes, all can appreciate the value of their work and develop greater selfesteem.
