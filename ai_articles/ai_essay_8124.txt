the debate over which english skill is more important for chinese learners is a longstanding one. while some people believe that reading should be given priority, others argue that speaking should be the focus. in my opinion, speaking should be the priority for chinese learners. first of all, speaking is essential for communication. in todays globalized world, english is the language of international communication. as a result, chinese learners need to be able to communicate effectively in english in order to interact with people from other countries. without a good command of spoken english, it is difficult to engage in meaningful conversations and build relationships. second, speaking is the best way to learn english. by speaking, learners can practice their pronunciation, intonation, and grammar. they can also learn new words and expressions through conversations. furthermore, speaking helps learners to think in english and develop their fluency. in contrast, reading can only help learners to acquire knowledge passively, without giving them the opportunity to practice their speaking skills. finally, speaking is the most enjoyable way to learn english. it is much more interesting to talk to someone than to read a book. when learners are engaged in conversations, they are more motivated to learn and more likely to remember what they have learned. in conclusion, speaking should be the priority for chinese learners. it is essential for communication, the best way to learn english, and the most enjoyable way to learn english. therefore, chinese learners should focus on speaking in order to improve their english proficiency.
