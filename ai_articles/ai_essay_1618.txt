important issue that deserves careful consideration. the electoral college system has some benefits. it encourages candidates to build wider geographic coalitions and avoid focusing only on highly populated areas. this helps represent the diverse interests of our large nation. however, it can result in a president being elected despite losing the nationwide popular vote. this has happened a few less susceptible than the electoral college to recount debates or chances of a candidate winning despite losing nationwide support. after considering both sides of this issue, i believe changing to a
