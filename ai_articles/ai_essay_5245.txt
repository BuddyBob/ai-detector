nowadays, work is definitely more stressful than it was in the past. there are a few reasons for this. first of all, the economy is much more unstable now than it was in the past. this means that people are worried about losing their jobs, and they are working harder to try to keep their jobs. secondly, there are more demands on peoples time. with the advent of the internet and email, people are expected to be available  hours a day,  days a week. this means that people have less time to relax and enjoy their leisure time. finally, the pace of work has increased. with the pressure to do more with less, people are working faster and harder than ever before. this can lead to a lot of stress. so, yes, work is more stressful than it was in the past. this can be a problem for people, as stress can lead to health problems. it is important for people to find ways to manage their stress, so that they can stay healthy and happy.
