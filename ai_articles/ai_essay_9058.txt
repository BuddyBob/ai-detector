as the world increasingly becomes more digital, it is important to consider the implications that this may have on our environment. we are becoming ever more reliant on technology and as we move away from paperbased products into a fully digitized landscape, there could be significant consequences for our planet. it is now possible to complete many of our everyday activities without having any direct interaction with nature whatsoever. from shopping online to streaming movies, playing video games and working digitally  much of what humans used to do in person or using physical material can now be done with far less energy expenditure than before. this means that natural resources like trees are no longer needed at such great levels in order to sustain human activity however, while this offers advantages such as convenience and cost savings, it also has potential drawbacks when considering its long term effects on the environment. the decrease in demand for things like paper products will lead to fewer trees being cut down which can help preserve forests around the world and reduce air pollution caused by deforestation but if too few resources are devoted towards environmental sustainability then entire species may become endangered due to an altered habitat. the increase in electronic waste ewaste can cause issues too ewaste contains pollutants that are toxic not only for humans but plants and animals alike so proper disposal methods must be implemented if these materials were used instead of their nondigital counterparts. in addition, power sources need to remain sustainable  renewable energy sources should replace fossil fuels wherever possible in order for us continue living comfortably while leaving minimal impact upon our planets ecosystems. overall, it is clear that digitalization offers numerous benefits yet caution needs to taken when adopting new technologies en masse since lack of foresight concerning environmental concerns could result in disastrous results down the line therefore increased awareness about how technological advancements affect nature should become widespread amongst all individuals who value a healthy relationship between mankind and mother earth
