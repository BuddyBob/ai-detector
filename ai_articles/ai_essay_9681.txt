due to no longer having physical contact and interaction with colleagues on a daily basis. this could potentially contribute to mental health problems such as depression or anxiety if left unaddressed for too long, which is why employers should make sure their staff are aware of potential issues related to loneliness before introducing a fully remote setup for their employees. to combat this problem, employers should look into offering support services for those struggling mentally while adjusting to this new way of life. additionally, regular checkins between team members via video conferencing software like zoom should encourage social interaction and help build better relationships among coworkers even when physically apart. another risk posed by remote work is that it makes
