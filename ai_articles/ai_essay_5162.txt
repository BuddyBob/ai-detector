some people might disagree with the statement that education is expensive. after all, one can argue that an education is an investment, and that the benefits of an education, such as a better job and a higher salary, outweigh the costs. however, i believe that the statement is true, for a few reasons. first, an education is expensive in terms of both time and money. in order to get a good education, one has to spend years in school and then, if one wants to pursue a higher degree, spend even more time and money. second, an education is expensive in terms of opportunity costs. for example, while one is in school, he or she is not working and is therefore not making money. furthermore, the cost of education often prevents people from pursuing certain careers. for example, a person might want to become a doctor, but the cost of medical school might prevent him or her from doing so. finally, an education is expensive in terms of the potential consequences of a failure to educate oneself. a failure to educate oneself can lead to a number of negative consequences, such as unemployment, poverty, and even imprisonment. in conclusion, i believe that education is expensive. however, the consequences of a failure to educate oneself are even more expensive, and this is why i believe that an education is worth the cost.
