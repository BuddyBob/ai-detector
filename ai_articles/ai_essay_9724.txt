technology has forever changed the way we communicate. the use of emails, text messages, and social media platforms have made it easier than ever to keep in touch with friends and family from around the world. but what effect is this constant connectivity having on our relationships?. the instant nature of modern communication means that relationships are more easily cultivated, but also less meaningful. with conversations limited to  characters or a brief email exchange, it can be difficult to build any real depth into a friendship or relationship. as such, many people find themselves invested in superficial friendships which lack commitment due to the convenience and immediacy offered by technologydriven communication tools. on the other hand, technology can provide an invaluable opportunity for those who would otherwise struggle with facetoface conversation due to shyness or fear of rejection. for these individuals, connecting through digital devices provides a much needed lifeline allowing them to reach out without having to confront their anxieties head on. whats more  these connections may ultimately lead some people towards forming deeper relationships as they become more confident communicating in person. it could be argued then that while technology has undoubtedly had an impact upon how we interact socially its effects should not necessarily be viewed as wholly negative nor positive  instead they must simply be acknowledged. those looking for meaningful connections must still make an effort beyond sending off emails and tweets if they want strengthen their bond with others  relying solely upon digital forms of contact will only take you so far before you hit a dead end street!
