the environmental impact of fast fashion has been a hot topic in the news lately. fast fashion refers to cheaply produced clothing that is designed to quickly mimic current trends, and while it can be tempting due to its affordability, it often comes with an enormous cost. not only do fast fashion brands overrun our closets with too many items and add to the everincreasing pile of waste created by the textile industry, but their production also contributes significantly to global climate change as well. at first glance, fast fashion may appear to be a great option for those on tight budgets or who want fashionable clothes without breaking the bank however, this type of clothing is less about quality and more about quantity at a low price point  meaning these garments are made from materials that are not very durable or sustainable. in addition, because these garments are made so quickly they tend not to last long before needing replacing  making them even more wasteful than traditional clothes which could easily last much longer if taken care of properly. unfortunately, when we buy into fast fashions disposable mindset we contribute heavily towards pollution and climate change since most of these fabrics come from nonrenewable sources like petroleumbased synthetic fibers such as polyester or acrylic which are widely used. moreover, many dyes used in manufacturing require large amounts of energy and water consumption resulting in toxins being released into our rivers and streams poisoning wildlife habitats along with local resources needed for human consumption such as drinking water supplies. furthermore producing cheap clothes takes away jobs from individuals living in poorer countries where labor exploitation is rampant since manufacturers must find ways to cut costs while still meeting demand  most commonly taking advantage of workers who lack safety precautions or access to fair wages thus leading us further down an unsustainable path . its essential that consumers make better informed choices when deciding what kind of clothing they purchase opting for higher quality products mabe using organic cotton over cheaper synthetics will benefit both people involved throughout all stages if its production
