distance learning provides students with many benefits compared to traditional classes. one key advantage is that students can attend classes on their own time. with distance learning, no matter where a student is or when they are available, they can attend and complete their courses. this makes it easier for students who for various reasons cannot attend physical classes on time, or who are unable to attend every day. also, when a student opts for distance learning, they can attend classes from anywhere. no longer do students have to be cooped up in a traditional classroom now they can choose to study from the comfort of their own home, or even from a cafe or library in their hometown. this makes learning more convenient and accessible to a variety of students.
