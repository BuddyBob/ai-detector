there are two schools of thought when it comes to teaching children how to interact with others teaching them to compete or teaching them to cooperate. i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. one reason children should be taught to cooperate is that it is essential for their development. cooperation helps children learn how to get along with others, to share, and to problem solve. these skills are important in all aspects of life, from school to work. another reason children should be taught to cooperate is that it can help them in their future careers. cooperation is a key component of teamwork, and teamwork is essential in many fields. for example, a doctor cannot perform surgery alone he or she needs a team of nurses and surgeons to help. a musician cannot create a masterpiece without a team of composers, arrangers, and performers. and a business cannot succeed without a team of employees who are able to cooperate. finally, i believe that teaching children to cooperate is the right thing to do. we should be teaching our children to work together to solve problems, rather than teaching them to compete against each other. when children learn to cooperate, they are more likely to be successful in life.
