in todays society, many people are inclined towards seeking luxuries and conveniences that make their lives easier. while this may seem harmless, it can actually hinder the development of strong and independent individuals. i strongly agree with this statement, and in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my view. firstly, the availability of luxuries and conveniences leads to a lack of resilience and resourcefulness in people. when people are used to having everything at their fingertips, they become dependent on these things and forget how to solve problems on their own. for instance, in the past, people had to rely on physical maps and compasses to find their way around, but nowadays, gps has made navigation much simpler. while this technology is undoubtedly convenient, it has led to a decline in peoples ability to read maps and navigate without assistance. this has resulted in people becoming more reliant on technology, which can be problematic if the technology fails or is unavailable. secondly, the pursuit of luxuries and conveniences often leads to a lack of appreciation for the simple things in life. for example, people who are used to eating at fancy restaurants may overlook the value of cooking a meal from scratch or going for a picnic in the park. this can lead to a lack of enjoyment in everyday life and a tendency to seek out more and more extravagant experiences. people who are always seeking out new and exciting experiences may become jaded and never satisfied with what they have, which can ultimately lead to unhappiness and dissatisfaction. thirdly, luxuries and conveniences can create a sense of entitlement and privilege in people. when people are used to having everything they want, they may become arrogant and entitled, expecting others to cater to their every whim. this can lead to a lack of empathy and understanding towards those who may not have the same privileges. for instance, people who have always had access to highquality healthcare may not understand the struggles of those who do not have access to basic medical care. this lack of empathy can ultimately lead to a society that is divided and lacking in compassion. in conclusion, while the luxuries and conveniences of contemporary life may seem harmless, they can actually prevent people from becoming strong and independent individuals. by relying too heavily on technology and convenience, people may lose their ability to solve problems and appreciate the simple things in life. additionally, the pursuit of luxuries can create a sense of entitlement and privilege, which can lead to a lack of empathy and understanding towards others. therefore, it is important for individuals to remember the value of hard work, resourcefulness, and empathy, even in a world that is increasingly focused on convenience and luxury.
