when education is mentioned, we can easily think of schools, universities and young people. however, there are many other aspects of life that can be considered as education. for example, if you want to become a doctor, you need to go to medical school. if you are interested in a career in business, then you have to study for a business degree. the list goes on and on. so, what is education? it is the process of acquiring knowledge and skills that will help you achieve your goals in life. in other words, it is about acquiring skills and knowledge that you will use in the future. this is what we call education, and this is how we define it in this article.
