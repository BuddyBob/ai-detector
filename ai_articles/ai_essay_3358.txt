raising responsible and respectful elementary school children is an important lesson that should be taught in every classroom. respect can come in many forms whether its respect for elders, peers, teachers and most importantly yourself. learning should be embraced in everything a student does and curiosity should be nurtured. learning allows for a students knowledge to expand beyond the school curriculum to help them make informed decisions in their everyday lives. lastly, communication is a key factor which not only helps build relationships with peers but with adults as well. communication aids in understanding, trust and cultural awareness. it is important for children to understand the importance of respecting self and others, being a lifelong learner and being able to effectively communicate.
