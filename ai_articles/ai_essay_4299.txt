working on school work alone or with a group can be beneficial. working alone can provide a high level of concentration since you are not being distracted by others. it can also help foster independence, as it encourages the student to take responsibility for their own studies and investments in learning. on the other hand, working in a group can also provide many benefits. groups can bring together multiple diverse perspectives and ideas that can create a richer understanding of a particular topic. additionally, working in a group can provide social support and motivation, encouraging the student to be an active participant in the learning process. each option has its advantages and disadvantages, but ultimately, successful school work can often depend on the individual student and their method of studying best.
