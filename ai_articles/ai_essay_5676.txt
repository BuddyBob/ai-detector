nowadays, electronic cards have become increasingly popular for holiday greetings. however, some people are of the opinion that paper cards are still more preferable than electronic cards. in my opinion, i agree with this opinion since paper cards have a more personalized feel and can be used differently than electronic cards. to begin with, paper cards have a more personalized feel than electronic cards. this is because paper cards can be carefully crafted and handwritten with messages that make them even more endearing and heartfelt. one can also customize them in different ways by adding the photos or drawings. for instance, for a special occasion like a wedding, one could get special cards with photos of the couple printed on them. this simply cannot be achieved with electronic cards. furthermore, paper cards can be touched and feel like a physical representation of the persons feelings and emotions. in addition, paper cards can be used differently than electronic cards. for example, paper cards can be framed and displayed in the home as a sign of remembrance. or they can be kept in a special box and brought out to be looked at and read each year. this is something that electronic cards cannot do. they cannot be framed and displayed in the home, nor can they be kept in a special box. in conclusion, paper cards have a more personalized feel and can be used differently than electronic cards. they also serve as a reminder of the bond between two people and can be cherished for years to come. however, electronic cards are more convenient and costeffective and can be sent to multiple people at once, making them ideal for those who are short on time. thus, both paper cards and electronic cards have their own benefits and it is up to the individual to decide which one to use.
