the consequences of a failure to educate are both financial and social. on the financial side, a lack of education can lead to decreased earnings and decreased opportunities. for example, someone who does not have a college degree may be unable to find a job that matches their skills and may have to take lowerpaying jobs. additionally, a lack of education can lead to a decreased quality of life, as people who do not have a college degree may not be able to find good jobs or may not be able to afford to live in desirable areas. on the social side, a lack of education can lead to social isolation. people who do not have a college degree may not be able to find friends who share their interests or who have similar levels of education. additionally, people who do not have a college degree may not be able to find jobs that require a college degree, which can lead to social isolation. overall, a lack of education has both financial and social consequences. these consequences are especially pronounced in an increasingly globalized world, where a lack of education can lead to a decreased ability to compete in the global economy.
