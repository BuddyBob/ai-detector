in recent years, the rise of internet platforms has had a great impact on the development of individual character. the ubiquity of social media, particularly, has had a profound effect on how people interact and express themselves. this, in turn, can lead to social pressure for people to act a certain way in order to conform to ideals and expectations of others. this pressure can have both positive and negative influences on a persons character, depending on how they choose to respond to it. for example, a person might choose to yield to the pressure and become more outgoing in an effort to present a certain image of themselves, or they could reject the pressure and remain true to their own values and beliefs. in either case, it is important to remember that the development of character relies heavily on a persons own decisions and actions, not on the influence of internet platforms.
