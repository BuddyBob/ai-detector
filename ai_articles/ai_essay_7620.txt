originality is a term that is often associated with creativity and uniqueness. however, many people believe that originality does not necessarily mean thinking of something that has never been thought before. instead, it means putting old ideas together in new and innovative ways. while some may disagree with this notion, i believe that there is truth in this perspective. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my opinion. to begin with, it is important to understand that originality does not exist in a vacuum. all ideas are built upon previous concepts, experiences, and knowledge. therefore, it is impossible to come up with a completely new idea that has no basis in anything that has come before it. however, what sets groundbreaking ideas apart is the way in which they combine existing ideas in a way that has not been done before. for instance, steve jobs, the founder of apple, did not invent the smartphone or the tablet. however, he took existing technologies and combined them in a way that revolutionized the tech industry. by bringing together a touch screen interface, a music player, and a phone, jobs created the iphone, which changed the way we communicate and consume media. similarly, he combined a tablet computer with a touch screen and stylus to create the ipad, which transformed the way we work and access information. furthermore, originality is often about finding new applications for existing ideas. for example, the concept of ridesharing existed before uber and lyft came onto the scene. however, these companies revolutionized the transportation industry by using technology to connect drivers and riders in realtime. they also came up with new pricing models and customer experiences that had not been seen before. as a result, they disrupted the traditional taxi industry and created a new market that has since been adopted by other industries, such as food delivery and grocery shopping. another example of originality through the combination of existing ideas can be seen in the field of music. many popular songs use chord progressions and melodies that have been used before. however, what sets these songs apart is the way that the artist combines these elements with their own unique style and lyrics. for instance, the band coldplay is known for using simple chord progressions and lyrics that are often criticized for being cliché. however, their songs are beloved by millions of fans because of the emotional resonance they create through the combination of these elements with the bands unique sound and aesthetic. in conclusion, i believe that originality does not necessarily mean thinking of something that has never been thought before. instead, it means taking existing ideas and finding new and innovative ways to combine them. this is evident in many of the groundbreaking inventions and ideas that have changed the world. by understanding that all ideas are built upon previous knowledge and experiences, we can embrace the power of combining old ideas in new ways to create something truly original.
