seeking multiple opinions when making an important choice or decision can help one make a better, more wellinformed choice for several key reasons different perspectives broaden ones thinking. when talking to only one person, you are likely to hear their perspective and way of thinking about the issue. however, people come from different backgrounds and life experiences that shape their views. talking to more than one person exposes you to a wider range of perspectives. this helps ensure you consider aspects of the choice you may not have thought of otherwise. you can gain insights by learning how others with differing views look at the decision. getting confirmation or dissent of opinions helps validate your own initial leanings. if the first person you talk to encourages option a but the second person argues strongly for option b, this signals the choice may not be as clearcut as you initially believed. getting consistent or varying views helps highlight where you need to examine the choice more carefully. conversely, if multiple people point out the similar advantages of one option over others, it can strengthen your confidence in leaning that way. practical strengths and weakness of each option become clearer. when asking for advice, people often focus on just the pros of the option they are pushing you towards. getting multiple opinions helps paint a fuller picture of both the practical benefits and downsides of each potential choice. this allows for a more wellrounded understanding needed to determine the best fit for your individual circumstances. ultimately, important decisions rarely have one single right answer and are often quite complex with many factors to weigh. discussing a choice with more than just one individual expands the knowledge and perspectives brought to bear on making the decision. this strengthening of the information base makes it more likely one can determine the optimal choice, as opposed to a potentially suboptimal decision made with only limited input and viewpoints considered. seeking the counsel of a few trusted individuals improves the quality and thoughtfulness of the final choice made.
