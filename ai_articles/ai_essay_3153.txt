it is often said that actions speak louder than words  and indeed, setting a good example through ones own behavior and choices can be an effective way to influence other people. demonstrating the desired behavior or attitude can show others what is valued and expected, and provides them with a template for following that example. however, there may be more impactful and direct ways to influence those around us. speaking openly and persuasively on the issue is one key way to influence others. by engaging in discussion or debate, we can both present evidence for a particular point of view and invite counterarguments. doing so can help to spark ideas and challenge perspectives in a way that simply modeling the desired behavior may not. furthermore, addressing a wide variety of individuals allows a message to reach diverse audiences, and to be tailored in a way that increases its persuasive power. in some cases, a combination of behavior and discussion may be the most effective method of influencing others. by providing an example to follow and discussing why it is being pursued, it is possible to shape the thoughts and beliefs of those around us. ultimately, the best approach may arise from an understanding of the individuals needs and preferences. while setting a good example is one reliable method, there may be better ways to influence other people depending on the situation.
