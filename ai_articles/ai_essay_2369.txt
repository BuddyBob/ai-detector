selfreliance is an important tool for making decisions and feeling secure in them. when we rely on ourselves for decision making, we take full ownership of the choices we make. this act of selfdetermination helps us feel confident in the decisions we make, no matter what the outcome. additionally, selfreliance encourages us to prioritize what matters most in life, so we can focus our time and effort on what really matters. by allowing ourselves to take responsibility over our lives, we can better identify what is truly meaningful and valuable in life, and make decisions that align with our core values.
