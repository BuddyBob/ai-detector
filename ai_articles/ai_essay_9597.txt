the importance of selfcare in college is often overlooked. for many students, the transition to college can be difficult due to its inherent demands. in order to have a successful academic and social experience while studying, it is essential that they are able to practice healthy habits and make time for themselves. this includes taking care of ones mental and physical health by engaging in activities such as regular exercise, getting enough sleep every night, eating nutritious meals, limiting consumption of alcohol and other substances, having fun with friends while still setting boundaries on partying too much or too late at night. selfcare also involves learning how to manage stress effectively this may include talking through problems with friends or family members when feeling overwhelmed instead of bottling up emotions which could lead to further distress later on down the line. above all else however, selfcare requires individuals take some me time  something that many college students tend forget about especially during finals season when theres pressure from professors for good grades. everyone has different ways of relaxing whether its listening music or podcasts, reading a book for pleasure not just textbooks!, playing video games or boardgames with your roommates, going out into nature with appropriate measures taken regarding covid restrictions  any activity which helps you unwind should be incorporated into your weekly routine! taking breaks throughout the day will help keep focus levels high so you dont burn out before reaching completion point on whatever task you need to do next such as writing an essay or studying for an upcoming testexam. in conclusion therefore prioritising selfcare whilst in university does not necessarily mean neglecting studies but rather finding balance between workstudy life alongside leisure activities so that both areas are given attention accordingly  allowing everything else outside these two categories such as relationships  family ties stay strong too!
