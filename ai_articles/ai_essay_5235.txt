there is certainly no lack of opinions on whether work is more or less stressful than in the past. however, it is difficult to make a valid comparison without considering the many variables that could affect such a claim. for one, work habits and expectations have changed dramatically in recent decades. in the past, employees were typically expected to put in long hours on the job and work weekends if needed. today, with the rise of the knowledge economy, workers are typically expected to be available during regular business hours, and to be able to toggle easily between tasks. moreover, technology has created new sources of stress in the workplace. the constant barrage of emails, text messages, and social media notifications can be overwhelming and distracting. and the pressure to constantly be on can lead to burnout. finally, the job market is much more competitive now than it was in the past. this can lead to stress and anxiety about keeping ones job or finding a new one. all of these factors together create a far more stressful work environment than in the past. of course, there are some people who enjoy the challenge and stimulation of a more hectic work life. but for many, the stress is simply too much.
