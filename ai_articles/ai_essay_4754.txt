i believe that children should be taught to cooperate, rather than compete. there are many reasons for this belief. first, cooperation is more likely to lead to success than competition. studies show that competition almost always leads to loser syndrome, in which individuals lose focus, give up, and become negative in their attitudes and behavior. in contrast, cooperation leads to synergy, or the enhancement of the abilities of those involved. second, competition can be harmful to the development of selfconfidence and selfesteem. when children are often forced to compete against others, they may begin to feel inferior and insecure. this can have a negative impact on their overall wellbeing. third, competition often leads to bitterness and hostility. when one person wins and the others lose, it can be difficult to maintain friendships. in fact, some studies have even shown that competition can lead to violence. in conclusion, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate, rather than compete. this is because cooperation leads to success, is more likely to lead to positive attitudes and feelings, is beneficial to the development of selfconfidence and selfesteem, and is less likely to lead to violence.
