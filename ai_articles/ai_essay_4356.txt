the life of michelangelo is an example of how setting your aim too high and falling short can be a greater danger than setting it too low and achieving your mark. while michelangelo was never without ambition, he shifted his expectations and expectations of others depending on the task at hand. he once said, the greater danger for most of us lies in setting our aim too low, and achieving our mark. this quote reflects his ability to recognize his limitations and adjust his goals accordingly. he knew that if he set too stringent requirements that he could not achieve, he would be worse off than if he had set lower expectations and achieved them. while he had to check his own hubris, michelangelo was rewarded with numerous successes throughout his life, likely due in part to his ability to set realistic expectations and strive to meet them. his own experience and success affirm the danger of setting goals too high and not being able to achieve them.
