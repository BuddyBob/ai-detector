to many people, as the speed of the world is faster and faster, more stressful and less leisurely they feel. this is not the case. in fact, the opposite is true. todays work is less stressful than it used to be. in the old days, work was a way to make a living. nowadays, it is a means to an end. work is no longer the end in itself. it is the means by which we achieve our goals. we work because we have to work, and we work for the sake of our work. if we dont work hard enough, we will not be able to achieve the goals that we set for ourselves. therefore, if we want to have a good worklife balance, then we need to take care of ourselves as much as possible. that is why i believe that the most important thing for a person to do is to get enough sleep, exercise regularly, eat healthy food and take a break from work every once in a while. these are the things that will help us to live a healthy and happy life.
