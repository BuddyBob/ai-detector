as the saying goes, its not a matter of if you will fail, but when. this is because, in order to be truly successful, we must first persevere through failure. take the athlete, for example. no matter how much natural talent an athlete may have, they will not reach elite status without putting in countless hours of rigorous training and enduring untold failures and mistakes along the way. each time the athlete falls short of their goal, they must evaluate their performance, adjust their methods, and press forward. only by embracing failure and learning from it can an athlete progress. or take a young entrepreneur launching a new business venture. rather than fearing failure, they must campaign forward, willing to make mistakes and learn along the way. one famous example of this is the cofounder of apple computers, steve jobs. after his founding team was terminated and the companys projects were deemed unsuccessful, jobs rose once again and pushed through, ultimately gaining success in the long run. in conclusion, failure is not a dead end nor a sign of weakness  it is simply a portion of the path to success. it is only through actively engaging in failures and learning from them that we can ultimately create the triumphant success we strive for.
