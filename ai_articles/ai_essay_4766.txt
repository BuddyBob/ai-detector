one of the most important things that children should learn is how to cooperate. this skill is essential for success in life. there are many reasons why cooperative learning is better than competitive learning. first, cooperative learning is more enjoyable for children. they enjoy working together and share the excitement and rewards of success. this is especially important for young children, who are still developing their social and emotional skills. when they are enjoying themselves, they are more likely to learn. second, cooperative learning is more effective. when children are competing against each other, they are more likely to give up quickly or to blame each other if things go wrong. this can lead to frustration and anger, which can have negative effects on the childrens learning. third, cooperative learning is more likely to lead to success. in a competitive environment, it is easy for students to give up when things get hard. this can lead them to have lower academic standards than their classmates, regardless of their ability. by contrast, when students are cooperative, they are more likely to persevere and achieve their goals. there are many reasons why children should learn to cooperate. these reasons make cooperative learning the better option for childrens education.
