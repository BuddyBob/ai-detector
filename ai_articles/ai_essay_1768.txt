drivers should not use cell phones in any capacity while operating a vehicle operating a motor vehicle requires a drivers full attention at all times. however, with the rise of cell phone ownership, many drivers have begun using their phones even while driving. this dangerous distraction puts both the driver and other people on the road at risk of accident or injury. for this reason, drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity when driving. there are several reasons why cell phone use while driving is too great a distraction. first, cell phones divert a drivers eyes from the road. even brief glances away from the road to check a phone can cause a driver to miss important visual cues like traffic signals, pedestrians, or other vehicles. second, cell phones divert a drivers mental focus and attention. engaging in phone conversations, reading or sending texts or emails, or using apps all involve significant cognitive workload that removes much of a drivers attention from actually operating the vehicle. numerous studies have found that driving while distracted by a phone is just as dangerous as  if not more dangerous than  driving while intoxicated. allowing any cell phone use also opens the door for increasingly distracted behavior over time. handsfree options like bluetooth enable more lengthy phone conversations while driving, but conversations still divert mental focus. texting while stopped at red lights could become more widespread and lead to multitasking even while driving. a total ban helps set a clear boundary that phones have no place in the drivers seat. instead of cell phone use, the safest option is for drivers to fully focus their eyes, hands, and mind on the task of driving. this protects not just the driver, but also other people sharing the road, whether in other vehicles, on bikes, or as pedestrians. a total ban on cell phone use while driving is prudent policy to help prevent accidents and promote public safety on our roads. drivers should abstain from cell phone use until safely parked.
