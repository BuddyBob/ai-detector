there is no one answer to the question of whether children should be taught to compete or cooperate. some people believe that competition is the key to success, while others think that cooperation is more important. i believe that both competition and cooperation have their place in a childs education. competition can teach children to be ambitious and to work hard to achieve their goals. it can also help them to learn how to deal with disappointment and to cope with defeat. however, competition can also lead to rivalry and bitterness between children. cooperation teaches children to work together for a common goal. it helps them to develop teamwork skills and to learn how to compromise. cooperation can also lead to friendships and to a sense of community. however, cooperation can also lead to groupthink and to a lack of individual initiative. in my opinion, both competition and cooperation have their benefits and their drawbacks. i believe that it is important for children to learn both skills. competition can teach them to be ambitious and to work hard, while cooperation can teach them to work together and to get along with others.
