entering high school provides an opportunity for adolescents to develop their understanding of the world and begin preparing for life beyond it. by engaging in a career decision early in their academic career, high school students benefit from having a clearer direction in their studies as they move toward postsecondary years. understanding and focusing on their desired profession early on provides a benefit in the form of more specialized instruction and knowledge. at the same time, making career decisions in high school can also pose some limitations. confined to the structure of their school, high schoolers might limit their opportunities to explore a wider range of experiences. without more exposure to potential paths, students may determine their future based on an incomplete understanding and be unable to take advantage of opportunities that arise later on. additionally, when students put all their efforts into a single subject or field, they may also miss out on other useful lessons and skills which could be useful in any avenue.
