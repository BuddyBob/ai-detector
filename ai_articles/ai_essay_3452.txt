when making decisions, it can be useful to consider advice from multiple people in different situations, as it can help provide a broad perspective. having multiple viewpoints can help identify potential benefits and drawbacks that might otherwise be overlooked. for example, if making a career decision, receiving advice from a mentor in the profession, a current colleague, and a former classmate who once pursued the same option can provide a comprehensive assessment of the pros and cons of the potential option. on the other hand, there can be drawbacks to receiving and considering advice from too many different people. having multiple perspectives can be difficult to process all at once, and it might cause confusion and conflicting opinions. it is important to weigh each persons advice carefully, or else it could create too much doubt or indecision when making a choice.
