i have to say that i absolutely disagree with the trend of students living outside of college campuses. when i am a college student, i am always looking for ways to save money and i think that living in an apartment is the best way to do that. i believe that apartments are a much more affordable option than renting a house or a condo. one of the main reasons why apartments are so affordable is because they are not considered to be a real house. houses are typically used for living and dining in and they are not meant for studying. on the other hand, apartments are usually furnished and they are usually considered to be a place where people can stay for a short while. another reason why apartments are so affordable is because they are not associated with a college campus. when you live in an apartment, you are not associated with a student body or with the professors who are teaching in thecampus. you are instead associated with the apartment itself and with the people who live in it. lastly, apartments are usually easier to maintain than houses. houses can usually be managed by the tenants themselves, but apartments usually require more effort from the landlords. this is because apartments are often equipped with more amenities, such as air conditioning and heating.
