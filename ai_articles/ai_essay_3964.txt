schools have a responsibility to incorporate energy and resource conservation into their everyday practices. here are five easy ways schools can use to to begin conserving energy and resources. . utilize natural light by installing skylights or utilizing natural sunlight wherever possible. . switch to led lighting, which is energy efficient and has a longer life span. . teach students about the importance of conservation and ways they can help  encourage reducing waste, properly disposing of recyclables, and using reusable items wherever possible. . implement watersaving initiatives, such as installing lowflow faucets and toilets, collecting rainwater for landscaping and lawns, and practicing efficient irrigation of lawns. . utilize renewable energy sources, such as solar power or wind turbines. to implement these changes, school administrators could create a green team committee or task force made up of students and staff to develop and promote green initiatives. schools can also work to find grants or other funding to support their efforts. additionally, school administrators and faculty can hold special assemblies or classroom lessons to teach students about the importance of conservation and ways to get involved.
