really is. another way in which advertisements make products appear much better than they are is through the selective use of images. companies often use images that make their products look more person. similarly, digital technology can be used to make products look far better than they actually are. for example, computergenerated images are often used to display products in a more visually appealing way. finally, advertisements often make products seem better than they actually are by omitting important details. this is especially true in the food industry, where advertisements often control procedures. by leaving out these details, companies can make their products appear much better than they actually are. in conclusion, i agree that advertisements often make products seem much
