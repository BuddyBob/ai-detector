when it comes to working with a group, there are several benefits. one major benefit is the exchange of cultural knowledge between group members. working in a group encourages people to learn from each other and to share both their commonalities and differences. it also encourages the development of communication and language skills, as it provides an often fun environment to practice the use of language in different contexts. working with a group also provides support to each group member, as individuals learn from each other and share experiences. furthermore, the effectiveness of group work in terms of learning has been demonstrated to be higher when compared to learning on an individual basis. on the other hand, one of the disadvantages of working with a group is that it can be time consuming and can lead to teamwork issues if proper guidelines are not followed.
