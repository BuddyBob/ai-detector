the benefits of a plantbased diet are well established, and for those looking to make a lifestyle change towards better health and wellness, incorporating more vegetables into their daily consumption is an essential first step. eating a plantbased diet has been linked to lower risk of heart disease, diabetes, obesity, cancer and many other chronic illnesses. it can also provide numerous mental health benefits due to the high levels of antioxidants found in fresh fruits and vegetables. however, transitioning from an unbalanced omnivorous diet to one that mainly consists of grains, legumes, nutsseeds, fruits and veggies can be difficult if taken on all at once. one way to gradually transition into eating mostly plants is by taking small steps each day instead of trying everything at once. start with adding more vegetable dishes onto your plate along with whole grains such as brown rice or quinoa replace beef or chicken in recipes with protein sources like beans or lentils introduce vegan snacks such as nutsseeds or hummus opt for fruit smoothies instead of sugary drinks cook up some new vegetarian meals every week using spices youve never used before  theres no need to do it all at once! small changes can eventually lead up to larger ones so take your time when making this switch  it may take some trial  error but remember that rome was not built in a day!. in addition to these dietary shifts it is important for those considering eating plantbased foods regularly should also consider their overall life style choices such as exercising regularly and getting enough sleep each night so the bodys systems remain balanced while going through this transition period. for example get out for walks around your neighbourhood during lunchtime instead of sitting inside watching television  any amount helps even if its just  minutes outside per day ! this will help ensure everything else stays on track alongside the dietary shift  thereby setting yourself up for success over the long term . eating plant
