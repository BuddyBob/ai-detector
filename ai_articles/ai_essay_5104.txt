there are different opinions on which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. some people think that we should give priority to reading in english, while others think speaking is more important. i agree that speaking is more important. the ability to speak is the most important aspect of communication. if we cannot speak, we cannot communicate with others. the ability to read is not as important as the ability to speak, because we can always look up words that we dont know. another reason why speaking is more important is that it is more difficult than reading. reading is a passive activity, while speaking is active. when we speak, we have to generate the words ourselves. this is much more difficult than simply reading the words on a page. finally, speaking is more important because it is more useful. we use spoken english much more often than written english. we use spoken english to communicate with others in our daily lives. we use written english mostly for reading and writing. in conclusion, speaking is more important than reading. this is because speaking is necessary for communication, it is more difficult than reading, and it is more useful.
