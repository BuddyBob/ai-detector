as humans, we are constantly influenced by the people and environments around us. these influences have a major impact on our characters and how we view ourselves. the people we associate with, our families, our culture, our environment, our education, and our personal experiences  all contribute to the shaping of our character. take, for example, the choices we make in our friendships. we usually pick people who share similar values and perspectives, and those who can act as mentors, motivate and inspire us. on the flip side, those we choose to associate with also influence our values, morals and overall character. our parents are the people who usually have the strongest influence on our development. they are our primary caretakers and our first models for behavior, values and beliefs. parents often pass on their history, their memories, and their beliefs, helping to shape our character, attitude and disposition. family environment also has an impact on our characters. studies show that family dynamics greatly shape our personalities, particularly when it comes to adherence to social values. our upbringing, either in an authoritarian or democratic family, in an organized or chaotic home, in an accepting and nurturing environment or in an unsafe one, all shape our character and attitude toward the world. similarly, culture and society play an important role in the shaping our character. culture and environment directly shape how our behavior, values, goals and behavior are shaped. as we interact with the world, the experiences we have and the ideas we are exposed to shape who we become. in conclusion, our characters are formed by the people and influences around us, from the small and seemingly insignificant details with which we come into contact with, to the significant conversations and interactions with our families, to the sociocultural dynamics with which we are surrounded. all together, these factors shape our values, behaviors and vision for life.
