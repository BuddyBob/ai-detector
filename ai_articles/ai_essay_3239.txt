the advantages of working alone compared to working with a group are numerous. firstly, working alone allows you to have greater control over the task youre tackling. you dont have to worry about incorporating another persons ideas or accommodating their wants and needs. secondly, working alone gives you the freedom to be as creative as youd like. you can explore different ideas without feeling constrained by other peoples opinions. lastly, it can be more efficient to work alone, since you have fewer obstacles to maneuver. this is especially true for tasks that need to be completed on a tight timeline. for example, if youre working on an essay and only have one day to finish it, youll likely work faster and more efficiently alone, rather than having to collaborate with a group.
