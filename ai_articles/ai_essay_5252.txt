the amount of work that people have to do these days is definitely higher than it was in the past. in addition, the work is often more stressful and less leisurely. there are a number of reasons for this. first of all, there are simply more demands on peoples time. in the past, people generally had one job. now, many people have multiple jobs. in addition, people are expected to be available  thanks to technology. this means that people have less time to relax and enjoy themselves. second, the work itself is often more stressful. this is because there is more competition and more pressure to perform. in the past, people could often coast by with doing just the bare minimum. now, people have to work harder to stand out and to keep their jobs. finally, there is less leisure time in general. this is because people have to work more and because there are more demands on their time. in the past, people might have had an hour or two to themselves after work. now, they are lucky if they have a few minutes. all of this adds up to a situation where work is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. this is a trend that is likely to continue into the future.
