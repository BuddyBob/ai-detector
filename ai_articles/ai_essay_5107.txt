different people may have different opinions on which skill of english is more important for chinese learners. some people think that we should give priority to reading in english, while others think speaking is more important. personally, i think both skills are important and we should pay attention to both of them. on the one hand, reading is a very important skill. if we want to improve our english, we need to read more. reading can help us learn new words and phrases. it can also help us improve our grammar and our understanding of the english language. in addition, reading can also help us improve our writing skills. on the other hand, speaking is also a very important skill. if we want to be able to communicate with people in english, we need to be able to speak the language. speaking can help us practice our pronunciation and our fluency. it can also help us learn how to use the english language in a realworld context. both reading and speaking are important skills. we should pay attention to both of them.
