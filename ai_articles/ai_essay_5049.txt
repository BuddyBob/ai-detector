nowadays, computer games are very popular among children. however, some people think that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual as well as psychological development. therefore, they suggest that effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing them. i agree with the opinion that computer games have more negative effects than positive ones on children. first of all, computer games are very addictive and children who play them often become addicted to them. this addiction can lead to many problems such as poor performance in school, social isolation, and even depression. moreover, computer games are often violent and children who play them can become more aggressive. finally, computer games can have a negative impact on childrens health. for example, children who play computer games for long hours can suffer from obesity, backaches, and eye problems. there are several measures that can be taken to prevent children from playing computer games. first, parents should limit the amount of time their children spend playing computer games. second, parents should choose appropriate computer games for their children. third, parents should encourage their children to play other games that are more beneficial for their development. in conclusion, computer games have more negative effects than positive ones on children. parents should take measures to prevent their children from playing them.
