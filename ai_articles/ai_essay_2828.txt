numerous studies have shown that after school homework clubs are beneficial for both students and their surrounding schools. these clubs provide a safe and secure environment for students to complete their school work and develop their study habits. for the students, homework clubs offer a tailored learning experience designed to help them enhance their academic skills and potential. by having access to a tutor or mentor, students are given the chance to receive individual support outside of the classroom. learning in this manner can help students gain confidence and think more deeply about the material they are studying. this can lead to improved grades and a better understanding of the material. additionally, after school homework clubs encourage collaboration and problemsolving skills in a supervised environment, enhancing students social and teamwork abilities. homework clubs also provide further benefits for the school as a whole. by providing this service, schools demonstrate to their students that they are invested in their future and want to see everyone succeed. this gives the students a sense of trust and belonging, making them more likely to have a positive outlook towards education. eagerness to learn can spread to their peers, creating a stronger learning environment in the school. in addition, after school homework clubs may lead to an increase in parental engagement in the students learning. with access to schoolprovided lessons and resources, parents can more easily follow their childs progress and assist them when needed. overall, after school homework clubs are immensely beneficial for both students and their surrounding schools. with the tailored learning experience, students have a chance to improve their study habits while the school increases parental engagement. this can lead to successful academic outcomes and improved grades.
