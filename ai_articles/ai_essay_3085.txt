my love of music started when i joined the school choir in elementary school. i was curious about different types of instrument, and soon began to learn the recorder, xylophone, percussion, and the violin. as i practiced and explored these instruments, i began to participate in musical contests, concerts, and any other opportunity to showcase my talents. my hard work paid off when i became the leader of a school band, and we wrote and performed a song for an important event. i was proud to have contributed to something so meaningful. music has been a passion of mine ever since.
