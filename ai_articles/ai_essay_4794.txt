there are a few different stances one could take when it comes to the topic of children learning to compete or cooperate. some people may think that it is important for children to learn how to compete in order to be successful in life. others may think that it is more important for children to learn how to cooperate in order to get along with others. i happen to believe that both aspects are important, and that children should be taught to do both. one reason why it is important for children to learn to compete is because it can help them to achieve their goals. if a child is determined to become the best basketball player in the world, for example, they will need to learn how to compete and work hard to achieve their goal. competition can also help children to learn how to deal with disappointment. if they dont win the race, for example, they will need to learn how to deal with the feeling of losing and move on. another reason why it is important for children to learn to cooperate is because it can help them to get along with others. if a child can learn how to cooperate with their classmates, for example, they will likely have more friends and be more successful in school. cooperation can also help children to resolve conflicts. if two children are fighting, for example, they can learn to cooperate and resolve the conflict. in conclusion, i believe that it is important for children to learn to compete and cooperate. competition can help children to achieve their goals, while cooperation can help children to get along with others. i think that it is important for children to be taught both of these skills, so that they can be successful in life.
