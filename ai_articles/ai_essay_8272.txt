there are many benefits to traveling in a group led by a tour guide. first and foremost, group travel is much more affordable than traveling alone. group tours are also more flexible than individual tours. this means that you can customize your trip to fit your interests and needs. additionally, group tours provide a more immersive experience than traveling alone. this is because a tour guide can take you to unique and interesting locations that you would never find on your own. finally, group tours are a great way to meet new people. traveling in a group allows you to make new friends and learn about different cultures. this is a invaluable experience that you can never get from traveling alone.
