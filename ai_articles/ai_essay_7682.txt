there has always been a tendency for young people to seek out immediate fame and recognition, but this does not mean that pursuing longterm, realistic goals should be discouraged. in fact, it is important to encourage young people to look beyond the here and now and focus on goals that will help them grow and develop in the long run. to begin with, young people should be reminded that fame and recognition, while appealing in the shortterm, often do not last for long. even if a young person succeeds in achieving some degree of fame, it may eventually fade away due to changing trends or simply the passage of time. on the other hand, longterm, realistic goals can provide a lasting sense of accomplishment and selfworth that can benefit a young person throughout their life. another reason to encourage young people to focus on longterm goals is that it can help them develop better life skills and habits. longterm goals often involve working on something over an extended period of time, which can help develop discipline and patience. it can also help young people learn how to plan and break down tasks into manageable steps. these are all crucial life skills that can help young people throughout their life and that can be developed by focusing on longterm goals. finally, pursuing longterm, realistic goals can provide young people with something to strive for. this can be especially important for young people who are feeling directionless and unsure of what to do with their lives. by focusing on meaningful goals, young people can set a course for their future and have something to work towards. this can give them a sense of purpose and direction that can help them develop into wellrounded individuals. in conclusion, young people should be encouraged to pursue longterm, realistic goals rather than seek immediate fame and recognition. longterm goals can provide young people with lasting accomplishment, teach them important life skills, and give them something to strive for. by focusing on longterm goals, young people can gain the skills and confidence they need to succeed later in life.
