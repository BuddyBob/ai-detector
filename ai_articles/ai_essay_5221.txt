i think that work nowadays is more stressful and less leisurely than in the past. first, workdays are often much longer than they were in the past. in the s, workdays typically lasted from am to pm. in contrast, todays workdays can last from am to pm or even longer. this is because our society has become more demanding and people are expected to work more hours per day. second, work today is often more demanding than it was in the past. in the s, most people worked at manual labor jobs such as farming or manufacturing. today, many people work at jobs that require more intellectual skills, such as engineering or accounting. these jobs are often more demanding because they require people to use their brains more than their muscles. third, work today is often more stressful than it was in the past. in the s, most people lived in small communities and knew their neighbors. today, most people live in large cities and never see their neighbors. this is because most jobs today require people to work in isolation. for example, a person working in an office must work alone and never see the people who are working in the next office. this is a much more stressful environment than the environment in which people used to work in the s. all of these factors  the length of the workday, the demands of the job, and the stress of the environment  contribute to the fact that work nowadays is more stressful than it was in the past.
