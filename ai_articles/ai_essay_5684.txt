in todays world, environmental concerns have become a major issue, and we are constantly being advised to adopt ecofriendly practices. one such practice is to use electronic cards instead of paper cards for holiday greetings. while some people believe that electronic cards lack the charm and functionality of paper cards, i disagree with this notion. in this essay, i will provide specific reasons and examples to support my argument. firstly, electronic cards offer a wide range of customization options that paper cards cannot match. with electronic cards, you can add music, animations, and even videos to make your greeting more personalized and engaging. for instance, you can create a video message with your family members singing a christmas carol, which can be shared with your loved ones across the globe. this level of customization is not possible with paper cards, which are limited to text and images. secondly, electronic cards are more convenient and costeffective than paper cards. with electronic cards, you can send your greetings instantly to multiple recipients with just a few clicks. this saves you time and money that would have been spent on buying cards, envelopes, and stamps. additionally, electronic cards are ecofriendly as they do not contribute to the cutting down of trees, which is a major environmental concern. lastly, electronic cards are more accessible than paper cards. with electronic cards, you can send your greetings to people who are far away or those with disabilities that make it difficult for them to read paper cards. for instance, people with visual impairments can use screen readers to listen to electronic cards, which makes them more inclusive. in conclusion, while some people believe that electronic cards lack the charm and functionality of paper cards, i disagree with this notion. electronic cards offer a wide range of customization options, are more convenient and costeffective, and are more accessible than paper cards. therefore, i believe that electronic cards are a better option for holiday greetings, as they are not only ecofriendly but also offer more benefits than paper cards.
