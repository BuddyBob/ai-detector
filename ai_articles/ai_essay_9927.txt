the growth of urban farming in detroit has been an inspiring example of how a struggling city can use agriculture to rejuvenate its economy and improve the quality of life for its citizens. from abandoned lots being transformed into flourishing gardens, to rooftop greenhouses providing fresh produce to local businesses, urban farming is giving detroiters the opportunity to take back control of their food system. at the center of this movement are several organizations that have taken on the challenge of developing sustainable methods for growing local food within city limits. one such group is earthworks urban farm, which was founded by devita davison in  with just two acres near i and a vision for creating a model selfsustaining farm enterprise. with help from grants and donations from corporations like chevrolet, ubs financial services, and comerica bank, earthworks now covers more than five acres at multiple sites around detroit. they grow over  varieties of fruits and vegetables organically using rotational crop systems that replenish soil nutrients without needing chemical fertilizers or pesticides. additionally they employ more than  full time staff members who are dedicated to empowering youth through education about healthy eating habits and sustainable agricultural practices. earthworks has had tremendous success since opening its doors  years ago they provide produce year round to over  restaurants throughout michigan as well as numerous farmers markets across southeast michigan they also work closely with community partners such as access community health center acch and garden resource program collaborative grpc helping them feed thousands every week through their food box program which provides low income families with fresh produce at no cost or on sliding scale prices depending on need. but perhaps most importantly they create dozens jobs in the cities neighborhoods bolstering economic activity while promoting nutrition security among those living in poverty stricken areas  all made possible through earth friendly growing practices thanks to urban farming initiatives taking root across motor citys landscape!
