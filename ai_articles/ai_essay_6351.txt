i disagree with the statement that the best way to travel is in a group led by a tour guide. while a tour guide can be a great resource for learning about a new place, i believe that there are many benefits to traveling independently. first, traveling independently allows for more flexibility and freedom. you can choose your own itinerary and explore a place at your own pace. this is especially important for those who want to get off the beaten path and see more of the local culture. second, traveling independently can be more affordable. group tours can be expensive, especially if you are paying for airfare, accommodations, and meals. by traveling on your own, you can save money by cooking your own meals and booking cheaper accommodations. third, traveling independently allows you to meet more people. when you are traveling with a group, you are mostly limited to the people in your group. but when traveling independently, you are more likely to meet locals and other travelers. this can be a great opportunity to learn about a new culture and make new friends. overall, i believe that there are many benefits to traveling independently. it allows for more flexibility and freedom, it can be more affordable, and it provides an opportunity to meet new people. i would encourage everyone to travel independently at least once in their life.
