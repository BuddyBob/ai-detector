the significance of the hummingbird in mexican culture is often overlooked, yet it has been an iconic symbol for centuries. in ancient times, the aztecs believed that hummers were a representation of their god huitzilopochtli, who was thought to bring good fortune and success. this belief travelled through time and into modern society as people continued to associate these small birds with luck and prosperity. in mexico today, the hummingbird is still seen as a sign of good luck and hope. it appears on coins, jewelry pieces like pendants or earrings, artwork including paintings and sculptures its even used in traditional clothing designs like huipils traditional blouses. the bird can be found throughout urban areas adorning street signs or benches along with other folk art depictions that are popular among locals such as masks or ceramics pieces. this admiration for hummingbirds extends beyond just material things but also into folklore stories passed down generations where they are regarded as messengers from higher dimensions who help guide lost souls back home safely. one tale tells how two boys get separated while out playing together near a river when one boy falls into the water suddenly a hummingbird appears and leads him back home unharmed  this story alone illustrates how much faith mexicans have placed in the positive powers associated with these creatures over time. hummingbirds represent more than just symbols of luck  they embody resilience, courage  determination which is why so many identify with them deeply on both personal  spiritual levels alike. they are resilient because despite their size they can fly long distances without rest plus have strong hearts that beat rapidly at several hundred beats per minute enabling them to sustain flight better than larger birds do this quality has served as inspiration for many mexicans who pushed forward during difficult circumstances for themselves or family members despite any obstacles standing in their way by using this same spirit of perseverance  hardwork to reach goals within life no matter what hardships
