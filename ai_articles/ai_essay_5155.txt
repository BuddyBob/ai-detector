there is no doubt that education is expensive. however, the consequences of a failure to educate, especially in an increasingly globalized world, are even more expensive. for one, a lack of education can lead to a decrease in productivity. in a globalized world, where businesses must compete on a global scale, having a workforce that is unable to communicate effectively, read and write in multiple languages, and think critically is a major disadvantage. furthermore, a lack of education can also lead to social problems. for example, in countries where a high percentage of the population is illiterate, there is a high rate of crime and poverty. in addition, a lack of education can lead to a decrease in the quality of life for those who do have it. for example, in countries with a high level of education, there is a higher level of income and life expectancy. in conclusion, a lack of education is expensive both in terms of money spent on education itself, and the social and economic consequences of a population that is unable to compete in a globalized world.
