working with a group can be a great benefit because it allows you to work with a diverse range of skills and opinions. when working with others you can discuss ideas and share the workload. you can also get more done in less time as the work can be divided and delegated. on the other hand, when working alone you get complete control over the entire project which can be beneficial in terms of planning, focus, and personal goals. you dont have to rely on anyone else so you dont need to worry about any potential disagreements that might arise. in the end, it can be beneficial to weigh the pros and cons of each option and decide which method is best suited for the task at hand.
