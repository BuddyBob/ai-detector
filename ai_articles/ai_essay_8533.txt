intelligence, or eq. emotional intelligence is defined as the ability to accurately perceive, understand, express, and regulate emotions in oneself and others. it involves empathy, selfawareness and social skills such as communication and relationship management. eq has been studied for decades by researchers from various disciplines including psychology, education, business and medicine. while iq intelligence quotient was once thought to be a reliable predictor of success in life, studies have shown that eq may actually be more important than iq when it comes to achieving success in ones personal life or career. in fact, experts believe that emotional intelligence may even be more important than technical skills when it comes to job performance. this means that having strong emotional intelligence can give individuals an edge over those with similar qualifications but lower eqs when competing for jobs or promotions. the benefits associated with high levels of emotional intelligence are numerous studies suggest that people with higher eqs tend to have better relationships both at work and home due to their improved interpersonal skills they also experience lower stress levels due to their greater ability to manage negative emotions through selfregulation techniques like mindfulness meditation or cognitive behavioral therapy cbt. further research has also linked increased emotional intelligence with better overall physical health outcomes as well as increased creativity and problem solving abilities which are invaluable assets in todays everchanging world . developing
