not only eliminated exhaust pollutants within the communitys confines but also encouraged more ecofriendly modes of transportation, which could, in turn,help reduce global warming. secondly, the reduction of car usage can dramatically improve urban air quality, manifest through the experiences of paris and bogotá. when paris enforced a partial driving ban due to severe pollution, congestion this advantagerosenthal, . the inclination towards reducing car usage also aligns with a marked shift in societal behaviors. recent studies suggest a decrease in the number of cars bought, miles
