extended school days have seen both positive and negative impacts. on one hand, the day, hour model has been known to reduce costs and provide more instructional time since longer days are compressed into fewer days. teachers also often report more engagement amongst their students during the longer days since students have fewer distractions. however, there are drawbacks as well. for instance, since students are in school for longer periods, there is less time to attend to their physical, mental, and emotional needs. parents can find it more difficult to coordinate pick up and drop off for their children, which can be quite stressful for them. given these potential consequences, we recommend only implementing the day,  hour model if certain safeguards are put in place to make sure that student and parent needs are met.
