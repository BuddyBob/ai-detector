english is a language that is spoken by millions of people around the world. as a result, it has become increasingly important for chinese learners to develop their english skills. while there are many different skills that can be developed, some people believe that reading should be given priority, while others believe that speaking should be given priority. in my opinion, both reading and speaking are equally important for chinese learners. reading is an important skill for chinese learners to develop because it allows them to gain a better understanding of the english language. by reading, learners can become familiar with the different grammar rules and sentence structures that are used in english. additionally, reading can help learners to increase their vocabulary and become more comfortable with the language. furthermore, reading can help learners to gain a better understanding of the culture and customs of englishspeaking countries. at the same time, speaking is also an important skill for chinese learners to develop. speaking allows learners to practice their pronunciation and intonation, which are essential for communicating effectively in english. additionally, speaking can help learners to become more confident in their ability to communicate in english. furthermore, speaking can help learners to develop their listening skills, as they must listen carefully to understand what is being said. in conclusion, both reading and speaking are important skills for chinese learners to develop. reading can help learners to gain a better understanding of the english language, while speaking can help them to practice their pronunciation and intonation. therefore, both skills should be given equal priority when it comes to learning english.
