there are two schools of thought when it comes to how children should be taught. some people think that children should learn to compete, while others think that children should be taught to cooperate. i believe that children should be taught to cooperate. there are many benefits to cooperation. when people cooperate, they are able to accomplish more than they could on their own. they are also able to learn from each other and grow together. cooperation fosters understanding and respect between people. competition, on the other hand, can often lead to negative consequences. when people compete, they are often focused on beating the other person, rather than on working together to achieve a common goal. this can lead to conflict and resentment. competition can also be exclusionary, making people feel like they are not good enough or that they dont belong. i believe that cooperation is a better way to teach children because it helps them to develop important life skills. cooperation teaches children how to work with others, how to resolve conflicts, and how to be part of a team. these are skills that will help them throughout their lives.
