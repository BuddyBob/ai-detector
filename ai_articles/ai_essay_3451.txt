although there are plentiful excuses for students not to attend school, including illness, family trips, or lack of interest, natural disasters and other issues in the students neighborhood can also make it difficult for them to attend. the impact of absences and making up an entire year of schooling can be significantly detrimental to a students academic success. to help students in difficult circumstances, one solution is to offer distance learning opportunities, which allows students to stay on track without having to attend in person. however, many argue that the effectiveness of distance learning is limited compared to the traditional inperson classroom setting, making it a controversial topic. ultimately, there is no easy answer, and it should be approached differently depending on the specific situation.
