many of us strive to build our selfesteem each day. some use achievement, while others gravitate towards being praised. achievement awards us with a sense of accomplishment, and in turn may boost our selfesteem. in modern society, there is a lot of pressure to achieve. we are consistently striving for success, even if it is not our own. this can lead to us becoming focused on external goals. on the other hand, praise is often seen as a way to build selfesteem without the pressure that comes with achievement. being complimented allows us to feel more positively about ourselves. however, it may not always be rooted in reality, as it often relies on somebody elses opinion. it also can become overused and stop having the same effect. in general, true selfesteem is better built through achievement because it relies on our own successful pursuits, rather than external validation. it helps us to develop confidence in our own abilities, and learn where we need to work harder. though there is pressure involved, focusing on our own goals as our source of validation can prove to be extremely rewarding.
