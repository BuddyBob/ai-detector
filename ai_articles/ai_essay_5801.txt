the internet has revolutionized the way we access information. it has made information more readily available and has allowed us to communicate with people all over the world. however, i do not believe that the internet will ever completely replace the book or the written word as the main source of information. one of the main reasons why the internet will never completely replace books and written words is because books and written words are physical objects. we can touch them, feel them, and smell them. they are tangible objects that we can hold in our hands. the internet is an intangible object that we can only access through a computer or a phone. another reason why books and written words will never be replaced by the internet is because books and written words can be used for reference. we can go back and look at them again and again. the internet is constantly changing and it is difficult to keep track of all the information that is available on it. finally, books and written words are a form of entertainment. we can read them for pleasure and we can learn a lot from them. the internet can also be used for entertainment and for learning, but it is not as focused on learning as books are. in conclusion, i believe that books and written words will never be replaced by the internet. they are physical objects that can be used for reference and for entertainment. the internet is an intangible object that is constantly changing and it is not as focused on learning as books are.
