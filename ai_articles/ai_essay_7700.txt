leadership has been defined in myriad ways, with experts positing diverse perspectives. some scholars believe that leaders are born, while others suggest they are made. while it is true that leaders may be born with certain traits, such as charisma or intelligence, these qualities are not sufficient to make someone a great leader. instead, leaders are primarily created by the demands that are placed upon them. i agree with this statement and will use specific reasons and examples to support my argument. first, it is essential to understand that leaders emerge in response to a specific need or challenge. this demand can arise from various sources, such as the need for change, the desire to solve a complex problem, or a crisis that needs to be addressed. for instance, during the covid pandemic, leaders emerged all over the world to help manage the crisis. these leaders were not necessarily born with innate leadership abilities, but their response to the pandemic demanded them to step up and lead their communities, organizations, or countries. second, demands placed upon potential leaders can be a catalyst for personal growth and development. the challenges that demand leadership skills also provide opportunities for leaders to develop skills such as communication, decisionmaking, and problemsolving. for example, a leader who is facing a significant challenge, such as a merger or acquisition, must learn to communicate effectively with stakeholders, make tough decisions, and resolve conflicts. the demands placed upon the leader can be a great opportunity to develop and refine these skills. third, leaders are created by the followers who perceive them as such. leadership is not a oneway relationship it involves a dynamic interaction between leaders and their followers. therefore, the demands placed upon a leader must be met by followers who recognize and support their leadership. for example, a ceo who leads a company through a challenging period of economic downturn needs the support and trust of employees, shareholders, and customers to be recognized as a great leader. finally, leaders are created through experience. no one is born a leader, and there is no onesizefitsall approach to leadership. leaders must learn and adapt to new situations through their experiences. the more experience a leader has, the better they are at adapting to new challenges, developing new skills, and inspiring others. for example, a leader who has successfully led a team through a crisis is more likely to be effective in the next crisis they face. in conclusion, leaders are primarily created by the demands placed upon them. these demands can arise from various sources, such as the need for change, the desire to solve a complex problem, or a crisis that needs to be addressed. while some leaders may be born with certain traits that make them effective, these qualities are not sufficient to make them great leaders. great leaders emerge as a response to the demands of the situation and are recognized as such by their followers. furthermore, leadership is a dynamic process that involves personal growth, learning, and experience. therefore, it is essential for potential leaders to embrace the demands that come with their roles, learn from their experiences, and strive to become better leaders.
