in recent years, the prevalence of technology in everyday life has changed the way we think and act. however, its impact on youth mental health is often overlooked or ignored. technology can be a powerful tool for learning, connecting with loved ones, and building relationships but it can also be a source of anxiety, depression and other forms of mental health issues when not used responsibly. the negative effects of technology are particularly pertinent to todays youth who have grown up surrounded by digital devices like phones, tablets and computers. with such easy access to apps that allow them to stay constantly connected with friends online as well as access vast amounts of information on any topic imaginable, young people can quickly become overwhelmed if they dont learn how to use it responsibly. this leads to teens feeling increasingly isolated from their peers due to spending too much time online rather than engaging in realworld activities such as sports or volunteering which could benefit them more socially and emotionally. furthermore, social media sites like facebook and instagram create an unrealistic expectation for what is considered normal  setting unrealistic standards for physical appearance which then causes low selfesteem among many teens accompanied by feelings of insecurity about themselves compared to others online. its important for parents and guardians alike to understand how their children interact with technology so that they may better help protect them from potential damage caused by overuse or misuse without completely depriving them from having access at all times  this balance is key! parenting styles should incorporate both restrictions such as limiting screen time as well creating guidelines around responsible usage such attending school before gaming. additionally providing educational resources promoting good habits while using technology will ensure your kids know the risks associated with being constantly plugged in digitally while helping develop critical thinking skills needed when navigating the virtual world safely. overall protecting our youths mental wellbeing starts firstly through educating ourselves on how best we can nurture our own healthy relationship with tech so that those same values may be passed down
