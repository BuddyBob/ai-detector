in recent years, the trend of plantbased diets has become increasingly popular among health enthusiasts and those looking to lead a more sustainable lifestyle. plantbased diets focus on whole, unprocessed foods like fruits, vegetables, whole grains, beans and legumes while avoiding or minimizing animal products such as meat and dairy. while many people might consider veganism or vegetarianism the same thing as a plantbased diet, there are some distinct differences between them. veganism is regarded as one of the most extreme forms of plantbased eating because it does not include anything derived from animals  even honey for example. vegetarianism is slightly less restrictive in that it allows for dairy products or eggs but no meats including fish. a plant based diet encompasses both vegan and vegetarian styles of eating but also includes other sources of nutrition such as small amounts of seafood or occasional dairy items depending on individual preferenceneeds. the benefits associated with adopting a mostly plant based diet are numerous improved digestion due to an increased intake in fiber, stronger immunity against chronic illnesses such as obesity, heart disease etc., higher energy levels due to better nutrient absorption and overall enhanced well being due to avoidance of processed foods. additionally following this type of fare can provide environmental advantages since fewer resources must be used when producing these types of food items instead opting for animal related ones which require more land space for grazingproduction purposes
