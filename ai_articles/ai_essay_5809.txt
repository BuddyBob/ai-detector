the internet has become the goto source for information for many people. you can find out about almost anything with just a few clicks. however, there are still many people who prefer books as their main source of information. there are a few reasons for this. first, books are generally more reliable than the internet. with books, you know that the information has been vetted by experts and is more likely to be accurate. second, books often provide more indepth information than you can find online. if you want to learn about a topic, you can find a book that will give you a comprehensive overview. third, books are more portable than laptops or smartphones, so you can take them with you wherever you go. of course, there are also advantages to using the internet as your main source of information. the internet is more uptodate than most books, so you can find the latest news and research on a topic. you can also find a wider range of information on the internet than you can in most libraries. and, of course, its more convenient to search for information online than it is to go to the library. in the end, its up to each individual to decide which source of information is best for them. some people prefer the reliability and depth of books, while others prefer the convenience and uptodateness of the internet.
