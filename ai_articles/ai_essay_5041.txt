nowadays, computer games are very popular among children. however, some people think that computer games have produced more negative effects than positive ones on childrens physical, intellectual as well as psychological development. therefore, they suggest that effective measures should be taken to prevent children from playing them. in my opinion, i disagree with this idea. although computer games may have some negative effects on children, i believe that the positive effects are more significant. to begin with, computer games can help children develop their intelligence. in many games, children need to solve problems in order to win. this can help them develop their logical thinking and problemsolving skills. in addition, computer games can also help children improve their memory and concentration. they need to remember the rules of the games and concentrate on the screen in order to win. this can help them develop their memory and concentration. another positive effect of computer games is that they can help children develop their social skills. in many games, children need to cooperate with other players in order to win. this can help them develop their communication and teamwork skills. in addition, computer games can also help children develop their leadership skills. in many games, children need to take charge and lead their team to victory. this can help them develop their leadership skills. in conclusion, computer games have both positive and negative effects on children. however, i believe that the positive effects are more significant. computer games can help children develop their intelligence, memory, concentration, social skills, and leadership skills.
