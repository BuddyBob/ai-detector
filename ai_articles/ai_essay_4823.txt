there are two schools of thought when it comes to teaching children how to interact with others. some people believe that children should learn to compete, while others think that children should be taught to cooperate. i believe that children should be taught to cooperate with others. one reason why i believe this is because competition can often lead to negative feelings such as envy, resentment, and bitterness. these negative emotions can damage relationships and make it difficult to work together. cooperation, on the other hand, fosters positive feelings such as trust, respect, and teamwork. these positive emotions make it easier to work together and build strong relationships. another reason why i believe children should be taught to cooperate is because cooperation leads to more successful outcomes. when people work together, they can pool their resources and knowledge to achieve a common goal. this is often more effective than having each person working independently. there are a few exceptions to this rule. for example, competition can be helpful in situations where people need to be motivated to achieve a goal. however, in most cases, cooperation is the better option. in conclusion, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate with others. competition can often lead to negative emotions and unsuccessful outcomes. cooperation, on the other hand, fosters positive emotions and leads to more successful outcomes.
