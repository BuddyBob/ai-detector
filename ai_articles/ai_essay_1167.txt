to multiple people, you can see what they all have to say and then make a decision based on that. this can help you feel more confident in your choice because you know that you have considered by doing this, you can feel more confident in your choice because you know that you have considered multiple opinions. in conclusion, seeking multiple opinions can help you make a better choice
