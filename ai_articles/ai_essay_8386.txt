starting point from which to understand the field of knowledge and to build and expand upon it. the past achievements of those who went before us have laid the foundation for us to make a significant contribution in any field. for example, in the field of medicine, the past achievements of scientists, doctors, and researchers have made possible the treatments and cures we have today. from the discovery of antibiotics to the invention of vaccines, the past had to be studied and understood in order to make the advances in medical science that we now enjoy. in the sciences, newtons laws of motion, einsteins theory of relativity, and darwins theory of evolution, among many others, provide the foundation for us to better understand the universe, our place in it, and how to make advances in the field. we build on the work of those who went before us, using their discoveries as the basis for our own exploration and experimentation. in other fields as well, the past helps us to understand the current state of knowledge and how to make a contribution to it. for example, in literature, the works of authors like shakespeare and hemingway provide us with a starting point from which to understand the conventions of writing and to build upon them. similarly, in the field of art, the works of da vinci and picasso provide a basis for understanding the principles of composition and the use of color and light, and can influence the work of an artist just starting out. in short, it is impossible to make a significant contribution to any field of endeavor without first being strongly influenced by past achievements. from medicine to literature, from art to science, the past provides us with the knowledge and understanding we need to make a meaningful contribution of our
