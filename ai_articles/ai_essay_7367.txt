success is an ambiguous term that can be defined differently depending on ones perspective. however, it is universally acknowledged that academic and professional success is a crucial component in the pursuit of a fulfilling life. while some argue that survival in a new environment is enough to achieve success, i firmly believe that success requires an individual to go a step further and change the environment they are in. this essay will explore why i agree with this statement and provide specific reasons and examples to support my position. to begin with, surviving in a new environment is no easy feat. it requires individuals to adapt to new cultures, people, and ways of thinking. however, merely surviving in a new environment does not necessarily equate to success. for instance, an international student may struggle to adjust to a new culture and social norms. however, if they do not make an effort to learn, grow and impact their environment positively, they are unlikely to achieve true success. on the other hand, changing the environment one is in is a sign of true success. it entails making a positive impact that is felt by others and leaves a lasting legacy. a good example is the renowned physicist, albert einstein, who changed the world with his groundbreaking theories about space and time. his contributions to science paved the way for future discoveries that have transformed the world we live in today. furthermore, changing the environment also requires individuals to think creatively, take calculated risks, and be innovative. this is because creating change often involves going against the norm and challenging existing systems. a good example of this is steve jobs, the cofounder of apple inc. he transformed the world of technology by introducing revolutionary products such as the iphone, ipod, and ipad. his innovative thinking disrupted the status quo and led to the birth of a new era of technology. in addition, changing the environment also involves creating opportunities for others. when individuals use their success to empower others, they create a ripple effect that positively impacts society. for instance, oprah winfrey, one of the worlds most successful media personalities, has used her success to create opportunities for others through her charity initiatives such as the oprah winfrey leadership academy for girls. her efforts have positively impacted the lives of many young girls who would otherwise have limited opportunities. in conclusion, success in academic and professional spheres goes beyond survival in a new environment. while adaptation to new cultures and ways of thinking is essential, true success requires individuals to impact their environment positively and create change. those who have made significant contributions to the world have done so by thinking creatively, taking calculated risks, and being innovative. they have also created opportunities for others, leaving a lasting legacy that positively impacts society. therefore, i agree that success is not merely surviving in new environments but creating positive change in them.
