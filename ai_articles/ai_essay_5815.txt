the internet has become the goto source for information for many people. you can find out about anything you want with just a few clicks. however, there are still many people who prefer books. there are a few reasons for this. first, books are more reliable than the internet. anyone can post anything on the internet, without any factchecking. this means that you cant always trust what you read online. books, on the other hand, are usually written by experts in the field and have to go through a rigorous editing process before they are published. this means that you can be sure that the information in a book is accurate. second, books are more indepth than most internet sources. if you want to learn about a topic, you can find a book that will go into great detail about that topic. on the internet, you are usually limited to articles or blog posts, which are usually only a few thousand words long. third, books are more convenient than the internet in some ways. you can take a book with you anywhere you go and read it whenever you have some free time. with the internet, you need to have an internet connection and a device to access it. overall, books are still a better source of information than the internet for many people.
