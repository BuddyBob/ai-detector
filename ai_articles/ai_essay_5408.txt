nowadays, edictionaries are increasingly popular among students. however, some teachers think that the overuse of edictionaries might have more disadvantages than advantages for english learning. these teachers argue that, like the use of calculator affecting the skill of calculating, reliance on edictionaries may lead to the deteriorating of our spelling ability. i disagree with the opinion of these teachers. to begin with, edictionaries can help us to learn english words more efficiently. in the past, we had to look up words in paper dictionaries, which was not only timeconsuming, but also inconvenient. with the help of edictionaries, we can look up words with just a few clicks. for example, when we encounter an unknown word while reading, we can quickly look it up in the edictionary, which saves us a lot of time. in addition, edictionaries usually provide more information than paper dictionaries. they often give us the pronunciation, definition, example sentences, and even synonyms and antonyms of the word we are looking up. with all this information, we can learn the word more thoroughly. last but not least, edictionaries are more portable than paper dictionaries. we can carry them around in our phones or laptops and use them anytime and anywhere. this is especially helpful when we travel abroad and need to look up words in a foreign language. in conclusion, i believe that the overuse of edictionaries has more advantages than disadvantages for english learning.
