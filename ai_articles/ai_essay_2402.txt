coding is an essential part of robotics that allows for the movement and actions of robots to be controlled. it involves programming the robot to move how the user intends, such as lifting an arm, turning its head, and more. in order to ensure accuracy, coding must be detailed and precise. teams of people can collaborate to build robots, with each team member playing an important role. for instance, one team member might be responsible for coding, while another creates diagrams to represent wiring or designs. some teams might also involve members who specialize in problem solving and working out complex engineering tasks. working as a group allows for faster problemsolving and helps support coming up with innovative solutions. group members can also offer feedback and input to each other to improve their designs or troubleshoot any issues that may arise. by combining individual efforts, teams are better equipped to build a functional, reliable robot.
