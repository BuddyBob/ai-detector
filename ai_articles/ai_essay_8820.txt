for centuries, the city of paris has been renowned for its iconic monuments and romantic atmosphere. from the eiffel tower to notre dame cathedral, visitors from around the world flock to this beautiful city each year on a quest to experience all it has to offer. however, many tourists overlook one of pariss most prized attractionsthe parc des butteschaumont. built in  by napoleon iii, parc des butteschaumont is a stunning urban oasis tucked away in northeast paris an unexpected respite among a sea of buildings and streets. the park covers more than twenty acres of sprawling green fields and hills crisscrossed with winding paths that lead up towards its highest point a rocky outcrop known as temple de la sibylle which houses an impressive greek temple at its peak. this scenic outlook provides breathtaking views of both downtown paris and northern france beyond it  making it the perfect spot for watching the sunrise or sunset over the city of lights!. parc des butteschaumont also offers plenty to do besides admiring picturesque vistas  including picnic areas for enjoying lunch with friends or family, playgrounds for children complete with climbing walls!, gardens full of native plants such as lavender and rosemary that bloom throughout spring  summer months, even boat rides across lac daumesnil! furthermore, if youre looking for something truly unique there are several sculptures sprinkled around park grounds including jean carriès famous sculpture the lovers  two intertwined figures embracing one another atop their own pedestal  which adds just enough whimsy  charm no matter what time you visit!. so next time you find yourself visiting paris dont forget about this hidden gem nestled within bustling city limits take some time out your itinerary  explore every corner parc des butteschamuont
