there is no denying that advertisement plays a significant role in our life, which affects what we buy and how we think. it is, therefore, not surprising that some people believe that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this idea to some extent for the reasons given below. to begin with, one of the reasons why i think that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are is that they always show the best side of the products. they never reveal the flaws of the products because their ultimate goal is to sell as many products as possible. for example, when a new phone is about to be released, the advertisement will only show the phones best features, such as its design, new functions, and so on. they will never mention anything about the phones battery life, which is probably one of the most important factors that people consider when buying a new phone. as a result, people may end up being disappointed with the product because it does not live up to their expectations. another reason why i think that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are is that they often use celebrities to endorse their products. we all know that celebrities are not like us, they are richer and more famous. therefore, when they endorse a product, it gives people the impression that the product is really good because even celebrities use it. take the example of clothes, people tend to buy the same clothes that celebrities wear even though they may not look good in them. this is because they think that since celebrities can pull off any style, they can too. in reality, this is not always the case. in conclusion, even though i agree that most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are, i do not think that they are completely misleading. this is because advertisements also have to reveal some truth about the products in order to gain peoples trust.
