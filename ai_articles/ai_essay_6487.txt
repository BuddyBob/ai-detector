most advertisements make products seem much better than they really are. i agree with this statement for a few reasons. first, many advertisements use models or actors to sell the product. the models are usually airbrushed or have perfect lighting to make them look flawless. this creates an unrealistic standard for the product. second, advertisements use persuasive language to make the product sound essential. they might use phrases like you deserve the best or treat yourself. third, ads often show the product in use in a perfect scenario. for example, a shampoo ad might show a woman with long, flowing hair in a field of flowers. in reality, the shampoo might not make your hair any different. i think that advertisers should be more honest about their products. they should show regular people using the product in everyday situations. this would give consumers a more realistic idea of what to expect. additionally, advertisers should use plain language to describe the product. they should avoid using persuasive language that could mislead people.
