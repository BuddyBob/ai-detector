although the world of fashion can often be thought of as superficial and insubstantial, it is in fact an incredibly important part of our culture. fashion is much more than just a way to express ourselves it has been used throughout history to show distinction between social classes, advertise political beliefs, and even reveal information about personal relationships. in ancient egypt, clothing was often used as a way to differentiate between peoples roles in society and their wealth. clothes were expensive due to the cost of materials and labor therefore higher ranking individuals were able to afford luxury fabrics which further emphasized their positions in society. furthermore, during this period fabric dyes were also limited resulting in color being another marker for social class  with lighter colors symbolizing higher ranking individuals while darker colors indicated lower positions within society. this trend continued into medieval europe where ones station could easily be determined by their wardrobe choices alone. as time progressed these societal markers shifted from solely being based on materialistic items such as clothes or jewelry but what remains true today is that fashion still serves an important role in showing our place within different societies  whether intentional or not. fashion has also been used over the years for political expression  most notably by teenagers during world war ii when young adults wore bright red shirts with v for victory! written across them illustrating their support for freedom from nazi occupation. during this same time period women started wearing trousers instead of skirts although at first seen as scandalous this soon became accepted as a sign of strength and independence amongst both men and women alike who wanted change in the traditional gender roles set forth by previous generations. even after ww had ended there was still unrest among citizens who wanted reform particularly concerning civil rights issues which resulted in several other forms of fashion protest including afros becoming a popular hairstyle choice among african americans wanting equality under law enforcement agencies that didnt recognize black beauty standards nor equal legal treatment regardless if they conformed
