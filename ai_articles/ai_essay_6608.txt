there is no doubt that successful people try new things and take risks. after all, if they only did what they already know how to do well, they would never achieve anything beyond their current level of success. trying new things and taking risks is how successful people learn and grow. for example, consider the case of a successful businessman who decides to start a new business venture. he could simply stick to the tried and true business model that has made him successful in the past. however, he knows that in order to achieve even greater success, he must take risks and try new things. so, he invests in a new business that is outside of his comfort zone. of course, not every new thing that a successful person tries will be a success. however, even failures can be beneficial if they are approached in the right way. a failure can teach a valuable lesson that can be applied to future endeavors. in conclusion, it is clear that successful people try new things and take risks. this is how they learn and grow. it is also how they achieve greater levels of success.
