technology has had a dramatic effect on peoples lives in modern society. weve gone from not being able to communicate with someone on the other side of the world to being able to send messages and stay in contact with anyone instantly. its undoubtedly changed the way we communicate and interact with one another and its made our lives much easier in many ways. on one hand, technology has made it easier to connect with people from all walks of life. social media has made it possible for us to find out more about each other, make connections with new people and even to stay in touch with family and friends who may be separated by vast distances. on the other hand, however, technology has made it easier for us to overestimate the size and strength of our human connections. we often use technology as a crutch to maintain contact with someone but dont truly make an effort to form a meaningful relationship with another person that extends beyond the superficial. in some cases, technology has replaced real human contact and this can lead to a lack of meaningful relationships which is certainly not beneficial for anyone. overall, technology has had a huge effect on how humans interact. its undeniable that it has connected us more than ever before and made communicating much easier. however, it is important to remember that technology should not replace reallife human contact and interactions and that it should be used in moderation to maintain healthy human relationships.
