there is no right or wrong answer when it comes to whether children should be taught to compete or cooperate  it depends on the individual child and the familys values. however, i believe that children should be taught to cooperate, for a few reasons. first, cooperation helps children learn to get along with others. this is important, both in school and in life. in order to be successful, children need to be able to work together with their classmates and colleagues. second, cooperation teaches children how to compromise. this is another important life skill. in order to get along with others, children need to be able to compromise  that is, to find a middle ground that everyone can agree on. finally, cooperation helps children learn to be leaders. often, the best way to get things done is to work together as a team. but sometimes, someone has to take the lead. cooperation helps children learn how to be effective leaders. all of these reasons are important, and they show why cooperation is such an important life skill.
