success is something that many people strive for, but few actually achieve it. this is largely due to the fact that success requires hard work and dedication  qualities that not everyone possesses. however, two key factors that everyone can work to cultivate are a positive attitude and a sense of humility. through embracing these inner qualities, anyone can take their first steps into achieving personal success. firstly, no one gets very far without an uplifting attitude. we all know the power of positive thinking, and how it can often be the difference between success and failure. a positive attitude serves as an inner compass that shows us the path to our dreams, and helps us to stay motivated and inspired even when things get tough. additionally, a good attitude breeds motivation and optimism. this is invaluable when taking risks and pushing our limits, for it allows us to believe that no matter the outcome, well get better with each attempt. in contrast, a sense of humility is just as important in the pursuit of success. it is a trait that keeps us grounded and realistic it reminds us not to get too caught up in our successes or failures, and to remember that no one ultimately knows what the future holds. a humble frame of mind also encourages us to always be open to learning new things, and to stay determined no matter how long the road ahead. one impressive example of a person possessing both a positive attitude and humility was vivian paley, a kindergarten teacher, author, and nobel prize nominee. despite facing multiple failures and setbacks during her life, she managed to stay incredibly positive. throughout her career, paley embraced humility both in her teaching and in her works. she worked tirelessly to give her students the best educational opportunities, while striving to remain humble and dedicated in everything she did. in conclusion, cultivating a positive attitude and a sense of humility are integral elements in achieving success. not only do they form the bedrock of resilience that allows us to endure any kind of failure, but they also allow us to remain focused and motivated on our goals, no matter the challenges. with the right attitude and an open heart, anyone can open the doors leading to personal success.
