the issue of whether young people are contributing enough to their communities is a topic of much debate in todays society. while some people argue that young people are not giving enough time to help society. firstly, young people are contributing to their communities in several ways, especially through volunteering. many young people are actively involved in volunteering programs that aim to help disadvantaged people in their communities. for example, they may volunteer at local food banks, shelters, or hospitals. these programs provide young people with the opportunity to give back to society and make a positive impact on peoples lives. secondly, young people are also contributing to their communities by participating in community events and initiatives. they often participate in community projects such as cleanup campaigns, fundraising events, and community celebrations. these events provide young people with the opportunity to meet and interact with people from different backgrounds, and it helps to strengthen community bonds. thirdly, young people today are more socially conscious and aware of their role in society. they are often involved in social activism, advocating for issues such as climate change, gender equality, and racial justice. they use social media platforms to raise awareness and mobilize people to take action. by doing so, they are making a significant contribution to society and helping to bring about positive change. finally, it is important to note that young people have busy lives, and they face many challenges and pressures. they have to juggle school, work, and other commitments, and it can be challenging to find time to contribute to their communities. however, despite these challenges, many young people still make an effort to give back to society. in conclusion, i strongly disagree with the view that young people are not giving enough time to their communities. young people are actively participating in various community services and programs, and they are playing an essential role in improving society. they are volunteering, participating in community events, and advocating for social issues. while there is always room for improvement, it is clear that young people are making a significant contribution to their communities, and we should acknowledge and support their efforts.
