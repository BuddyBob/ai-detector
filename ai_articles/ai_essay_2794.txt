the idea that first impressions are almost impossible to change is common and pervasive in our society. it is often believed that what we feel and think upon our first encounter with something or someone will often remain with us for our entire relationship with them, and that changing those impressions is unlikely or impossible. research has confirmed that first impressions are hard to shake, and are often more influential than we think. in one study, participants judged actors more harshly upon reading their negative reviews than they did reading their positive reviews. this indicates that, even if something or someone is reconsidered or rehabilitated, people can still be heavily influenced by their initial impressions. for example, if you meet someone new that you dont immediately like, it can be difficult to shake the feeling and give that person a fair chance. the same can be said for trying new food, shopping for clothing, visiting a place for the first time, and any other number of situations. i believe this statement holds a lot of truth. when we are presented with something new, our imaginations often draw upon snap judgments and preconceived ideas to make sense of it. we try to streamline our process of understanding by labeling things we dont know with identities we do recognize. therefore, it is almost impossible to change that first impression.
