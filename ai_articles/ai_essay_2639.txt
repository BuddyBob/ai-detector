in my opinion, adding an extra half hour to the school day would not be beneficial for students. firstly, it would be detrimental to our families. cut into the already limited time our families have together, an extra half hour of school would be an unnecessary strain. this would affect students of all ages, from kindergarteners who need their parents attention to teenagers balancing work and school  making parenting and family time much more difficult. moreover, an additional half hour would directly interfere with studentathletes schedules. any extra time spent in the classroom would be time away from practices, conditioning, or participating in games. the extra half hour would make it increasingly difficult to not only keep up in school but to also support their team both physically and mentally. furthermore, it would be a hassle to quickly squeeze in doctors appointments or any other obligations students may have after school. having just thirty minutes extra wouldnt be enough time to travel to appointments, so students would have to miss class or find a new doctor song slot. finally, it is worth noting that the school day is already long enough. between student life obligations and homework, students have already committed much of their time to education. asking for an additional half hour of their day would be taxing, both mentally and physically. in conclusion, an extra half hour of school would not be beneficial for students, and i am opposed to the school board making this decision.
