the principals view that all students should be involved in a sport, club, or after school activity is an interesting one that warrants serious consideration. on the one hand, there is much to be said for the benefits of student participation in activities of this kind. students can learn teamwork and social skills, have the opportunity to exercise, learn additional skills and compete against one another, and become active members of their schools community. on the other hand, it is important to consider that this view is not suitable for all students. for some, the commitment and pressures of regular activity may be too great, or they may already be involved in activities outside of school that already fill their schedules. a sensible approach is necessary in which all students are encouraged to participate in as many activities as possible, but no one is left feeling overwhelmed. ultimately, the principals view is one worth considering, taking into account a balance between pressure and fun.
