dear senator, i am writing to express my view that the united states should change its presidential election system from the electoral college to a popular vote. while the electoral college was designed to give smaller states more influence, todays system can result in a president being elected despite losing the national popular vote. this goes against the principle of one person, one vote, and risks undermining the legitimacy of election outcomes. it is certainly understandable why the founders established the electoral college originally. as article ii of the constitution outlines, they wanted to strike a balance between the interests of smaller and larger states by having each states influence in presidential elections be determined in part by its number of representatives and senators. however, in recent elections this system has sometimes produced outcomes not aligned with the overall national preference. for example, in  and  the winner of the popular vote did not become president. those who argue to keep the electoral college point out that candidates might campaign differently without it. however, allowing a president to win without a plurality or majority of voters risks disenfranchising large sections of the public and weakening the mandate of the eventual winner. while the electoral college may have served its original purpose, today it no longer seems justified given advances in communication and travel that make campaigning nationwide more feasible. a national popular vote would align better with principles of equal representation and avoid scenarios where millions more people vote for the candidate who loses. it would motivate candidates to build broader coalitions and energize more voters across state lines. some counter that small states might get less attention, but candidates would still campaign widely to earn votes nationwide. a popular vote also has the advantage of simplicity  the candidate with the most votes wins. in conclusion, while the electoral college system aimed to balance various interests at the nations founding, today it risks undermining faith in elections by allowing presidents to be chosen despite losing the national popular vote. a national popular vote for president would align better with democratic principles of one person, one vote and give every american an equal say in choosing their leader. for these reasons, i ask that you support changing to a popular vote system for electing the president.
