while cell phones have become ubiquitous in modern society, using a cell phone while driving can seriously endanger a driver and others on the road. therefore, drivers should not be allowed to use cell phones in any capacity when operating a vehicle. there are several reasons why cell phone use behind the wheel is dangerous. firstly, cell phones are a major distraction for drivers. taking ones eyes off the road and hands off the wheel to operate a phone diverts a drivers attention away from their primary task of safely navigating traffic. numerous studies have shown that distracted driving from phone use impairs driving ability as much as or more than drinking and driving. even handsfree phone conversations divert the mental capacity needed for full attention on driving. secondly, cell phone use increases the risk of accidents. texting while driving is especially hazardous, as it requires taking both eyes and mental focus away from the road for prolonged periods. but even seemingly passive activities like making a call or checking notifications introduce cognitive distraction. research shows cell phone use is a leading factor in automobile accidents. it has been linked to increased risk of rearend collisions and less reaction time to unexpected road hazards. finally, phonedistracted driving endangers more than just the driver. when a driver crashes due to phone use, it often harms others sharing the road as well. innocent lives can be lost due to completely preventable cell phone errors. as vehicles become more formidable weapons when not under full control, anything compromising a drivers attention could have deadly consequences for passengers and bystanders. in conclusion, the serious threat cell phones present to traffic safety demands they not be used by drivers at all. a total ban on all phone handling best protects both drivers and the public by eliminating distractions on the road. although inconvenient, the responsibility of drivers is to drive safely without compromising focus. for the welfare of all, cell phones should remain out of reach when vehicles are in motion.
