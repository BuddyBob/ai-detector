some people argue that it is solely up to an individual to choose their own character traits. they believe that we have the ability to choose traits such as kindness, rudeness, helpfulness, etc. however, i believe that our character is formed by influences beyond our control. for example, we often see people around us display certain qualities and behaviors. we may be exposed to these characters day after day, and with enough of this exposure, our own mindset and attitude can begin to shift and adapt. additionally, there are certain lifestyle habits and environmental circumstances that can have a large influence on who we become. education, familial relationships, economic status, etc. all play a role in our overall character. similarly, genetics are also a huge factor in our overall character development. our values, thought processes, and communication patterns can all be linked to our genetic makeup. this can be seen in the many cases of people who grew up in very different environments, and yet have similar behavioral traits and characteristics. in conclusion, while we may be able to choose certain character traits, it is important to remember that our character is much more complex than that. many outside influences play a role in the development of our character. embracing this understanding of character development can help us appreciate the complexities of our personality and the various factors that come together to shape who we are.
