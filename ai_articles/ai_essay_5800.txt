the internet has certainly made information more accessible than ever before, but i do not believe that it will ever completely replace the book or the written word as the main source of information. one reason why is that the internet is not always reliable. for example, if you are looking for information about a specific topic, and the internet is not providing a satisfactory answer, you can always go to a library or bookstore and look for a book that has been written about that topic. additionally, books can be read in different ways that the internet cannot. for example, you can flip through the pages of a book to find the information that you are looking for, whereas on the internet, you might have to search for specific words or phrases. finally, books can be annotated, which allows readers to add their own thoughts and observations to the text. the internet does have its advantages, such as being able to share information quickly and easily, but i believe that books will always be a main source of information for many people.
