under the electoral college, candidates need to have transregional appeal to secure the necessary electoral votes for victory. this ensures that the president represents various regions of the country consequence of the constitutions malapportionment of the senate. by providing a larger political weight to large states, the electoral college ensures that candidates must campaign across both large
