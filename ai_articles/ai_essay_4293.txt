the school boards proposal to add an additional hour and a half to the school day has sparked much debate. on the one hand, the added school time could result in students having more time to devote to learning and preparation for standardized tests. however, it could also have a negative impact on their lives. the additional time spent in school could lead to fewer hours available for extracurricular activities, as well as lead to disruptive changes in students daily routines. furthermore, this extra time could mean that students have even less time to complete homework assignments at home. some argue that the additional hour and a half of schooling should be added as it could improve educational outcomes. however, others maintain that this additional time could be put to better use outside of the classroom. they believe that students should be able to find their own ways of finding balance between study and extracurricular activities. ultimately, there is no clearcut solution to the dilemma. it is important to weigh all factors and perspectives, as well as carefully consider how best to make use of the students time, both in the classroom and out.
