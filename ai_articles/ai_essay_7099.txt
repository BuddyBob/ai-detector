king of every thing but jack of nothing may be point of view of few people who think broad knowledge doesnt help. i agree with this statement. i think it is important to know a lot of things, but i dont think specialization is the best way to go about it. for example, if you want to be a doctor, you need to study medicine, biology, chemistry, physics, and so on. if you only study one of these subjects, then you wont be as good at it as someone who has studied all of them. on the other hand, a person who only studies one subject may not be able to do well in other subjects because they are not as wellrounded as they would be if they had studied them all.
