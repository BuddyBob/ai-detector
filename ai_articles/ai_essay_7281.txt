it is often said that the character of a society can be best understood by examining the character of the men and women that the society chooses as its heroes or role models. the reason being that heroes and role models represent a societys highest ideals. while there is some truth to this statement, i believe that it is an oversimplification of the complexity of society and its values. while heroes and role models do play a role in shaping society, they are not the only factors that influence its character. to begin with, it is important to recognize that society is not monolithic. it is made up of diverse individuals with different beliefs, values, and aspirations. as a result, the heroes and role models that one segment of society admires may not be the same as those admired by another segment. for example, a society may have heroes who are athletes, entrepreneurs, or scientists, but these individuals may only be admired by certain groups within the society. consequently, the character of the society as a whole cannot be fully understood by examining the character of its heroes alone. furthermore, the heroes and role models that a society chooses may not necessarily represent its highest ideals. instead, they may represent its lowest common denominator. this is particularly true in societies where materialism, consumerism, and celebrity culture dominate. in such societies, the heroes and role models may be individuals who are famous for their wealth, beauty, or popularity, rather than for their integrity, compassion, or wisdom. this does not necessarily mean that the society as a whole lacks these virtues, but it does mean that the values of the society are being shaped by commercial interests rather than by a genuine desire to promote the common good. nevertheless, there is some truth to the idea that heroes and role models can reveal a societys highest ideals. when a society chooses heroes who embody virtues such as courage, compassion, and generosity, it is a sign that these values are important to the society. for example, the civil rights movement in the united states was led by heroes such as martin luther king jr., who embodied the virtues of nonviolence, justice, and equality. by choosing such heroes, the society was expressing its commitment to these ideals. in addition, heroes and role models can serve as inspiration for individuals within a society. when people see others who have achieved great things through hard work, dedication, and perseverance, they are more likely to strive for excellence themselves. for example, the success of entrepreneurs such as steve jobs and elon musk has inspired countless individuals to pursue their own entrepreneurial dreams. by choosing heroes who embody the virtues that the society values, the society is not only expressing its highest ideals but also providing a roadmap for individuals to follow. in conclusion, while the character of a society can be partially understood by examining the character of its heroes and role models, this is not the only factor that influences the societys character. society is complex and diverse, and its values are shaped by a multitude of factors. nevertheless, heroes and role models can reveal a societys highest ideals and serve as inspiration for individuals within the society. therefore, it is important for society to choose heroes who embody virtues such as courage, compassion, and wisdom, and to promote these values in all aspects of its culture.
