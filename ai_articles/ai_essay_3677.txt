when it comes to deciding on a college course or career path, the process can seem daunting. thats why it is important to research the differences between university and college courses for different career fields and make a list of college courses available for various career fields. creating a timeline is also a helpful way to plan out your next steps and identify potential career options that are best for you. dont be afraid to ask questions! talking to students who have already made decisions on college courses or career paths can be a great way to learn from their experiences. it is also important to investigate the resources available to students to help them during this process. with the right resources and a clear plan, you will be well prepared to pursue your college course or career path.
