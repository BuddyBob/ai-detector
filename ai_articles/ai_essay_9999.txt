it is often said that the best things in life are free however, most people would not consider reading a good book to be one of them. as an avid reader myself, ive come to realize that although you may pay for the physical copy of a book, the experience and knowledge gained within its pages reward readers infinitely more than just dollars. the joys of reading can be seen everywhere libraries filled with shelves upon shelves of books offer free access to those seeking knowledge or entertainment. furthermore, online platforms like project gutenberg make thousands and thousands of books available for download absolutely free! with so much information at our fingertips  from borgess labyrinths to tolstoys war and peace it seems almost silly not to take advantage and read as much as possible. reading is also incredibly beneficial for mental health. studies have shown that when we lose ourselves in a good story we reduce stress levels by , leading us into a state which resembles relaxation meditation. not only does this provide necessary breaks from everyday worries but it also provides an escape away from reality until we decide otherwise. reading offers clear benefits socially too  conversations flow easier when backed up with interesting facts or entertaining stories discovered while flipping through the catcher in the rye or pride  prejudice on lazy sunday afternoons!. in summary, there are countless reasons why everyone should make time each day for some enjoyable reading material  whether borrowed or bought  because despite what many think, you dont necessarily need money to gain pleasure from literatures source of hidden treasures!
