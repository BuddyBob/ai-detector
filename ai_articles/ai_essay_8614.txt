when looking at the topic of modern day education, its hard not to marvel at its evolution. what was once a simple system focused on teaching the three rs  reading, writing and arithmetic  has become an everexpanding network of knowledge seeking to prepare students for an unpredictable future. it is no longer enough for todays children to simply learn about basic academic concepts these days educators are tasked with helping them develop skills that will last well beyond their school years. in particular, one area that deserves special attention when considering modern day education is emotional intelligence eq. while we may have all heard of iq or intelligence quotient  which reflects the cognitive ability and problem solving skills needed for success in everyday life  eq measures emotional capabilities such as selfawareness, empathy and stress management. in order to thrive in our increasingly complex world, young people must be able to read social cues and interact effectively with others around them while also understanding how their own emotions can influence decisions they make. these are soft skills which traditional schooling often overlooks yet are essential if children are going to become successful adults. fortunately more schools than ever before recognize the need for this type of learning and many now offer courses designed specifically around eq development or incorporate it into existing classes like literature or science where discussions around feelings naturally surface. the goal is usually twofold firstly, teach students techniques they can use throughout life such as managing anger constructively and communicating clearly secondly equip teachers themselves with strategies so they can support kids who may struggle in certain areas due to personal circumstances outwith school walls but whose overall potential could still be unlocked given the right environment. . . ultimately any educational approach should strive towards promoting growth in every aspect imaginable rather than focusing solely on attaining set objectives only by doing this will todays learners truly be prepared for whatever challenges lie ahead
