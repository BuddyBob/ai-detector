fitness is an important aspect of health that has become increasingly popular in our society. for a number of reasons, many people are turning to exercise and physical activity as part of their daily routine. in this essay i will discuss why exercise should be incorporated into everyones lives and how it can benefit us in the long run. one reason to include exercise into your regular schedule is that it helps to improve cardiovascular health. cardiovascular exercises such as running, jogging, cycling or swimming all raise heart rate which strengthens your heart muscle and reduces strain on arteries. this means that exercising regularly can help reduce high blood pressure, cholesterol levels and lower risk factors for developing coronary artery disease, stroke or other cardiovascular conditions. in addition to these benefits, regular aerobic activity also increases lung capacity allowing you to take deeper breaths when exerting yourself physically and reducing shortness of breath during everyday activities like walking up stairs or carrying grocery bags home from the store. another key benefit of incorporating fitness into your life is weight management. exercise burns calories so if done consistently over time you will see results in terms of fat loss along with gain in lean muscle mass depending on intensity level and type of workout being followed strength training vs cardio. exercise enables you to keep body fat percentage within a healthy range while simultaneously increasing metabolism due not only burning excess calories but also by building more lean muscle tissue  meaning even at rest you are burning more calories than before! additionally active individuals notice faster recovery times after workouts which makes sticking with a consistent program easier as well because less stress is put on muscles each time theyre worked out compared with someone who doesnt exercise regularly at all pushing themselves too hard leading potential injuries instead! finally dieting alone typically leads to slow progress whereas combining both proper nutrition plus frequent physical activity produces better results much faster since energy expenditure increases significantly when combined together rather than one without the other making hitting goals attainable sooner rather than later even when trying lose significant amounts weight quickly .
