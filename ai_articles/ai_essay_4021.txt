working in groups or working alone both have their distinct advantages and disadvantages. on one hand, working in groups can provide support, collaboration, and the potential for great ideas to come out of open discussion. on the other hand, individual work can foster autonomy, a higher degree of creativity, and focus on specific tasks. ultimately, the decision of which approach to take depends heavily on the individual. experiences can change an individuals perspective on which approach yields better results. a person who was successful in completing a project on their own might find more solace in individual work, while a person who was part of a successful team project may favor the benefits of working in a group environment.
