one of the most important skills of english for chinese learners is still largely debated. while some people think that reading should be given priority, others argue that speaking should be emphasized instead. in my opinion, speaking should be given priority over reading for chinese learners. to begin with, speaking english is essential for chinese learners as it allows them to connect with native speakers and gain a better understanding of the language. speaking english gives chinese learners the opportunity to practice the language in realtime, as it requires them to apply the knowledge they have acquired from their reading. speaking also allows chinese learners to interact with native english speakers and learn the nuances of the language, such as slang and idioms, that are difficult to pick up from simply reading. in this way, chinese learners will be able to express themselves accurately and confidently in english. moreover, speaking english is a more practical skill as it is used more frequently than reading. for example, when chinese learners are travelling abroad, they will need to be able to converse with native speakers to get directions and ask for help. in addition, many professions require the ability to speak english, so learning to speak the language is important if the learners want to pursue their career. finally, speaking english is essential for chinese learners to build confidence. as they practice and improve their speaking skills, they will be able to engage in conversations with more ease and feel more confident about speaking the language. this, in turn, will make them more motivated to continue learning the language and improve their fluency. in conclusion, although reading is important for chinese learners, speaking should be given priority over reading. speaking english allows them to gain a better understanding of the language, be more practical and build their confidence.
