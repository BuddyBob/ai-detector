there is no denying that computer games are popular among children. and while they may have produced some negative effects on childrens physical, intellectual and psychological development, i believe that the positives outweigh the negatives. on the physical level, computer games can help children improve their handeye coordination and problemsolving skills. for instance, the popular game angry birds requires players to use physics to calculate the right angle and power to launch the birds so that they destroy the pigs fortresses. on the intellectual level, computer games can help children develop their critical thinking and reasoning skills. many games, such as civilization or simcity, require players to make strategic decisions in order to achieve victory. and on the psychological level, computer games can help children learn to deal with frustration and disappointment. for example, in the game pacman, players often lose lives and have to start over from the beginning. this teaches children how to cope with setbacks and keep on trying. in conclusion, while computer games may have some negative effects on children, the positives definitely outweigh them. i believe that parents should allow their children to play computer games, but should monitor their childrens playing time to ensure that it does not have a negative impact on their development.
