it is important to understand the ways in which you can influence another person. there is a difference between talking to someone about your personal experience versus talking softly and calmly about their behaviour. when discussing your experience, you may get the person to empathise with your point of view and possibly adjust their behaviour accordingly. talking softly and calmly focuses more on pushing the person towards a goal. this method is gentler and can encourage the other person to act in the desired way. regardless of the approach, it is important to consider the consequences of both methods and to choose wisely.
