the purpose of a university education is a subject of debate among academics and lay people alike. while some argue that the main purpose of a university education is to prepare students for employment, others believe it is to enable critical thinking and develop a range of skills. i believe that while preparing students for employment should be a primary focus of university education, itshould also encompass other functions. firstly, university education should equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary for them to compete in the job market. this can involve teaching students how to write applications, practice job interviews and develop their communication skills. furthermore, universities should be teaching students about the tools which are applicable for the industry they wish to enter, enabling them to be job ready on graduation. this practical training can give graduates an advantage over those without a university education when seeking employment. secondly, university education should involve critical thinking and analysis. university provides a platform for students to explore academic topics, analyse and evaluate different areas of knowledge. this aids in the development of critical thinking, a skill which is invaluable in all areas of life and a trait which employers increasingly seek in job applicants. finally, university education should encourage students to develop their creativity. through the university experience, students should be supported in developing their own creative ideas, exploring and testing them out to discover their full potential. this can involve arts, design, or any other creative fields, and can provide students with a confidence which can be beneficial in their future career. in conclusion, while preparing students for employment should be a primary focus of university education, it is also important for universities to provide their students with critical thinking skills and the opportunity to develop their creativity. this can provide students with the tools necessary to be successful in their chosen career, as well as in life.
