fashion is something that can be seen everywhere in the world. it influences our daily lives, from what we wear to how we act and think. fashion has become a global phenomenon, with people all over the world embracing styles from different cultures. for many, fashion serves as an outlet of expression and creativity. the debate on sustainable fashion versus fast fashion continues to rage on in both academic and everyday circles alike  an important conversation that must take place in order for us to move forward into a more efficient future of clothing production. sustainable fashion is one way that consumers are attempting to make positive changes within the industry by choosing quality items made from responsibly sourced materials instead of purchasing cheap, disposable clothing manufactured from poorquality fabrics and harmful practices like sweat shops or environmental degradation. fast fashion brands may provide consumers with affordable trends but oftentimes ignore ethical considerations related to sustainability issues such as labor conditions and resource use during production. sustainable fashion practices have been gaining momentum over recent years due largely in part to increased consumer education about the implications their buying decisions hold for society at large this trend towards informed consumption puts pressure on corporations within the industry who must now adjust their policies accordingly or risk losing profits due to boycotts led by socially conscious shoppers. as such, its essential for those interested in making meaningful changes within the industry begin today by researching where materials come from before they buy anything new  asking questions like where was this item made? or what type of fabric is used? can help guide customers down a more mindful path when shopping for clothes moving forward into  and beyond!
