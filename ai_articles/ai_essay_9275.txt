in todays world, it is becoming increasingly difficult to find moments of peace and relaxation. everyone seems to be constantly on the go, always rushing from one place to another with their days filled with work and obligations. as a result, many people are turning towards mindfulness practices such as yoga or meditation in order to bring some calm back into their lives. one particular mindful practice that has been gaining popularity lately is forest bathing. forest bathingor shinrinyokuis an ancient japanese therapy that involves taking a leisurely stroll through nature while intentionally engaging all five senses sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch. ultimately its goal is for the individual to become deeply attuned with hisher self and environment by connecting with natures energy in order to revitalize both body and mind. the idea behind this practice comes from the belief that spending time amongst trees can have profound healing effects on our physical health like lowering cortisol levels as well as our mental health by reducing anxiety. as someone who has recently taken up forest bathing myself i can honestly say it has had a tremendous impact on my life from being physically active outdoors instead of just sitting inside all day to feeling much calmer after going out for walks in nearby parks or woodlands where i can connect more deeply within myself and bask in natures pure beauty without any distractions or outside stimulus ruining my experience. even though these trips dont typically last longer than an hour at most they still feel incredibly nourishing each time i come back after returning home close having spent quality time outdoors breathing fresh air amid lush greenery which helps me recharge my batteries afterwards like nothing else ever could!. all things considered there really isnt anything quite like forest bathing when it comes down finding serenity amidst hectic modern lives so if youre looking for something new try giving
