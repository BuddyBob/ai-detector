animals have always been an essential part of human life, serving various purposes such as companionship, food, and clothing. however, there is a debate on whether animals should be treated as pets or resources. in my opinion, animals should be treated as pets rather than resources because they have emotions and feelings just like humans. firstly, animals have the ability to feel emotions such as happiness, sadness, and pain. they can form strong bonds with humans and other animals, which is evident in the way they react when their owners return home after a long day or when they lose a companion. treating animals as resources means that their emotions are not taken into consideration, and they are subjected to inhumane treatment, which is cruel and unethical. secondly, animals play a crucial role in maintaining the ecological balance of the planet. they help in pollination, seed dispersal, and pest control, among other things. treating animals as resources means that they are exploited for human gain without considering their role in the ecosystem. this can lead to the extinction of species and ecological imbalance. thirdly, animals are intelligent and have the ability to learn and adapt to their environment. this is evident in the way they can be trained to perform various tasks such as guiding the blind, detecting drugs, and providing emotional support to people with mental health issues. treating animals as resources means that their intelligence is not valued, and they are subjected to inhumane treatment, which can affect their mental health and wellbeing. in conclusion, animals should be treated as pets rather than resources because they have emotions and feelings, play a crucial role in maintaining the ecological balance of the planet, and are intelligent beings. treating animals as resources is cruel and unethical, and it can lead to the extinction of species and ecological imbalance. therefore, it is important to treat animals with respect and compassion, just like we would treat our fellow human beings.
