driverless cars sound pretty dangerous, if you ask me. if something technology wise goes wrong, then youre screwed. and who do we blame for a technology screw up? the driver, or the manufacturer? we dont know. which is why i dont like them. lets start off with what could go wrong. what if the car doesnt notify you if there was an accident, or if there was some sort of problem with the car itself? what if the car just broke down due to technological issues? lets say that someone got injured due to one of these errors. who takes the blame, the person who was driving, or the people who madesold the car? there are reasons that autonomous cars are illegal in some states. say you werent paying attention to pedestrians, and you hit someone who was crossing the street because the car wouldnt let you take control. what do you do about that? all in all, i dont like driverless cars. its easy enough for a driver to kill someone with a regular car, so how would a driverless car make it any better? technology could screw up, and that would be the end of it. dont make those things. theyre dangerous.
