driverless cars are quickly becoming popular as they progress in technology. imagine getting in a car and it taking you to your destination with just speaking to it or typing it in somewhere? would you trust it? would you be for or against driverless cars? i am against driverless cars. although driverless cars have been a fantasy to the real world, they are also a danger. driverless cars are only safe in certain situations who would want to be in a partially safe car? or better yet who would trust a partially safe car? driverless cars need the driver to take over once driving conditions are slightly modified. for example, if there is construction up ahead the car will make the driver take over. what if its early in the morning and the driver falls asleep or if its late at night and the driver falls asleep in the car and doesnt here the alert? the car will keep going right into the construction site. driverless cars arent so driverless in all honesty. the car still needs a driver to take action in slightly complicated road complications, for example in traffic. these cars are made to sound like the best thing in the world, but they do not include safety hazards nor their flaws. flaws as in not being able to drive around a part of the road that is under construction. driverless cars are not reliable. a driver is still needed to be in the vehicle when driving. in conclusion, i am against driverless cars. i will remain this way until they can go around small complications on the road, dont require a driver, and can handle traffic. until then the driverless car is not safe enough to go out into busy streets.
