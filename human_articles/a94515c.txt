the article driverless cars are coming describes an advancement in technology and transportation. in this scenario, google is engineering a vehicle that transports someone from one location to another without the person driving. there are both positives and negatives to this socalled solution, however. as the author attempts to inform the reader about this advancement, he states both positive and negative aspects of these driverless cars. the google car is suggested to use half of the fuel of todays taxis and have the flexibility of a bus. as a result, the number of people driving around solving the issue of unnecessary, slow traffic is limited and people can save an exponential amount of money due to fuel. another positive quality of the car is that, like the article is called, it is almost driverless. it can do the majority of the driving on its own, involving steering, accelerating, and braking. one of the few problems with this car is that although it is basically driverless, it still requires human interaction for certain situations. some of these include human skills such as navigating through work zones and accidents. this requires the car to notify the human to intervene amd temporarily steer the car. with this, i see too many issues. first, what would happen if a driver misses the notification from the car to takeover the wheel? second, what would happen if  cars moving in opposite directions malfunction? third, as the author stated, who would be liable for an accident due to this car? these are questions i feel are much too serious and should be answered first before this car is produced and sold. therefore, i am against the idea of this driverless car.
